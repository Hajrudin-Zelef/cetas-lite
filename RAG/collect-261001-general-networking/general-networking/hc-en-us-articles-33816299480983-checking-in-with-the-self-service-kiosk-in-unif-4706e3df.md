# hc-en-us-articles-33816299480983-checking-in-with-the-self-service-kiosk-in-unif-4706e3df

Checking in with the Self-Service Kiosk in UniFi Access
This guide is for visitors or guests who received a visitor invitation to access a site secured by UniFi Door Access. If you’re an admin trying to configure guest or visitor access, click here.
Requirements
- You've received an email, SMS, or QR code with your visit details, including the visit time, person to visit, and door access credentials (e.g, QR code, PIN, or NFC card).
Note: If you have not received any visit details, please contact your primary point of contact at the location you'll be visiting.
Checking in at a Self-Service Kiosk
- Approach an iPad at your scheduled visit time.
- Open your invitation email and find the QR code.
- Hold your QR code in front of the camera and pause briefly as the system processes your code.
- Follow the on-screen instructions, which may include:
  - Scanning your ID card
  - Taking a face photo
  - Waiting for admin approval
  - Printing your visitor badge
- Attach your visitor badge to your clothing and use the QR code on the badge to unlock doors during your visit.
FAQs
Can I check in at the self-service kiosk outside my scheduled time?
No. Access expires automatically after your scheduled visit time and is no longer available at the kiosk.
