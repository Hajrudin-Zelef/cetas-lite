# enterprise-en-configure-lacp-on-huawei-switch-thread-793922987021615104-66721385-697ead2e

In this configuration we will look to configure LACP between two Huawei switches. The use of LACP allows us to aggregate two or more ports between two devices, which gives us immediate benefits of redundancy if one of the connections fails or even an increase in the bandwidth available between the two devices. In our case we have 2 1Gbps ports on each switch that will be aggregated, which allows us to have 2Gbps between both switches.
The configuration is quite simple, in the scenario presented we have two switches and assuming that we only have 1G between the two devices, but the network requirements have increased and at this moment the 10G is no longer enough for our connection and it is necessary to increase this connection. Then we establish a second connection between the two devices and perform an aggregation with both switches using LACP. In other words, we will transform these two connections into a single logical channel with a total capacity of 2Gbps.
