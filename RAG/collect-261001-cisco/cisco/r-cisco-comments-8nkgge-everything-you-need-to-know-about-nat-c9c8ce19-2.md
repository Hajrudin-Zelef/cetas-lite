# r-cisco-comments-8nkgge-everything-you-need-to-know-about-nat-c9c8ce19

I'm going to be jumping out of the ISP world and back into enterprise so I'm not super familiar with what's going on out there.
Palo makes one product really well, the NGFW. Unfortunately it’s not a part of an ecosystem for layers of security. Their add-on products to the NGFW are sub par.
BTW, Palo isn’t even tested as an IPS against Cisco / Fortinet / others. They’re a really good NGFW, not much more.
The smaller ASAs are still around, and will be for a bit longer. The direct replacements are the FirePower 4K/9K models.
