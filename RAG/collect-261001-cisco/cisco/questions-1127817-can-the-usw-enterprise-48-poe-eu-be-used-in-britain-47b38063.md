# Can the USW-Enterprise-48-PoE-EU be used in Britain?

*Score : 0 | Source : https://serverfault.com/questions/1127817/can-the-usw-enterprise-48-poe-eu-be-used-in-britain*

I need to purchase a USW-Enterprise-48-PoE as our infra,
As the USW-Enterprise-48-PoE-UK is currently sold out, is there anything stopping me from getting a USW-Enterprise-48-PoE-EU and powering it with a standard UK C13 cable? I cannot see any other difference between the two models.
Also asked on UI forums but no response.

---

### Reponse — score 3

Can the USW-Enterprise-48-PoE-EU be used in Britain?
Yes, the datasheet says "Universal AC input, 100-240VAC, 10A Max, 50/60 Hz".

---

### Reponse — score 2

I have contacted Ubiquiti support who advised that,
Yes, you can use the EU model in Britain. I checked the datasheet, there is only a plug difference, other parameters are same.
