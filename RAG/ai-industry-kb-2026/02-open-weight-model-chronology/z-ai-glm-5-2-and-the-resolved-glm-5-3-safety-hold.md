# Z.ai — GLM-5.2 and the resolved GLM-5.3 safety hold

- **Mid-February 2026 — DeepSeek V4 pre-release reporting (not a separate model).** The Information reported V4 would release "around mid-February, just before or after the Spring Festival" (CNY Feb 17, 2026 — Year of the Horse); internal benchmarks said to surpass Claude and GPT on coding. It slipped: Huawei/Cambricon chip transposition plus two last-minute architecture updates (Engram memory, DualPath — "launched nine days after the New Year began") pushed the launch to the April 24 preview. Ecosystem confusion mixed three phenomena as "V4": a fabricated benchmark leak (SWE-bench Verified 83.7%, AIME 2026 99.4% — Epoch AI confirmed the FrontierMath scores were fabricated), the "MODEL1" GitHub leak parsed as Engram architecture, and the "Hunter Alpha" mystery model revealed to be Xiaomi's. "sealion-lite" (DeepSeek V4 Lite, 1M context, natively multimodal) leaked under NDA via an inference provider on 2026-02-26 [UNVERIFIED beyond the single leak].
- **2026-04-24 — DeepSeek V4 Preview.** V4-Pro: 1.6T total / 49B active per token (~3%); V4-Flash: 284B total / 13B active (~4.6%); both 1M native context, MIT open weights. CSA/HCA attention variants in a sparse MoE stack. Several sources describe April 24 as a Preview, not a final launch — family checkpoints followed: V4-Flash-0731 (official checkpoint July 31, 2026; Terminal-Bench 2.1 82.7, AA Index 50); V4-Pro 0813 (GA revision August 13, 2026); V4-Flash-Vision-Exp (August 31, 2026; 305B = 284B MoE backbone + 32-layer vision encoder, 14×14 patches — first V4 vision weights, MIT). [VENDOR] V4 launch figures: LiveCodeBench 93.5, GPQA Diamond 90.1, SWE-Verified ~80, Codeforces 3206; trained on 32T+ tokens with zero irrecoverable loss spikes, without Nvidia — on Huawei Ascend silicon. Note: one router registry lists V4-Pro 0813 as `open_weights: false`/proprietary — treat the 0813 GA revision's weight status as ambiguous; the April/July checkpoints are MIT on HF.
- **2026-09-10 — DeepSeek V4.1-Flash GA.** Not a V4 checkpoint refresh but a new architecture family [VENDOR]: 552B MoE, Causal Encoder-Decoder (40-layer Transformer split into 20-layer causal encoder + 20-layer decoder; decoder's global KV cache projected from the encoder's final hidden states), Compressed Sparse Attention 2 (CSA2), FP4 (E2M1) KV caching, shared attention indices across layers, global KV cache **890 bytes/token** (~1/4 of V4-Flash ≈ 75% reduction; ~1/437 of V1), SWA Bounded Replay (persistent KV footprint ~1/8 of V4-Flash), 196B-parameter **Engram** conditional memory module accessed sparsely via token lookup, MoE layout of 1 shared + 384 routed experts (6 active per token), continuously controllable reasoning effort dial (1–100), native vision (image tokens billed as text). Trained from scratch on 45T tokens (text+images); context extended to 1M at the 34T mark; 1M context with up to 384K output tokens; activates 8B per token during prefill, 16B during decode. MIT weights on Hugging Face. [VENDOR] Launch figures: GPQA Diamond 90.9, Terminal-Bench 2.1 90.6, Codeforces 3471, DeepSWE v1.1 74.2% vs Opus 5's 74.0%; the technical report itself concedes harder benchmarks: Terminal-Bench 3.0 30.0 vs Opus 5's 43.3, Terminal-Bench 4.0 31.2 vs 51.8, HLE 36.8 vs 56.3 (Opus 5)/44.5 (GPT-5.6 Sol) — strength is tool-using agentic execution, not unaided abstract reasoning; DeepSWE scores swing 65.5–74.2% depending on harness. No independent reproduction of the architecture figures as of 2026-09-22 [UNVERIFIED as independent measurement].
- **2026-09-14 — V4-Pro retirement from the API.** From 12:00 Beijing Time, all `deepseek-v4-pro` traffic routes to V4.1-Flash at V4.1-Flash prices, until V4.1-Pro ships; DeepSeek's stated rationale: "V4.1 Flash has comprehensively surpassed V4 Pro across all key metrics" [VENDOR]. `deepseek-v4-flash` and `deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp` remain callable via compatibility routing; new integrations should use `deepseek-flash`.
- Pricing lineage (see §14 for the full pricing table): V4-Flash API at $0.14/M input / $0.27–0.28/M output (Simon Willison: "currently the best value-per-intelligence model out there"); V4.1-Flash from 2026-09-10 at off-peak $0.15/$0.60 and peak $0.30/$1.20 per M in/out — peak windows exactly double off-peak; weekends off-peak. Output pricing roughly doubled off-peak from the old Flash tier.

### Z.ai — GLM-5.2 and the resolved GLM-5.3 safety hold

- **2026-06-16 — GLM-5.2 weights (CORRECTION: 753B total / ~40B active, not 744B).** The "744B" figure is the FP8-build/VRAM shorthand; the config-based architectural total is 753B. MIT license; native 1M-token context; IndexShare sparse attention (reuses attention indices across groups of layers to cut long-context inference cost); MoE with 256 routed experts, 8 active + 1 shared; 78 layers; MLA + sparse attention. Held the top open-weight position on Artificial Analysis at release (v4.1 score 51), framed in coverage as "the top frontend coding" open model with agentic strengths. Superseded by GLM-5.3.
- **2026-08-14 — GLM-5.3 announced; weights held ~2 weeks for safety review.** A first for the GLM line: Z.ai held the weights because cyber capabilities grew faster than expected. 743B parameters (announcement figure; same base architecture as GLM-5.2 — "every gain from scaled post-training/RL on long-running tasks, not a bigger model" [VENDOR]). [VENDOR] Reported: Terminal-Bench 3.0 28.3 (from 4.6), DeepSWE v1.1 66.9, CyberGym 84.5% (top score, ahead of Anthropic Mythos 5 at 83.8% and GPT-5.6 Sol at 83.6%), ExploitBench 54.4% (from 24.4%); GLM-5.3 found 2,436 vulnerabilities across 269 open-source projects (1,097 critical/high; 53 with CVEs; oldest flaw 1981; average bug hidden 26.6 years).
- **2026-08-26 — GLM-5.3-Flash weights (MIT).** 320B MoE / 18B active, native multimodal; previously ran anonymously as "Ox Alpha" on OpenRouter (topped usage charts after its August 20 debut) — confirmed by Z.ai to Bloomberg. Terminal-Bench 2.1 84.3 vs Claude Opus 4.8's 85.0. API $0.15/M input.
- **2026-08-28/29 — GLM-5.3 weights shipped (`zai-org/GLM-5.3`).** 753B total / 40B active, 756GB native FP8 across 141 files, bespoke GLM-5.3 License. Wave 1's open question resolved: the hold was a delay, not a policy reversal — "the first crack in release-openly-immediately did not escalate." AA Index 60 (v4.1 scale) / ≈45 (v4.3 scale) — ties or leads the open-weight pack on independent composites. No GLM-5.4 or successor found released by 2026-09-22. Independent Ed-o-meter (28 real-world tasks, Aug 23, 2026): 100% pass rate, 9.3/10 rubric, at ~1/5 the cost of GPT-5.5 (full run $0.28 vs $1.43). API $1.40/M input / $4.40/M output; GLM Coding Plan from $18/month.
