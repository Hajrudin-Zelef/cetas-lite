# Timeline and context

- **Ruei-Tsang Chang** — sales manager, Super Micro's Taiwan office; co-defendant in the indictment; placed on leave.
- **Ting-Wei Sun** — contractor; co-defendant; company ties terminated.
- **James C. Barnacle Jr.** — FBI Assistant Director in Charge (NY Field Office); DOJ's public framing of the Super Micro case.
- **Howard Lutnick** — US Commerce Secretary; signed the June 12, 2026 BIS directive on Anthropic's Fable 5/Mythos 5 (incident detail → §17) and the June 30 lifting letter.
- **Sen. Mark Warner (D–VA)** — vice chair of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence; June 11, 2026 hearing quote on the Mythos red-team exercise (incident detail → §17).
- **Reps. Ted Lieu (D–CA) and Nathaniel Moran (R–TX)** — sponsors of the AI Kill Switch Act (introduced July 23, 2026; status → §17).
- **Letitia James** — New York Attorney General; served OpenAI a subpoena June 12, 2026 on behalf of a 42-state AG coalition ahead of its IPO (incident detail → §17).
- **Xiao Hong and Ji Yichao** — co-founders of Butterfly Effect (Manus); subject to March 2026 NDRC exit bans during the Meta-acquisition review.
- **Philipp Emanuel Weidmann** — creator of the Heretic open-weight guardrail-stripping tool; told the FT he removed Gemma 4's guardrails within 90 minutes of its release (regulation context for open-weight enforceability).
- **Noam Schwartz** — CEO of the AI safety research group **Alice**; co-publisher (with the FT) of the May 25, 2026 Heretic investigation.
- **Xiao Hong and Ji Yichao** — co-founders of Butterfly Effect (Manus); subject to March 2026 NDRC exit bans during the Meta-acquisition review; resident in Singapore at the time.
- **Benchmark** — led Manus's $75M Series A (early 2025); the US-capital link that the "Singapore-washing" structure was built to preserve.
- **Tencent and HongShan Capital** — Manus investors; the Chinese-capital link the NDRC review foregrounded.
- **European AI Office** — now enforcing GPAI model obligations (Articles 53–55) against general-purpose model providers.
- **Meta** — buyer of the prohibited ~$2B Manus acquisition; told the BBC the transaction complied fully with applicable law.
- **MOFCOM / NDRC** — MOFCOM opened the Manus acquisition review (January 8, 2026); the NDRC issued the final prohibition (April 27, 2026).
- **Mozilla** — publisher of the State of Open Source AI 2nd edition (September 15, 2026); its "default-open" conclusion is the market-side argument behind the EU's open-source compliance advantage.
- **Cloud Security Alliance** — publisher of the July 2026 research note on EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations, a key compliance-playbook source for deployers.
- **The AI Integrity / open-coder-ai compliance-logging projects** — open tooling tracks for EU AI Act transparency and compliance logging, showing the regulatory response is already being built in the open-source ecosystem.
- [COMMUNITY] The compliance-logging and transparency-catalog tooling emerging around the EU AI Act is community-built open source, not vendor product — regulators have not published reference implementations, so the ecosystem is writing the de facto compliance stack itself. Enterprise teams should treat these [COMMUNITY] projects as the current best-available templates while watching for official guidance that could supersede them.
- **James Uthmeier** — Florida Attorney General; sued OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman individually on June 1, 2026 (ChatGPT as a "defective product"), with a parallel criminal investigation tied to the 2025 FSU shooting (incident detail → §17).
- **Steve Marshall** — Alabama Attorney General; launched a separate OpenAI investigation in August 2026 after the sandbox-escape breach [SINGLE-SOURCE, winzheng.com only] (incident detail → §17).
- **The BIS as enforcement actor:** the Bureau of Industry and Security's June 12, 2026 order against Anthropic made export-control the operative US instrument for frontier-model gating — executive authority, not statute — with Commerce Secretary Lutnick as signatory on both the order and the June 30 lifting letter.
- **Gen. Joshua Rudd** — NSA director / Cyber Command commander (confirmed March 10, 2026, 71–29 vote); the source of the Mythos red-team claim relayed by Sen. Warner on June 11 (incident detail → §17).
- **Tom Brown** — Anthropic Chief Compute Officer; recipient of the June 30, 2026 Lutnick lifting letter that ended the 18-day Fable 5/Mythos 5 suspension (incident detail → §17).
- **Andy Jassy** — Amazon CEO; flagged the Fable 5 jailbreak report to the White House, triggering the June 12 BIS order (incident detail → §17) — the investor-reports-its-own-investee dynamic at the center of the order's politics.
- **The 100+ cybersecurity researchers** who publicly objected to the June 12 blanket model suspension, arguing it hampered legitimate defensive research — the constituency whose pushback shaped the June 30 conditional-restoration terms (incident detail → §17).
- **D'Andrea & Partners** — the advisory firm whose analysis ("for the First Time") is the primary-adjacent source characterizing the NDRC Meta–Manus prohibition as the first AI-sector use of China's foreign-investment security review; its published decision text (Index No. 000013039-2026-00026) is this file's primary record of the ruling.
