# Cerebras (CBRS) — priced May 13–14; first earnings beat, stock faded on margins

- **January 2026 — xAI Series E: $20B at $230B** (cross-ref §20) — weeks before the merger.
- **February 2, 2026 — SpaceX acquires xAI (VERIFIED).** Musk announced SpaceX would acquire xAI in an **all-stock deal**: xAI at ~$250B, SpaceX at ~$1T, combined **~$1.25T** — billed as the largest merger ever (vs AOL–Time Warner's ~$350B in 2000). Terms: **0.1433 SpaceX shares per xAI share**; some xAI executives could take cash at $75.46/share; otherwise no cash changed hands. Musk's stated motivation: **orbital data centers** — terrestrial datacenters face energy/land constraints; "space-based AI will become the lowest-cost way to generate computing power within two to three years." xAI brought Grok, AI training clusters, and the X platform's real-time data; SpaceX filed an FCC request for up to **1 million** orbital-datacenter satellites.
- **Implication:** there is no standalone xAI IPO to expect — the SpaceX IPO is the de-facto xAI public listing.
- **April 2026 — SpaceX confidential S-1 filed.** Roadshow began **June 6**.
- **June 11, 2026 — PRICED: $135/share** (fixed price, no range — take-it-or-leave-it); **555.6M Class A shares**; **$75B raised** — more than double Saudi Aramco's $29.4B (2019); valuation at pricing **~$1.77T**, 7th most valuable US company, ahead of Tesla.
- **June 12, 2026 — DEBUT: Nasdaq ticker SPCX.** Opened ~$150–$168, closed **$160.65 (+19%)**; market cap above **$2T**; within three sessions reached **~$2.7–2.8T**, overtaking Amazon and briefly passing Microsoft into the global top five.
- **Structure:** retail allocation **~30% (~$22.5B)** — vs 5–10% typical — via Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood, SoFi, E-Trade; greenshoe for **83.3M** more shares (~$11.2B); total demand ~**$250B** (~4× oversubscribed); options records: **1.8M contracts day one** vs Meta's 364K record (2012).
- **Control:** Musk holds **~82.4% of voting power** (controlled company, Nasdaq governance exemptions) and ~42% economically; **366-day lockup**; his stake valued ~$688–866B at pricing levels.
- **What listed (424B4):** rockets produced ~**one-fifth of revenue** — the listed company is a satellite-telecom/AI-buildout business (Starlink recurring revenue + AI infrastructure); prior-year revenue ~$19B, still unprofitable; **Q1 2026: $4.69B revenue (+15% YoY), $4.28B net loss**; Morningstar intrinsic estimate $780B (~48% below pricing) [DIRECTIONAL]; **18,712 BTC** (~$1.45B) disclosed.
- **Forward plan:** proceeds fund AI compute expansion + Starlink; "Starmind" orbital-datacenter program: two **AI1** prototype satellites (~150 kW, Grok-running chips, ~70m span) launching early 2027; ~1 GW/year orbital compute by late 2027; commercial operation possibly 2028.
- **Caveat [UNVERIFIED]:** the "$2.1–2.8T" June/July market-cap figures and SpaceX's current (~Sept 2026) market cap and share price are heavily secondary-sourced; no fresh primary quote was pulled for Sept 22 — caveat explicitly.

### Cerebras (CBRS) — priced May 13–14; first earnings beat, stock faded on margins

- **Background:** the 2024 IPO was shelved over **CFIUS review of the G42 (Abu Dhabi) stake** — since divested; new S-1 filed **April 17, 2026**; 2025 revenue **$510M (+76% YoY)**, net income **+$237.8M** (vs ~–$500M in 2024). (IPO mechanics cross-ref wave2/05 §5.)
- **May 13, 2026 — PRICED: $185** (above the $150–160 marketed range; 30M shares; 4.5M-share greenshoe → total proceeds up to ~$6.4B).
- **May 14, 2026 — DEBUT: Nasdaq ticker CBRS.** Opened **$350 (+89%)**, closed **$311.07 (+68.2%)** — the largest IPO of 2026 *at that point* (SpaceX later eclipsed it); day-one market cap ~$66–95B (sources differ on fully-diluted basis — flagged in wave2/05).
- **Post-IPO price action:** popped to **$386** the next day, then faded **~30% into the $237 range** (typical post-IPO hangover: lock-up fears, profit-taking); closed **$226.72** on June 23 earnings day.
- **June 23, 2026 — Q1 earnings (first as a public company):** revenue **$193.4M, +92% YoY**, beating the ~$181–185M consensus; EPS **–$0.04** vs –$0.16 est.; hardware +60% to $111.6M; cloud/services +167% to $79.8M; Q2 revenue guide **$194M** (vs $178M est.); FY2026 revenue guide **$855–865M** (vs $824.8M est.); gross margin 46.5% in Q1 but full-year guide **38–41%** (temporary system lease-back from a customer — CEO said investors "misinterpreted"); stock fell **9–11% after hours** (~$226.72 → ~$201).
- **Landmark contracts (announced with earnings):** multi-year **OpenAI deal: 750MW, valued >$20B**; multi-year **AWS partnership** bringing Cerebras inference to Amazon datacenters. Coverage: Morgan Stanley **Overweight, $250 target**; Citigroup **$340**; Needham **Buy**.
