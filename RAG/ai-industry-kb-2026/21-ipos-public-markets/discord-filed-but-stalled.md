# Discord — filed, but stalled

- **September 18, 2026 (Friday) — public S-1 filed.** London-based neocloud **Nscale** filed a Form S-1 with the SEC (reported Sept 21–22 by The Register, Investor's Business Daily) to list on the **NYSE under ticker NSCL**. The S-1 does **not** specify share count, price, or final valuation. **NOT priced — state clearly.**
- **Target (press-reported, not in the S-1) [COMMUNITY]:** raise **up to $3B** at **~$30B** (Investor's Business Daily); The Register cites an FT figure of **up to $35B**. Bookrunners: **Goldman Sachs + Morgan Stanley** (The Register); IBD adds **J.P. Morgan** as a lead.
- **Business:** spun out of bitcoin miner **Arkon Energy** (2024; CEO **Josh Payne**) — the same arc as CoreWeave (ex-crypto-mining → GPU cloud). Datacenters in Europe and the US; 17 contracted sites (5 active); **1.37GW** active + contracted capacity; plans to expand to **10GW**. Projects include an AI campus for OpenAI in Norway and the Monarch Compute Campus (West Virginia) with Nvidia and Microsoft.
- **Financials (S-1):** H1 2026 (six months to June 30): revenue **$10.4M → $140.6M**, net loss **widened from $368.9M to $1.02B** — classic loss-heavy neocloud expansion. As of Aug 31: **$103.4B in active + contracted total contract value (TCV)**, up from $38B at end-2025 — but only **$2.6B** was attached to active contracts; the rest depends on projects being financed and built. **Anthropic agreements account for up to ~$44.6B** of TCV — with a warning that Nscale had not secured binding commitments for the financing to fulfill them [COMMUNITY; the Anthropic–Nscale/Nvidia/Lambda $35B deal structure is unnamed-sources reporting].
- **Neocloud queue:** analysts say **Crusoe** (met bankers about a listing in **August 2026**; then raised **$3.9B at a $30.9B valuation in September 2026**, giving it room to wait) and **Lambda** (reportedly discussing a **~$3B raise ahead of a possible 2027 IPO**) are also exploring listings — press-reported, not company-announced [COMMUNITY].

### Discord — filed, but stalled

- **January 2026 — confidential S-1 filed** (Bloomberg); leads **Goldman Sachs + JPMorgan**; March debut targeted (TechCrunch).
- **March target missed; stalled as of Sep 22, 2026.** An EDGAR review as of **July 24, 2026** found no public S-1, ticker, or date; prediction markets resolved **"No IPO by June 30"** (96% of volume).
- **Valuation tension:** $15B (2021 round) → secondary marks ~**$7–8.5B** mid-2026 [COMMUNITY]; revenue ~$600–725M ARR; ~200M MAUs.
- **Discord remains the canary for consumer-tech IPO appetite** — a consumer deal, unlike the infrastructure deals that priced in 2026.

### Pricing mechanics and deal terms worth noting

- **Cerebras priced above range** — $185 vs a $150–160 marketed range — the classic signal of an oversubscribed book; the +68% day-one close confirmed demand, and the subsequent ~30% fade to the $237 range was textbook post-IPO hangover (lock-up fears, profit-taking), not a fundamental re-rating: Q1 beat the consensus two months later.
- **SpaceX priced fixed, take-it-or-leave-it** — $135/share, no marketed range — the issuer dictated terms; the ~4× oversubscription (~$250B demand) and 1.8M day-one options contracts (vs Meta's 364K in 2012) show the book absorbed it. The ~30% retail allocation (~$22.5B via Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood, SoFi, E-Trade) is an order of magnitude above the typical 5–10% — a deliberate Musk-era retail-participation design.
- **Greenshoes:** Cerebras 4.5M shares (~$6.4B total proceeds potential); SpaceX 83.3M shares (~$11.2B) — both standard 15% over-allotments, both indicative of books that could take more.
- **Lockups as overhang calendar:** Musk's 366-day SpaceX lockup expires ~June 2027; Cerebras's standard 180-day lockup expired ~November 2026 — both are the dates when post-IPO selling pressure historically concentrates.
- **Control discounts and premiums:** Musk's ~82.4% voting control makes SPCX a controlled company with Nasdaq governance exemptions — a known valuation discount factor for governance-sensitive holders, evidently not binding on the June book. Cerebras has no such overhang; its OpenAI 750MW (>$20B) multi-year deal and AWS partnership are the demand-side anchors analysts cite (Morgan Stanley Overweight/$250; Citigroup $340; Needham Buy).
- **What "not priced" costs in practice:** Anthropic's $15B revolver (Morgan Stanley leading; Goldman, JPMorgan, Citi prominent) is pre-IPO balance-sheet ballast — the kind of facility that lets an issuer price into volatility rather than wait for it. OpenAI has no such disclosed facility; its deferral to 2027 is the alternative strategy — wait for the window rather than armor the balance sheet.
