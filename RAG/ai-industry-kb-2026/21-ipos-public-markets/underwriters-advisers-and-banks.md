### Banks and advisers: who is on every deal

- **Goldman Sachs** appears on OpenAI (reported), Anthropic (stabilization agent, unfinalized), Nscale, Discord (reported), CoreWeave's Sept 17 raise, and Nebius's share sale — the single most recurrent name across the 2026 AI pipeline.
- **Morgan Stanley** appears on OpenAI (reported), Anthropic (lead-left, unfinalized; leading the $15B revolver), Nscale, and Nebius's share sale.
- **J.P. Morgan** appears on Anthropic (support + revolver), Nscale (lead), Discord (reported), and CoreWeave's Sept 17 raise.
- **Citigroup and Barclays** round out Anthropic's reported support tier; **Deutsche Bank** joins CoreWeave's September raise.
- **Retail plumbing:** Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood, SoFi, E-Trade carried SPCX's ~30% retail allocation — the broadest retail syndicate on a mega-IPO to date.
- The pattern: a tight oligopoly of four bulge-bracket banks (Goldman, Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Citi) staffs every live or near-live AI listing — issuer-side leverage in 2026 sat with whoever could credibly walk away, which is why Anthropic's revolver-first sequencing and OpenAI's deferral both read as negotiating posture as much as market timing.

### Regulators and process actors

- **SEC** — confidential S-1 review (Rule 135 announcements); the 15-day public-registration-before-roadshow rule procedurally foreclosed any OpenAI Q4 2026 listing; EDGAR as the filing-of-record (no OpenAI public S-1 as of Sept 10, 2026; Discord's January confidential filing never surfaced publicly by July 24).
- **CFIUS** — its review of the G42 (Abu Dhabi) stake shelved Cerebras's 2024 IPO; the stake was since divested, clearing the path for the May 2026 IPO.
- **42 state attorneys general** — subpoena served on OpenAI June 12, 2026 (wave4/03 §6), a live IPO-risk disclosure item.

### Context: why 2026 priced infrastructure, not labs

The 2026 window opened with tech-stock volatility (the NYT's June framing of OpenAI's deferral) and closed, for the labs, with safety optics (Altman's September framing). What actually cleared the market were businesses with physical revenue to underwrite: inference silicon (Cerebras: $510M 2025 revenue, profitable) and a satellite-telecom business carrying recurring Starlink revenue plus an AI-buildout story (SpaceX: ~$19B prior-year revenue, rockets only ~one-fifth of revenue per the 424B4). Both priced into euphoria — CBRS +68% day one, SPCX +19% to $2T+ — and both faced immediate skepticism (Cerebras margin guidance; Morningstar's $780B intrinsic estimate for SpaceX [DIRECTIONAL]). Meanwhile the neoclouds — CoreWeave, Nebius, Nscale — funded multi-GW buildouts through converts, ATMs, and (pending) an IPO while equity markets absorbed the dilution: NBIS +178% YTD despite ~$12B in conversion overhang. The confidential-S-1 pipeline is the mirror image: Anthropic racing the pre-midterms window with a $15B revolver as ballast; OpenAI keeping the option open while its public registration stays off EDGAR. Consumer AI never got its test — Discord's stalled January filing makes it the canary that never flew.
