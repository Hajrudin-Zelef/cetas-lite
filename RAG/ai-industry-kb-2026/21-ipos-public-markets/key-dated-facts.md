# Key dated facts

Add up the year's AI capital formation and the public/private split is stark. **Private:** Anthropic $65B, OpenAI $122B [COMMUNITY], xAI $20B, Databricks (Series L at $134B), Crusoe $3.9B, Etched $700M — trillion-scale marks set without a single public filing. **Public IPOs priced:** Cerebras ~$5.55B, SpaceX $75B. **Public follow-ons:** Nebius ~$9B+ in converts plus a $1B equity sale, CoreWeave $3B converts + $2.92B ATM program. The public market's 2026 AI intake (~$95B across IPOs and follow-ons) is smaller than the private intake — and almost all of it is infrastructure balance-sheet funding, not lab equity. The implication for 2027: the labs' eventual listings are *liquidity* events for existing holders at private marks, not capital raises — the underwriting question is whether public buyers accept the private price, which is exactly the referendum Anthropic's pre-midterms window is designed to run first.

## Key dated facts

### OpenAI — filed, but 2026 listing explicitly ruled out

- **June 8, 2026 — confidential S-1 confirmed [VENDOR].** OpenAI confirmed it had confidentially submitted a draft S-1 to the SEC, announced under Rule 135; it said it had "not decided on timing" and that going public could "still be a while away." Widely reported as the confirmed filing date.
- **[UNVERIFIED] May 22 dating dispute.** One digest (pedro-bright/the-ledger, citing earlier reporting) and one aggregator (aitoolsrecap) date the confidential filing to **May 22** rather than June 8. The company-announced, widely reported date is **June 8** — use June 8 as confirmed; treat "May 22" as unverified.
- **June 12, 2026 — 42-state AG subpoena served on OpenAI** (cross-ref wave4/03 §6) — an IPO-risk disclosure factor for any future prospectus.
- **June 2026 (reported)** — the NYT reported OpenAI was already leaning toward a 2027 listing given tech-stock volatility and its own financial challenges.
- **August 19, 2026 — Friar "2027" all-hands.** CFO Sarah Friar told an all-hands that OpenAI will be a public company in **2027**, "or sooner if the business continues to inflect" (aitoolsrecap, updated Aug 28).
- **September 10, 2026 — still nothing on EDGAR.** No public OpenAI registration statement had appeared on EDGAR as of Sept 10 — procedurally barring any Q4 2026 listing.
- **September 12, 2026 — Altman rules out 2026.** CEO Sam Altman told Fortune's Alyson Shontell that "given everything happening with safety, right now would be an ill-advised moment to go public" — and, pressed directly: "I would say not 2026, yeah. We've got a lot of stuff to do." Reported by TechCrunch, digitalmarketreports, tallwire.
- **Deal shape (reported, not company-confirmed) [COMMUNITY]:** target raise ~**$60B**, target valuation **above $1T** (vs $852B last private round, March 2026); advisers **Goldman Sachs + Morgan Stanley**.
- **Financials (reported, mostly unaudited) [COMMUNITY]:** revenue surpassed **$20B by end-2025** (CFO-confirmed); one aggregator cites **$25B ARR / 900M+ weekly actives / 50M+ subscribers** in 2026 — treat as secondary, not audited; projected **~$14B net losses in 2026**; profitability not expected until ~**2030**; "losing $1.22 for every $1 of revenue" per one aggregator [COMMUNITY] — do not treat as audited.
- **Structure (confirmed):** recapitalization completed **October 28, 2025** — for-profit is **OpenAI Group PBC**; the OpenAI Foundation controls via special voting/governance rights and holds **26%** (~$130B at recapitalization); **Microsoft holds ~27%**; employees/investors 47%.
- **Implication:** the confidential filing is now an *option* rather than a countdown; OpenAI explicitly deferred to 2027+ on safety optics, with the July 22 breach disclosure (wave4/01, wave4/03) as visible context.

### Anthropic — filed; prospectus due late September, listing before midterms

- **May 28, 2026 — Series H: $65B at $965B post-money** (cross-ref §20) — the first time Anthropic eclipsed OpenAI on paper.
- **June 1, 2026 — confidential S-1 announced [VENDOR].** Anthropic announced it had confidentially submitted a draft Form S-1 to the SEC — four days after the Series H. Reported by NY Post, Forbes Africa, pulse2, techthreedots.
- **August 13, 2026 — FT: backers expect ~$2T.** The FT reported six backers expect a listing valuation of **~$2T or more** — an *investor expectation*, not company guidance [DIRECTIONAL]; Anthropic executives have not settled a target even privately and declined comment. A 107% premium to the $965B Series H. A **$3T** figure in some aggregators is one backer's arithmetic (30× a projected $100–120B 2026 run-rate) [DIRECTIONAL].
- **September 3, 2026 — Axios: revenue run rate and accounting caveat.** Annualized revenue run rate **$47B in May 2026** (company), up from ~$30B earlier in 2026 and $10B in 2025; Axios reported 2026 annualized revenue would exceed **$65B**. Accounting caveat: Anthropic records the full value of some Claude sales through cloud partners, then books partners' portions as expenses — net presentation would reduce reported revenue **6–10%**.
- **September 4, 2026 — Reuters trajectory.** The public prospectus — previously expected right after Labor Day — is now expected **late September**; roadshow **mid-October at the earliest**; listing targeted for **days before the November 3, 2026 US midterm elections**; a **$15B revolving credit facility** (Morgan Stanley leading, with Goldman, JPMorgan, Citi prominent) is being finalized ahead of the listing; those four banks also lead the IPO.
- **Underwriter lineup (FT, unfinalized) [COMMUNITY]:** Morgan Stanley lead-left, Goldman stabilization agent, JPMorgan, Citigroup, Barclays in support roles — explicitly "not finalized."
- **Backers:** Google (~14%), Amazon, Microsoft, Nvidia.
- **Implication:** Anthropic is the furthest along of the two labs and is racing a pre-midterms window; its filing sets the template for how a frontier lab reports financials, and OpenAI may benefit from watching how investors price Anthropic first (PitchBook analyst Harrison Rolfes).
