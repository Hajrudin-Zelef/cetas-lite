# Sequencing: how the 2026 window actually unfolded

- **The record book was rewritten once:** SpaceX's $75B raise more than doubled the largest IPO in history (Saudi Aramco's $29.4B, 2019). The previous 2026 record-holder — Cerebras at ~$5.55B — held the title for less than a month (May 14 → June 11).
- **Retail participation hit a new extreme:** SPCX's ~30% retail allocation (~$22.5B) vs the 5–10% norm, executed across Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood, SoFi, and E-Trade — combined with 1.8M day-one options contracts, it marks the most retail-accessible mega-IPO on record.
- **Oversubscription held:** ~$250B demand for SpaceX (~4×); Cerebras priced above its marketed range. Demand was not the 2026 problem — issuer willingness was: two labs filed and only one is marching toward a listing.
- **The financing-mix shift:** 2026's public-market AI capital formation ran through converts and ATMs (Nebius $5.75B converts, CoreWeave $3B converts + 35M-share ATM) as much as through IPOs — the neocloud model funds GW-scale buildouts with debt-like instruments while equity absorbs dilution on momentum.
- **The valuation gap the market must close:** Anthropic's $965B Series H → ~$2T investor expectation is a 107% premium [DIRECTIONAL]; Nscale's $103.4B TCV rests on $140.6M of H1 revenue with only $2.6B of contracts active; Discord's secondary marks ($7–8.5B) sit at roughly half its 2021 $15B round. Each 2026–2027 listing is a referendum on whether private marks survive public underwriting.

### Sequencing: how the 2026 window actually unfolded

- **January–February: the merger quarter.** xAI's $20B Series E (Jan) set up the $1.25T all-stock SpaceX merger (Feb 2); Discord's confidential S-1 (Jan) and Databricks's Series L at $134B (Feb) framed the year as a listing year. None of the three produced a priced IPO by September.
- **March–April: paperwork.** SpaceX's confidential S-1 (Apr), Cerebras's new S-1 (Apr 17) after the CFIUS-G42 overhang cleared — the pipeline was being built, not priced.
- **May–June: the two pricings.** Cerebras priced May 13 (+68% debut May 14); Anthropic's Series H ($65B/$965B, May 28) reset the private ceiling; Anthropic's confidential S-1 (Jun 1) and OpenAI's (Jun 8) filed within a week of each other; SpaceX priced Jun 11 (+19% debut Jun 12) and rewrote the record book. June 12 also brought the 42-state AG subpoena on OpenAI — the risk-factor headline of the lab pipeline.
- **June 23: the first reality check.** Cerebras's Q1 beat (+92% revenue) sold off 9–11% after hours on margin guidance — the market's first 2026 verdict that AI-infrastructure growth must also show a margin path.
- **August: positioning.** The FT's ~$2T Anthropic backer expectation (Aug 13), Friar's "2027" all-hands (Aug 19), Nebius's $5.75B convert close (~Aug 19–26), Crusoe's banker meetings — everyone set their mark or their financing ahead of the autumn window.
- **September: the window narrows.** Reuters's pre-midterms Anthropic timeline (Sept 4), Altman's "not 2026" (Sept 12), CoreWeave's $3B convert + ATM (Sept 17), Nscale's public S-1 (~Sept 18) — the quarter ends with exactly one live pricing candidate (Nscale), one racing candidate (Anthropic), one deferred (OpenAI), and one stalled (Discord).

### What the lab prospectuses will have to disclose

Anthropic's eventual public S-1 will be the first time a frontier lab reports under full SEC disclosure — the template OpenAI will later follow. Items already visible in this wave that will have to appear as risk factors or accounting notes:

- **Revenue presentation:** the Axios-flagged partner-accounting (full Claude-through-partner sales recorded gross, partner portions booked as expenses; net presentation would cut reported revenue 6–10%) [COMMUNITY] — a near-certain MD&A and risk-factor disclosure.
- **Customer concentration:** Nscale's S-1 shows Anthropic agreements accounting for up to ~$44.6B of Nscale's TCV — the mirror image is Anthropic's own concentration disclosures around cloud partners and neocloud capacity commitments.
- **Regulatory overhang:** OpenAI's June 12, 2026 42-state AG subpoena (wave4/03 §6) and the July 22 breach disclosure (wave4/01) are live risk-factor items for any 2027 OpenAI registration.
- **Governance:** OpenAI's Foundation special-voting rights and 26% stake; SpaceX's controlled-company exemptions (the precedent the labs' dual-class structures will be read against).
- **Path to profitability:** OpenAI's projected ~$14B 2026 net losses with profitability not expected until ~2030 [COMMUNITY] — the "we may never achieve profitability" paragraph, in the lab's own words, will be the most-read page of the filing.

## Implications

1. **2026's public-market story is "two priced, one public S-1 pending, two filed."** The only AI IPOs that actually priced are an inference-silicon maker (Cerebras) and a rocket company that bought an AI lab (SpaceX). Both debuted strongly but faced skepticism (CBRS margins; SPCX profitability/valuation). Nscale's public S-1 may make it the third priced AI-adjacent IPO of 2026 — watch the pricing window.
2. **The labs are keeping their options open.** Confidential filings give Anthropic and OpenAI SEC-review optionality; only Anthropic is actively marching toward a listing (pre-midterms window). OpenAI has officially deferred to 2027+, citing safety optics — the July 22 breach disclosure (wave4/01, wave4/03) is the visible context.
3. **The mega-round is the new pre-IPO.** $65B (Anthropic), $122B (OpenAI), $20B (xAI), $3.9B (Crusoe) — private rounds at trillion-scale valuations make the IPO a liquidity event, not a capital event. Details in §20.
4. **Infrastructure prints cash before models do.** Cerebras priced above range; CoreWeave, Nebius, and Nscale funded multi-GW buildouts via converts/ATMs while equity markets absorbed them. Public-market appetite in 2026 is for AI *infrastructure* revenue, not lab economics — Nscale's $1.02B H1 loss on $140.6M revenue is the stress test of that thesis.
5. **Perplexity and the rest are 2027+.** Srinivas's 2028 anchor, Figure's 2027+ candidate status, Databricks's deferral, Lambda's possible 2027 raise-then-IPO path — the queue beyond Nscale is dated 2027 or later.
6. **For RAG freshness:** track EDGAR for the Anthropic public S-1 (expected late Sept 2026), the Nscale pricing outcome, and any OpenAI 2027 announcement; re-check OpenAI's stance after the midterms. Discord remains the canary for consumer-tech IPO appetite. Re-check SpaceX (SPCX) current market cap and share price — the June/July $2.1–2.8T figures are secondary-sourced and stale.
7. **Governance is now a priced risk factor.** Musk's 82.4% voting control, OpenAI's Foundation special-voting structure, the CFIUS-G42 episode that killed Cerebras's 2024 attempt, and the June 12 AG subpoena all show that 2026-era AI listings carry governance and regulatory overhangs the book must explicitly underwrite — they belong in the risk-factors section of every one of these prospectuses, not in footnotes.
8. **The xAI precedent ends the "lab IPO" as a pure play.** The largest merger ever ($1.25T) folded the highest-profile would-be lab IPO into a rockets-and-satellites issuer; the listed vehicle investors got was 80% satellite-telecom/AI-buildout. Any future lab listing — Anthropic first — will be read against that template: investors now ask what *non-model* revenue the issuer brings.
