# Forecast vs realized — the audit's evidence ledger (Sept 2026)

- **Sam Altman (OpenAI)** — superhuman coding/research assistant by mid-2026; **automated AI researcher by March 2028**; AGI roughly **2026–2028** (tracker aggregation of the "Reflections" blog). Implied definition: automating the AI researcher — i.e., recursive self-improvement onset. [COMMUNITY/trackers] (source: skynetcountdown.com/agi-timeline)
- **Dario Amodei (Anthropic)** — **"powerful AI" by late 2026–2027**: "a country of geniuses in a datacenter." Deliberately avoids the term "AGI." Implied definition: Nobel-level across domains. (source: interview statements 2026; medium.com/@shamil.yagiyayev horizon-3)
- **Demis Hassabis (Google DeepMind)** — **"real possibility" of AGI by 2029** (Google I/O, May 2026); a tracker cites **2031–2034** (2agi.me); a **Sept 2026 interview** says **3–10 years**, "the foothills of the singularity." Implied definition: cross-domain scientific capability. Internally spread over five years — the single most elastic 2026 forecast. (sources: Google I/O May 2026; 2agi.me/blog/hassabis-agi-prediction-2026; Sept 2026 interview)
- **Elon Musk (xAI)** — **smarter than any individual human by end-2026** ("as soon as this year," Davos, Jan 2026); **all of humanity combined around 2030–2031**; on Grok 5: **"3 years of AGI+ leaves a 10–20% chance we all die"** (Feb 2026, Forbes). The doom-pole of the 2026 attention market. [DIRECTIONAL] (sources: Davos Jan 2026; newslaundry.com 2026/09/21)
- **Yann LeCun (Meta)** — dissenting forecast: current LLM/scaling approaches are **insufficient** for AGI; requires major breakthroughs in world models, mental reasoning, and planning. Argued 2026 that the field is on the wrong path. (source: public statements 2026)

### Forecast vs realized — the audit's evidence ledger (Sept 2026)

A compact accounting of which 2026 claims have measured support:

- **Supported by measured facts**: Sora consumer product discontinued (OpenAI Help Center, primary); DALL-E 2/3 deprecated 2026-05-12; Gemini Omni Flash API GA 2026-06-30 with published pricing; MCP ecosystem scale (9,652 registry records, 97M monthly SDK downloads); Devin/Cursor revenue run-rates (vendor-reported, unaudited but cash-flow adjacent).
- **Forecasts quoted as industry fact, primary unverified**: every agent-economy market figure (Gartner 40%/40%, Grand View $10.9B, IDC 1.3B, McKinsey $700–800B, KPMG $50B, Wolters Kluwer 44%) — all secondary-cited, methodologies not independently reviewed in this pass.
- **Executive forecasts, by nature unverifiable until their dates pass**: all five AGI timeline claims. Altman (2026–2028), Amodei (late 2026–2027), Musk (end-2026 individual parity) have checkable dates arriving within the next 18 months; Hassabis's ranges are wide enough to be non-falsifiable on that horizon; LeCun's dissent is structural, not dated.
- **Reported but single-sourced**: Sora $5.4B/$2.1M economics (CIOL); Kling $240M ARR (deep-research compilation); Cursor $60B deal (tech-insider/the-ledger, acquirer disputed).
- The ledger's honest bottom line: in 2026, the "AI revolution" had more dated forecasts about its arrival than measured facts of its value. The measured facts that exist are mostly about products that died or pivoted.

### The February doom-maximum in quotes

- **Musk at Davos (Jan 2026)**: AI smarter than any individual human "as soon as this year"; robotaxis "very, very widespread" in the US by end-2026; on the Terminator framing — "We don't want to end up in a James Cameron movie Terminator." The joke and the forecast were delivered in the same breath; only the forecast propagated.
- **Musk on Grok 5 (Feb 2026, Forbes)**: "3 years of AGI+ leaves a 10–20% chance we all die." The single loudest 2026 doom quote, and the one the Sept 21 Newslaundry op-ed uses to name the attention-market mechanism.
- **The arc**: January–February is the volume peak of the entire 2026 narrative — Davos's performed revolution plus the doom pole at maximum. Everything after March is the narrative being audited against economics (Sora), employment data (May), measurement (July), and its own machinery (September).
- [DIRECTIONAL] The doom-maximum coincides exactly with the period when there was least measured evidence and most event staging — which is the audit's point: volume and evidence were inversely correlated in early 2026.

### The AGI definition problem (why the dates cannot be merged)

- Each speaker defines the finish line differently, which is why wave3's audit refused a consensus date:
  - **Altman** — operational: automating the AI researcher (recursive self-improvement onset). Economic-technical hybrid.
  - **Amodei** — capability bar: Nobel-level across domains ("country of geniuses in a datacenter"); deliberately avoids the term "AGI."
  - **Hassabis** — scientific: cross-domain scientific capability. His own 2026 statements span 5 years (2029 → 3–10 years), making his forecast internally wider than the spread between Altman and Amodei.
  - **Musk** — comparative: smarter than any individual human (2026), then smarter than humanity combined (2030–2031). Shifts from individual parity to collective superintelligence without naming the mechanism.
  - **LeCun** — pedagogical: human-level learning efficiency and planning; argues scaling alone cannot reach it, so no date is given.
- **Tracker provenance** [COMMUNITY] — the two most-cited aggregations (skynetcountdown.com/agi-timeline; 2agi.me/blog/hassabis-agi-prediction-2026) are community trackers that mix blog posts, interviews, and second-hand attributions. Their methodology varies; entries inherit that looseness. The RAG keeps tracker-sourced dates labeled as aggregations, not primary statements.
- The practical consequence: comparing "Altman 2026–2028" with "Hassabis 2029" is comparing an operational onset claim with a scientific-capability claim. Downstream synthesis must not rank these on a single timeline.

### The Sora shutdown as the year's economic rupture — precise dates

- **2025-09-30** — Sora 2 launch as standalone iOS app (VentureBeat): synchronized audio, Cameos, Sora 2 Pro for ChatGPT Pro subscribers. (One secondary page dated this "2026-09-30" — a one-year-off error, corrected here.)
- **2026-03-24** — shutdown announced via the official Sora account; API developers notified of the Sora 2 deprecation the same day. Secondary reporting attributes the call to Altman on applications-CEO Fidji Simo's advice ("side quests"). A reported $1B Disney partnership was affected; per the Wall Street Journal, Disney was informed shortly before the public announcement and no money had changed hands.
- **2026-04-26** — Sora web/app discontinued. Moderation disputes (MLK Jr. estate, Robin Williams estate) had run through its life.
- **2026-09-24** — Sora API discontinued (maintenance-only until then); users export at sora.chatgpt.com/sunset; data permanently deleted afterward. Imminent, not retrospective, relative to the 2026-09-22 writing date of the audit.
- The surviving pieces: the Sora 2 *model* remains in ChatGPT paid tiers; the team continues as a world-simulation research unit (CIOL, secondary; no OpenAI-primary confirmation found). "OpenAI abandoned video" is wrong; "OpenAI exited the consumer video product" is right.
- Economic reading: a flagship-quality product died in under seven months for economics, not capability — and its death was announced within two months of Davos's peak agentic optimism. [DIRECTIONAL]
