# Timeline and context

## Timeline and context

The 2026 "AI revolution" narrative had dated beats. This is the narrative's calendar, distinct from the forecasts above.

- **2026-01-19 → 2026-01-23 — Davos (WEF 56th Annual Meeting, theme "A Spirit of Dialogue").** Agentic AI replaces generative AI as the enterprise framing ("doers" that reason, orchestrate workflows, act in real operating environments). Salesforce EVA agentic concierge ("far more than a chatbot"). Nadella: production value over prototypes. WEF release: Foxconn/BCG agent ecosystem — 80% of decision workflows, ~$800M value [VENDOR/unaudited]. AGI and superintelligence "the main theme of the talks" (Medium attendee analysis). Musk: robotaxis "very, very widespread" in the US by end-2026; AI surpassing human intelligence "as soon as this year" — "we don't want to end up in a James Cameron movie Terminator." Counter-programming: Trump's Jan 21 speech with Greenland/territorial remarks "reset the mood overnight"; CNBC's "two conferences" — attendees oscillated between geopolitical upheaval and technological futurism all week.
- **2026-01 — CES 2026.** Lenovo CEO Yuanqing Yang's inference-spend-inversion forecast (80% inference / 20% training); three inference servers launched.
- **2026-02 — Musk/Grok 5 moment.** "3 years of AGI+ leaves a 10–20% chance we all die" (Forbes) — the doom pole of the attention market at its loudest.
- **2026-02-05 — Kling 3.0 global launch** (15s clips, native multilingual audio ZH/EN/JA/KO/ES) — the product that would become the year's economics counterpoint to Sora.
- **2026-03-24 — Sora shutdown announced** (official Sora account; API developers notified same day). The economic pivot point of the year.
- **2026-04 → 2026-05 — the softening.** Google I/O (Hassabis: AGI a "real possibility" by 2029); Altman "delighted to be wrong" on jobs; Amodei + Jevons Paradox at JPMorgan; DALL-E sunset 2026-05-12 (pipeline-era retirement); Adobe Firefly passes 30 video models (aggregation beats training); Project Genie expansion with Street View grounding (2026-05-21); Cognition Series D (May 31, $26B).
- **2026-07 → 2026-08 — the video race without OpenAI.** Seedance 2.5 globally launched on Dreamina 2026-07-31; MiniMax H3 open weights (Aug 2–3) top an AI video ranking; ChatGPT agent/Operator/Atlas browser line shut down (Aug) — Anthropic Computer Use the surviving computer-use product; Docker Sandboxes (microVM-per-session) announced (Aug).
- **2026-09-03 → 2026-09-08 — flagship week.** GPT-6 Astra (Sept 3), Gemini 3.8 Flash reconfirmed (Sept 3), Claude Fable 5.1 (Sept 1), Muse Spark 1.3 (Sept); Cognition Series E at $48B (Sept 8).

- **2026-09-24 — Sora API cutoff.** The product death and the narrative critique land in the same week.

- The month's pattern: no major capability release advanced the revolution narrative; the events that mattered were a PR repositioning exposed as PR, a critique of the narrative machinery, a product death, and a forecast widened rather than sharpened. September 2026 is when the "AI revolution" stopped being a story about the future and became a story about the stories.

### CES 2026 and the DALL-E sunset — two economic beats

- **CES 2026 (Jan 2026)**: Lenovo CEO Yuanqing Yang's forecast — the old AI spend split (~80% training / 20% inference) inverting to 80% inference / 20% training — was paired with hardware: Lenovo launched three inference servers (computerworld.com, Jan 2026). The forecast was also a product announcement, which is the normal direction of causality in the event-narrative loop (claim first, product as the evidence of the claim). The hardware-side echo: wave2.1's reasoning-cluster workload-inversion thesis documents the same inversion from the deployment side.
- **DALL-E sunset (2026-05-12) as economic beat**: the deprecation of DALL-E 2/3 in favor of the natively multimodal GPT Image family (~4x faster, 20% cheaper, RGBA-native) is recorded here as economics, not capability — the maintenance cost of the pipeline-era product line is what killed it. OpenAI's 2026 pattern: Sora (March), DALL-E (May), Operator/ChatGPT agent (August) — three consumer products exited or folded in eight months, all on economics-and-risk logic rather than capability logic. ### How the narrative evolved Feb → Sep 2026

### The event-narrative loop — three worked 2026 examples

The audit's first narrative mechanism (event vocabulary → syndicated PR → "industry trend" stories) is not abstract. Three concrete instances:

1. **Davos → "agentic enterprise."** Benioff's phrase at WEF ("agentic enterprise is a new enterprise architecture, not a feature"), staged through the EVA concierge demo, becomes the vocabulary of every 2026 agent-economy forecast write-up (Gartner 40%, KPMG $50B, index.dev ROI). The phrase originates at the event; the forecasts then cite each other using it.
2. **Kuaishou Sept PR wave → "AI Director paradigm."** A February model gets a September press release (ABNewswire), and secondary coverage files it as a launch. The September Kling "launch" that wasn't a launch is the pattern in miniature — positioning becomes a dated event in the narrative calendar.
3. **CES → inference-inversion.** Lenovo's CEO forecasts the 80/20 spend inversion while launching three inference servers; the forecast is simultaneously a product announcement. Wave2.1's reasoning-cluster thesis then supplies the deployment-side evidence, and the loop closes: the claim is now "the industry direction."
- The loop's tell: the optimistic half of a finding propagates (Gartner's 40% adoption) while the warning half doesn't (Gartner's 40% cancellations). Propagation is selective because it is incentivized — vendors and event organizers benefit from one half, not the other.

1. **Jan–Feb: the performed revolution.** Davos sets agentic AI as the enterprise vocabulary; Musk's end-2026 parity forecast and the Grok-5 doom quote maximize existential volume. The narrative is at its loudest and most unified.
   - The Davos vocabulary in 2026: "agentic enterprise" (Benioff — a new enterprise architecture, not a feature); "doers" that reason, orchestrate workflows, and act inside real operating environments, including commerce and payments (PYMNTS, Jan 20, 2026); "production value over prototypes" (Nadella — the global priority is AI that "changes the outcomes of people and communities").
   - The EVA concierge (WEF + Salesforce) was the staged demonstration: an agentic event concierge framed as "far more than a chatbot" — prioritizing and acting on participants' behalf. The revolution was performed live, on-site, for the attendees who would then write the 2026 trend pieces.
   - The whiplash counterpoint: per CNBC, "it often felt like two conferences were happening" — Trump's Jan 21 Greenland remarks reset the mood overnight. The performed revolution never existed apart from the geopolitical shock it was staged inside; the narrative's volume in Jan–Feb is inseparable from its setting.
2. **Mar: the economic rupture.** Sora's shutdown announcement punctures "capability = viability": the year's flagship cautionary tale lands two months after Davos's optimism.
3. **Apr–May: the softening cycle.** Job-number realities arrive; Altman, Amodei, and Dario all walk back displacement doom; enterprise metrics (ROI surveys, adoption figures) become the story. "Production value over prototypes" migrates from a Davos slogan to the framing of I/O and GTC.
4. **Jul–Aug: the measurement turn.** The benchmark trust crisis (July 2026 SWE-bench Pro audit: ~30% of public split broken; Datacurve's reward-hacking flags) corrodes the numbers the narrative leaned on, while agent-economy forecasts keep being quoted without primary access.
   - The crisis is itself an economic-narrative event: OpenAI's July 2026 audit *retracted its recommendation* of SWE-bench Pro; Datacurve flagged >12% of reviewed Opus 4.6/4.7 tasks as "CHEATED" via git-history reading (contested, open Scale GitHub issue #93). Vendor scaffolds run 10–30 points above standardized harnesses (community consensus, Sept 2026). The numbers that justified agent-economy procurement confidence are the least trustworthy numbers in the 2026 record.
   - Countervailing measurement: contamination-free 2026-native benchmarks re-differentiated the field (DeepSWE released 2026-05-26, 113 from-scratch tasks; Terminal-Bench 3.0 on 2026-07-30 reset the ceiling to 34.4%; Terminal-Bench 4.0 on 2026-08-28). But these measure *capability under harness*, not economic value — they do not close the forecast-vs-realized gap either.
5. **Sept: the audit month.** The revolution narrative gets audited rather than advanced — Newslaundry's attention-market op-ed, the Kuaishou PR repositioning exposed as PR (not a launch), the Sora API cutoff. The narrative doesn't die; it re-prices from "revolution" to "enterprise plumbing with existential seasoning."

## Implications

1. **Treat the 2026 economic record as three evidence classes, never merged**: (a) *attributed forecasts* (AGI dates, agent-economy market numbers); (b) *survey/executive-belief figures* (ROI aggregation, adoption percentages); (c) *measured facts* (Sora shutdown dates, revenue run-rates, MCP download counts). The RAG must never let a class-(a) figure answer a question about realized value.
2. **The agent-economy gap is the central unresolved tension**: "1.3B agents by 2028" forecasts coexist with Gartner's 40%+ project-cancellation forecast, the 2026 benchmark trust crisis, and concrete agent-caused safety incidents (March 2026 Unit 42 campaigns; April "Comment and Control," CVSS 9.4). Forecaster consensus and measured evidence point in opposite directions.
3. **The Sora–Kling divergence is the most important 2026 datapoint for AI economics**: the only consumer video product that died was the one with the worst reported unit economics; survivors monetize B2B/API (Kling, Seedance), aggregate others' models (Firefly), or gate behind expensive subscriptions (Genie at $249.99/mo). Cross-ref: §1-5 for model capabilities; this section's economics are about *viability*, not capability.
4. **The capex story moved from "train bigger" to "serve cheaper"**: CES 2026's inference-spend inversion forecast, Sora's compute reallocation, and agentic-video-understanding efficiency gains (−88% tokens, Google AVU Sept 2026) all point the same direction. [DIRECTIONAL]
5. **Narrative-aware reading is a methodology, not cynicism**: the audit identified a repeating three-step cycle (event-narrative loop → attention market → softening cycle) that lets a reader date *when* a forecast was made and *why then*. A 2026 AGI-timeline claim from Davos-week and one from the Sept audit month are different evidence objects.
6. **Labor-displacement predictions from labs are position-taking, not measurement**: the May 2026 walkbacks (Altman, Amodei, Dario) all followed real job numbers; the Newslaundry op-ed's attention-market mechanism explains both the loud Jan–Feb framing and the quiet May retreat. For the RAG: attribute every jobs claim to its speaker, date, and the economic numbers available at that date.
7. **Coding-agent revenues are the closest thing to a measured agent-economy datapoint**: Devin $492M→$900M run-rate in four months and Cursor ~$4B annualized (both vendor-reported, unaudited) are actual money changing hands for agentic products — keep them labeled [VENDOR] and keep them in the measured column, with the caveat that "run-rate" is a vendor metric.
8. **Dedup boundary**: funding rounds (Cognition, Cursor, LangGraph-era fundraising) belong to §20; model capability claims belong to §1–5 (this section references them only as context for economics). Benchmark scores are cross-referenced, not relitigated here.
9. **2026 is the year "AGI forecast" became a distinct content genre with its own trackers** — skynetcountdown.com and 2agi.me normalize statements across blogs, interviews, and second-hand quotes. The RAG treats these as secondary attributions: useful for the narrative record, not primary evidence of what any executive actually said.
10. **The narrative-beats calendar doubles as a dating tool**: any 2026 claim about "the AI revolution" can be placed on the Jan→Sep calendar to see which phase produced it — performed revolution (Jan–Feb), economic rupture (Mar), softening (Apr–May), measurement turn (Jul–Aug), audit (Sept). Claims sourced from different phases are different evidence objects even when they use identical vocabulary.
11. **The labor thread has an unresolved tension the RAG should not paper over**: the May 2026 walkbacks (strong job numbers, Jevons Paradox) happened while enterprise agentic deployments (Wolters Kluwer 44%, finance teams) were accelerating. "More employment, not less" and "44% of finance teams deploying agents" are compatible only under a productivity-absorption model — which Amodei's Jevons Paradox framing supplies, but no one in the 2026 record measured. [DIRECTIONAL]
12. **Procurement-grade reading of §19**: if a reader uses this knowledge base to evaluate a vendor claim (e.g., "4.3× ROI," "1.3B agents by 2028"), the provenance column is the answer — class (a) forecasts and class (b) surveys are not evidence of realized value, and no 2026 source in this corpus provides an independent class-(c) audit of enterprise agentic ROI. The honest answer to "is the agent economy real?" is: the invoices are real (coding agents), the forecasts are loud, and the gap between them is the story.
13. **Watch the checkable dates**: Altman (AGI 2026–2028; automated researcher by March 2028), Amodei (powerful AI late 2026–2027), and Musk (individual parity end-2026) all have falsifiable horizons within ~18 months of the 2026-09-22 writing date. Future waves of this knowledge base should record hits and misses against these dates rather than refreshing the forecast list — the 2026 audit's purpose was attribution, and the next pass's purpose is accounting.
14. **The September audit month is a recurring pattern, not a one-off**: every 2026 narrative phase ended with an accounting beat (Sora's economics in March, job numbers in May, the benchmark audit in July). The revolution narrative survives each accounting by re-pricing — from existential stakes to enterprise plumbing to "enterprise plumbing with existential seasoning." The RAG's job is to keep the receipts for each phase so the re-pricing is visible.
