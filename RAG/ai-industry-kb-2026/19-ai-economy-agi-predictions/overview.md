# 19. AI Economy & AGI Predictions
Keywords: AGI timelines 2026, agent economy forecasts, Gartner agentic AI, Davos 2026 agentic enterprise, automated AI researcher, powerful AI Amodei, AI jobs displacement, Jevons Paradox AI, AI capex inference shift, Sora unit economics, Kling ARR, inference spend inversion, IDC 1.3B agents, McKinsey bank savings, KPMG agentic spend, May 2026 narrative softening, attention market AI fear, coding agent revenue, Cursor acquisition, Cognition Devin valuation, Sora shutdown 2026, Foxconn BCG agent ecosystem, forecast versus realized value, AI video product economics

## Summary

- 2026 produced a durable two-track story: an **executive forecast layer** (AGI timelines, agent-economy market numbers, labor-displacement predictions) and a **measured layer** (spend surveys, cancellations, product deaths). The two tracks rarely touch — the RAG treats them as separate evidence classes throughout.
- **AGI timeline claims remain attributed forecasts, never consensus.** The dated 2026 record: Sam Altman (AGI 2026–2028; automated AI researcher by March 2028; superhuman coding/research assistant by mid-2026), Dario Amodei ("powerful AI" late 2026–2027; "a country of geniuses in a datacenter"), Demis Hassabis ("real possibility" by 2029 at Google I/O May 2026; 2031–2034 in one tracker; 3–10 years in a Sept 2026 interview), Elon Musk (smarter than any individual human by end-2026, stated at Davos Jan 2026; all of humanity combined around 2030–2031), Yann LeCun (dissent: current LLM/scaling approaches insufficient; world models and planning breakthroughs required).
- **May 2026 brought a narrative-softening wave**: Altman said he was "delighted to be wrong" about the jobs apocalypse; Amodei invoked the Jevons Paradox at a JPMorgan/Dimon briefing; Anthropic briefing summaries quoted Dario's "AI won't destroy jobs, it'll help companies grow." Apocalyptic framing retreated as enterprise adoption metrics became the story.
- **The agent economy is forecasts-heavy, measurement-thin.** Gartner: 40% of business software ships task-completing agents by end-2026, but 40%+ of enterprise agentic-AI projects canceled by 2027. Grand View: $10.9B global AI-agents market in 2026, 49.6% CAGR. IDC: 1.3B agents in enterprise workflows by 2028. McKinsey: 15–20% bank cost cuts, ~$700–800B. KPMG: $50B agentic-AI spend in 2025. Wolters Kluwer: 44% of finance teams deploy in 2026 (up from 6%). These are secondary-cited market-research figures describing spend intent and executive belief, not measured realized value.
- **Davos 2026 (Jan 19–23) set the year's framing vocabulary**: agentic AI displaced generative AI as the enterprise story; Salesforce's EVA agentic concierge; Nadella's "production value over prototypes"; Foxconn/BCG claiming an agent ecosystem automating 80% of decision workflows for ~$800M value (company-reported, unaudited). The same week showed the narrative performing inside geopolitical whiplash (Trump's Jan 21 Greenland remarks; CNBC's "two conferences").
- **The investment-macro beat of 2026 was the training→inference spend inversion**: Lenovo CEO Yuanqing Yang forecast at CES 2026 (Jan) that the ~80/20 training/inference spend split would invert to 80/20 inference/training; Lenovo shipped three inference servers. Sora's shutdown (announced 2026-03-24) is the sharpest economic datapoint of the year: reported ~$5.4B annual compute spend vs ~$2.1M lifetime revenue (CIOL, secondary, unverified methodology) — frontier video quality did not solve frontier video economics, while Kuaishou's Kling (reported $240M ARR Dec 2025, secondary) monetized the same category.
- **The narrative mechanisms identified in the audit**: (1) the event-narrative loop (Davos/I/O/GTC vocabulary → syndicated PR → "industry trend" stories); (2) the attention market (Sept 21, 2026 Newslaundry op-ed: doomers and accelerationists both monetize existential stakes into valuation and regulatory positioning — "forecasts are free; measurement is expensive"); (3) the softening cycle (apocalyptic framing retreats when adoption metrics arrive, reprices as enterprise plumbing).
- **Video generation is the canonical case of narrative meeting economics**: Sora 2 was a capability flagship that died as a product in under seven months (announced shutdown 2026-03-24, app dead 2026-04-26, API dead 2026-09-24) while Chinese labs (ByteDance Seedance, Kuaishou Kling) and aggregators (Adobe Firefly) monetized the same modality through B2B/API and subscription channels. The any-to-any model scoreboard lost its 2024–2025 flagship; the commercial lead moved to labs with working unit economics.
- **The labor-displacement thread follows the same arc as the capability thread**: maximalist predictions in Jan–Feb (Musk's doom quote, "jobs apocalypse" framing), walkbacks in May on contact with job numbers (Altman, Amodei, Dario), and a productivity-absorption model (Jevons Paradox) left unmeasured by year-end.
- **What the 2026 record does NOT contain**: no primary-access verification of any agent-economy market-research report (Gartner, Grand View, IDC, McKinsey, KPMG figures are all secondary-cited); no measured, independently audited ROI study for enterprise agentic AI beyond vendor-side survey aggregation (index.dev); no primary ByteDance source for Seedance economics. The RAG records these absences as part of the evidence.
- **The ~$300B IPO framing (CIOL, analyst interpretation)** — Sora's shutdown read by analysts as removing a loss-making product ahead of a reported ~$300B OpenAI IPO. Not the official reason (which was compute reallocation), but it shows what public markets in 2026 were expected to punish: prestige products with catastrophic unit economics.
- **Gartner's two halves are the audit's cleanest exhibit**: 40% of business software shipping task-completing agents by end-2026 (the half that survives into vendor decks) and 40%+ of enterprise agentic projects canceled by 2027 (the half that doesn't) come from the same forecaster. The narrative machinery systematically keeps the optimistic half and drops the warning half.
- **The checkable-date horizon is short**: Altman (2026–2028), Amodei (late 2026–2027), and Musk (end-2026) all made falsifiable predictions within ~18 months of the 2026-09-22 cutoff. The next pass of this knowledge base should record hits and misses against them rather than refreshing the forecast list — attribution was this pass's job; accounting is the next one's.
- **OpenAI's 2026 product exits form a pattern, not a list**: Sora (March), DALL-E (May), Operator/ChatGPT agent (August) — three consumer lines exited or folded in eight months, all on economics-and-risk logic. The revolution narrative kept running on enterprise vocabulary while the consumer frontier narrowed to what could be monetized.
- **The MCP layer is the one 2026 agent-economy number with third-party scale evidence**: 9,652 registry server records (May 24, 2026), 97M monthly SDK downloads (March 2026), 78% of enterprise AI teams with an MCP-backed agent in production (April 2026). Infrastructure adoption is the measured substrate beneath the forecast superstructure — integration plumbing is real even where the value forecasts are not.
