# AI Industry Knowledge Base 2026

**Scope.** Consolidation of 6 news-wave corpora — waves 1, 2, 2.1, 3, 3.1, 4 and 5 — covering AI-industry events of 2025–2026. Consolidated, deduplicated and reconciled on 2026-09-22. Approximately 13,000 lines across 22 sections.

**Methodology.** The source corpora were collected as successive news waves; claims were deduplicated across waves, and contradictions were either reconciled to the best-supported dating or exposed explicitly in the text (see the per-section Consolidation notes, Annex A). Every factual claim carries a provenance label (legend below); source URLs are quoted verbatim from the original waves. No new claims were introduced during consolidation.

**Label legend.**

- `[VENDOR]` — vendor or first-party claim (company blog, press release, executive statement).
- `[PRIMARY]` — primary disclosure: arXiv paper, official technical report, or first-party documentation.
- `[VERIFIED]` / `[CONFIRMED]` — claim corroborated by a second independent source or confirmed in primary material.
- `[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]` — partially corroborated; some elements remain single-sourced.
- `[SECONDARY]` — press or analyst reporting with attribution, not independently corroborated.
- `[COMMUNITY]` — community source: GitHub, dev blogs, forums, social media.
- `[UNVERIFIED]` — single-sourced or explicitly unconfirmed; treat with caution.
- `[DIRECTIONAL]` — trend inference or directional estimate by the consolidator, not a reported fact.
- `[EDITORIAL]` — consolidator synthesis or instruction; not a source claim.

Minor inline tags (`[ANALYSIS]`, `[POLICY]`, `[CONTRADICTED]`, `[VENDOR-REPORTED]`, etc.) appear ad hoc where a claim needed a narrower flag; they carry their plain-English meaning.
