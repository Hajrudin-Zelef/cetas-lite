# Revenue, pricing, and unit economics

- **Agility:** 65,000+ operational hours across 9 customer facilities [VENDOR]; 100,000+ totes moved at GXO Spanx; TMMC 7-Digit commercial agreement (Feb 2026); Schaeffler purchase agreement. The strongest paid-deployment evidence set in the industry.
- **Figure:** 40 Figure 03 at BMW Spartanburg (June 2026, logistics sequencing) [SECONDARY]; Catalyst Brands Reno deal (May 2026) [SECONDARY]; ~740 robots deployed vs. 660 employees [VENDOR]. Strong manufacturing evidence; deployment evidence thinner and task-supervised.
- **UBTech:** 1,079 full-size humanoids delivered in FY2025 (>80% industrial) [filing]; BYD, Geely, FAW-VW, Foxconn, SF Express customer list; ¥264M border contract [SECONDARY]. The strongest filing-backed industrial delivery record.
- **1X:** consumer deliveries begun 2026; no primary-source unit count. Strongest consumer evidence; no industrial evidence.
- **Unitree:** ~5,900–7,000 H1 shipments [trackers]; ≤250 productive industrial units (SemiAnalysis, 2025). Strongest shipment evidence; weakest productive-deployment ratio.
- **EngineAI:** zero deployment evidence; the entire 2026 record is DEMO (teleoperated combat).
- **Tesla:** zero "material factory usage" (Musk, Jan 2026). Pre-production.
- **Scoreboard reading [DIRECTIONAL]:** rank the industry by deployment evidence, not shipments, and the 2026 leader board inverts — Agility and UBTech lead, Unitree trails its own volume, and Tesla sits at zero. This is the table the taxonomy was built to produce.

### Revenue, pricing, and unit economics

- **Unitree (2025 filing):** RMB 867.8M humanoid robot revenue at an average recognized price of **RMB 166,400/unit** (~$23K); 5,215 sold vs. 5,511 shipped (296-unit recognition gap). Price ladder (Aug 2026 US listings): R1 AIR $4,900 → R1 $5,900 → G1 $13,500 → H2 $29,900 → H2 Plus $100,000. The $4,900 R1 AIR is the cheapest walking humanoid listed in 2026 — the price-disruption fact of the year.
- **Agility Digit (SPAC deck, illustrative):** ~**$8,500/month** per Digit RaaS. At ~$30/hr human-labor comparator, the deck math works only if Digit's productive hours approach human shift equivalents — a claim no filing supports. $250K was the 2020 purchase price; $45K listings are wrong (RoboZaps). **$300M in contracted Digit v5 orders** [VENDOR] — multi-year RaaS with warrants vesting on deployment.
- **1X Neo:** $20,000 purchase or **$499/month** subscription ($200 refundable deposit) — the consumer humanoid price anchor. No primary-source unit-delivery count verified for 2026.
- **Figure 03:** **$20K target price** [VENDOR]; no verified transaction price published. At 35 DOF with tactile hands and 2.3 kWh battery, the target implies aggressive cost engineering that no audit verifies.
- **Tesla Optimus:** $20,000–$30,000 target [VENDOR]; pre-production. Sanhua's ~$685M linear-actuator order (Oct 2025, ~180,000 robots' worth per watchers [SECONDARY]) is the largest single upstream bet on Optimus economics — its meaning depends entirely on the ramp materializing.
- **UBTech FY2025:** humanoid segment revenue RMB 820.6M (+2,204% YoY) on 1,079 delivered full-size humanoids — implying ~RMB 760K per delivered unit (~$105K), an enterprise price band an order of magnitude above Unitree's. Net loss narrowed to RMB 790M on RMB 2.0B total revenue. The **¥264M (~$37M) China–Vietnam border customs/patrol contract** [SECONDARY] shows non-manufacturing revenue streams exist but says nothing about unit economics.
- **Skild AI:** **~$30M revenue within months in 2025** [VENDOR] against a $14B+ valuation — a ~460× revenue multiple that makes the "brain" bet explicit.
- **Cross-ref:** all 2026 funding events (Skild $1.4B, PI ~$1B, Agility $2.5B SPAC, Unitree ¥6.1B raise, SoftBank–ABB $5.4B) are consolidated in §20; this section carries only their unit-economics implications.
