## Figures and metrics

- **Global humanoid shipments, H1 2026 — Counterpoint Research:** >22,000; AgiBot ~9,700; Unitree >7,000; then Galbot, UBTech, Leju (same order); Chinese-vendor share not stated.

### The tracker disagreement, in full

- **Smart Analytics Global (via Bloomberg/TechTimes):** ~19,100 global H1 2026; AgiBot ~8,400; Unitree ~5,900; Galbot, UBTech, Leju (same order); Chinese-vendor share >97%. Application split: industrial and commercial uses >70%. Both trackers rank the same five vendors in the same order — the one robust signal in the dataset.
- **Counterpoint Research:** >22,000 global H1 2026; AgiBot ~9,700; Unitree >7,000; same vendor order; Chinese-vendor share not stated. Application split: entertainment/performance + data production + research >60%; intelligent manufacturing 13%; warehousing/logistics 5%. Taxonomies incompatible with SAG — do not blend.
- **Humanoid Robot Scene Application Alliance (China industry source):** >30,000 H1 shipments in China (>18,000 bipedal + >12,000 wheeled). Note the wheeled inclusion — this single definitional choice explains much of the China/Global gap.
- **China Humanoid Robotics and Embodied Intelligence Committee of 100:** China's H1 volume above 40,000, called 97% of global. The highest estimate in the set; treat as the industry-advocacy ceiling.
- **Reading rule [DIRECTIONAL]:** the leader board (AgiBot > Unitree > Galbot > UBTech > Leju) is the finding; the denominator (19,100 → >40,000) is the uncertainty. Any claim of the form "X% of humanoids do Y" that does not name its tracker and its application taxonomy is unusable.
- **AgiBot caveat:** its ~8,400–9,700 figure reaches us only via trackers; no AgiBot filing-level unit/revenue split was found. Of all the H1 leaders, AgiBot is the least independently verified — a specific follow-up gap.

- **Unitree R1 AIR at $4,900 (Aug 2026 listings [SECONDARY]):** the cheapest walking humanoid listed in 2026 — a price that undercuts some robot vacuum-and-mop combos' annual enterprise contracts and reframes the "humanoids are expensive" objection into "humanoids are cheap but not yet useful." [DIRECTIONAL]
- **The full Unitree ladder (Aug 2026):** R1 AIR $4,900 → R1 $5,900 → G1 $13,500 → H2 $29,900 → H2 Plus $100,000. The ladder's span — 20× from cheapest to dearest — shows Unitree segmenting research, education, and industrial buyers with distinct SKUs rather than one general-purpose machine.
- **The research-revenue mechanism:** at $4,900–$13,500, labs buy R1/R1 AIR/G1 the way they once bought quadcopters — which is exactly why 73.6% of humanoid revenue is research/education. Low prices manufacture the R&D installed base; the installed base manufactures the data; the data may one day manufacture capability. [DIRECTIONAL]
- **Application split — directly contradictory:** SAG: industrial and commercial uses >70% of H1 shipments. Counterpoint: entertainment/performance + data production + research >60%; intelligent manufacturing 13%; warehousing/logistics 5%. Incompatible taxonomies — do not average or blend.
- **Unitree 2025 (IPO filing):** 5,716 produced / 5,511 shipped / 5,215 sold; RMB 867.8M humanoid robot revenue; avg. recognized price RMB 166,400/unit; Jan–Sep 2025 revenue mix 73.6% research/education, 17.39% commercial/consumer, 9.01% industry; only RMB 15.702M (29.29% of the industry slice) from clearly operational scenarios; SemiAnalysis: ≤250 units in productive industrial pilots/deployments in 2025.
- **UBTech FY2025 (filing):** 1,079 full-size humanoids delivered (>80% industrial); humanoid segment revenue RMB 820.6M (+2,204% YoY, now the largest segment); total revenue RMB 2.0B (+53%); net loss narrowed to RMB 790M.
- **Figure BotQ:** ~1 robot/90 min (Apr 2026) → ~1 robot/hour (24× in 120 days) [VENDOR]; 1,000th Figure 03 on 2026-07-26 [VENDOR]; ~740 robots deployed vs. 660 employees (June 2026) [VENDOR]; 12K units/yr line capacity [VENDOR].
- **Agility:** 65,000+ operational hours across 9 customer facilities [VENDOR]; 100,000+ totes moved at GXO Spanx; RoboFab 70,000 sq ft, 10,000 units/yr peak capacity [VENDOR].

### Valuation against revenue (2026)

- **Unitree:** ~$51B debut market cap vs. RMB 867.8M (~$120M) 2025 humanoid robot revenue — a multiple in the low hundreds on the robot business, with 73.6% of that revenue from research/education. The market priced optionality on autonomy, not throughput.
- **Figure:** $39B (flat Sept 2025 primary / Q2 2026 secondary) vs. no public revenue figure — the secondary flatness is the market's 2026 verdict that BotQ throughput alone did not reprice the company.
- **Skild AI:** $14B+ on ~$30M revenue [VENDOR] — the "brain" premium in its purest form.
- **Agility:** $2.5B SPAC valuation vs. filings showing revenue "a small fraction" of the implied value (GeekWire) — the public-market test of RaaS economics is the SPAC close, expected later 2026.
- **Physical Intelligence:** >$11B (in talks) vs. no public revenue — priced on pi0.5/pi0.7 cross-embodiment claims [VENDOR].
- **SoftBank–ABB:** $5.4B for $2.3B in sales (7,000 employees) — the only 2026 robotics transaction priced on an existing industrial revenue base, and the consolidator's counter-thesis to the startup multiples. [DIRECTIONAL: the ABB multiple is the reality anchor for the whole table.]

### Deployment evidence scoreboard (2026)

- **Agility:** 65,000+ operational hours across 9 customer facilities [VENDOR]; 100,000+ totes moved at GXO Spanx; TMMC 7-Digit commercial agreement (Feb 2026); Schaeffler purchase agreement. The strongest paid-deployment evidence set in the industry.
- **Figure:** 40 Figure 03 at BMW Spartanburg (June 2026, logistics sequencing) [SECONDARY]; Catalyst Brands Reno deal (May 2026) [SECONDARY]; ~740 robots deployed vs. 660 employees [VENDOR]. Strong manufacturing evidence; deployment evidence thinner and task-supervised.
- **UBTech:** 1,079 full-size humanoids delivered in FY2025 (>80% industrial) [filing]; BYD, Geely, FAW-VW, Foxconn, SF Express customer list; ¥264M border contract [SECONDARY]. The strongest filing-backed industrial delivery record.
- **1X:** consumer deliveries begun 2026; no primary-source unit count. Strongest consumer evidence; no industrial evidence.
- **Unitree:** ~5,900–7,000 H1 shipments [trackers]; ≤250 productive industrial units (SemiAnalysis, 2025). Strongest shipment evidence; weakest productive-deployment ratio.
- **EngineAI:** zero deployment evidence; the entire 2026 record is DEMO (teleoperated combat).
- **Tesla:** zero "material factory usage" (Musk, Jan 2026). Pre-production.
- **Scoreboard reading [DIRECTIONAL]:** rank the industry by deployment evidence, not shipments, and the 2026 leader board inverts — Agility and UBTech lead, Unitree trails its own volume, and Tesla sits at zero. This is the table the taxonomy was built to produce.

### Revenue, pricing, and unit economics

- **Unitree (2025 filing):** RMB 867.8M humanoid robot revenue at an average recognized price of **RMB 166,400/unit** (~$23K); 5,215 sold vs. 5,511 shipped (296-unit recognition gap). Price ladder (Aug 2026 US listings): R1 AIR $4,900 → R1 $5,900 → G1 $13,500 → H2 $29,900 → H2 Plus $100,000. The $4,900 R1 AIR is the cheapest walking humanoid listed in 2026 — the price-disruption fact of the year.
- **Agility Digit (SPAC deck, illustrative):** ~**$8,500/month** per Digit RaaS. At ~$30/hr human-labor comparator, the deck math works only if Digit's productive hours approach human shift equivalents — a claim no filing supports. $250K was the 2020 purchase price; $45K listings are wrong (RoboZaps). **$300M in contracted Digit v5 orders** [VENDOR] — multi-year RaaS with warrants vesting on deployment.
- **1X Neo:** $20,000 purchase or **$499/month** subscription ($200 refundable deposit) — the consumer humanoid price anchor. No primary-source unit-delivery count verified for 2026.
- **Figure 03:** **$20K target price** [VENDOR]; no verified transaction price published. At 35 DOF with tactile hands and 2.3 kWh battery, the target implies aggressive cost engineering that no audit verifies.
- **Tesla Optimus:** $20,000–$30,000 target [VENDOR]; pre-production. Sanhua's ~$685M linear-actuator order (Oct 2025, ~180,000 robots' worth per watchers [SECONDARY]) is the largest single upstream bet on Optimus economics — its meaning depends entirely on the ramp materializing.
- **UBTech FY2025:** humanoid segment revenue RMB 820.6M (+2,204% YoY) on 1,079 delivered full-size humanoids — implying ~RMB 760K per delivered unit (~$105K), an enterprise price band an order of magnitude above Unitree's. Net loss narrowed to RMB 790M on RMB 2.0B total revenue. The **¥264M (~$37M) China–Vietnam border customs/patrol contract** [SECONDARY] shows non-manufacturing revenue streams exist but says nothing about unit economics.
- **Skild AI:** **~$30M revenue within months in 2025** [VENDOR] against a $14B+ valuation — a ~460× revenue multiple that makes the "brain" bet explicit.
- **Cross-ref:** all 2026 funding events (Skild $1.4B, PI ~$1B, Agility $2.5B SPAC, Unitree ¥6.1B raise, SoftBank–ABB $5.4B) are consolidated in §20; this section carries only their unit-economics implications.
