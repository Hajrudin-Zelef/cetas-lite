# Main actors

## Main actors

- **Unitree Robotics** — volume leader and price disruptor (H1 2026: ~5,900–7,000 units by tracker); IPO 2026-08-19 at ~$51B debut market cap; own filing shows 73.6% of humanoid revenue from research/education. Issuer of the filing that anchors this chapter's measurement discipline.
- **AgiBot / Zhiyuan** — shipment leader by both trackers (H1 2026: ~8,400–9,700); CATL-backed; >70% of global market jointly with Unitree by tracker counts; no filing-level disclosure found (gap).
- **Figure AI** — Western deployment/manufacturing leader: Figure 03, BotQ (~1/hour), Helix 02 VLA, BMW Spartanburg (40 units, logistics sequencing), Catalyst Brands Reno deal; Series C >$1B at $39B (flat secondary Q2 2026).
- **Tesla (Optimus)** — pre-production at scale: Fremont conversion (1M/yr design capacity), three certified Chinese suppliers (Tuopu, Joyson, Sanhua), Gen 3 teased; the 2025 miss record (fewer than 500 shipped vs. ~10,000 promised) governs expectations.
- **Agility Robotics** — paid-deployment specialist: Digit RaaS (GXO Spanx, TMMC Feb 2026, Schaeffler), Digit v5 (NVIDIA Halos, Dec 2026), $300M contracted v5 orders [VENDOR]; $2.5B SPAC (AGLT, Churchill XI, $200M Foxconn PIPE).
- **1X Technologies** — first consumer humanoid deployment: Neo ($20K / $499-mo), Redwood AI, disclosed Expert Mode teleoperation.
- **UBTech** — industrial-deployment leader by audited units: 1,079 full-size humanoids delivered in FY2025 (>80% industrial); Walker S2 at BYD, Geely, FAW-VW, Foxconn, Airbus; HKEX 9880.
- **EngineAI** — combat-spectacle marketer: T800 teleoperated cage events (URKL Jul 2026, CyberHero Sep 2026, REK Sep 18 2026 — reported wrist injury); the teleop fine print contradicts autonomy claims.
- **Skild AI** — $1.4B Series C at $14B+ (SoftBank-led, Jan 2026); Skild Brain omni-bodied foundation model; ~$30M 2025 revenue [VENDOR].
- **Physical Intelligence (π)** — ~$1B at >$11B talks (Mar 2026); pi0.5/pi0.7 cross-embodiment claims [VENDOR]; Anthropic acquisition talks denied by CEO Hausman; OpenAI an investor.
- **NVIDIA** — stack provider: Isaac Root platform (Computex Jun 2026, Unitree H2 flagship), Jetson Thor, Halos safety architecture (Digit v5), Isaac GR00T models. GR00T N1 and Thor GA date to 2025; 2026 is integration year.
- **NVIDIA's 2026 as a strategy statement [DIRECTIONAL]:** Isaac Root (research platform, Unitree H2 flagship, Western institutions only) + Halos (safety certification, Digit v5) + the Isaac Lab/Cosmos simulation stack (Skild's training substrate) together describe NVIDIA's bet: own the compute, the sim, and the safety standard; let vendors fight over the bodies. It is the picks-and-shovels play of the humanoid cycle.
- **Karol Hausman, Sergey Levine, Chelsea Finn, Deepak Pathak, Abhinav Gupta** — the researcher-founders (ex-DeepMind, Berkeley, CMU) whose movement from labs to startups (PI, Skild) is the talent signal underwriting the $25B "brain" valuations. The contested-talent layer: Anthropic–PI talks (denied), OpenAI as PI investor.
- **GeekWire** — the outlet that applied the filing-level discipline to the Agility SPAC (revenue "a small fraction" of the $2.5B valuation), cited here as the example of the measurement posture this chapter adopts throughout.
- **Google DeepMind** — Gemini Robotics 1.5 (2025-09-25, not 2026); no 2026 model release found (coverage gap).
- **SoftBank** — $5.4B ABB Robotics acquisition (announced Oct 2025, close expected mid-to-late 2026; $2.3B sales, 7,000 employees) under the "Physical AI" thesis; also led Skild's Series C.
- **BMW** — Figure 02 pilot completed Nov 2025 (30,000+ vehicles, 90,000+ parts, 11 months; whistleblower safety lawsuit); 40 Figure 03 at Spartanburg (June 2026, logistics sequencing); Leipzig pilot announced for summer 2026.
- **Foxconn** — $200M PIPE lead in Agility SPAC; also a UBTech Walker S2 customer for iPhone assembly deployment [SECONDARY].
- **Apptronik, Sanctuary AI, Boston Dynamics, Amazon warehouse robotics, Covariant, Dexterity** — thin-coverage: no material 2026 findings in this pass (Apptronik Apollo appears only as DeepMind's cross-embodiment test platform). Gaps, not findings.
- **Hugging Face** — robotics coverage (HopeJR 2026-09-01; CVE-2026-25874) consolidated in §16; cross-ref only.
- **Smart Analytics Global, Counterpoint Research, Humanoid Analytics, SemiAnalysis** — the tracker/analyst layer whose conflicting methodologies are themselves a finding of this chapter.
- **Tuopu Group, Ningbo Joyson Electronic, Zhejiang Sanhua Intelligent Controls** — the three Chinese suppliers Tesla certified in September 2026 (actuators/chassis, sensors, thermal); Sanhua's ~$685M actuator order (Oct 2025) is the upstream bellwether for the Optimus ramp.
- **RoboZaps** — the trade outlet whose two preserved judgments frame the year: the Tesla forecast record (missed several-fold) and the UBTech verdict ("By commercial evidence the S2 leads the industry; by independently verified capability, no humanoid yet leads anyone").
- **Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada, GXO, Schaeffler, Mercado Libre, Catalyst Brands** — the named commercial customers whose Digit/Figure agreements (Feb 2026, Jun 2024, Cheraw SC, San Antonio, Reno) constitute the audited evidence for paid deployment in 2026.
- **Dongfeng Liuzhou** — UBTech's manufacturing partner, hitting a 10-minute production pace (Sept 2026) [SECONDARY]; the Chinese factory-floor answer to BotQ.
- **Hero Esports (CyberHero)** and **REK (Robot Entertainment Kombat)** — the combat promoters (Riyadh Sep 9, San Francisco Sep 18) whose events are the evidence base for the teleoperation-vs-autonomy framing of this chapter.
- **SoftBank** — $5.4B ABB Robotics acquisition (announced Oct 2025, close expected mid-to-late 2026; $2.3B sales, 7,000 employees) under the "Physical AI" thesis; also led Skild's Series C.
- **Galbot and Leju** — third and fifth in both H1 2026 trackers, ahead of/behind UBTech respectively. No company-level 2026 material surfaced in this pass; they are named in the leader board and flagged as thin coverage. Their presence in the top five on both trackers — despite near-total Western press invisibility — is itself evidence of the China-domestic demand base.
- **Donnie Yen, Elon Musk (as amplifier), Frankie LaPenna** — the celebrity layer of robot combat: Yen attended URKL; Musk shared the T800 clip ("Robot fights are fun"); LaPenna was the human in the REK cage (Sep 18, 2026, reported wrist injury). Celebrity amplification is a documented vector by which DEMO events get misread as capability milestones.
- **Ulrich Wieland (BMW VP)** — the named customer voice (via carscoops) confirming the Spartanburg logistics use case and the Leipzig European-automotive first.

**Context:** 2026 was the year humanoid robotics achieved manufacturing repeatability (BotQ's 1/hour, Unitree's IPO-scale volumes, Walker S2's 10-minute pace) without achieving deployment reality — the productive deployments number in the hundreds against tens of thousands of shipments. The venture market bet the $25B+ "brain" thesis (Skild, PI) while hardware margins and autonomy remained unverified. Safety governance lagged the spectacle: a trade-show floor with unrestrained punching robots (IFA 2026) and a teleoperated robot hospitalizing no one but injuring a fighter (REK, Sep 18) produced the year's only verified combat injury — and no new regulatory event.

**2024–2025 background that governs 2026 readings:** the Figure–BMW Figure 02 pilot (Jan 2024 → Nov 2025) is the only completed Western automotive humanoid pilot with public numbers (11 months, 30,000+ vehicles, 90,000+ parts); the Amazon–Agility $150M Series B (2022) explains Amazon's R&D-only posture; GR00T N1 and Jetson Thor (GTC/CES 2025) are the 2025 substrate on which all 2026 NVIDIA integrations were built; the Hugging Face/Pollen acquisition (Apr 2025) and Reachy Mini launch (Jul 2025, $299/$449) explain the 2026-09-01 HopeJR unveiling (see §16). None of these are 2026 events — they are the baseline against which 2026 claims must be read, and the most common reporting error of the year was presenting them as new.

### The year's three phases

- **Phase 1 — Capital formation (Jan–Mar 2026):** Skild's $1.4B (Jan 14), Helix 02 (Jan), TMMC's 7-Digit commercial agreement (Feb), PI's ~$1B talks (Mar 27). The quarter priced the thesis: brains first, bodies second.
- **Phase 2 — Manufacturing proof (Apr–Jul 2026):** BotQ's 24× ramp (Apr), Fremont conversion (May), Catalyst Brands deal (May 26), BMW's 40 Figure 03 (June), Isaac Root at Computex (June 1), the Agility SPAC (June 24), the 1,000th Figure 03 (July 26), URKL's opener (July 16). The quarter proved repeatability; it did not prove deployment.
- **Phase 3 — Public markets and spectacle (Aug–Sep 2026):** Unitree's $51B IPO debut (Aug 19), the ladder-climbing demo (Aug), CyberHero Riyadh (Sep 9), the REK cage fight (Sep 18), Tesla's Ningbo supplier certifications (Sep 16–17). The quarter priced the story and staged the spectacle — with the teleop fine print attached to the biggest headline.
- **The phase the year skipped:** sustained, unsupervised, revenue-positive industrial deployment at fleet scale. No quarter of 2026 produced it; December 2026 carries the four named checkpoints that could.
