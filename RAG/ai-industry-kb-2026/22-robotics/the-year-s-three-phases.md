**Context:** 2026 was the year humanoid robotics achieved manufacturing repeatability (BotQ's 1/hour, Unitree's IPO-scale volumes, Walker S2's 10-minute pace) without achieving deployment reality — the productive deployments number in the hundreds against tens of thousands of shipments. The venture market bet the $25B+ "brain" thesis (Skild, PI) while hardware margins and autonomy remained unverified. Safety governance lagged the spectacle: a trade-show floor with unrestrained punching robots (IFA 2026) and a teleoperated robot hospitalizing no one but injuring a fighter (REK, Sep 18) produced the year's only verified combat injury — and no new regulatory event.

**2024–2025 background that governs 2026 readings:** the Figure–BMW Figure 02 pilot (Jan 2024 → Nov 2025) is the only completed Western automotive humanoid pilot with public numbers (11 months, 30,000+ vehicles, 90,000+ parts); the Amazon–Agility $150M Series B (2022) explains Amazon's R&D-only posture; GR00T N1 and Jetson Thor (GTC/CES 2025) are the 2025 substrate on which all 2026 NVIDIA integrations were built; the Hugging Face/Pollen acquisition (Apr 2025) and Reachy Mini launch (Jul 2025, $299/$449) explain the 2026-09-01 HopeJR unveiling (see §16). None of these are 2026 events — they are the baseline against which 2026 claims must be read, and the most common reporting error of the year was presenting them as new.

### The year's three phases

- **Phase 1 — Capital formation (Jan–Mar 2026):** Skild's $1.4B (Jan 14), Helix 02 (Jan), TMMC's 7-Digit commercial agreement (Feb), PI's ~$1B talks (Mar 27). The quarter priced the thesis: brains first, bodies second.
- **Phase 2 — Manufacturing proof (Apr–Jul 2026):** BotQ's 24× ramp (Apr), Fremont conversion (May), Catalyst Brands deal (May 26), BMW's 40 Figure 03 (June), Isaac Root at Computex (June 1), the Agility SPAC (June 24), the 1,000th Figure 03 (July 26), URKL's opener (July 16). The quarter proved repeatability; it did not prove deployment.
- **Phase 3 — Public markets and spectacle (Aug–Sep 2026):** Unitree's $51B IPO debut (Aug 19), the ladder-climbing demo (Aug), CyberHero Riyadh (Sep 9), the REK cage fight (Sep 18), Tesla's Ningbo supplier certifications (Sep 16–17). The quarter priced the story and staged the spectacle — with the teleop fine print attached to the biggest headline.
- **The phase the year skipped:** sustained, unsupervised, revenue-positive industrial deployment at fleet scale. No quarter of 2026 produced it; December 2026 carries the four named checkpoints that could.
