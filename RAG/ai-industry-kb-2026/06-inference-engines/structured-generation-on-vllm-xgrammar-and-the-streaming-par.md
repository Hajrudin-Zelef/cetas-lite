### Structured generation on vLLM: XGrammar and the Streaming Parser Engine

- **XGrammar is the default structured-generation backend in vLLM (v0.7+)** — and equally in SGLang (v0.4+), TensorRT-LLM, MLC-LLM, OpenVINO GenAI, and Modular MAX: by 2026 the *library* is universal, not an engine differentiator. The independently better-attested figure remains the **~3x compressed-FSM speedup over standard guided decoding**, with JSON compliance rising from 90–94% (unconstrained) to 96–98.2% (constrained) [COMMUNITY; "sub-40-microsecond per-token overhead, up to 80x throughput" claims are vendor-adjacent — treat as directional].
- **XGrammar-2 (released 2026-05-04, MLC blog):** major upgrade for agent applications — **Structural Tag**, a composable JSON protocol uniformly expressing OpenAI harmony format, tool calling, reasoning channels, and custom output structures; cross-grammar caching, repetition-state compression, batching + speculative-decoding support. **Adopted by xAI, Databricks, DeepSeek** and other leading AI companies; integrated by SGLang, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, MLC-LLM for strict tool calling.
- **Syntax convergence:** XGrammar adopted **GBNF** as its grammar syntax, making GBNF the de facto shared grammar language across vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp-family, and llguidance (official converters both ways) — one grammar export targets every open stack.
- **vLLM v0.25.0 — Streaming Parser Engine (#46610):** unified tool-call/reasoning parsing on top of structured decoding, with parsers for Kimi k2.5/k2.6/k2.7, seed_oss, and DeepSeek V4.
- (SGLang's remaining differentiator here is jump-forward decoding, which skips LLM forward passes on deterministic grammar spans — covered in sibling 06b, kept minimal here.)
