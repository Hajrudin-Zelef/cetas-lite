# 20. Startups & Major Funding Rounds
Keywords: startup funding, mega-rounds, Series G, Series H, Series C, Series D, Series E, primary round, tender offer, secondary sale, employee liquidity, M&A, acquisition, IPO, valuation, post-money valuation, pre-money valuation, down round, up round, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Mistral AI, Cognition, Devin, Cursor, Anysphere, Cerebras, Groq, Etched, Skild AI, Runway, Harvey, Helsing, Anduril, Thinking Machines Lab, Reflection AI, SSI, Periodic Labs, Physical Intelligence, Perplexity, 1X Technologies, Figure AI, Black Forest Labs, Epirus, Lila Sciences, Windsurf, sovereign wealth, SPV, debt financing, neocloud, AI chips, transformer ASIC, defense tech, robotics, humanoid robots, world models, legal AI, strategic investors, NVIDIA capital, talent war, 2026 AI funding.

## Summary

The first three quarters of 2026 produced the largest private AI financings ever recorded, by a wide margin. The single biggest event was OpenAI's $122B primary round at $852B post-money, closed March 2026 — the largest private financing in Silicon Valley history at the time — followed in August 2026 by a $7B self-funded employee tender at the same $852B valuation. The second tier was defined by Anthropic's $30B Series G at $380B post-money, closed February 12, 2026, and xAI's $20B Series E at $230–240B, closed around January 6–7, 2026.

Infrastructure capital was equally aggressive. Cerebras raised a $1B Series H at $23B in February 2026 and IPO'd on May 14, 2026 under ticker CBRS (IPO pricing mechanics are covered in §21; one mention only here). Etched repriced three times in eight months: ~$500M at ~$5B in January, $300M at $10.3B in July, and ~$700M at ~$21B in August. Groq raised $1B across June–August 2026 while pivoting from AI chipmaker to inference neocloud after Nvidia's reported $20B December 2025 licensing deal. Defense-tech produced Europe's largest rounds ever: Helsing's $1.8B Series E at $18B (July 13, 2026) and Anduril's $5B at $61B (May 2026), with Quantum Systems adding $1.2B at $8B in the same month.

Robotics capital concentrated in Skild AI's $1.4B Series C at >$14B (January 14, 2026). Coding and legal AI monetized through both primaries and secondaries: Cognition closed a $1B+ Series D at $26B post-money (May 27, 2026); Harvey raised twice — $200M at $11B in March and $550M at $15.5B on September 9, 2026; SpaceX agreed to acquire Anysphere/Cursor for $60B in all-stock (June 16, 2026), the largest AI-adjacent M&A of the period. Mistral AI closed €3B at ~€21B/$24B on September 8, 2026, described by the company [VENDOR] as the largest private equity round by a European technology company.

Several frontier labs sat in reported fundraising talks as of September 22, 2026 without a confirmed close: Thinking Machines Lab (≥$1B at ≥$40B), Reflection AI ($2.5B at $25B pre), Physical Intelligence (~$1B at >$11B), Periodic Labs (hundreds of millions at ~$7B), Perplexity (>$30B valuation talks), and 1X Technologies (up to $1B at ≥$10B). These are kept strictly separate from closed rounds below. The May 2026 Anthropic "$65B at $965B" figure reported by AP/TechXplore is contradicted by secondary sources ($30B at ~$900B) and is NOT stated as fact anywhere in this chapter.

Dating discipline is enforced: Thinking Machines Lab's $2B at $12B closed July 15, 2025 (Reuters) — not 2026; Figure AI's $1B+ Series C at $39B closed September 2025; Black Forest Labs' $300M Series B at $3.25B closed December 2025; Epirus's $250M Series D closed March 2025; Perplexity's $200M at $20B was September 2025; Windsurf was acquired July 2025. None are misdated into 2026 here.

- **Four labs above $200B in verified 2026 primary raises**: OpenAI ($122B @ $852B), Anthropic ($30B @ $380B), xAI ($20B @ $230–240B), plus the disputed Anthropic May figure handled separately.

- **Defense as a first-class asset class**: $17.4B global defense-tech VC in H1 2026 vs $11.2B all of 2025 (Dealroom).

- **European ceiling broken**: Mistral (€3B) and Helsing ($1.8B) now compete at US mega-round scale.

## Key dated facts

### OpenAI

- **March 2026 — $122B primary at $852B post-money.** Closed March 2026; final committed figure up from a $110B February announcement (an earlier record in this corpus cited $100B; the $122B figure reflects the closed final amount). VERIFIED. Anchored by Amazon, NVIDIA, SoftBank; Microsoft continued. Co-led by SoftBank alongside a16z, D.E. Shaw Ventures, MGX, TPG, and T. Rowe Price-advised accounts. [VENDOR/PR] First-time $3B raised from individual investors through bank channels (company disclosure). Largest private financing in Silicon Valley history at close. Revenue context reported alongside: ~$2B/month, >40% from enterprise [VENDOR]. Confidential IPO filing June 8, 2026; reported IPO target valuations $852B–$1T (IPO mechanics → §21).
- **August 10, 2026 — $7B employee tender at $852B valuation.** Secondary transaction, NOT a primary round: ~$7B of employee shares bought back by OpenAI itself at the March $852B valuation. VERIFIED (Bloomberg/CNBC). Distinctive: self-funded by OpenAI's own cash rather than outside buyers — cap-table discipline ahead of a potential IPO. Per-person cap cited at up to $50M in one report vs $30M in another [UNVERIFIED cap detail]. CEO Altman cited $687/share in June. Prior secondaries for context: $6.6B at ~$500B (October 2025, Thrive/SoftBank/Dragoneer/MGX/T. Rowe Price; up to $10.3B authorized, ~2/3 taken); $1.5B in 2024 at ~$86B.
