# 20. Startups & Major Funding Rounds
Keywords: startup funding, mega-rounds, Series G, Series H, Series C, Series D, Series E, primary round, tender offer, secondary sale, employee liquidity, M&A, acquisition, IPO, valuation, post-money valuation, pre-money valuation, down round, up round, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Mistral AI, Cognition, Devin, Cursor, Anysphere, Cerebras, Groq, Etched, Skild AI, Runway, Harvey, Helsing, Anduril, Thinking Machines Lab, Reflection AI, SSI, Periodic Labs, Physical Intelligence, Perplexity, 1X Technologies, Figure AI, Black Forest Labs, Epirus, Lila Sciences, Windsurf, sovereign wealth, SPV, debt financing, neocloud, AI chips, transformer ASIC, defense tech, robotics, humanoid robots, world models, legal AI, strategic investors, NVIDIA capital, talent war, 2026 AI funding.

## Summary

The first three quarters of 2026 produced the largest private AI financings ever recorded, by a wide margin. The single biggest event was OpenAI's $122B primary round at $852B post-money, closed March 2026 — the largest private financing in Silicon Valley history at the time — followed in August 2026 by a $7B self-funded employee tender at the same $852B valuation. The second tier was defined by Anthropic's $30B Series G at $380B post-money, closed February 12, 2026, and xAI's $20B Series E at $230–240B, closed around January 6–7, 2026.

Infrastructure capital was equally aggressive. Cerebras raised a $1B Series H at $23B in February 2026 and IPO'd on May 14, 2026 under ticker CBRS (IPO pricing mechanics are covered in §21; one mention only here). Etched repriced three times in eight months: ~$500M at ~$5B in January, $300M at $10.3B in July, and ~$700M at ~$21B in August. Groq raised $1B across June–August 2026 while pivoting from AI chipmaker to inference neocloud after Nvidia's reported $20B December 2025 licensing deal. Defense-tech produced Europe's largest rounds ever: Helsing's $1.8B Series E at $18B (July 13, 2026) and Anduril's $5B at $61B (May 2026), with Quantum Systems adding $1.2B at $8B in the same month.

Robotics capital concentrated in Skild AI's $1.4B Series C at >$14B (January 14, 2026). Coding and legal AI monetized through both primaries and secondaries: Cognition closed a $1B+ Series D at $26B post-money (May 27, 2026); Harvey raised twice — $200M at $11B in March and $550M at $15.5B on September 9, 2026; SpaceX agreed to acquire Anysphere/Cursor for $60B in all-stock (June 16, 2026), the largest AI-adjacent M&A of the period. Mistral AI closed €3B at ~€21B/$24B on September 8, 2026, described by the company [VENDOR] as the largest private equity round by a European technology company.

Several frontier labs sat in reported fundraising talks as of September 22, 2026 without a confirmed close: Thinking Machines Lab (≥$1B at ≥$40B), Reflection AI ($2.5B at $25B pre), Physical Intelligence (~$1B at >$11B), Periodic Labs (hundreds of millions at ~$7B), Perplexity (>$30B valuation talks), and 1X Technologies (up to $1B at ≥$10B). These are kept strictly separate from closed rounds below. The May 2026 Anthropic "$65B at $965B" figure reported by AP/TechXplore is contradicted by secondary sources ($30B at ~$900B) and is NOT stated as fact anywhere in this chapter.

Dating discipline is enforced: Thinking Machines Lab's $2B at $12B closed July 15, 2025 (Reuters) — not 2026; Figure AI's $1B+ Series C at $39B closed September 2025; Black Forest Labs' $300M Series B at $3.25B closed December 2025; Epirus's $250M Series D closed March 2025; Perplexity's $200M at $20B was September 2025; Windsurf was acquired July 2025. None are misdated into 2026 here.

### This chapter in one picture

- **Four labs above $200B in verified 2026 primary raises**: OpenAI ($122B @ $852B), Anthropic ($30B @ $380B), xAI ($20B @ $230–240B), plus the disputed Anthropic May figure handled separately.
- **Compute hardware as a financing theme**: Cerebras (IPO + Series H), Groq (neocloud pivot), Etched (three-step repricing), NVIDIA as near-universal strategic participant.
- **Defense as a first-class asset class**: $17.4B global defense-tech VC in H1 2026 vs $11.2B all of 2025 (Dealroom).
- **Liquidity engineering**: self-funded tenders, authorized secondaries, SPV structures — IPO pressure deferred, cap tables concentrated.
- **European ceiling broken**: Mistral (€3B) and Helsing ($1.8B) now compete at US mega-round scale.

## Key dated facts

### OpenAI

- **March 2026 — $122B primary at $852B post-money.** Closed March 2026; final committed figure up from a $110B February announcement (an earlier record in this corpus cited $100B; the $122B figure reflects the closed final amount). VERIFIED. Anchored by Amazon, NVIDIA, SoftBank; Microsoft continued. Co-led by SoftBank alongside a16z, D.E. Shaw Ventures, MGX, TPG, and T. Rowe Price-advised accounts. [VENDOR/PR] First-time $3B raised from individual investors through bank channels (company disclosure). Largest private financing in Silicon Valley history at close. Revenue context reported alongside: ~$2B/month, >40% from enterprise [VENDOR]. Confidential IPO filing June 8, 2026; reported IPO target valuations $852B–$1T (IPO mechanics → §21).
- **August 10, 2026 — $7B employee tender at $852B valuation.** Secondary transaction, NOT a primary round: ~$7B of employee shares bought back by OpenAI itself at the March $852B valuation. VERIFIED (Bloomberg/CNBC). Distinctive: self-funded by OpenAI's own cash rather than outside buyers — cap-table discipline ahead of a potential IPO. Per-person cap cited at up to $50M in one report vs $30M in another [UNVERIFIED cap detail]. CEO Altman cited $687/share in June. Prior secondaries for context: $6.6B at ~$500B (October 2025, Thrive/SoftBank/Dragoneer/MGX/T. Rowe Price; up to $10.3B authorized, ~2/3 taken); $1.5B in 2024 at ~$86B.

### Anthropic

- **February 12, 2026 — $30B Series G at $380B post-money.** Announced and closed February 12, 2026. VERIFIED (multi-source; update trail documented in the January-2026 record roundup). Co-leads: GIC and Coatue. Reported participants: D.E. Shaw Ventures, Dragoneer, Founders Fund, ICONIQ, MGX. Closed after January negotiations reportedly started around $10B at ~$350B — the deal grew substantially in size. Reported annualized revenue around $14B [UNVERIFIED — secondary-source figure]. This is the last VERIFIED Anthropic close of 2026.
- **May 28–29, 2026 — reported mega-raise [CONTRADICTED, NOT stated as fact].** AP/TechXplore reported a $65B financing at a $965B valuation, naming Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia as leaders. Several secondary sites instead describe a $30B raise at ~$900B — a direct conflict on BOTH amount and valuation. Verdict: CONTRADICTED. Do not cite any May 2026 Anthropic amount as fact. Awaiting primary confirmation.

### Cerebras

- **February 2026 — $1B Series H at $23B.** VERIFIED. Led by Tiger Global; AMD joined the round; Benchmark raised a $225M dedicated SPV. Nearly triple the $8.1B Series G (September 2025, $1.1B, Fidelity/Atreides). Confidential S-1 filed February 20, 2026; G42 divested to clear CFIUS (no longer listed as investor).
- **May 14, 2026 — IPO on Nasdaq (CBRS).** One mention only; IPO pricing mechanics → §21. A deep-dive reports $5.55B raised at ~$43.9B pricing with a $67B day-1 close, but pre-IPO estimates ranged up to $3.5B — IPO proceeds are CONTRADICTED across sources and are presented with attribution, not as unqualified fact. Financials: 2025 revenue $510M; Q2 2026 cloud/services $127.7M (+287% YoY) vs hardware $82.1M (+17%); core gross margin 40.6%. Reported-but-single-source: >$10B OpenAI deal [UNVERIFIED].

### Groq

- **June 2026 — $650M pivot raise.** Funds the pivot from AI chipmaker (LPUs) to neocloud/data-center operator running Nvidia systems, after Nvidia's reported $20B December 2025 licensing deal (which also hired founder/CEO Jonathan Ross, president Sunny Madra, and ~90% of the engineering team). One low-quality digest claimed the deal "collapsed" — CONTRADICTED by TechCrunch/Bloomberg/Sacra; treated as noise.
- **August 17, 2026 — $350M Series A at $3.5B.** Led by Disruptive, with NVIDIA expected to participate — roughly half the $6.9B valuation of September 2025. Alex Davis (Disruptive founder) became executive chairman. Company disputes the "down round" framing, calling it a new valuation for the "post-Nvidia-licensing-deal version of Groq." Operating footprint: 13 data centers, 6M+ developers, 54MW → 200MW planned by 2027. ~$1B raised in roughly 60 days; $1.7B+ total disclosed.

### Etched

- **January 2026 — ~$500M at ~$5B.** Led by Stripes; participants Peter Thiel, Positive Sum, Ribbit Capital. Company founded 2022 by Gavin Uberti, Chris Zhu, Robert Wachen; Sohu is a transformer-hardwired 4nm ASIC (TSMC), pitched as 10–20x a GPU on transformer inference [VENDOR — no independent benchmarks or public pricing as of September 2026].
- **June 30, 2026 — exits stealth** with working A0 silicon, 8-chip rack-scale system, >$1B signed customer contracts, ~$800M cumulative raised, first racks scheduled to ship summer 2026.
- **July 23, 2026 — $300M Series C at $10.3B.** Led by Sequoia; a16z, SK Hynix, Jane Street participated.
- **August 2026 — ~$700M at ~$21B.** Led by Jane Street (also a customer); reported by Sentinel/TechCrunch-syndicated digests. One digest calls it "Series B" — round lettering is CONTRADICTED across sources; lettering omitted in favor of amounts/dates. Michael Burry shared the WSJ piece (August 18, 2026), calling Etched "serious competition for NVDA." Cumulative totals conflict ($800M across four rounds vs ~$1.5B summed from individual rounds) — see Contradictions in wave source.

### Helsing

- **July 13, 2026 — $1.8B Series E at $18B.** Closed July 13, 2026. VERIFIED. Largest private defense-tech financing in European history; largest-ever round for a European defense startup. Lead: Dragoneer (most sources; one report names Goldman Sachs as lead — lead attribution slightly inconsistent); Lightspeed co-lead; participants Disruptive, ICONIQ, Growth Equity at Goldman Sachs Alternatives, JPMorganChase, Canada Pension Plan Investment Board, General Catalyst, Plural, StepStone. Company said demand significantly exceeded allocation; remains majority European-owned. Prior: €600M Series D at €12B (June 2025, Daniel Ek's Prima Materia lead). Context: HX-2 strike drone (Bundeswehr €269M initial order, framework up to €1.46B), Altra battlefield software, proposed CA-1 autonomous fighter.

### Anduril

- **May 2026 — $5B at $61B.** VERIFIED. Leaders: Thrive Capital and Andreessen Horowitz. Valuation roughly doubled; use includes production scale-up and Arsenal-1 (Ohio). Preceded by March 2026 reports of talks for up to $8B at ~$60B.

### Skild AI

- **January 14, 2026 — $1.4B Series C at >$14B.** Closed mid-January 2026. VERIFIED (Bloomberg). Founded 2023 by CMU professors Deepak Pathak and Abhinav Gupta; builds "Skild Brain," an omni-bodied robotics foundation model. Lead: SoftBank Group. Participants: NVIDIA NVentures, Macquarie Capital, Bezos Expeditions, 1789 Capital, Disruptive; existing Lightspeed, Felicis, Coatue, Sequoia increased; strategics Samsung, LG, Schneider Electric, CommonSpirit, Salesforce Ventures; plus TF Capital, Mirae Asset, Destiny. Total raised to date ~$2B. Traction claim: $100M ARR run rate within 10 months of first commercial deployment; 8 → 60+ customers in 2026 [VENDOR — cryptobriefing]. History: $300M Series A (July 2024, $1.5B); $500M Series B (May 2025, $4.7B).

### Runway

- **February 10–15, 2026 — $315M Series E at $5.3B.** VERIFIED (TechCrunch, company blog). Gen-4.5 world-model release drove the round. Lead: General Atlantic. Participants: NVIDIA, Adobe Ventures, AllianceBernstein, AMD Ventures, Fidelity, Mirae Asset, Emphatic Capital, Felicis, Premji Invest. Total raised $860M.
