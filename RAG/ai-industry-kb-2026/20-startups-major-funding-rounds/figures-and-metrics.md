## Main actors

### The four mega-labs

- **OpenAI** — $122B primary (March 2026) + $7B self-funded tender (August 2026), both at $852B. Also a strategic investor (Merge Labs lead, $250M at $850M). Confidential IPO filing June 8, 2026.
- **Anthropic** — $30B Series G (February 12, 2026) at $380B, co-led by GIC and Coatue; May 2026 follow-on CONTRADICTED, excluded from fact claims. AMD–Anthropic contingent equity deal (July 22, 2026, up to $5B milestone-linked equity) is a strategic instrument, not a cash round (from earlier waves).
- **xAI** — $20B Series E (January 6–7, 2026) at $230–240B; absorbed by SpaceX February 2, 2026 (detail as recorded; no URL in source).
- **Mistral AI** — €3B (September 8, 2026) at ~€21B/$24B, PSG Equity/Samsung/Scaleup Europe Fund leading; Europe's largest private tech round per company claim.

### Compute-infrastructure companies

- **Cerebras** — Series H $1B at $23B (February 2026, Tiger Global); IPO May 14, 2026 (CBRS); confidential S-1 February 20, 2026; G42 exit for CFIUS clearance.
- **Groq** — $650M (June) + $350M at $3.5B (August 17, 2026) funding a neocloud pivot after the December 2025 Nvidia licensing event; Disruptive leading the August round with Alex Davis as executive chairman.
- **Etched** — three 2026 raises totaling ~$1.5B across eight months; transformer-specific Sohu ASIC; Jane Street as August lead and customer; Michael Burry endorsement August 18, 2026.

### Defense-tech

- **Helsing** — $1.8B Series E at $18B (July 13, 2026); Dragoneer lead per most reports, Lightspeed co-lead; majority European-owned; HX-2 drone, Altra software, CA-1 fighter concept.
- **Anduril** — $5B at $61B (May 2026); Thrive Capital and a16z leading; Arsenal-1 (Ohio) scale-up.
- Context peers: **Quantum Systems** ($1.2B at $8B, July 2026), **Castelion** ($1B, August 20, 2026, hypersonics), **Harmattan AI** ($200M at $1.4B, Dassault Aviation, January 2026).
