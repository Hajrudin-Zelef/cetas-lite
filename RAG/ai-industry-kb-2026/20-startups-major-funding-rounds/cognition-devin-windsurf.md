### Cerebras

Nearly triple the $8.1B Series G (September 2025, $1.1B, Fidelity/Atreides). Confidential S-1 filed February 20, 2026; G42 divested to clear CFIUS (no longer listed as investor).
- **May 14, 2026 — IPO on Nasdaq (CBRS).** One mention only; IPO pricing mechanics → §21. A deep-dive reports $5.55B raised at ~$43.9B pricing with a $67B day-1 close, but pre-IPO estimates ranged up to $3.5B — IPO proceeds are CONTRADICTED across sources and are presented with attribution, not as unqualified fact. Financials: 2025 revenue $510M; Q2 2026 cloud/services $127.7M (+287% YoY) vs hardware $82.1M (+17%); core gross margin 40.6%. Reported-but-single-source: >$10B OpenAI deal [UNVERIFIED].

### Groq

- **June 2026 — $650M pivot raise.** Funds the pivot from AI chipmaker (LPUs) to neocloud/data-center operator running Nvidia systems, after Nvidia's reported $20B December 2025 licensing deal (which also hired founder/CEO Jonathan Ross, president Sunny Madra, and ~90% of the engineering team). One low-quality digest claimed the deal "collapsed" — CONTRADICTED by TechCrunch/Bloomberg/Sacra; treated as noise.
- **August 17, 2026 — $350M Series A at $3.5B.** Led by Disruptive, with NVIDIA expected to participate — roughly half the $6.9B valuation of September 2025. Alex Davis (Disruptive founder) became executive chairman. Company disputes the "down round" framing, calling it a new valuation for the "post-Nvidia-licensing-deal version of Groq." Operating footprint: 13 data centers, 6M+ developers, 54MW → 200MW planned by 2027. ~$1B raised in roughly 60 days; $1.7B+ total disclosed.

### Etched

- **January 2026 — ~$500M at ~$5B.** Led by Stripes; participants Peter Thiel, Positive Sum, Ribbit Capital. Company founded 2022 by Gavin Uberti, Chris Zhu, Robert Wachen; Sohu is a transformer-hardwired 4nm ASIC (TSMC), pitched as 10–20x a GPU on transformer inference [VENDOR — no independent benchmarks or public pricing as of September 2026].
- **June 30, 2026 — exits stealth** with working A0 silicon, 8-chip rack-scale system, >$1B signed customer contracts, ~$800M cumulative raised, first racks scheduled to ship summer 2026.
- **July 23, 2026 — $300M Series C at $10.3B.** Led by Sequoia; a16z, SK Hynix, Jane Street participated.
- **August 2026 — ~$700M at ~$21B.** Led by Jane Street (also a customer); reported by Sentinel/TechCrunch-syndicated digests. One digest calls it "Series B" — round lettering is CONTRADICTED across sources; lettering omitted in favor of amounts/dates. Michael Burry shared the WSJ piece (August 18, 2026), calling Etched "serious competition for NVDA." Cumulative totals conflict ($800M across four rounds vs ~$1.5B summed from individual rounds) — see Contradictions in wave source.

### Helsing

- **July 13, 2026 — $1.8B Series E at $18B.** Closed July 13, 2026. VERIFIED. Largest private defense-tech financing in European history; largest-ever round for a European defense startup. Lead: Dragoneer (most sources; one report names Goldman Sachs as lead — lead attribution slightly inconsistent); Lightspeed co-lead; participants Disruptive, ICONIQ, Growth Equity at Goldman Sachs Alternatives, JPMorganChase, Canada Pension Plan Investment Board, General Catalyst, Plural, StepStone. Company said demand significantly exceeded allocation; remains majority European-owned. Prior: €600M Series D at €12B (June 2025, Daniel Ek's Prima Materia lead). Context: HX-2 strike drone (Bundeswehr €269M initial order, framework up to €1.46B), Altra battlefield software, proposed CA-1 autonomous fighter.

### Skild AI

Founded 2023 by CMU professors Deepak Pathak and Abhinav Gupta; builds "Skild Brain," an omni-bodied robotics foundation model. Lead: SoftBank Group. Participants: NVIDIA NVentures, Macquarie Capital, Bezos Expeditions, 1789 Capital, Disruptive; existing Lightspeed, Felicis, Coatue, Sequoia increased; strategics Samsung, LG, Schneider Electric, CommonSpirit, Salesforce Ventures; plus TF Capital, Mirae Asset, Destiny. History: $300M Series A (July 2024, $1.5B); $500M Series B (May 2025, $4.7B).
