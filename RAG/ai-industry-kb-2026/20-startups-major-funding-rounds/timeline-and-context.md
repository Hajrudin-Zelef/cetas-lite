# Timeline and context

## Timeline and context

### January 2026 — the record ten days

- ~$25B of AI funding announced in about ten days across xAI, Anthropic (negotiating), Skild, Etched, Cyera, Parloa, Merge Labs, Harmattan, LMArena, Deepgram [SECONDARY roundup].
- **January 6–7**: xAI closes $20B Series E at $230–240B (initial $15B target exceeded).
- **January 14**: Skild AI closes $1.4B Series C at >$14B (SoftBank lead).
- **January (month)**: Etched closes ~$500M at ~$5B (Stripes-led). Inferact raises $150M [UNVERIFIED; brief flag only]. RadixArk reported at a $400M valuation [UNVERIFIED; brief flag only].

### February 2026 — Anthropic's Series G and Cerebras's last private round

- **February 10–15**: Runway closes $315M Series E at $5.3B (General Atlantic lead).
- **February 12**: Anthropic closes $30B Series G at $380B (GIC/Coatue co-leads).
- **February 20**: Cerebras confidentially files S-1; G42 divests to clear CFIUS.
- **February (month)**: Cerebras closes $1B Series H at $23B (Tiger Global lead; AMD joins; $225M Benchmark SPV).
- **February 2**: xAI absorbed by SpaceX (as recorded in wave source; no URL).

### March 2026 — OpenAI's $122B and Harvey's first 2026 raise

- **March**: OpenAI closes $122B primary at $852B post (Amazon, NVIDIA, SoftBank anchors; Microsoft continues; SoftBank co-leads with a16z, D.E. Shaw, MGX, TPG, T. Rowe Price).
- **March**: Harvey closes $200M at $11B.
- **March 26**: WSJ (via Reuters) reports Reflection AI talks — $2.5B at $25B pre [UNVERIFIED].
- **March 28**: Bloomberg reports Physical Intelligence talks — ~$1B at >$11B [UNVERIFIED].

### May 2026 — Cerebras IPO, Cognition, Anduril

- **May 14**: Cerebras IPOs on Nasdaq (CBRS); pricing mechanics → §21; proceeds figure conflicted ($5.55B at ~$43.9B pricing / $67B day-1 close vs up-to-$3.5B pre-IPO estimates).
- **May 27**: Cognition closes $1B+ Series D at $25B pre / $26B post (Lux, General Catalyst, 8VC).
- **May**: Anduril raises $5B at $61B (Thrive, a16z); talks had surfaced in March for up to $8B at ~$60B.
- **May 28–29**: AP/TechXplore reports Anthropic $65B at $965B — CONTRADICTED by secondary sources ($30B at ~$900B); not stated as fact.

### June 2026 — the SpaceX–Anysphere deal and Groq's pivot raise

- **June 8**: OpenAI confidential IPO filing (from earlier waves; mechanics → §21).
- **June 16**: SpaceX agrees to acquire Anysphere/Cursor for $60B in stock (Reuters-confirmed; regulatory pending; expected close Q3 2026).
- **June 22**: Groq confirms $650M pivot raise (neocloud repositioning post-Nvidia licensing).
- **June 30**: Etched exits stealth (A0 silicon, 8-chip rack-scale, >$1B signed contracts).

### July 2026 — Helsing and Etched's Series C

- **July 13**: Helsing closes $1.8B Series E at $18B — Europe's largest-ever defense-tech financing. Quantum Systems raises $1.2B at $8B the same month.
- **July 23**: Etched closes $300M Series C at $10.3B (Sequoia lead).
- **July 27**: NVIDIA × SSI strategic partnership announced (Vera Rubin access, ~10x capacity) [VENDOR]; claimed $5B NVIDIA investment NOT verified.

### August 2026 — OpenAI's self-funded tender, Groq's reset, Etched's third repricing

- **August 10**: OpenAI's $7B self-funded employee tender at $852B closes (Bloomberg/CNBC).
- **August 17**: Groq closes $350M at $3.5B (Disruptive lead; NVIDIA expected); company rejects "down round" framing.
- **August 18**: Michael Burry shares WSJ piece calling Etched "serious competition for NVDA."
- **August 20**: Castelion raises $1B to scale low-cost hypersonic weapons production.
- **August (month)**: Etched ~$700M at ~$21B (Jane Street-led) reported; round lettering conflicted.
- **August 2026**: Physical Intelligence talks still described as "early discussions" — NOT closed. Perplexity talks at >$30B with NVIDIA reportedly negotiating — NOT closed.
