# hc-en-us-articles-29026995210775-unlocking-doors-with-qr-code-in-unifi-access-f2c7e027

Unlocking Doors with QR Code in UniFi Access
This guide is for visitors or guests who received a QR Code to access a site secured by UniFi Door Access. If you’re an admin trying to configure guest or visitor access, click here.
Requirements
- You've received an email, SMS, or QR code with your visit details, including the visit time, person to visit, and door access credentials.
Note: If you have not received any visit details, please contact your primary point of contact at the location you'll be visiting.
Unlocking Doors with QR Code
- Approach a reader at your scheduled visit time.
- Open your invitation email and find the QR code.
- Tap Scan QR Code on the reader.
- Hold your QR code in front of the camera and pause briefly as the system processes your code.
- Once recognized, the door will unlock.
FAQs
Can I unlock a door outside my scheduled visit?
No, your QR code is automatically revoked once the visit time ends.
