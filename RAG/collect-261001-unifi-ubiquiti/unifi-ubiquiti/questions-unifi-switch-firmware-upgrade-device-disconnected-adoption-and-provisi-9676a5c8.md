# questions-unifi-switch-firmware-upgrade-device-disconnected-adoption-and-provisi-9676a5c8

@UI-Team
I ran firmware updates on several of our Unifi switches (US-8 and US-24). During the update one of the switches failed.
These switches are connected to one another, and I ran a manual upgrade. What I think happened was that I tried to upgrade the second switch right when the first one was still updating. Long story short, the US-24 could not finish the update and came back with a Disconnected status.
I tried all the following:
None of the above worked.
I ended up getting a paperclip and pressing the device using the "Reset" button on the front panel.
This worked.
After the reset I could adopt the device once again, and apply the firmware update.
At the time I was trying to find possible solutions to this issue online, but I could not. So I am posting this topic as a reminder.
Maybe someone else will have the same issue.
