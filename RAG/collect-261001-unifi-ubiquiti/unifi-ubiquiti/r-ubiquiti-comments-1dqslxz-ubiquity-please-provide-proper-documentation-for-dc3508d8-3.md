I'm the owner of a small MSP. I do not use the UDM routers, as I consider them consumer gear. However, I have over 100 clients set up on EdgeRouters and they work fantastically well.
The EdgeRouter firewall has always seemed very straightforward to me. It's set up similar to Cisco routers using Cisco's "reflexive firewall" feature. By the way, I'm talking about old-school Cisco's like the 2800 and 2900 series, not the modern stuff.
I have a standard firewall config I use on all of the EdgeRouters, and then modify it to suit the client.
For my clients that have more restrictive firewall requirements, or who have compliance requirements they need to meet, then I use FortiGate units instead.
