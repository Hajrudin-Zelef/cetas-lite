# unifi-vpn-setup-guide-e318520d

Log in to your UniFi device, then go to Settings > VPN and switch to the VPN Server tab. Click Create New to set up a new VPN server:
Choose OpenVPN as your connection type, then add a new VPN User to generate your .ovpn configuration file.
Now, open the VPN Tracker OpenVPN Connection Creator for UniFi. Here you will be prompted to upload the configuration file you downloaded from your UniFi gateway:
Once you've uploaded your configuration file, your UniFi gateway settings will be automatically imported.
Now, all that's left to do is save your configuration in your account using secure end-to-end encryption and connect to your UniFi on your Mac, iPhone or iPad in VPN Tracker!
