# article-introducing-the-next-generation-of-unifi-design-center-00c3d5a0

From planning to deployment, Design Center provides a seamless way to visualize, optimize, and prepare your UniFi network across web and mobile.
Import a floor plan from images or a PDF and Design Center automatically generates walls, creating an accurate foundation for RF coverage simulations.
Define zones and client capacity requirements, then let Design Center determine ideal access point locations and recommend an optimized WiFi channel plan.
Combine multiple 2D floor plans into a cohesive 3D building model for a clearer view of your entire deployment and enhanced RF planning.
Add your design to Site Manager for pre-provisioning, then watch it come to life in InnerSpace as part of the UniFi management experience.
Persistent syncing across web and mobile keeps your latest project available wherever planning or deployment takes you.
Whether you are migrating to UniFi or setting up a brand new deployment, Design Center delivers intelligent planning, visualization, and deployment tools without recurring software fees.
