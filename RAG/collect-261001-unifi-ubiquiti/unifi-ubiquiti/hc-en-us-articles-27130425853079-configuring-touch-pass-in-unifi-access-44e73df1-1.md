Configuring Touch Pass in UniFi Access
Touch Pass is a UniFi Access feature designed for business use, making door unlocking effortless. With Touch Pass, users can unlock doors by simply tapping their phone or watch on a compatible reader—no need to open a mobile app or press any buttons! For a complete list of supported unlock methods, click here.
| Update your UniFi OS, as well as your Access and Protect applications and devices, to the latest versions for the newest features and optimal performance. | 
Requirements
UniFi device and version support
- UniFi OS 4.4.6 or later
- Access application 4.1.15 or later
- 
          Touch Pass is compatible with NFC readers certified by Apple
          and
          Google.
