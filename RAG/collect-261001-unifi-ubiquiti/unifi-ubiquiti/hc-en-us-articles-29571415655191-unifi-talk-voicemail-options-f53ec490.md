# hc-en-us-articles-29571415655191-unifi-talk-voicemail-options-f53ec490

UniFi Talk Voicemail Options
Proper voicemail setup ensures that you can receive and manage messages effectively. You can configure voicemail on your Touch phone, or through the UniFi Talk application.
Setting Up Voicemail
Touch Phone
- From the Keypad, dial *86 or long-press 1.
- Follow the audio prompts to configure your voicemail.
Flex Phone
- Press the MESSAGE button to access voicemail.
- Follow the audio prompts to complete the voicemail configuration.
UniFi Talk
- Go to Assignments > Users, select a user and open their Settings.
  - Alternatively, go to Assignments > Groups and select a Group.
- Scroll to Voicemail to record or generate a greeting and adjust timeout settings.
Listening to Voicemail
To play Voicemail on a Touch Phone:
- Press the Voicemail button.
- Follow the prompts to access and listen to your messages.
You can also listen to voicemail in the Call Logs section of the Talk application. For more information, click here.
