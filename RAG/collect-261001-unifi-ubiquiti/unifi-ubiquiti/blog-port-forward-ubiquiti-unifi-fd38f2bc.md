The UniFi controller software enables centralized administration from a single interface. With its multi-site management, the UniFi security gateway provides scalable management through access points and switches. The diverse UniFi APs sometimes lag or must be sufficient while providing access.

Prerequisites to Ubiquiti UniFi port forwarding
There are some of the things you can collect handy for the further port forwarding process:
- The static IP address of the UniFi device
- UniFi controller software
- Access to the router’s gateway
- Port numbers in TCP/UDP
Ubiquiti UniFi port forwarding in steps
Here are the detailed steps to configure port forwarding manually to your UniFi router:
- Access the web interface by entering the IP address of your router
- Sign in with your credentials
- Find the section on port forwarding
- Create a rule for port forwarding; give it a name like UniFi control ports
- Enter the internal IP address, one collected earlier.
- Select the protocols and the port numbers you wish to forward.
- Save and apply the changes made.
- You are good to go! Check if changes are applied successfully or not!

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between a port and a forward port in UniFi?
In networking terms, a port is the communication endpoint on the device or a server. A forward port is a term used to address a port that we wish to communicate with through port forwarding.
How to open ports on UniFi UDM?
Here’s how you can open ports in UniFi UDM:
– Log in to your UDM Pro firmware settings
– Look for the port forwarding section of the router and click “Create New Port Forward Rule.” 
– Enter the name for your port forwarding rule.
Is port forwarding the same as VPN?
Port forwarding is the process that allows access to devices through ports. On the contrary, a VPN is a type of network that shields your network through its encryption technique.
What are UniFi controller ports?
General UniFi controller ports are:
– TCP 8080 and 8443
– TCP 6789
– TCP 27117
– UDP 3478
– UDP 10001
– TCP 8881
– TCP 22
