# §6 Market context (2026)

## §6 Market context (2026)

- **IDC Q1 2026**: Ethernet switch market $15.4B (+39.8% YoY); data-center segment $10B (+61%); NVIDIA 21.5%, Arista 20.7%, Cisco 17.8% [independent].
- **Dell'Oro Q1 2026**: 800G the fastest-growing segment; 800G switches = 35.8% of DC segment revenue [independent — via trade press].
- **NVIDIA networking revenue**: ~$14.8B in Q1 FY2027 (+199% YoY) [vendor-reported]; Ethernet demand "roughly on par" with InfiniBand [secondary].
- **HPE Networking**: $2.9B Q3 FY26; Networks-for-AI $700M quarterly orders; combined AI backlog $7.6B [vendor-reported].
- **Dell**: networking revenue not separately disclosed; AI-server revenue dominates systems mix (FY26) [independent — Next Platform].
- Structural shift: AI buyers procure networking **bundled with GPU stacks**, redrawing vendor standing — incumbents (Cisco, Arista) now compete against NVIDIA's full-stack platform [independent — IDC/Business Insider].
