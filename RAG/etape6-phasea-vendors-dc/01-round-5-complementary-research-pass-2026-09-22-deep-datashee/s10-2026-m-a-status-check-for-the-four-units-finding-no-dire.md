### S10. 2026 M&A status check for the four units — finding: no direct deals (new)

- CRN's "The 10 Biggest Tech M&A Deals in 2026 (So Far)" (updated ~Sept 2026) contains **no acquisition of Dell Networking, NVIDIA Networking, HPE/Aruba Networking, or Cisco Meraki units** [independent — crn.com]. The closest networking-adjacent channel deal: **Myriad360 acquired Advizex (announced February 2026)** — combining a top NVIDIA InfiniBand partner with a Dell Titanium / HPE and Cisco AI-networking partner; combined revenue ~$1.2B after the follow-on F3 Technology Partners asset acquisition (August 2026) [independent — crn.com].
- Lenovo completed its **Infinidat** storage acquisition (April 9, 2026; $500M–$1.5B analyst range) — storage, not networking, but relevant as the infrastructure-vendor consolidation backdrop [independent — crn.com].
