# starting-a-basic-wireless-isp-wisp-with-mikrotik-nexrein-blog

Internet connectivity has become an essential service for homes, businesses and rural communities. For entrepreneurs and network professionals, starting a Wireless Internet Service Provider (WISP) presents an opportunity to deliver reliable connectivity while building a scalable business. With MikroTik networking equipment, setting up a basic WISP is both affordable and flexible.

## What is a WISP?

A Wireless Internet Service Provider (WISP) delivers internet access using wireless technologies instead of relying entirely on fiber or cable infrastructure. WISPs commonly serve areas where traditional broadband is limited, expensive or unavailable.

Using wireless links, a WISP can distribute internet connectivity from a central point to multiple customers over short or long distances.

## Basic Requirements for a WISP Setup

Before launching your WISP, you need several essential components.

### 1. Reliable Internet Source

Your WISP requires a strong upstream internet connection from an ISP or fiber provider. The speed and reliability of this connection determine the quality of service you provide to customers.

### 2. MikroTik Router

A MikroTik router acts as the core of your network. Popular options include:

- 
MikroTik RB4011
- 
MikroTik CCR series
- 
hAP series for smaller deployments

The router manages routing, authentication, bandwidth control and security.

### 3. Wireless Access Equipment

To distribute connectivity wirelessly, you need radios and access points. Common MikroTik wireless products include:

- 
Base station antennas
- 
Sector antennas
- 
Point-to-point radios
- 
Customer Premises Equipment (CPE)

These devices create wireless links between your tower and subscriber locations.


### 4. Tower or Elevated Mounting

Height matters in wireless networking. Installing antennas on towers, rooftops or masts improves signal coverage and reduces obstructions.

Maintaining clear line-of-sight is critical for stable performance.

### 5. Power Backup and Protection

Power interruptions can disrupt service. Use:

- 
UPS systems
- 
Surge protection
- 
Backup power solutions

These protect equipment and maintain uptime.

## Basic MikroTik WISP Configuration

A simple MikroTik WISP setup usually follows these steps:

### Configure WAN Internet Access

Connect the upstream ISP connection to your MikroTik router and verify internet access.

### Create LAN and Subscriber Network

Assign local IP ranges and establish the internal network that customers will use.

### Enable PPPoE or Hotspot Authentication

Authentication helps manage customer accounts and billing.

PPPoE is commonly used because it allows:

- 
Username and password access
- 
Speed limitation
- 
User management
- 
Session tracking

### Set Bandwidth Control

MikroTik Queue Management allows operators to:

- 
Limit user speeds
- 
Prevent bandwidth abuse
- 
Prioritize important traffic
- 
Maintain fair network performance

### Secure the Network

Security is essential. Configure:

- 
Firewall rules
- 
Strong passwords
- 
RouterOS updates
- 
Remote access restrictions

This reduces risks from unauthorized access and attacks.

## Challenges to Expect

Starting a WISP involves more than installing equipment.

Common challenges include:

- 
Line-of-sight limitations
- 
Weather interference
- 
Power reliability
- 
Customer support demands
- 
Licensing and regulatory requirements
- 
Initial infrastructure costs

Proper planning helps minimize these issues.
