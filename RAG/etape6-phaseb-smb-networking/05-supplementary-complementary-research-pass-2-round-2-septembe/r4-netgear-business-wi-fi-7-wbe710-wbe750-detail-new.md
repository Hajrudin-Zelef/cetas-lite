## R11. Market shock — US FCC foreign-made consumer-router import ban, Mar 2026 (new; verify against FCC record)

- On **~Mar 23, 2026**, FCC Chair Brendan Carr announced a national-security determination blocking new imports of foreign-made consumer routers; existing models sell through channel stock only [secondary — webpronews, appleinsider, gadgetreview, crunchupdates; **verify against FCC docket before treating as fact**].
- Companies listed as affected include **TP-Link, Ubiquiti**, Asus, Linksys, Synology, Arris, Amazon/eero, Google Nest, Calix, Sagemcom [secondary — appleinsider].
- Market impact claims [secondary, unverified]: TP-Link ~65% of US consumer router unit volume; Morgan Stanley estimated +15–25% average US router prices; relocation cost +15–40% depending on destination; only DoD/DHS can grant exemptions; enterprise/industrial equipment follows separate frameworks.
- Sources: https://www.webpronews.com/fccs-ban-on-chinese-made-routers-reshapes-the-networking-hardware-market-and-creates-clear-winners/ ; https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/04/15/whitelisted-which-router-manufacturers-arent-affected-by-fccs-ban ; https://www.webpronews.com/the-router-lockout-inside-the-fccs-unprecedented-ban-on-foreign-made-consumer-networking-equipment/
- **Caution:** sourced from tech blogs only; no primary FCC order text located. If confirmed, this is the single largest 2026 market-structure event for the SMB/consumer networking segment (directly affects TP-Link and Ubiquiti's consumer lines; NETGEAR's margin-windfall narrative is analyst opinion, not fact).
