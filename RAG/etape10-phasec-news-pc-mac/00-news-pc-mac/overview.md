# Step 10 Phase C — PC & Mac News (February → September 2026)

## 0. Executive Summary

- 2026 was a **gap year for discrete gaming GPUs**: NVIDIA reportedly launched no new gaming GPU, the RTX 50 "Super" refresh ("Kicker") was deprioritized, and RTX 60 (Rubin) slipped to 2027–2028; AMD's RDNA 5 slipped to late 2027/early 2028; Intel canceled Celestial discrete gaming GPUs [secondary].
- The **memory crisis** drove everything: DRAM/GDDR supply diverted to AI accelerators, GPU prices soared, and Apple pulled 512GB Mac Studio configs in March 2026 [secondary].
- **CPUs moved forward**: Intel launched Panther Lake (Core Ultra Series 3) on Intel 18A at CES 2026 and confirmed Nova Lake (Core Ultra 400) for end of 2026; AMD confirmed AM5 through 2029 at Computex 2026 with Zen 6 desktop pushed to H1 2027 [secondary].
- **Apple** refreshed pro desktops late: M5 Ultra + M6 announced August 25, 2026, shipping September 22, 2026 (Mac mini $899, Mac Studio $5,499) [official].
- **Windows 12 did not happen**: a viral March 2026 report was retracted; Microsoft focused on fixing Windows 11 (26H2) and teased NVIDIA N1X Arm hardware instead [secondary].
- **GTA VI** held its November 19, 2026 date through Take-Two earnings; **Switch 2** (launched June 2025) sold 15.39M copies of Mario Kart World by end of June 2026 [secondary].

## 2. Chronology — March 2026

### 2.1 The Windows 12 rumor and its retraction
- 2026-03-02: PCWorld published a report claiming an AI-first Windows 12 ("Hudson Valley Next," CorePC modular architecture, 40 TOPS NPU requirement, possible subscription tier) targeting October 2026; it went viral (14,000+ Reddit upvotes) [secondary].
- The report was debunked by Windows Central's Zac Bowden (contacts familiar with the Windows roadmap: no Windows 12 in the 2026 pipeline; CorePC was a 2023 internal project believed dropped; the "Hudson Valley Next" codename traced to 2023; UI screenshots were a 2022 leaked concept) [secondary]. URL: https://www.windowscentral.com/microsoft/windows-11/no-an-ai-focused-windows-12-is-not-coming-this-year-false-report-gets-the-facts-completely-wrong
- PCWorld retracted the piece: "It does not meet PCWorld's standards and should not have been published" (executive editor Brad Chacos); the article was a translation of a German PC-Welt piece presented as original reporting [secondary]. URL: https://www.tweaktown.com/news/110356/ai-focused-windows-12-operating-system-is-not-coming-this-year/index.html
- Microsoft's actual 2026 Windows roadmap: fix Windows 11 — reduce AI bloat, address top user feedback, bring back the movable taskbar; next major version Windows 11 26H2 [secondary]. URL: https://www.windowslatest.com/2026/03/05/microsoft-isnt-launching-a-subscription-based-windows-12-ai-os-in-2026-the-rumors-are-just-ai-hallucinations/
