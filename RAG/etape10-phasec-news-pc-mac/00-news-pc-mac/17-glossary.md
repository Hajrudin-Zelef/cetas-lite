# 17. Glossary

## 17. Glossary

- **BLLC** — Big Last Level Cache; Intel's rumored large-cache gaming tech (Nova Lake), answer to AMD 3D V-Cache [unverified].
- **Copilot+ PC** — Microsoft certification requiring 40+ TOPS NPU for on-device AI features.
- **Kicker** — reported codename of the canceled/deprioritized RTX 50 Super refresh [secondary].
- **Medusa** — AMD Zen 6 mobile APU family (Medusa Point / Medusa Halo) [unverified].
- **Nova Lake** — Intel Core Ultra 400 client CPU family, end of 2026 [secondary].
- **Olympic Ridge** — AMD Zen 6 desktop CPU family, H1 2027 [secondary].
- **Panther Lake** — Intel Core Ultra Series 3 mobile CPUs on Intel 18A, launched Jan 2026 [secondary].
- **Project Amethyst** — AMD–Sony joint next-gen graphics R&D (Neural Arrays, Radiance Cores) [secondary].
- **Rubin** — NVIDIA's next datacenter/gaming GPU architecture generation (informal label) [unverified].
- **TOPS** — trillions of operations per second (INT8), the standard NPU metric in 2026 marketing.
- **UDNA** — AMD's planned unified GPU architecture succeeding RDNA/CDNA split [secondary].
- **UltraFusion** — Apple's multi-die interconnect (4.4TB/s on M5 Ultra) [official].
