# 3. Chronology — April 2026

## 3. Chronology — April 2026

### 3.1 Intel cancels Arc Celestial discrete gaming GPUs
- 2026-04-24: Leaker Jaykihn (@jaykihn0) posted that Intel canceled discrete gaming GPUs for the Xe3P "Celestial" architecture "long ago" and that Xe4 "Druid" was "up in the air" — picked up by Tom's Hardware, VideoCardz, WCCFTech [secondary/unverified — leak, not officially confirmed].
- VideoCardz (2026-04-25) reported: no gaming-focused discrete cards planned under Celestial; Xe3P reserved for Crescent Island / Crescent Island Workstation AI datacenter GPUs (160GB LPDDR5X, customer sampling H2 2026) [secondary]. URL: https://videocardz.com/newz/intel-reportedly-has-no-xe3p-celestial-arc-gaming-gpus-planned-xe4-druid-up-in-the-air
- Consequence: Arc B580/B570 (Battlemage, launched December 2024, $249) remain Intel's latest discrete gaming GPUs; by late 2025 Intel had crossed 1% discrete GPU market share per Jon Peddie Research [secondary]. URL: https://geekrealmhub.com/intel-arc-celestial-gaming-gpu-cancelled/
- Xe3P still ships in integrated form: Panther Lake iGPUs, a special Nova Lake-S desktop CPU with 12 Xe3P cores, and Nova Lake-H mobile [unverified]. URL: https://ranzware.com/intel-has-reportedly-cancelled-discrete-gaming-gpus-for-the-upcoming-xe3p-arc-celestial-family-gaming-gpu-remains-uncertain-even-for-the-next-gen-xe4-druid-lineup-that-lands-in-2027
- The Arc Pro B70 workstation GPU (BMG-G31 "Big Battlemage") was positioned for AI, not gaming [secondary]. URL: https://www.club386.com/intel-arc-celestial-cancelled-leak/
- 2026-04 (reported May 2026): a hack revealed Rockstar still made ~$1M/day from GTA Online [secondary]. URL: https://www.polygon.com/grand-theft-auto-6-delay-release-date-confirmed/

## 7. Chronology — August 2026

### 7.1 Apple: M5 Ultra + M6, new Mac mini and Mac Studio
- 2026-08-25: Apple announced M5 Ultra ("most powerful chip ever") and M6 at a virtual event (first reported by TechCrunch) [secondary]. URL: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/25/apple-debuts-m5-ultra/
- M5 Ultra specs [official]: quad-die (two fused dual-die M5 Max via next-gen UltraFusion), up to 36 CPU cores (12 super + 24 performance), up to 80 GPU cores with per-core Neural Accelerator, 32-core Neural Engine, up to 512GB unified memory, 1.2TB/s bandwidth (+50% vs M3 Ultra), 4.4TB/s inter-die bandwidth; up to 1.25x single-thread / 1.3x multi-thread vs M3 Ultra; 4.3x peak AI compute vs M3 Ultra; 33 streams of 8K ProRes 422 playback; Thunderbolt 5 + RDMA clustering (4 Studios = 3x inference) [official]. URL: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/25/apple-announces-new-mac-studio-with-m5-ultra-chip/
- M5 Max (also in new Mac Studio): 18-core CPU, up to 40 GPU cores, up to 128GB unified memory, 614GB/s; 3.9x AI performance claim vs previous generation [official].
- M6: 2nm process; new CPU complex, dual 16-core Neural Engine, more unified-memory bandwidth (per Apple VP Sri Santhanam) [secondary].
- Pricing/availability [secondary]: M6 Mac mini $899 (16GB/256GB); M5 Pro Mac mini $1,699; M5 Ultra Mac Studio $5,499 (96GB/1TB); preorders opened late August, availability September 22, 2026; 512GB-memory Studio config ships late October (>$10,000 expected) [secondary]. URL: https://tech-insider.org/apple-m6-m5-ultra-ai-computer-strategy-2026/
- Independent reception: The Verge measured ~30% CPU uplift (M5 Ultra vs M3 Ultra) and up to 63% GPU uplift in Cinebench; Engadget recorded ~14GB/s SSD writes; reviewers flagged the $899 mini's lost value positioning and 16GB base memory forcing swap on larger local AI models [independent]. URL: https://particle.news/story/apple-releases-m6-mac-mini-and-m5-maxm5-ultra-mac-studio
- The Information reported the unusually early (pre-iPhone-event) announcement was driven by unexpectedly strong enterprise AI demand for Mac mini/Mac Studio [secondary]. URL: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/25/apple-announces-new-mac-studio-with-m5-ultra-chip/
- 2026-08-20: "Mission Control Intel" GPU roadmap analysis — RTX 60 and RDNA 5 pushed to mid-2027/early 2028; RTX 50 Super refresh as bridge; current-gen price bottom mid-2026 [secondary, AI-generated blog — treat cautiously].
