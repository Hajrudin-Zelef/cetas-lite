## Full summary

This June 25, 2026 edition of the Vision-IA newsletter leads with Anthropic going on the offensive. In a letter obtained by CNBC, the company accuses Chinese giant Alibaba of conducting "the largest known distillation attack to date" against its models—massively querying Claude to absorb its know-how and replicate it in its own systems without paying for years of research. Distillation consists of querying a model a very large number of times to replicate its behavior in a competing, cheaper model. Anthropic calls the maneuver an illicit campaign and plain theft of intellectual property; Claude is the targeted flagship model. The newsletter notes this highlights how fragile frontier labs' technological barriers may be if a model can be copied by mass querying, and adds a new front to Sino-American tech rivalry on IP grounds, likely leading to stricter API access and litigation.

OpenAI unveiled Jalapeño, its first in-house processor, built with Broadcom and designed exclusively for inference (not training), expected to run at scale by end of 2026, aiming to reduce dependence on Nvidia. This mirrors Apple's strategy of controlling its own silicon. GPT-5.5 rolled out to the free tier of ChatGPT (improved context understanding, no action required), putting frontier capability in hundreds of millions of hands. Google DeepMind announced "computer use" for Gemini 3.5 Flash: the model can perceive the screen, click, fill forms, and chain multi-step actions autonomously—an affordable model bringing agentic capability to scale, attacking Anthropic's and OpenAI's agents.

The "Research" section covers Microsoft Research's open-source Talos, which reanalyzes genomic data over time; deployed on ~5,000 undiagnosed patients, it yielded 241 new diagnoses (+5.1% yield) with minimal false positives, leaving on average only 1.3 genetic variants per patient (90% of cases). Self-taught engineer Tom Di Mino claims to have deciphered Linear A (a 3,500-year-old Minoan script unsolved for 70 years) using Python scripts driven by AI agents in five months, producing a 408-entry lexicon and 40 phonetic values—though Cambridge and Rutgers linguists have not validated the "proposed" results. A researcher challenged Microsoft's quantum "breakthrough," alleging basic methodological errors in the Python validation code (166 HN points).

Additional briefs include Claude arriving in Slack via Claude Tag (beta, powered by Claude Opus 4.8, accessing enterprise databases/tools/repos); Anthropic reportedly surpassing OpenAI in enterprise adoption per Ramp (34.4% vs 32.3%) at a $965B valuation; Google's new Interactions API (one call provisions a remote Linux sandbox for agents); Figma's Config 2026 AI canvas; General Motors replacing 1,000 workers with 50 robots; Facebook's AI companion app for creators; Qualcomm acquiring Modular for ~$4B; L'Oréal feeding ChatGPT official product data; Agility Robotics' SPAC IPO at $2.5B; Europe resisting the US MATCH Act on ASML chip equipment exports; a Tesla fatal crash lawsuit; Faraday Future pivoting to robots; OpenAI's Codex growth and collapsing AI prices; a US representative admitting use of Claude on a defense amendment; and Amazon investing in TRISO nuclear fuel for data centers.
