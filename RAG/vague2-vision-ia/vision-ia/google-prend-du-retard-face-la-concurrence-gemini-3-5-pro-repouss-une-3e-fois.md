## Full summary

This Vision-IA newsletter (Jul 18, 2026) leads with Bloomberg reporting that Google has again delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro, its flagship model, for lack of adequate results, especially in coding — the third consecutive delay of a model promised for June at the May I/O conference. The last stable version remains Gemini 3.1 Pro (February 2026). In late June, Google retrained the model with new code data, producing internally "disappointing" results (Bloomberg cites ten current or former employees); the rebuilt version was unreliable and prone to hallucination. Rivals advanced: OpenAI shipped GPT-5.6 Sol (88.8 on Terminal Bench 2.1), Meta launched Muse Spark 1.1, and China's Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, now No. 1 on the Frontend Code Arena ahead of Claude Fable 5. Google is considering a "stopgap" release; internally, 75% of new code is now written by AI, up from 50% in late 2025. Other headlines: Agility Robotics opened a 5,600 m² factory in Fremont, California, near Tesla's planned Optimus site; its Digit humanoid (1.75 m, 65 kg, 28 DoF, 16 kg payload, 8h battery, ~$250,000) is already deployed at Amazon, GXO, Schaeffler and Toyota, has moved 100,000+ bins at GXO, and has $300M in signed orders; Agility is preparing a SPAC listing (Churchill Capital Corp XI, $2.5B valuation, $620M raised). Google launched Gemini Omni Flash in Google Vids, enabling prompt-based video editing and personal avatars (10-second generation limit vs Sora's 60, SynthID watermark, 18+ English-only). Anthropic cut Fable 5 access and doubled prices from July 20 ($10/M input, $50/M output, the highest Anthropic has charged), pushing subscribers to usage-based billing. Research: Colibrì runs GLM-5.2 (744B MoE) on a PC with 25 GB RAM; Grok 4.5 beats Claude Opus 4.8 as an orchestrator at half the cost; NVIDIA and Hugging Face open large-scale video-model fine-tuning; planning a weekend with GPT-Live. Also: xAI open-sourced Grok Build, Amazon Zoox recalled 105 robotaxis, a Hyundai strike over humanoid robots, Kaiser nurses denounce an empathy-scoring AI, the EU forces Google to open Android to rival AI assistants, and Meta may rent compute to Anthropic for up to $10B.

## Key points

- Gemini 3.5 Pro delayed a third time over poor coding results; last stable is Gemini 3.1 Pro from February 2026.
- Rivals pulled ahead: GPT-5.6 Sol, Meta Muse Spark 1.1, and Kimi K3 (No. 1 Frontend Code Arena).
- Google considers a stopgap release; 75% of its new code is now AI-written (vs 50% in late 2025).
- Agility Robotics opens a Fremont factory and is already generating revenue with Digit, preparing a SPAC listing.
- Anthropic cut Fable 5 access and doubled API prices from July 20, angering subscribers.
- Colibrì runs a 744B-parameter MoE model on a 25 GB-RAM PC by streaming experts from SSD.
- The EU orders Google to open 11 Android functions to rival AI assistants by July 2027.
