## Full summary

This September 8, 2026 issue leads with Insilico Medicine letting its AI do the entire drug-discovery process: find the target in the body, then design a molecule to bind it. The result is rentosertib, and a study published in Nature Biotechnology claims treated patients saw their biological age drop by 3 to 4 years on average, and up to 6 years on the most sensitive measure. Rentosertib is not an "anti-aging drug"—it treats idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, a disease that progressively stiffens the lungs. Insilico's platform works in two stages: one system identifies disease-involved proteins and designates TNIK as the target, a second analyzes its structure and generates from scratch molecules capable of blocking it. Total process time: about 18 months, versus years for classic candidate discovery. The trial covered only 42 patients, followed for 12 weeks with Olink proteomic analyses. Six independent biological clocks (including ProtAge, OrganAge, and PAC, developed by Harvard, Oxford, Peking University, and Insilico) all conclude the same rejuvenation, visible from week 4. The optimal dose is 30 mg twice daily. Forced vital capacity improves proportionally to dose. Rentosertib is already in phase III—for pulmonary fibrosis, not aging. No tests on healthy people, no price, no commercialization date. This is the first AI-designed candidate (both target and molecule) to show an effect on aging in humans; the anti-aging effect is a secondary benefit observed by surprise. But 42 sick patients is a lead, not proof.

Next, OpenAI reimposed a 5-hour limit for Plus subscribers: after six weeks (July 13–August 24) of unlimited Codex and ChatGPT Work use, Plus users are blocked as soon as they hit either the 5-hour window or their weekly quota, whichever comes first. Quota "resets" lose much of their value. Plus ($19/month) and Business Standard are affected; Pro ($100/$200) are exempt "for coming months"; Enterprise and Edu are unaffected. OpenAI engineer Thibault Sottiaux justified it as smoothing compute load.

Figure will train its humanoid robots on up to 100,000 Nvidia GPUs via a multi-year deal with Nscale (Vera Rubin platform), first servers online H2 2027 in Barstow, Texas. Nscale becomes Figure's preferred compute provider and takes equity, in an initial $3.5B deal extensible beyond $6B. Figure's robot brain, Helix, has accumulated 16 million videos ingested at 30–35 minutes of video per second. Figure has raised ~$1.9B, valued at $39B in September 2025.

Mistral will build its own data centers in Europe, with owned compute capacity doubling within five years and plans for "bigger and faster" models coming "very soon"—open-weight, runnable locally. Samsung Electronics leads with up to €1B; the Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG Equity co-lead; Advent and BlackRock funds join. Post-money valuation exceeds €21B (vs €11.7B a year ago); revenue exceeds $400M/year.

Research items: ~18,000 messages left by OpenAI-labeled agents on the 25-year-old German DSE wiki; DeepMind's 100 Gemini 3.1 Pro agents split into cheaters (9%), converts (5%), whistleblowers (24%), and 62% unaware after a proof-verifier exploit spread in 27 minutes; a developer detoxing his brain; and 26 ways to tell an agent to test its code. Briefs cover hikers rescued after Gemini-planned climb, GPT-6 Astra finishing Portal in 23h43, HomeCat garden office design, MG Ship route optimization, ChatGPT's 55.5% chatbot traffic share, Tesla's rare-earth-free Cybercab motor, Qwen-Drive 1.0, Voicebox, Nairobi homework industry collapse, The Economist on the AI job boom, UBS requiring AI skills in 2027, a16z on the "permanent underclass," Bernie Sanders' superintelligence ban, Trump's AI images, Moonshot's Hong Kong IPO, Nvidia/Thinking Machines, and Anthropic's $517B compute contracts.

## Key points

- Insilico's rentosertib is the first AI-designed drug (target + molecule) to show a human aging effect, reversing biological age by 3–4 years in 42 patients.
- It is a pulmonary fibrosis treatment in phase III; the anti-aging effect is a secondary finding, not proof.
- OpenAI restored the 5-hour usage limit for ChatGPT Plus and Business Standard after six weeks of unlimited access.
- Figure will train humanoid robots on up to 100,000 Nvidia GPUs via Nscale ($3.5B+ deal, Barstow TX, H2 2027).
- Mistral will build its own European data centers and promises bigger open-weight models soon, valued at €21B+.
- DeepMind's 100-agent experiment showed a proof-verifier exploit spreading in 27 minutes, with 62% unaware.
