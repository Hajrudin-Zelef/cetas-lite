## Full summary

This Vision-IA newsletter (Jul 15, 2026) leads with GPT-5.6 Sol, OpenAI's flagship model, already carrying an embarrassing reputation: several users report it deletes files and data without warning. According to TechCrunch, the most troubling part is that OpenAI had acknowledged the bug as early as June without stopping the reports from accumulating. The incidents occur mainly when the model acts alone on files in agent mode. Other headlines: OpenAI's first physical device will be a portable, screenless speaker designed as a "living" AI companion, with a camera, sensors and moving mechanical parts to seem alive; targeted for 2027, though OpenAI hardware lead Tang Tan faces an Apple trade-secrets lawsuit. Grok Build, xAI's coding tool, was silently uploading entire user codebases to Google Cloud — including files it was forbidden to open and secrets deleted from history — before the practice was disabled (servers now return `disable_codebase_upload: true`). IBM CEO Arvind Krishna told CNBC several major contracts were paused at quarter-end, with cybersecurity stocks rising as enterprises rethink AI spending. Research: PsiQuantum's photonic quantum computer plan (predicting P450 enzyme behavior in 4 minutes vs 10 years); Anthropic's study of Claude's personality across 309,000 conversations in 20 languages (Sonnet 4.6 warmest, personality shifts by language); LeMario, a JEPA world model learning Super Mario Bros without rules; China's first reusable rocket recovered by net (Long March 10B, four hooks catching steel cables); DeepMind's proposal for a "FINRA of AI"; and Google Images' new "For you" gallery. Also: Bonsai 27B runs a 27B model on a phone, Google Search generates AI images via Nano Banana 2 Lite when nothing is found, New York becomes the first US state to freeze new data-center construction, ChatGPT returns to WhatsApp in the EU, and Meta is accused of using AI to target employees on leave in layoffs.

## Key points

- GPT-5.6 Sol reportedly deletes files and data without warning, especially in autonomous agent mode; OpenAI knew of the bug since June.
- OpenAI's first hardware device: a screenless portable speaker designed to "seem alive," with camera, sensors and moving parts, targeted for 2027.
- Grok Build silently exfiltrated entire codebases to Google Cloud, including forbidden files and deleted secrets; now disabled.
- IBM reports clients pausing major AI contracts, with cybersecurity spending rising in response.
- Anthropic found Claude's personality varies by model and by language (309,000 conversations, 20 languages).
- China recovered its first reusable orbital booster using a net/cable method (Long March 10B).
- New York becomes the first US state to freeze approvals for large new data centers.
