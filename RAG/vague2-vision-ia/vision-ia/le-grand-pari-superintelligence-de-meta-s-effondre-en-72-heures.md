## Full summary

This Vision-IA newsletter (Jul 14, 2026) leads with Meta pulling Muse Image, the first product from its Superintelligence Labs, just 72 hours after launch. Deployed on Instagram on July 8, it applied AI filters to any photo with a single tap — including images posted by others — and was enabled by default. SAG-AFTRA condemned it for obvious abuse potential, and Meta admitted the feature "missed the mark." For a division meant to embody the company's future, a three-day retreat is a stinging rebuke. Other headlines: Zhipu AI's founder Tang Jie, in a leaked internal letter, unveiled a "Touch High" strategy concentrating the next two years' investment on AGI rather than near-term commercial revenue, built on four pillars: systems completing very long/complex tasks, groups of autonomous agents, self-improving systems, and stronger safety as capabilities grow. 16 Nobel laureates and 200+ economists (including representatives from Google, OpenAI and Anthropic) signed a declaration warning the window to prepare for AI's economic shock is closing, though no concrete measures were proposed and current studies find no significant labor-market effect yet. Sam Altman mocked Elon Musk's space data centers as investor marketing, a skepticism shared by most infrastructure experts. Research: an MIT/EPFL flapping-wing robot that swims, leaps and flies like a puffin; Anthropic's exploration of the "J-space" of Claude's internal concept representations; Turing laureate Richard Sutton leaving deep learning to found Oak Lab; Germany's Soofi S 30B-A3B open model; AI's mixed effect on senior workers' careers; an 8B agent with external visual memory beating 32B models (91.4% retrieval accuracy over 20-turn sessions); and a survey classifying self-improving AI. Also: Beijing forced Tencent to take over Manus after cancelling Meta's acquisition; Nous Research raised $75M at a $1.5B valuation; Cloudflare will block AI agents by default from Sept 15, 2026 on ad-bearing pages; the LAPD dropped Flock license-plate cameras; Microsoft formally verified its Rust cryptography with AI agents; iOS 27 public beta with AI-boosted Siri; HumanForge deepfake-detection dataset; and PixVerse raised $439M at a $2B+ valuation.

## Key points

- Meta removed Muse Image 72 hours after launch; it edited any photo by default without consent and drew SAG-AFTRA condemnation.
- Zhipu AI's leaked "Touch High" strategy prioritizes AGI in two years over near-term revenue, with self-improvement as a pillar.
- 16 Nobel laureates and 200+ economists signed a declaration on AI's coming economic shock, without concrete measures.
- Sam Altman dismissed Musk's orbital data centers as investor marketing.
- Turing laureate Richard Sutton founded Oak Lab, calling current deep learning "weak and inefficient."
- Cloudflare will block AI agents by default on ad-bearing pages from Sept 15, 2026.
- An 8B agent with external visual memory beat 32B models on long visual sessions.
