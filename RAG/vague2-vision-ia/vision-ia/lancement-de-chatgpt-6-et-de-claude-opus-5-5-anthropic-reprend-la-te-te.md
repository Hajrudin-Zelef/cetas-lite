## Full summary

This September 23, 2026 newsletter issue covers a dense day of AI news. OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, only 90 minutes after Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5. Both GPT-6 variants are faster, cheaper derivatives of the flagship GPT-6 Astra. API prices drop roughly 50%: Sol costs $2/$10 per million input/output tokens (vs. $4/$20 for GPT-5.6 Sol), while Luna drops to $0.10/$0.50 (vs. $0.20/$1.20). Sol handles complex tasks (especially coding), while Luna targets high-volume repetitive office work. Luna is opened to Free and Go accounts, with a progressive rollout from September 22. OpenAI attributes the savings to caching and inference improvements.

Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5, released less than two months after Opus 5 (July 24), is presented as its best-ever tested model, beating the larger and 150% more expensive Fable 5.1 on several benchmarks. It is multimodal (text and image) with a 1M-token context window. Pricing is $4 per million input tokens (-20%), $20 output, and prompt caching at $0.20 (-60%); a typical task costs about 40% less in practice. On Terminal-Bench 4.0 it scores 66.4% versus 52.3% for the previous generation. Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 are coming "in the coming weeks." This is the first model since Dario Amodei advocated deliberately slowing capability progress; because its biology and cybersecurity capabilities are judged comparable to Mythos, it gets the same guardrails and was evaluated by METR and Frontier Design before release.

Other items: Donald Trump announced at the UN General Assembly that the US would call AI "super intelligence" (SI), a purely oral declaration with no legal effect. AstroForge is developing Solo, a transformer-based AI to pilot its spacecraft without any radio; its first fully autonomous flight (Autonomy-1) is planned for 2027 on Stoke Space's first rocket. Research roundups cover Apollo (a Mistral co-developed ancient Greek LLM), rat-neuron video AI on AWS, OpenAI's claim of solving 100+ open math problems, aging as a genetic program starting before age 30, Neuralink restoring speech, Dassault testing AI on the Rafale, and a "pain axis" found in 25 open models. Additional briefs include Qualcomm's 30B on-device model, Meta admitting Muse draws on OpenClaw, Unreal Agent, Rabbit OS3, Toyota's 400,000 robots plan, Aurora's driverless trucks, and a Pentagon admission about an AI-assisted strike on an Iranian school.

## Key points

- OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna just 90 minutes after Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5, halving API prices.
- Claude Opus 5.5 is multimodal, 1M-token context, and beats the larger Fable 5.1 on several benchmarks at lower cost.

- AstroForge plans a fully autonomous spacecraft (Autonomy-1) with no radio for 2027.

- Anthropic slows capability progress per Dario Amodei but keeps competing aggressively on price.
