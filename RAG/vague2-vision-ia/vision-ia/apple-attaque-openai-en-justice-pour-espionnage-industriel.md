## Full summary

This Vision-IA newsletter (Jul 13, 2026) leads with Apple suing OpenAI, its former hardware chief Tang Tan, and its io devices division, accusing them of turning an aggressive recruiting campaign into a pipeline for stealing trade secrets. According to the complaint, more than 400 former Apple employees now work at the lab, a flow Apple describes as systematic and organized rather than ordinary turnover. Apple says it warned OpenAI by letter in February and received no response, and — beyond damages — it demands a redesign of OpenAI's unreleased device. This is the first major legal showdown in which a hardware giant attacks an AI lab on its own turf (devices), potentially delaying OpenAI's much-anticipated hardware. Other headlines: ex-Tesla Optimus scientist Rémi Cadène unveiled Northstar, a "faceless" humanoid built by his Paris startup UMA, with a neutral visor and soft-jointed shell, targeting European factories and warehouses first, with an open-source DNA (Cadène created Hugging Face's LeRobot). Claude Code gained an integrated browser letting the agent open, read and interact with web pages; sensitive write actions (purchases, account creation) pass through safety classifiers and require explicit validation. Musk and Altman reignited their feud on X after the Apple suit, with Altman claiming Musk is "obsessed" because of a new OpenAI model release. Research: Google's SensorFM health foundation model trained on 1+ trillion minutes from 5 million Fitbit/Pixel Watch users, beating references on 34 of 35 tasks; GPT-5.6 beats Fable-5 on coding for half the cost (DeepSWE: 72-73% at $8.40/task vs 70% at $13-22); a Barcelona device reviving donor eyes; AI plus quantum computing for drug design; 41% of long LinkedIn posts AI-generated (Pangram); AI boosting researchers but homogenizing science; and memory mechanisms for linear/state-space models. Also: open-source models face possible US regulation within six months, the viral "rogue robot" was a scripted demo, Gemini arrives in Waze, Caestro offers "hirable" AI employees, and Helsing raised €1.8B for European defense AI.

## Key points

- Apple sued OpenAI, Tang Tan and io devices over trade-secret theft tied to 400+ former Apple hires.
- Apple demands a redesign of OpenAI's unreleased device, not just damages, and says its February letter went unanswered.
- Rémi Cadène's Paris startup UMA unveiled Northstar, a deliberately non-anthropomorphic humanoid for European factories.
- Claude Code now includes a browser to act on the web, with human validation for purchases/account creation.
- Google's SensorFM health model beats prior references on 34 of 35 tasks.
- GPT-5.6 beats Fable-5 on coding at roughly half the cost per task.
- 41% of long LinkedIn posts are estimated AI-generated; open-source models may face US regulation within six months.
