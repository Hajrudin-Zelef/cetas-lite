## Full summary

This June 23, 2026 edition of the Vision-IA newsletter leads with SpaceX opening its Colossus data center to AI companies seeking compute. The first major contract, signed with open-source startup Reflection, can reach $6.3 billion: $150 million per month from July 1, 2026 to 2029, giving Reflection access to the latest Nvidia GB300 chips hosted in Colossus 2 near Memphis, Tennessee. Anthropic, Google, and Cursor have also reserved capacity. SpaceX is transforming into a compute infrastructure provider in a highly strained market, letting open-source labs access cutting-edge hardware without building their own data centers.

ByteDance unveiled five new AI models at its FORCE 2026 conference, headlined by Seedance 2.5, capable of generating videos longer than 30 seconds in a single take—a threshold most generators (around 5-10 seconds) struggle to pass—with commercial launch expected early July 2026, positioning it against Sora. Sakana AI (Japan) launched Fugu, a multi-agent orchestration system giving access to a pool of models via a single OpenAI-compatible endpoint, designed to free companies from vendor lock-in and geopolitical risks like export controls. It has two tiers (Fugu standard for low latency/coding; Fugu Ultra for complex analysis, paper/patent reproduction), an interchangeable agent pool, ~500 beta testers, and claims performance comparable to closed frontier models like Fable 5 and Mythos Preview.

A user populated a World of Warcraft server with 1,800 DeepSeek-powered AI bots for about €43/month (1,100 upvotes), illustrating how cheap conversational intelligence has become. The "Research" section covers OpenAI's GPT-5.5-Cyber (beats Anthropic's Mythos on cybersecurity benchmarks, shifts from detection to automatic vulnerability remediation, with 25+ security firms and several governments); free-particle neural cellular automata (44 HN points); Pew Research showing only 16% of Americans expect AI to have a positive impact in 20 years (14% among under-30s), while a UN report projects data center power consumption nearly doubling to 945 TWh by 2030 (Japan's equivalent); Google DeepMind's ~$75M investment in A24; and a Norwegian three-armed Sashimi-Bot with GelSight tactile sensing.

Additional briefs include Cursor's own internally trained model, native Git platform, and mobile app; Meta's own smart glasses without Ray-Ban (three styles, seven colors, one with Kylie Jenner); SemiAnalysis showing premium AI subscriptions are loss-making (ChatGPT Pro $200/month can consume up to $14,000 in compute; Claude Max ~$8,000; OpenAI goes negative above 11.4% usage), pushing Microsoft/Meta/Amazon to cap spending and startups like Lindy to switch to DeepSeek V4; Prometheus AI's $12B Series B; Anthropic-Micron memory co-design deal; Tesla's "Megapod" modular data center trademark; Microsoft's 2 GW gas-powered Texas data center; ASML's $400M 8nm lithography machine; China retaking the Top500 supercomputer lead with CPU-based Lineshine; Groq's $650M raise; Oracle cutting 21,000 jobs due to AI; NASA's faster Ernest rover; Sony's disappointing AI camera assistant; and AI virtual staging deceiving renters.
