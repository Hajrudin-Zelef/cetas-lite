## Full summary

This June 5, 2026 edition of the Vision-IA newsletter leads with Nvidia unveiling RTX Spark, a consumer chip capable of running AI models up to 120 billion parameters directly on a personal computer with no cloud dependency. RTX Spark delivers 1 PFLOP of compute in FP4 precision and includes 128 GB of unified memory; the GB10 chip runs models up to 120B entirely locally. Lenovo, HP, Microsoft, and Adobe have joined: Microsoft is re-architecting Windows, Adobe is rebuilding Photoshop and Premiere to exploit the chip, and Lenovo/HP are assembling the first RTX Spark PCs. The newsletter frames this as heavy AI becoming private, instant, and offline—rebalancing the power between personal PCs and hyperscalers. OpenAI embedded Codex, its coding agent, into ChatGPT for all users, with three knowledge-worker-oriented additions: role-based plugins, inline annotations, and Sites (building a complete web app in one prompt). Codex has 5 million weekly users, growth driven by non-developers, positioning it against Anthropic Cowork.

OpenAI deployed Dreaming, a new memory system letting ChatGPT durably retain preferences and coherent context across conversations. Ideogram and Reve simultaneously launched models (Ideogram 4.0, Reve 2.0) shifting from text prompts to layout-based approaches for precise composition control; Ideogram 4.0 is the best open-source model on Design Arena, excelling at text rendering and typography. The "Research" section covers xAI's Grok Imagine 1.5 (turning static images into cinematic video up to 720p from a text prompt); SemiAnalysis showing AI creates real economic value invisible to GDP (41% of US services GDP is measured via wages/hours, so AI productivity gains evaporate from statistics, a blind spot comparable to the Industrial Revolution); Richard Sutton (Turing Award, RL pioneer) and Banafsheh Rafiee proposing "enactive AI," where intelligence emerges from an agent interacting with its environment (four pillars: real experience, perception-action loop, autonomy, embodiment); Jeff Bezos-backed Flourish raising $500M at a $2.5B valuation to refound AI by studying the brain's actual neurological mechanisms and decode the "fundamental algorithm" of cognition; and Anthropic publishing a report on recursive self-improvement progress.

Additional briefs include Anthropic targeting $47B in annualized revenue before its IPO (up from $9B at end-2025, a 422% rise in six months), with CEO Daniela Amodei dismissing doubts about consumer AI model profitability; Apple approving Poke, the first AI agent on Messages for Business; data centers facing a water backlash (70% of Americans oppose nearby data centers, ~300,000 gallons/day consumption; Microsoft claims its new centers use as much water as one restaurant); Hello Robot launching Stretch 4; BYD developing humanoid robots via its "Yao-Shun-Yu" program (seventh generation) to sell through 150+ dealerships; Amazon's Proteus robot understanding natural language; Meta's Creator Assistant; TSMC unable to meet US AI chip demand, with the CEO warning RAM/NAND shortages will last years; the AI IPO race (some real-estate listings allegedly accepting Anthropic stock); and Elon Musk preparing SpaceX's giant IPO (~$2 trillion valuation).
