## Full summary

This April 23, 2026 edition of the Vision-IA newsletter covers Google Cloud Next 2026, where Google presented two new TPU generations—TPU 8t (training) and TPU 8i (inference)—designed to be faster and cheaper, representing its most direct offensive against Nvidia's dominance in AI infrastructure. Google still offers Nvidia GPUs (H100, B200) on its cloud, implicitly acknowledging their unavoidability. These chips are the eighth TPU generation, a program launched in 2015—rare longevity. The announcement comes as AI compute demand explodes and Nvidia GPU lead times stretch by months. Google deploys AI Overviews in Gmail for Work, generating instant summaries from multiple emails (Gemini-powered, first for Workspace Business/Enterprise users), part of a broader Workspace overhaul with AI in Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Google launched the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, letting companies build and deploy autonomous agents; it explicitly targets IT/technical users rather than non-specialists, differentiating it from OpenAI's workspace agents announced the same day. X (formerly Twitter) deployed "custom timelines," letting users pin up to 75 topics, powered by Grok—its largest UX overhaul in months, plus a "snooze topics" feature (24 hours), early access for Premium iOS subscribers, to counter user churn to Bluesky and Threads.

The "Research" section covers Microsoft Research's AutoAdapt, which automatically chooses between RAG and fine-tuning to adapt an LLM to a specific domain (law, medicine, incidents), optimizing hyperparameters under latency/cost/confidentiality constraints and turning weeks of manual iteration into reproducible processes; AI in law firms entering an operational phase, with the disruptive shift from hourly billing to value-based pricing, pushed by corporate clients demanding proof of efficiency; 10x Science raising $4.8M in seed to help researchers triage AI-generated drug candidates (the real challenge is now sorting, not discovery); and "Bitcoin: Killing Satoshi," the first IA-first feature film, using Midjourney extensively for VFX, presented at Cannes in May 2026 with Gal Gadot and Pete Davidson—yet with 154 team members, 107 real-costumed actors, and only 55 AI artists, showing "AI-generated" cinema remains very human.

Additional briefs include Senator Elizabeth Warren warning of an AI bubble that could trigger a financial crisis (comparing AI spending/leverage to the pre-2008 bubble, calling for Congressional action); OpenAI deploying Workspace agents for Business, Enterprise, Edu, and Teachers plans (analyzing customer feedback and posting to Slack, drafting follow-ups; hiring Peter Steinberger, creator of the viral OpenClaw); Jerry Tworek (ex-OpenAI) founding Core Automation, "the world's most automated AI lab," automating the AI research process itself; OpenAI making ChatGPT for Clinicians free for verified US health professionals; Google Maps gaining generative AI features; Sony AI's Ace robot beating top-ranked table-tennis players under official ITTF rules; North Korean hackers stealing ~$12M in 3 months using AI tools (a force multiplier for cybercrime); Pangram Labs finding some of the Pope's anti-AI tweets may themselves be AI-generated; Gemma 4 VLA running in real time on Jetson Orin Nano Super (vision-language-action for embedded robotics); Microsoft opening Teams to custom AI agents via SDK; Pixelle-Video, an open-source short-video generation engine (5,955+ GitHub stars); and ServiceNow's CEO betting AI fills vacancies through productivity rather than hiring.
