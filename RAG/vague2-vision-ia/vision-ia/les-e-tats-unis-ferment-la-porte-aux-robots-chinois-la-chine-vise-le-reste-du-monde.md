## Full summary

This August 31, 2026 issue leads with Washington tightening tariffs and restrictions from September on foreign-made drones and advanced robotic systems, as revealed by TechCrunch. But China already holds a massive industrial advantage: five Chinese manufacturers—AgiBot, Unitree, Galbot, UBTECH, and Leju Robotics—accounted for 86% of global humanoid robot deliveries in the first half of 2026. The measures will significantly raise prices on some imported drones and prevent new foreign devices deemed sensitive from getting authorizations. Tariffs from September 3, 2026 can reach 100% for drones over 25 kg, thermal cameras, and some docking stations; small drones face 25%. The FCC will refuse authorization for new foreign models on its Covered List, though already-purchased or previously authorized drones can still be used. The global market absorbed about 22,000 humanoid robots in H1 2026. DJI controls over two-thirds of the global drone market. Higher volumes reduce costs and produce more usage data, creating an industrial loop hard to break with tariffs. For European buyers, the effect may be inverse: Chinese manufacturers excluded from the US should shift efforts to Europe, Asia, Latin America, and the Middle East with cheaper machines.

Next, Sony Music Publishing, Warner Chappell, and affiliates filed a federal complaint on August 28, 2026 against Anthropic, Dario Amodei, and cofounder Benjamin Mann, accusing the company of copying tens of thousands of compositions, lyrics, and scores without license to train Claude, and sometimes reproducing lyrics in chatbot responses. Case 5:26-cv-09217 groups 35 plaintiff companies in the Northern District of California. The 48-page complaint cites LibGen, PiLiMi, Books3, The Pile, Common Crawl, MusixMatch, and LyricFind as alleged sources. Plaintiffs claim Anthropic took at least 5 million books from LibGen in June 2021 and 2 million from PiLiMi in July 2022 (figures for alleged libraries, not just songs). They seek up to $150,000 per work for willful infringement and $25,000 per removal of copyright information, plus a jury trial, damages, destruction of disputed copies, and disclosure of training data. Anthropic contests the accusations.

Claude Code: from September 14, 2026, Anthropic will present the permanent weekly quota as 25% above its initial level, yet subscribers will actually have 16.7% less capacity than today, because the current temporary bonus still brings the quota to 150%. The progression: 100 initially, 150 with the bonus, 125 from September 14. This affects Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise per-seat billing; 5-hour rolling caps don't change. Anthropic gives no fixed message/token counts. Web, Desktop, and Claude Code share the same usage envelope. Pro costs $20/month; Max $100 (5x) or $200 (20x).

SpaceX is building a foundry in Bastrop, Texas, capable of producing the most complex gas-turbine blades and vanes. According to Elon Musk, making these components in-house could cut 18 months off bringing new turbines online to power AI data centers faster. These parts operate at 3,000–3,600°F (~1,650–1,980°C) and must be cast as a single crystal with internal cooling channels and thermal coatings; only four companies currently master this at industrial scale. SpaceX reportedly acquired ~830 acres near Bastrop between March and June 2026. Global data center power consumption could rise from 485 TWh in 2025 to 950 TWh in 2030 (IEA). Research items cover cyborg cockroaches for rescue, the Roman Space Telescope, GPT-4o improving grades without proven learning, AI agents' poor time estimation, continuous diffusion LLMs, the longest straight-line land route, Haiku R1/beta6, the MX1 CXL device, OpenClaw Mac mini demand, and more.

## Key points

- Five Chinese manufacturers delivered 86% of global humanoid robots in H1 2026 despite new US tariffs up to 100%.
- The US measures may fragment the global market and push cheaper Chinese robots toward Europe and other regions.
- Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell sued Anthropic over tens of thousands of unlicensed songs used to train Claude.
- Claude Code's weekly quota is nominally raised 25% but effectively cut 16.7% from the current temporary bonus.
- SpaceX is building a Texas foundry for advanced gas-turbine blades to accelerate data center power.
- Global data center electricity could double from 485 TWh (2025) to 950 TWh (2030).
