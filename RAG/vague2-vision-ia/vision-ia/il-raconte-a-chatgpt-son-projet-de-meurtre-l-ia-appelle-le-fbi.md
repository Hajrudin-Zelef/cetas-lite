## Full summary

This Vision-IA newsletter (Aug 19, 2026) reports that Darren Shida Zhou, 25, a Goldman Sachs analyst in Florida, spent nearly two months describing to ChatGPT his intent to rape and kill his ex-partner, claiming he had bought weapons. OpenAI's safety systems flagged the exchanges as a serious threat, a human team reviewed them, and the company forwarded the file to the FBI. Zhou pleaded guilty on Aug 13, 2026. Conversations began in March and spanned about two months, with unambiguous statements such as "I'm going to kill her before the end of the month." He claimed to have bought an AR-15, a Glock and a 12-gauge shotgun, and to have sent his ex photos of the weapons, zip ties and latex gloves; his lawyer says he never actually owned them. The FBI passed the case to the Palm Beach County sheriff: arrest on May 29, 2026, two days in custody before bail. Three charges (aggravated stalking, written threats to kill or injure, unlawful use of a communication device), up to 25 years. Judge Scott Suskauer sentenced him to eight years' probation, two years of electronic bracelet, psychiatric evaluation, no contact, no weapons, no drugs or alcohol, with no criminal record entry. Goldman Sachs fired him. A troubling detail: ChatGPT's replies appear nowhere in the court file — only the man's messages are documented. Other items: an "AGI Bar" in Beijing's Zhongguancun serves $1.50 beer with free unlimited DeepSeek coding tokens via two Nvidia DGX Spark machines; OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens on Aug 18, auto-restricting under-18 users; a public dispute on X between Dario Amodei and Yann LeCun, David Sacks and Gavin Baker over open-source and power concentration; Vivodyne's robotic cabinets growing human tissue; MIT's "attribution decay" study on diffusion models; and the Stanford AI Index finding 84% of Chinese vs 38% of Americans enthusiastic about AI.

## Key points

- OpenAI's moderation flagged Zhou's two-month threat thread, had humans review it, and escalated to the FBI — establishing a concrete precedent.
- Zhou pleaded guilty Aug 13, 2026; sentenced to 8 years' probation, 2 years electronic monitoring, psychiatric evaluation, no record entry.
- ChatGPT's own responses are absent from the court file, raising transparency questions.
- Beijing's "AGI Bar" gives free unlimited DeepSeek coding tokens over WiFi using two Nvidia DGX Spark units.
- OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens (Aug 18) with automatic age prediction and parental controls.
- Amodei vs. LeCun/Sacks/Baker debate: whether open weights truly decentralize power or shift concentration to compute owners.
- MIT's "attribution decay": larger diffusion models reproduce removed training data (e.g., the Mona Lisa) anyway.
