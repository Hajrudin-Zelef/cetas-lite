## Full summary

This Vision-IA newsletter (Jul 22, 2026) leads with the French Parliament adopting, on July 21, a law banning social media registration before age 15 and requiring at least one parent's consent between 15 and 18. France would become the first EU country to take this step, after Australia in 2024. The law also bans mobile phones in high schools and regulates social-platform advertising. ARCOM must define a "robust and reliable" age-verification system, with technical modalities not yet set; administrative and criminal penalties are planned for non-compliant platforms, and Emmanuel Macron aims for enforcement from the September school year. The text still faces the Constitutional Council, with a decision expected within a month. Australia's example counsels caution: two 2026 studies show many minors already bypass the ban (VPNs, fake birth dates). A grey area: AI-boosted chatbots and social apps could fall under the law if classified as social networks, a point not yet legally settled. Other headlines: Google DeepMind launched three new Gemini models on July 21 — Gemini 3.6 Flash (17% fewer tokens than the previous generation, better coding at 49% on DeepSWE vs 37%, $1.50/M input and $7.50/M output), Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (350 tokens/s, $0.30/$2.50), and Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber with the CodeMender agent (restricted to vetted governments and partners) — while confirming it has started "its most ambitious training run to date" for Gemini 4. Alibaba unveiled Qwen-Image-3.0, supporting prompts up to 4,500 tokens and legible text from 10 pixels high in 12 languages (no benchmarks or open weights). Microsoft and Mistral expanded their partnership to deploy Mistral's Medium 3.5 and OCR 4 on Microsoft Foundry, runnable on-premises or fully offline via Azure Local. Research: a brain implant restored autonomy to a paralyzed man (90% success in daily tasks, ~85% movement-intent accuracy), China plans an asteroid-deflection test at Mach 26, Xiaomi-Robotics-1 shows more data beats bigger models, an AI assistant (JudgeGPT) gave Pakistani courts up to $38.50 return per dollar, Fractal open-source agent tool, Qwen Audio 3.0 TTS tops the Speech Arena, and more. Also: half of Deezer uploads are AI-generated, US data centers to quadruple electricity use by 2035, and Nvidia wants to supply all AI data-center chips.
