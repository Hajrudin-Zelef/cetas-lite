## Full summary

This June 24, 2026 edition of the Vision-IA newsletter is led by John Jumper, co-creator of AlphaFold and Nobel laureate in chemistry, announcing on X his departure from Google DeepMind after nine years to join Anthropic. According to The Rundown, this is Google's second major departure in a single week, after Noam Shazeer, co-lead of Gemini, left for OpenAI. The newsletter reads this as a signal that Anthropic—long perceived as a safety- and text-focused lab—is recruiting a scientific research signature to push Claude beyond code and conversation into life sciences, DeepMind's historic hunting ground. For Google, losing two heavyweights in seven days raises concerns about talent retention.

The edition also covers the open-source community cloning the behavior of Fable 5 without its weights: developer Taha K. (alias lordx64) published Qwable-v1 on Hugging Face, distilled from Claude Fable-5 but using none of Anthropic's weights—trained on Fable-5's tool-use traces (how the model calls tools and reasons through code). It went viral among open-weight developers (thousands of downloads, 10,000+ for quantized versions alone), creating a legal gray area: weights are protected, but agentic traces can be imitated. Mistral launched OCR 4, its new-generation optical character recognition model, hitting 464 Hacker News points, reinforcing its strategy of specialized tools. SpaceX raised $25 billion in bonds (order book near $90 billion) less than two weeks after its IPO—illustrating the capital intensity of frontier tech infrastructure.

The "Research" section covers GPT-5 Pro helping researchers solve a three-year-old mystery about T-cell behavior (with implications for cancer and autoimmune diseases); OpenAI supporting global standards via the Appia Foundation; an MIT/USC ultrasound wristband that reads muscle/tendon movements to give a robotic hand piano-playing dexterity; Alibaba's Qwen-AgentWorld (language world models for versatile agents); Google's Fitbit Air health coach; and MIT's PlasmoSniff, a breath-based pneumonia diagnostic (~10 minutes, no lab needed).

Additional briefs include Omio reorganizing its dev cycle around OpenAI models (technical work reduced to ~20% of prior level, projects from quarters to weeks); Superhuman acquiring GPTZero; Google Home's Familiar Faces identifying people by clothing/silhouette; Cerebras falling 10% post-IPO; MIT Technology Review on SAOT (semi-automated offside technology) at the 2022 World Cup final; GE Vernova's off-grid AI data center proof of concept; Morgan Stanley doubling its China humanoid robot forecasts; Madison Square Garden allegedly profiling anti-facial-recognition activists (404 Media, 300 HN points); Stringman, a ceiling-suspended DIY tidying robot; and Y, a malleable Electron-based coding agent.
