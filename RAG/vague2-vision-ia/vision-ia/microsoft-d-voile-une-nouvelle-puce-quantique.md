## Full summary

This June 4, 2026 edition of the Vision-IA newsletter leads with Microsoft announcing Majorana 2, a quantum chip whose qubits are reportedly 1,000 times more reliable than the previous generation and hold their quantum state for 20 seconds (versus the industry's few microseconds). The real story, however, is that this advance was orchestrated by Microsoft Discovery, the group's agentic AI platform for R&D, which reached general availability. The agents automated qubit measurements (from several weeks to an automated process), drove fabrication workflows, synthesized decades of siloed data, and spotted correlations invisible to human researchers. Microsoft targets a commercially scalable quantum computer by 2029. The newsletter frames this as AI agents conducting entire portions of the scientific process—if generalized to materials, chemistry, or pharma, the tempo of scientific discovery could accelerate abruptly.

The edition reports Macron inviting Sam Altman to the G7, a diplomatic effort to tie US AI giants to French interests and boost national AI capacity, positioning France as Europe's AI gateway (with Mistral its sole champion). Meta launched paid AI agents for enterprises via Meta One, a B2B subscription—a cultural and economic pivot from free, ad-funded services to direct AI monetization, competing with Microsoft, Google, and OpenAI. Nous Research released Hermes Desktop, an MIT-licensed open-source AI agent app for any device/platform, a counter-model to locked-down commercial agents.

The "Research" section covers Hyper (YC S26), an "enterprise brain" that ingests scattered company data (Slack, Docs, Email, Calendar) into a structured knowledge graph for agents, giving persistent context between sessions where MCP restarts from zero, keeping facts current and capturing style preferences and past decisions; OpenAI strengthening GPT-Rosalind, its biology model, with stronger biological reasoning, medicinal chemistry, advanced genomic analysis, and optimized experimental workflows; Ideogram 4.0 going open-weight with native 2K resolution, bounding-box control, improved text rendering (#1 open model on DesignArena, behind only OpenAI and Google closed systems); Amazon's "audit-then-score" method for evaluating long-context AI summaries (since PhD experts exceed only 60.8% accuracy on complex factual tasks), challenging the traditional ground-truth model; and Terence Tao arguing AI makes scientific experiments bolder by lowering cost and risk, with transformative uses in research rather than office work (citing WindBorne Systems' AI weather forecasts beating government systems).

Additional briefs include Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 12B, an open-source multimodal model (text, images, audio) running on a 16 GB RAM laptop, rivaling the 26B Gemma under Apache 2.0; Nvidia and Unitree's H2 Plus humanoid (1.80 m, Chinese mechanics + Nvidia control/AI); Suno raising $400M, doubling its valuation to $5.4B amid lawsuits from Universal, Sony, and Warner; Martin Scorsese advising Black Forest Labs and using FLUX to storyboard his next film; Google's Dreambeans turning users' lives into cartoons; OpenAI and Anthropic uniting to urge tracking of synthetic DNA sequences to prevent AI-enabled bioweapons; Amazon generating AI product images in search; the EU unveiling its tech-sovereignty plan; Amazon engineers denouncing $200B AI investment amid 30,000 layoffs; Meta/Microsoft/SpaceX/Coinbase and the DOJ removing 1M+ fraud accounts; Google promising net-positive water by 2030; and Broadcom's disappointing Q2 2026 despite stable AI chip forecasts.
