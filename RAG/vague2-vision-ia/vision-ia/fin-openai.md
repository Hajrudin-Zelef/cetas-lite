## Full summary

This July 10, 2026 edition of the Vision-IA newsletter leads with OpenAI shutting down its own ChatGPT Atlas browser, less than a year after its October 2025 launch. Atlas will be decommissioned on August 9, 2026. OpenAI openly explains the move as a decision to stop "side quests" and refocus on productivity, in order to catch up with Anthropic, now seen as the reference for AI-assisted work. Atlas, ChatGPT, and Codex are being merged into a single desktop application called ChatGPT Work, described as "an agent capable of acting in your apps and files, staying on a project for hours, and turning an objective into finished work."

The edition also reports that Anthropic is ending its unlimited AI subscription and moving Claude Fable 5 to usage-based billing, triggered by OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol, which scores 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index—just one point behind Fable 5—while costing $1.04 per task, roughly three times cheaper, and leading in agentic coding. ByteDance launched Seedream 5.0 Pro, an image model aiming at OpenAI's level with better text rendering, layout, infographics, and layer-based precise editing. Meta entered the coding battle with Muse Spark 1.1, targeting enterprises with agentic workloads and large-scale code migrations, competing against Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and Windsurf.

The "Research" section covers teleoperated humanoid robots ("Surgie", 1.5 m, 27 kg) performing real surgery on live pigs at UC San Diego (published in Nature), including one procedure by a robot duo with no human surgeon at the table. Anthropic discovered the "J-space" inside Claude Opus 4.6 via a "Jacobian lens" tool, revealing words the model plans to say before formulating them. An OpenAI system beat all humans at the AtCoder 2026 world final. Microsoft open-sourced Aurora 1.5, a weather foundation model. Ollama raised $65M (9M users, 176k GitHub stars). Character.AI launched c.ai Series (AI micro-dramas, a $26B market). Four US microreactors reached criticality.

Additional briefs include Databricks adopting Chinese open-source GLM 5.2 (Opus 4.8-level coding at 34% lower cost: $1.28 vs $1.94 per task), FableCut (browser video editor driven by AI agents), Zhipu AI mulling a custom ASIC after GLM-5.2 usage grew 27x, a $500M token bill incident, the 1X Neo robot's fast new hands, Anthropic's Claude Reflect, GPT-5.6 as Microsoft 365 Copilot default, an NHS AI blood test for uterine cancer (99.1% sensitivity, £30), Fidji Simo leaving OpenAI, Gradium raising $100M with Nvidia, NYT accusing OpenAI of hiding evidence, Meta's own AI chips in September, Musk promising Anthropic infrastructure access, three AI IPOs outweighing 25 years of tech exits, SK Hynix listing on Nasdaq, and more.

## Key points

- OpenAI shuts down ChatGPT Atlas browser on August 9, 2026 (launched October 2025), folding it with ChatGPT and Codex into "ChatGPT Work".
- Anthropic ends unlimited subscriptions and moves Claude Fable 5 to usage-based pricing under pressure from OpenAI's cheaper GPT-5.6 Sol.
- GPT-5.6 Sol scores 59 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (1 point behind Fable 5) at $1.04/task, ~3x cheaper.
- ByteDance's Seedream 5.0 Pro targets OpenAI-level image generation with strong text/layout and layer-based editing.
- Meta launches Muse Spark 1.1 for enterprise agentic code generation and large migrations.
- Humanoid robots performed live-pig surgery at UC San Diego, one with no human surgeon present.
- Anthropic found the "J-space" in Claude Opus 4.6, showing the model plans words before saying them.
- Databricks adopts GLM 5.2 as default coding engine at 34% lower cost than Opus 4.8.
