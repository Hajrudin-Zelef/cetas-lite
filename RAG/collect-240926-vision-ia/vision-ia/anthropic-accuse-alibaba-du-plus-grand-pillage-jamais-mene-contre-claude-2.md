# 🧠 **RESEARCH**

**Google opens its Interactions API to everyone** **:** Google is officially switching to its **Interactions API**, a new gateway for Gemini and its agents, replacing the old generateContent API. Its advantage: a single call is enough to provision a **remote Linux sandbox** where an agent can reason, execute code, browse the web, and manage files securely. Enough to industrialize long-running agentic applications, like the "Antigravity" coding agent provided by default.

**Figma turns its canvas into an AI-driven workshop** **:** At its **Config 2026** conference, Figma unveiled a redesigned canvas for full-stack development: users can now **edit code without leaving the tool** and **generate animations and transitions** from a simple natural-language description. A piquant detail noted by The Decoder: the AI that animates this new canvas does not belong to Figma, but to a third party.

**General Motors replaces 1,000 workers with 50 robots** **:** The automaker is pushing automation up a notch by entrusting **50 robots** with tasks previously handled by **1,000 employees**. A ratio that illustrates, brutally, the concrete impact of robotics and AI on industrial employment.

**Facebook launches an AI companion app for creators** **:** Meta is testing with a small group a **dedicated app for creators**, equipped with its recently launched AI assistant. The goal: to provide them with tools to produce faster and better. For now, the rollout remains limited to a selection of creators.

**Qualcomm acquires Modular for nearly $4 billion** **:** Qualcomm is taking over **Modular**, one of the most prominent chip software startups of the AI era, for about **$4 billion**. A resounding exit for the young company, and a major boost for Qualcomm's AI infrastructure offering.

**L'Oréal feeds ChatGPT with its own product data** **:** The cosmetics giant has signed a **partnership with OpenAI**: from now on, when a user asks ChatGPT about its brands, the assistant draws on **L'Oréal's official data** rather than on a mix of reviews and Reddit posts. The agreement also integrates OpenAI's image model into CreAItec, the company's in-house AI platform. OpenAI assures that results will not be biased in favor of the brand.

**Agility Robotics targets a $2.5 billion IPO** **:** A humanoid robot pioneer born out of Oregon State University in 2015, **Agility Robotics** is preparing to go public via SPAC. The operation values the company at **$2.5 billion** and is expected to raise **$620 million** to finance its expansion.

**Europe stands up to Washington in the chip war** **:** The American **MATCH Act** seeks to restrict exports of lithography machines from the Dutch company **ASML**, including models around ten years old. Europe is pushing in the opposite direction, against a backdrop of growing geopolitical tensions over access to the semiconductors essential to AI.

**Tesla sued after a fatal accident in Texas:** The family of a victim who died in an accident involving a Tesla in Texas has filed a **wrongful death liability lawsuit**. The case brings back to the forefront the still unclear question of the **legal liability of autonomous driving systems** and their manufacturers.

**Faraday Future switches from cars to robots:** After barely selling **about fifteen electric cars**, Faraday Future is attempting a 180-degree turn and unveiling a range of **humanoid and quadruped robots** (including an optional dog-headed model). An opportunistic pivot riding the wave of enthusiasm for AI robotics.

**OpenAI: Codex explodes and the price of AI collapses:** In an interview, Arnaud Fournier, head of deployment at OpenAI, explains how his teams are embedding AI at the heart of major corporations. He discusses the **explosive growth of Codex**, the customer feedback loop that feeds the models, and the **sharp drop in the cost of artificial intelligence**.

**A US elected official admits to using Claude for a defense amendment:** Representative **Anna Paulina Luna** claims she only used Claude for a simple spelling correction on an amendment to the **National Defense Authorization Act 2027**. Problem: screenshots shared on X suggest the AI generated actual legislative text, reigniting the debate over using AI to write laws.

**Amazon bets on TRISO nuclear fuel for its datacenters:** To power its increasingly power-hungry AI infrastructure, Amazon is investing in **X-Energy Xe-100** reactors and their **TRISO** fuel. Each particle, smaller than a millimeter, acts as a mini four-layer containment system: no failure detected after **300 hours at 1,600 °C**, and a storage stability estimated at **100,000 years**.
