**Sonos opens its speakers to AI agents and ChatGPT**

Sonos 27 overhauls navigation, room grouping, volume, and connection problem diagnostics.

Sonos 27voice makes it possible to request music suited to a mood and control the system in natural language.

Users will be able to create their own automations with Sonos Custom Agents.

Support for the Model Context Protocol will also allow external services like ChatGPT to send commands to the speakers.

**Fambot wants to become the chief of staff for families**

Fambot gathers information from emails, calendars, and WhatsApp groups.

It generates a daily to-do list and provides advance notice of appointments, school activities, and family events.

The service works on the web, on mobile, and via messaging, with future connections to schools and clubs.

The startup claims that the models it uses cannot exploit its customers' data for their training.

**Gemini reduces the tokens needed to analyze a video by 88%**

Agentic video analysis allows Gemini to dynamically search for useful sequences instead of uniformly processing every moment.

Google announces up to **88% fewer tokens**, **66% lower costs**, and **7% additional accuracy**.

The feature can find a specific moment, detect an anomaly, or count events in a long video.

It is available for Gemini 3.7 Flash, 3.6 Flash, and 3.5 Flash-Lite via the API, Google AI Studio, and Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.

**Google is looking for Hollywood works to train its models**

Google reportedly approached several major studios to negotiate licenses covering their protected films and series.

The studios would receive new payments, while Google would gain access to high-quality audiovisual data.

This content could strengthen its video generation and understanding models against OpenAI and other competitors.

For Hollywood, the dilemma is how to monetize its catalogs without contributing to tools likely to upend its own production.

**Google DeepMind acknowledges being slightly behind**

Koray Kavukcuoglu says returning to the forefront of frontier models is Google DeepMind's top priority.

However, no capability, release date, or concrete performance metric has been announced.

**The Chinese AI stock market wave remains largely invisible**

AI- and robotics-related IPOs are multiplying in China, with international exposure still limited.

Unitree is reportedly preparing a **$900 million** operation, backed by DeepSeek and a state oil group.

This wave could finance the manufacturing of robots and physical infrastructure at large scale more quickly.

However, it remains difficult for some global investors to access.

**ChatGPT connects to electronic medical records**

Healthcare organizations can now link ChatGPT to their electronic medical records and reliable industry sources.

Professionals will be able to query a patient's clinical context and more easily consult relevant research.

OpenAI presents the access as secure, without detailing in the provided announcement the compatible systems or pricing terms.

The tool targets institutions and caregivers, not direct public access to automated diagnosis.

**DLSS 5 arrives on September 3, but with only one game**

Nvidia will launch DLSS 5 on GeForce RTX 50 cards and through GeForce Now.

At launch, **NBA 2K27 will be the only compatible game**.

The technology uses generative AI to reconstruct and enhance images in real time, at the cost of a strong dependence on the GPU.

Its rendering, particularly on faces and movements, is already generating a lot of reaction from players.

**Blue Origin will build the Mars telecommunications relay**

NASA has entrusted Blue Origin with the development of the Mars Telecommunications Network.

The orbiter must be ready no later than **December 31, 2028**, to transmit images, scientific data, navigation, and communications.

The contract could reach **$700 million**.

This infrastructure will serve as a relay for future robotic missions and, over the longer term, for possible human missions.
