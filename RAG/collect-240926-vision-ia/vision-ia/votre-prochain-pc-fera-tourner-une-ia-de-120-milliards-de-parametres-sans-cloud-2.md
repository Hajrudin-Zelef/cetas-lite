# 🧠 **RESEARCH**

Anthropic shows dizzying growth: **$47 billion** in annualized revenue in May, compared with $9 billion at the end of 2025, a **422% increase in six months**. As an imminent IPO approaches, CEO Daniela Amodei dismisses doubts about the profitability of consumer AI models. Enough to fuel the euphoria around the next stock market listing.

Poke, a startup that lets you use AI agents via SMS, becomes the first AI agent officially approved by Apple for its Messages for Business platform. A first that paves the way for integrating agents into mainstream communication tools. Text messaging becomes a new playground for conversational AI.

70% of Americans now oppose the construction of data centers near their homes, mainly because of their colossal water consumption, around **300,000 gallons per day**. Faced with the backlash, Microsoft assures that its new centers would consume as much water as a simple restaurant. The environmental question is establishing itself as a major political issue for AI infrastructure.

California startup Hello Robot unveils the fourth generation of Stretch, its robot designed to assist individuals in daily life. This launch illustrates the growing maturity of practical domestic robotics and the arrival of AI products designed for the general public. Is Silicon Valley ready to put robots in our living rooms? Hello Robot is clearly making that bet.

Chinese electric vehicle giant BYD confirms it is developing humanoid robots in-house through its "Yao-Shun-Yu" program, already in its seventh generation. The company believes automotive AI and robotics share the same fundamentals. Above all, it plans to distribute these robots through its global network of more than **150 dealerships**, while also becoming one of its own biggest buyers for its factories.

Amazon deploys a new version of its autonomous robot Proteus, capable of understanding natural language instructions. Employees can talk to it like a colleague, without specialized software or a dedicated interface. An advance that accelerates warehouse automation for the e-commerce giant, and reopens the question of the place of humans in logistics.

Meta introduces Creator Assistant, an AI tool that analyzes your past posts to suggest new content ideas. Designed as a brainstorming partner, it identifies the best-performing topics and formats to help creators optimize their output. One more assistant in the platforms' race to retain their creators.

TSMC, the world's leading foundry, acknowledges it cannot meet US demand for AI chips, despite expanding its capacity. Its CEO C.C. Wei warns that RAM and NAND Flash shortages will last several years. A bottleneck that could slow innovation across the entire industry, for lack of available semiconductors.

AI giants, with Anthropic leading the way, are preparing their stock market listings amid unprecedented fervor. The euphoria is such that some real estate listings would now accept Anthropic shares as payment. One sign among others of the enthusiasm bubble surrounding this wave of tech IPOs.

The Verge's Decoder podcast deciphers SpaceX's upcoming IPO, valued at around **$2 trillion**, and how Elon Musk bypasses corporate governance rules. Meanwhile, X (formerly Twitter) is declining across all its key metrics. A concentration of wealth and power happening with very few safeguards.
