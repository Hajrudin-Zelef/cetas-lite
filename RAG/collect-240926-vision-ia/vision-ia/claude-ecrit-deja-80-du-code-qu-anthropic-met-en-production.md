# 🧠 **RESEARCH**

**If LLMs have human attributes, then so does Age of Empires II**

This paper takes a humorous approach to our tendency to attribute human qualities to large language models. Through a reductio ad absurdum, the authors highlight the methodological flaws of anthropomorphism applied to AI. A salutary read to keep a clear-eyed view of what an LLM really "understands."

**DeepSeek V4 Pro beats GPT-5.5 Pro on accuracy**

According to a benchmark, **DeepSeek V4 Pro** edges out OpenAI's **GPT-5.5 Pro** on the accuracy metric. The comparison set the community ablaze, confirming that Chinese models are now joining the top of technical rankings, and not just on price.

**Efficient, training-free diffusion models for a single image**

This work proposes a "training-free" method that allows diffusion models to be applied to a single image, without prior training and with fewer computational resources. The targeted applications are very concrete: image editing, inpainting and super-resolution. A promising avenue for democratizing AI-driven image manipulation.


# **🗞️MORE NEWS**

**ChatGPT launches a Lockdown mode against prompt injections**

OpenAI is rolling out a **Lockdown Mode** in ChatGPT that disables web access, Deep Research and Agent Mode to limit data exfiltration. The feature only blocks the last step of an attack chain, without addressing the underlying problem. But it adds a welcome layer of protection against a security threat that remains, to this day, unresolved.

**Perplexity has AI write its own search pipelines**

With "Search as Code," **Perplexity** lets its models write their search routines directly in Python rather than calling rigid APIs. The result: up to **85% lower token costs** and performance superior to OpenAI and Anthropic on key benchmarks. Filtering and deduplication are automated in a secure sandbox, an approach that could inspire the entire industry.

**Anthropic hires an OpenAI chip engineer ahead of IPOs**
