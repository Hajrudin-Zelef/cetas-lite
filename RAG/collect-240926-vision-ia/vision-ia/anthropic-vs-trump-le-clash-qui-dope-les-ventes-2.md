# 🧠 **RESEARCH**

**__Qualcomm beefs up smart glasses with the Snapdragon Reality Elite__** Unveiled at the Augmented World Expo, the new **Snapdragon Reality Elite** chip promises **+60% GPU performance** for the next wave of XR devices. It will notably power the **Aura** glasses running Android XR. A sign that hardware for smart glasses is finally starting to catch up with the sector's ambitions.

**__Google DeepMind wants to speed up housing construction in the UK__** In an official announcement, **Google DeepMind** is partnering with the British government to build an AI prototype designed to speed up urban planning decisions. The goal: to unclog real estate planning procedures. A very concrete use case of AI applied to public administration.

**__Databricks: +80% in sales, but margins under pressure__** Driven by AI agents that boost data analysis, **Databricks** posts growth above **80%**, for an annualized revenue of **6.9 billion dollars**. The flip side: the surge in activity drives up costs and erodes margins. The classic dilemma of profitability in the age of agents.

**__Apple reportedly preparing AirPods with AI cameras for 2027__** According to Bloomberg, Apple is testing **AirPods equipped with cameras** in the stems, expected by late **2027** and already being trialed under iOS 28. The goal: to give **Siri** visual context about the user's environment. A second foldable iPhone is also reportedly in the works for the same period.

**__ChatGPT drops below 50% market share, a first__** With **1.1 billion monthly users**, ChatGPT remains the most used AI assistant in the world, but its market share falls below the **50%** mark for the first time. **Gemini** (662 million) and **Claude** (245 million) are gaining ground. OpenAI's dominance is no longer as overwhelming as before.

**__For a Berlin court, Google's AI Overviews are not original content__** The Berlin court ruled that Google's AI summaries are merely a "new search format," over which Google would not have "decisive influence." The complaint came from a perfumer whose brands appeared next to counterfeits. A decision that contrasts with Munich's, which was far stricter on Google's liability.

**__DeepSeek V4 Pro: Claude's performance for 5% of the price?__** This analysis dissects how **DeepSeek V4 Pro** manages to approach **Claude** while costing **95% less**. The author details the technical trade-offs behind this spectacular price gap. An economic argument that will weigh heavily in enterprise adoption of models.

**__Android 17 arrives with redesigned multitasking and Gemini everywhere__** **Google** is rolling out **Android 17** and **Wear OS 7**: new multitasking tools, strengthened parental controls, and native integration of the latest **Gemini** models via a Pixel Drop. The update extends the presence of generative AI all the way to the wrist. AI is settling a little more into the daily lives of Android users.

**__Microsoft moves Copilot Cowork to usage-based billing__** Deeming the flat-rate plan "not viable," **Microsoft** is switching **Copilot Cowork** to a usage-based billing model. The company is even considering integrating an optimized version of **DeepSeek V4** as a budget option. An admission about the very real cost of AI agents in production.

**__China pushes its vision of AI security, without the G7__** Beijing has included AI in its white paper on global governance and says it is accelerating its cooperation efforts, even as the **G7** summit concludes without it. A distinct geopolitical stance on AI regulation. The battle over global standards is well and truly underway.

**__Snap unveils its lightweight AR glasses at $2,195__** **Snap** will bring to market in late **2026** a redesigned and lighter version of its augmented reality glasses, the **AR Specs**, priced at **$2,195**. The company presents them as a true "laptop." The bet on mainstream augmented reality continues, despite a still very high price tag.

**__GPT-NL: the Netherlands equips itself with a sovereign language model__** The Dutch research institute **TNO** presents **GPT-NL**, a model designed to guarantee the country's digital independence from foreign giants. One more building block in Europe's AI sovereignty strategy, in direct echo of the French decision on Palantir. The continent clearly wants its own models.

**__Aging at home: AI now watches over seniors__** Devices like **Sensi AI** automatically monitor elderly people at home, reassuring loved ones and supporting understaffed care services. A concrete and useful application of AI in the service of care. With population aging, the "aging at home" market promises to be considerable.

**__Robinhood lays off 10% of its workforce, without blaming AI__** Unlike many tech leaders who justify their cuts by "AI transformation," **Robinhood** CEO Vlad Tenev did not mention AI in his note announcing **10%** layoffs. An honesty that implicitly highlights how often AI serves as a convenient pretext for workforce reductions.

**__AI, an amplifier of the Dunning-Kruger effect?__** This heavily commented post argues that AI could reinforce the **Dunning-Kruger** effect: the least competent users overestimate their mastery by relying on these tools. A useful reminder of the need for real AI literacy to avoid cognitive traps.
