# 🧠 **RESEARCH**

For the reader, no additional tool is available today: Daybreak remains reserved for approved organizations. OpenAI nevertheless claims that **GPT-5.6-Cyber reaches 95%** on an internal test of advanced cyber queries, compared with **1.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol**, a considerable gap that remains to be confirmed by independent evaluations.


After years of resistance, Meta agrees to concretely modify Facebook and Instagram for users under 18. The agreement approved on **August 26 in Oakland** settles complaints from 48 states, Washington D.C. and several territories, which accused the group of designing its services to make minors dependent and illegally collecting children's data.

These protections will be activated automatically for at least **five years**. A teenager will be able to deactivate them only with a parent's consent, and Meta will have to submit to an independent annual audit. The company, however, admits no wrongdoing.

### **A few key figures**

Use will be capped at **2 hours per day**, with a block between **midnight and 6 a.m.** and notifications cut off from **10 p.m. to 7 a.m.**, as well as during school hours.
The apps will offer breaks after 60 or 90 minutes, plus a reminder after **15 minutes of continuous use**. A chronological feed and hiding “likes” must also be offered.
The amount varies depending on the scope retained: **$12.1 billion guaranteed**, up to **$17.1 billion over ten years**, while Meta rounds the total to about 18 billion.
About **$5.3 billion** depends on the adoption of comparable protections and payments by TikTok and YouTube. These two companies have not committed at this stage.
The funds must notably finance mental health services, after-school programs and phone-free school initiatives.

### **The context**

This agreement establishes a foundation, not immunity. Complaints from individuals, school districts, Florida and New Mexico continue, while Texas negotiated separately. For families, the most visible change will be the arrival of limits that cannot be discreetly neutralized from the teenager's account.

# 🧠 **RESEARCH**

Before Christmas, Hugging Face will commercialize a robotic duck capable of walking, getting back up, squatting, roller-skating and carrying **800 grams** with its beak. Its camera, lidar and two inertial measurement units allow it to perceive its environment and its movements. The SDK, the simulator and the training pipeline are open, in order to create behaviors through reinforcement learning and then transfer them to the real robot.

Published in Nature Computational Science, PULSE transforms **61 ordinary blood markers** into **251 metabolomic biomarkers** normally obtained with specialized equipment. In trials, models trained on these synthetic values predicted diseases with performance comparable to those using the real, expensive analyses. The method could broaden access to precision medicine, subject to clinical validation on more diverse populations.

Generation Lab claims that its "1 Generation" injection, composed of two existing drugs, blocks the spread of aging in the blood and restarts tissue repair. The company relies on a few internal testimonials, notably that of researcher Irina Conboy, but reveals neither the molecules nor a published clinical trial. As it stands, the promise remains impossible to reproduce or verify and looks more like a marketing operation than a demonstrated treatment.

Louis Abraham grouped pull request descriptions into **eight lexical styles**, with clustering based on KL divergence. One of these styles went from **1% of the corpus in early 2025 to about 45% in mid-2026**, with words like "load-bearing," "quietly," "latent," or "seam" particularly characteristic. The analysis covers texts attributed to humans, so it detects a way of writing associated with assistants, not proof that Claude directly wrote each description. The code and methodology are public.

Terminal-Bench-Science brings together **70 tasks** designed by researchers based on their real work in life sciences, physical sciences, and engineering. Agents must produce verifiable analyses, simulations, proofs, programs, or datasets, not answer a school questionnaire. Claude Opus 5, the best model evaluated in this first version, succeeds in only **30%** of the tasks, which leaves a wide margin before the autonomous scientific assistant.

OpenAI presents a randomized study conducted on a real university assignment with **more than 1,000 students**. The protocol compares the use of ChatGPT, critical thinking training, and their combination, measuring performance, quality, and originality. The available summary does not provide the detailed effect sizes, which are essential to determine whether the improvement is significant or merely statistical.

Google DeepMind tests an evaluation of Gemini Flash Lite in the cryptographically isolated Confidential Space environment. Google cannot view the evaluators' confidential questions, while they can neither examine nor copy the model's weights. The goal is to reduce benchmark contamination and enable independent testing of proprietary models without revealing each party's sensitive data.

The AI Assisted Editor beta gathers prompt-based retouching, background removal, and image expansion tools into a single bar. The "markup" feature allows you to circle an area, draw an arrow, or sketch a shape to indicate precisely what should change. This optional interface mainly targets users who know how to show a modification but struggle to describe it with the vocabulary expected by a generator.

IBM reminds us that a bad automated decision can implicate executives, the IT team, the model designers, and the system manufacturer. Good average performance eliminates neither individual errors nor their consequences, particularly in health, finance, or human resources. The article therefore recommends adapting human oversight to the level of risk, while keeping an identifiable person responsible for each important decision.
