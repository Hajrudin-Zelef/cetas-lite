# 🧠 **RESEARCH**

**Apple Maps will power navigation in future Ford electric vehicles.** Thanks to the new **MapKit for Automotive** SDK, Apple Maps becomes the official navigation system for Ford's future electric platform. The automaker will also use Apple's road data for its BlueCruise hands-free system, Apple's first foray into semi-autonomous driving.

**AMD challenges Nvidia with its Helios AI rack.** AMD unveils **Helios**, a "rack-scale" system it claims beats Nvidia's Vera Rubin on several metrics. Microsoft, OpenAI, Meta, Oracle, and Anthropic are among the planned customers, with delivery in late 2026. AMD is targeting a **$1.4 trillion** AI accelerator market by 2030.
