# 🧠 **RESEARCH**

**Superhuman acquires GPTZero, the AI text detector****.**

Superhuman, an AI assistant for email and productivity, is acquiring GPTZero, a specialist in detecting AI-generated content. A surprising move for a company that sells an AI writing assistant, but telling of market consolidation and the growing stakes around authenticity.

**Cerebras drops 10% after its first post-IPO report****.**

AI chipmaker Cerebras, which listed on the Nasdaq in May, saw its stock plunge **10%** after disappointing quarterly results, including a forecast of margin contraction. The first real failed stock market test for this AI pure player closely watched by Wall Street.

**SAOT: the AI that decided the 2022 World Cup final****.**

MIT Technology Review revisits Semi-Automated Offside Technology, which detects offside to the millimeter using computer vision. Deployed for the first time during the final between Argentina and France, it helped the referee validate a decisive goal, illustrating AI's entry into top-level refereeing.

**Off-grid AI data centers: a key proof of concept for GE Vernova****.**

Powering AI data centers with standalone power plants crossed a major milestone this week, a topic picked up by CNBC and The Rundown AI. Good news for GE Vernova, well positioned in energy infrastructure, as the electricity consumption of models becomes a strategic bottleneck.

**Morgan Stanley doubles its humanoid robot forecasts for China****.**

Breaking this morning, Morgan Stanley sharply raises its forecasts for humanoid robot shipments in China, as commercial deployment accelerates faster than expected in real-world scenarios. A sign that the industrialization of humanoids is finally leaving the lab.

**Stringman, the suspended robot that tidies your home****.**

This DIY robot suspends itself from cables stretched across the four corners of a room and picks up toys, clothes, and clutter using a gripper. Unlike humanoids, it covers the entire room from the ceiling and reaches under furniture. The plans are free on GitHub, with ready-to-assemble kits for those who want to avoid the DIY work.
