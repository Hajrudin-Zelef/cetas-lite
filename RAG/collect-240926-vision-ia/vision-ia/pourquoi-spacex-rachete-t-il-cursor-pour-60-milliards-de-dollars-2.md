**Big Tech attempts a last move to impose a single federal AI regulation**

Tech giants are intensifying their lobbying for a **single federal law** that would preempt the patchwork of **state-by-state** regulations. The issue is tied to **KOSA** and online child safety concerns.

**Satya Nadella defends his "Token Capital" theory**

In a long message, the boss of **Microsoft** argues that every company must build two assets: **human capital** and **"Token Capital"**, meaning the AI capabilities it owns outright. A discourse that suits Microsoft well, as it lacks a homegrown frontier model.
