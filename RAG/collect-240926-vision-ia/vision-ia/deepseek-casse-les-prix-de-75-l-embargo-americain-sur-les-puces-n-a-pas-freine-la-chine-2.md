# 🧠 **RESEARCH**

**AI models give the right answers but cite the wrong sources** — Researchers at Peking University demonstrated that GPT and Gemini regularly cite passages that **do not actually support their answers**, even when the final answer is correct. This phenomenon of "attribution hallucination" poses a critical risk in law and medicine. The team created **CiteVQA**, the first benchmark to systematically test this problem.

**Eli Lilly's retatrutide, an anti-obesity drug deemed "too effective"** — Retatrutide simultaneously targets **three metabolic receptors** (compared to just one for Wegovy or Zepbound), causing weight loss **greater than 20%** in phase 2. The efficacy is such that researchers are considering reducing dosages as a precaution. A potentially revolutionary advance for the treatment of obesity, even if it does not directly relate to AI.


# **🗞️MORE NEWS**

**Hugging Face launches LeRobot, an open-source humanoid 3D-printable for ~$2,500** — Hugging Face is democratizing robotics with a fully 3D-printable bipedal robot, accompanied by a complete ecosystem: hardware designs, simulation tools, calibration, and sim-to-real learning pipelines. At **~$2,500**, it is the most accessible entry point ever offered for humanoid robotics research.

**Bosch, Nvidia, and Hyundai: three major announcements in humanoid robotics** — Bosch will produce the HMND 01 robots for the European market, Nvidia and Kawasaki are creating a robotics center in San Jose, and Hyundai plans to deploy **more than 25,000 Boston Dynamics Atlas robots** in its American factories. The industrialization of humanoid robotics is accelerating on a large scale.
