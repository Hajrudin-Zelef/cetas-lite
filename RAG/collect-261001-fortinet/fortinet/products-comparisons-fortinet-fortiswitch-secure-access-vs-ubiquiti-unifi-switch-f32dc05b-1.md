# products-comparisons-fortinet-fortiswitch-secure-access-vs-ubiquiti-unifi-switch-f32dc05b

Fortinet FortiSwitch and Ubiquiti UniFi Switches compete in the network switch category. Fortinet FortiSwitch appears to have the upper hand in terms of security features and integration capabilities, while Ubiquiti UniFi Switches stand out with their cost-effectiveness and user-friendly management interface.
Features: Fortinet FortiSwitch excels in network management, tight integration with FortiGate, and offers potent security features, including device isolation and extensive analytics through its unified platform. Ubiquiti UniFi Switches are notable for their centralized management, intuitive graphic interface, and capability to incorporate the latest technologies swiftly, providing a versatile and cost-efficient solution.
Room for Improvement: Fortinet FortiSwitch can improve its standalone flexibility, enhance scalability, and better integrate firmware updates. Ubiquiti UniFi Switches need simpler VPN management, improved network visibility, and more advanced security options. Additionally, the dual management interface can complicate settings, and support may lack robustness without a paid plan.
Ease of Deployment and Customer Service: Both Fortinet FortiSwitch and Ubiquiti UniFi Switches offer extensive deployment options. Fortinet favors on-premise environments and has reliable, though sometimes slow, customer service. Ubiquiti supports hybrid and cloud deployment but is highly dependent on community support, limiting immediate live assistance.
Pricing and ROI: Fortinet FortiSwitch is a mid-to-high cost option but is more affordable than brands like Cisco, delivering clear ROI through operational savings and dependable security, with licensing costs potentially deterring small setups. Ubiquiti UniFi Switches offer a cost-effective one-time payment model without recurring fees, which appeals to budget-conscious users, especially small to medium-sized businesses, despite some concerns about supply and warranty constraints.
