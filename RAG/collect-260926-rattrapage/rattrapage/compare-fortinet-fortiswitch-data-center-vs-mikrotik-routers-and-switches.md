# compare-fortinet-fortiswitch-data-center-vs-mikrotik-routers-and-switches

MikroTik Routers and Switches and Fortinet FortiSwitch - Data Center compete primarily in the networking equipment category. Fortinet FortiSwitch appears to have the upper hand due to its seamless integration and extensive security features.

 **Features:** MikroTik Routers and Switches are noted for their versatility and affordability, offering extensive routing features, a robust firewall, and traffic management without special licenses. Users mention the solution's high performance relative to cost and its flexible configuration capabilities. Fortinet FortiSwitch - Data Center provides comprehensive network visibility, security integration, centralized management, port analytics, and advanced security features through FortiGate.

 **Room for Improvement:** MikroTik requires enhancements in wireless solutions and UI/UX simplification, along with better integration with third-party systems and wireless controller development. Fortinet FortiSwitch needs improvements in UI, specific feature integrations, simplified switch management, and enhanced scalability and troubleshooting.

 **Ease of Deployment and Customer Service:** MikroTik offers flexible on-premises deployment but lacks direct technical support, with users often relying on community forums. Fortinet FortiSwitch boasts robust on-premises and hybrid cloud deployment options, with more consistent direct technical support, leading to a generally positive customer support experience.

 **Pricing and ROI:** MikroTik is highly cost-effective, appealing to budget-conscious customers by eliminating ongoing licensing fees, leading to significant ROI. Fortinet FortiSwitch, though more expensive, offers value through FortiGate integration, making it a preferred choice for users prioritizing advanced features and security integration.
