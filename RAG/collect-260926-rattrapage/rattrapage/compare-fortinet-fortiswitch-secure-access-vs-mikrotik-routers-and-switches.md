# compare-fortinet-fortiswitch-secure-access-vs-mikrotik-routers-and-switches

MikroTik Routers and Switches and Fortinet FortiSwitch - Secure Access compete within the networking hardware category. MikroTik tends to be favorable due to its affordability, while Fortinet shines in security management features despite higher costs.

**Features:** MikroTik offers competitive pricing, ease of configuration, and a wide range of features including routing capabilities, traffic bonding, and a flexible IP firewall. Fortinet FortiSwitch emphasizes integration with FortiGate, offering enhanced security management, comprehensive monitoring, and a user-friendly interface for centralized control.

**Room for Improvement:** MikroTik could improve by enhancing their wireless solutions, simplifying user interfaces, and providing better documentation and support options. Fortinet FortiSwitch could become more attractive by making its pricing more competitive and improving interoperability features and management ease outside FortiGate.

**Ease of Deployment and Customer Service:** MikroTik relies heavily on community forums for deployment assistance, suggesting a need for better official support. Fortinet generally offers more reliable and responsive technical support, but users face challenges in reaching support and navigating systems, indicating a need for streamlined support channels.

**Pricing and ROI:** MikroTik is highly regarded for affordability and eliminating recurring fees, maximizing ROI with robust performance. Fortinet, being more expensive with recurring fees, justifies its cost with advanced functionalities, though it could improve competitiveness by revising its upfront pricing strategy while emphasizing its feature-rich platform.
