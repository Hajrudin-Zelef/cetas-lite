# Kimi K3's Rise Sparks a US-China AI Distillation Fight

- Benchmarks topped: Program Bench, Suite Marathon, Automation Bench, Browse Comp, Spreadsheet Bench 2 (vs GPT 5.6 Sora, Claude Opus).
- Timeline: Claude Opus public ~June 1; Kimi K3 shipped ~2 weeks later — implausible for distillation + training + shipping.
- Distillation definition: student model trained on teacher model's prompt/output pairs.
- Hardware dimension: US export-restricted chips allegedly used.
- Precedent: Elon Musk testified xAI distilled OpenAI models for Grok.
- Enforcement difficulty: open weights downloadable/storable; multi-terabyte model files; no retroactive removal.
