# What Is a Knowledge Graph for AI Agents? How Relationship Mapping Beats Wiki Links

- RAG retrieves "similar documents"; knowledge graphs retrieve precise facts along relationship paths.
- LLMs are weak at tracking precise structured facts over long contexts — graphs supply that.
- Multi-agent shared-memory use case: agents write/query the graph instead of passing context windows.
- Part of the same memory-architecture lineage as semantic-memory, persistent-memory, and frozen-snapshot guides.
