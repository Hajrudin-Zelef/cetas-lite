# ps6-et-xbox-helix-les-puces-seraient-finalisaes-40-contre-56-tflops-annoncas-frandroid

<!-- source: https://www.frandroid.com/produits-android/console/3258217_ps6-et-xbox-helix-les-puces-seraient-finalisees-40-contre-56-tflops-annonces -->

The leak comes from KeplerL2, a leaker followed for their information on AMD graphics chips, already behind several revelations about PC and console hardware. They shared these estimates in a discussion on the NeoGAF forum, spotted by the specialized site VideoCardz. Asked about the progress, they claimed that both chips had passed the *tape-out* stage.

*Tape-out* refers to the moment when a chip's design is finalized, then sent to the foundry to etch the first physical silicon. It is a concrete and hardly reversible stage: at this point, major feature changes are no longer expected, unless testing reveals a defect to be corrected. However, it guarantees neither the final features nor a release date.

They claim that the AMD chip chosen by Sony would reach 40 TFLOPS of raw power, versus 56 TFLOPS for the next Xbox.

To gauge the announced leap, the PS5 caps out at 10.28 TFLOPS and the PS5 Pro, launched at the end of 2024, at 16.7 TFLOPS. The 40 TFLOPS put forward would therefore represent roughly four times the raw power of the base PS5.

## The next Xbox ahead of the PS6, on paper

The next Xbox carries the codename "Helix." If its 56 TFLOPS hold up, Microsoft would beat Sony by about 40% in theoretical raw power. The gap would be consistent with the current generation, where the Xbox Series X (12 TFLOPS) already dominates the PS5 (10.28 TFLOPS), on paper.

Because TFLOPS don't tell the whole story. They measure raw floating-point computing power, without reflecting actual in-game performance. That also depends on the GPU architecture, memory bandwidth, the ray tracing engine and software optimizations. In other words, the 40% gap noted here might not show up once in-game.

Neither Sony, nor Microsoft, nor AMD has confirmed this information. The most solid element of everything circulating: both consoles would rely on a mix of AMD architectures, Zen 6 for the processor and RDNA 5 for the graphics portion. At the same time, Sony is also preparing a handheld PlayStation, codename Project Canis, which would target power equivalent to the PS5 thanks to AI.
