Sony is a Japanese multinational, a historic giant in many fields, including electronics, computing, photography, video games, as well as music and cinema. The company was created in 1946 and was at the origin of major innovations, such as the color video cassette, the Walkman, or even the CD and Blu-Ray.

On the phone side, Sony was previously in partnership with Ericsson, thus calling itself Sony Ericsson, before buying back the shares in 2011.

### What are the strengths and particularities of Sony Xperia smartphones?

Very clearly, the Xperia models position themselves in the high end, and highlight a more advanced photo experience. Sony is keen to allow more advanced, finer settings in the photo app, thus addressing more to pros and image enthusiasts. In addition, it is Sony sensors that are integrated. The manufacturing quality is also there, as well as a very sober and serious look.

Unfortunately, Sony smartphones suffer from too high a price positioning, which makes them less attractive to the general public.

### **How to find your way around the names of the Xperia range?**

The range is organized around three main series:

- **Xperia 10:** The mid-range focused on battery life and value for money.

- **Xperia 1:** The ultra-high-end flagship (pro photo, premium screen).

- **Xperia 5:** High-end performance in a more compact format.
