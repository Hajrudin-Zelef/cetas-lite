# intel-core-ultra-7-rtx-5070-et-acran-oled-ce-monstre-de-puissance-coate-200-a-de-moins-chez-boulange

<!-- source: https://www.frandroid.com/bons-plans/3257391_intel-core-ultra-7-rtx-5070-et-ecran-oled-ce-monstre-de-puissance-coute-200-e-de-moins-chez-boulanger -->

*GTA VI* is the unmissable video game release of late 2026. Yet it still doesn't have a release date on PC, but Rockstar's latest title will eventually make its appearance on computer. Its arrival can be prepared for right now since Boulanger is cutting 200 euros off the price of this gaming laptop that packs the Nvidia RTX 5070 and the Intel Core Ultra 7 255HX.

## The highlights of the Acer Predator Helios Neo 16S AI:

- A 16-inch OLED screen at 240 Hz
- The Intel Core Ultra 7 255HX and the RTX 5070
- A 1 TB SSD

Usually, the Acer Predator Helios Neo 16S AI is offered at a price of 2,299.99 euros, but on Boulanger's website, it benefits from a discount of almost 10% to drop to 2,099.99 euros.

### A configuration designed for AAA titles

To run the most demanding games, Acer equips its Predator with the Intel Core Ultra 7 255HX and 16 GB of RAM. To accompany them, the brand has set its sights on the Nvidia RTX 5070. DLSS 4 is therefore part of the package to improve graphics. An element to take into account at the time of purchase: the machine can tend to heat up when pushed to full power.

If gamers will be delighted by this laptop, creatives are not left out. AI features are present to optimize rendering speed or create images and videos from a prompt. This spec sheet also allows you to work on 3D software like Blender or do more advanced video editing on After Effects. Everything can be stored in the 1 TB SSD.

Unfortunately, as is often the case on such machines, battery life is not up to par. This Acer Predator Helios Neo 16S AI can last up to six hours in office use, but in gaming, the total drops to two or three hours depending on the titles. Note that the charger is included with the laptop.

### Perfect for streaming

Acer has given particular care to its screen with a 16-inch OLED panel at 240 Hz with a WQXGA resolution of 2,560 x 1,600 pixels. For colors, it's a full house since this laptop covers 100% of the DCI-P3 space.

The only small downside concerns brightness since it is still limited to 400 nits, which is not ideal outdoors. However, with this limited battery life and a weight of over two kilograms, its mobility is automatically quite reduced.

On the connectivity side, this laptop has three USB-A ports and two USB-C ports. A combo jack and an HDMI port complete this offering.
