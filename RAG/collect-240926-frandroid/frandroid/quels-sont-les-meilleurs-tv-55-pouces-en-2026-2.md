## Which 55-inch television should you choose in 2026? Our advice

### Why choose a 55-inch TV?

55 inches (140 centimeters) remained the size par excellence for a long time. It is a format particularly suited to 4K UHD definition. Nevertheless, we are seeing more and more 65-inch models, whose prices have dropped. But more prosaically, 55 inches is a size that can be integrated without too much trouble into most interiors.

To choose the size of your television, a trick that generally works very well is the "*4.5 centimeters per inch rule*." This indicates the minimum distance between you and the TV. **For example, on a 55-inch (4K) TV,** **you need to be about 2.47 meters away from the screen**.

If you are closer than 2.47 meters, you risk quickly tiring your eyes and losing your capacity for immersion. At 2.47 meters, it is the perfect immersion distance for all the action in movies and video games. Finally, this is only an approximation and, ideally, each user should determine at what distance they feel most comfortable.

### What are the dimensions of a 55-inch television?

The screen of a 55-inch TV measures 139.7 cm diagonally, and 121 cm long by 68 cm wide. This is therefore without counting the bezels as well as the height added by the stand.

### What is the necessary distance for a 55-inch 4K TV?

This is also one of the data points that conditions the purchase of a television. To find the ideal distance between your device and the couch, a small mathematical calculation is necessary. Simply multiply the size of your diagonal by 1.6. For a 55-inch TV, or 140 centimeters, this gives 224. Ideally, your couch should therefore be placed 2.24 meters from your interface for optimal conditions. Of course, this is intended for all those who want to fully enjoy the image.

### How did we make our selection?

Most 55-inch TVs available on the market support **4K UHD** definition, **HDR**, and have **modern OSes**. So, unless you are looking for an old or very, very inexpensive model, you should find at least one of these characteristics (and generally all three).

Beyond labels and standards, our choice fell on TVs that do not have major problems: brightness issues, poor viewing angles, very average finishes... Whether you go for the Xiaomi TV A Pro at 449 euros or the Sony Bravia 8 II at 2,499 euros, you will not be disappointed. For the highest prices, we also paid attention to some future-proof options, such as HDMI 2.1 and eArc support, which will satisfy the most demanding among you.

### Which is better, OLED or QLED?

There are many technologies with their qualities and their flaws. High-end TVs generally use OLED technology, which allows for exceptional contrast levels, but you also have very good TVs with QLED technology. This Samsung LED technology offers significant brightness levels that will be leveraged by HDR.

### Should you add a soundbar to your television?

Your TV's speakers are physically limited by the space they occupy in the TV chassis. Adding a soundbar, a subwoofer, or even satellite speakers to your TV will certainly improve sound quality. Soundbars are today the simplest and most economical means for this.
