Sony refines its singular formula with its Xperia 1 VIII, which represents its showcase. Facing competition fond of AI, the Japanese brand relies on hardware authenticity to seduce purists of image and sound.

The device adopts an unprecedented textured glass back and abandons the 21:9 format for a well-calibrated Full HD+ screen. The major evolution concerns photography: the former continuous zoom gives way to a very successful fixed 70 mm telephoto lens. The three sensors offer a natural rendering close to a real camera. The audio experience remains the best on the market thanks to the headphone jack and symmetrical speakers. However, these advantages come with heavy compromises for such a high sale price (1,499 euros). Indeed, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 undergoes severe thermal throttling under intense load, the screen's HDR brightness disappoints and the 30 W charging requires 1h25 of waiting for a full recharge. The interface remains too sober, even austere, and the 4-year software support is a bit short for this price range.

## A compact but powerful format

- Well-calibrated 120 Hz screen
- Its photographic quality
- High-level performance

- Heats up quickly

As is customary at Sony, the manufacturer renews its Xperia lineup without changing any organization in the hierarchy. The Sony Xperia 5 V constitutes the manufacturer's high-end without being the flagship of the brand.

In its construction, the device remains fairly classic with its 21:9 format, but which ensures a good grip in use. Also note the presence of an IP65 certification to protect it from splashes. Still in the protection department, Corning Gorilla Glass covers the screen. The 6.1-inch OLED panel offers adaptive refresh between 60 and 120 Hz with good-quality calibrations.

In terms of performance, these are ensured by a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 processor with 8 GB of RAM. The high-end smartphone is rarely put in difficulty. With the 5000 mAh battery and the chip's performance, battery life is good.

The photo part is composed of two rear sensors, both with a resolution of 12 megapixels. The photo quality is good without being dazzling. For a starting price of 999 euros, it's the high end without being the best Sony smartphone.

The Xperia 10 VI is the successor to the V. Sony continues to develop its mid-range, although the company is here in competition with giants such as Xiaomi, Oppo or even Google.

Sony delivers a sober phone and makes the choice of a compact format, with a good 6.1-inch screen in 21:9, unfortunately still limited to 60 Hz. The grip is thus particularly pleasant.

The SoC, a Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 5G, is not a lightning bolt but still does well in everyday use. On the photo side, you will find two sensors: a main 48-megapixel one and an 8-megapixel ultra-wide-angle one. The front lens provides good results. On night photos, however, don't expect miracles.

We will praise a very good battery life with the altogether classic 5000 mAh battery. You can thus last about a day and a half.

## The UFO from Sony

- A very masterful design
- Its professional camera
- Good performance

- Not very bright screen

From time to time, Sony allows itself some creative follies on its products. The Xperia Pro-I is a somewhat marginal smartphone on the market since it is above all designed for photography. The smartphone remains conservative with the same format and a standard design.

At the heart of the spec sheet, the phone benefits from a Snapdragon 888 processor which handles the animation. The presence of 12 GB of RAM in addition will allow you to run any application, but not only. This internal power will allow the product to best process photography.

Indeed, the smartphone is marginal in its way of offering an almost professional camera. The main sensor, in this case, is a Sony Exmor RS with a 1-inch format. The 12-megapixel resolution still offers more precision on the shots. It is completed by an ultra-wide-angle as well as a telephoto lens. It is above all the integrated Photo Pro application that allows a little more freedom in shooting.
