## Everything you need to know about Nothing smartphones: Our advice and explanations

### Who makes the Nothing Phone?

Nothing is an English brand founded in 2021 and headquartered in London. The creation of this company is the work of Carl Pei, former co-leader of OnePlus. The brand launched a very first product that year, earbuds, the Nothing Ear (1), before offering their first smartphone, the Nothing Phone (1) in 2022. A second range followed, the Nothing Phone (2), then the CMF, an entry-level variant. Then came the Phone (3a) and (3a) Pro in 2025, as well as the Phone (4a) in 2026, true mid-range champions.

Furthermore, Nothing offers, in addition to smartphones, other products such as earbuds, headphones, and watches. We can mention the excellent Nothing ear, and on the watch side, the Nothing CMF Watch Pro 2.

### What are the strengths of Nothing smartphones?

The **design** of Nothing phones is a real differentiator. In a world where all smartphones look alike, Nothing brings a breath of fresh air with a distinctive aesthetic. The back is transparent, revealing notably the screws as well as interactive LED strips, called Glyph. These, when they light up, provide various information to the user, particularly related to notifications, calls, and other reminders.

The other strength of the brand's smartphones is its system, called **Nothing OS**. The in-house OS also adopts a strong stance, both aesthetically and in terms of how it works, with a very strong focus on widgets. Clear and simple to use, easily customizable and very streamlined, and now enhanced with AI functions geared toward organization, it will suit a lot of people.
