In the meantime, GeForce owners have solutions. The simplest is called Bazzite, a community distribution based on Fedora that takes up the Steam Deck-style game mode and directly includes the Nvidia drivers. It covers RTX cards and recent GTX 16 series, with one caveat: Nvidia support in game mode remains in beta, with acknowledged bugs, and the team then recommends a desktop image instead. Tinkerers may prefer Nobara, more open but more demanding, while Pop!_OS remains a safe bet for running a slightly older green GPU.

If you want to understand how these distributions are installed, our guide to installing Linux on a PC covers the step-by-step method. Last option, the wisest if your card is recent: wait a few months.
