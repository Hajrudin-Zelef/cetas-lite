# test-de-l-iphone-18-pro-le-meilleur-iphone-est-aussi-le-plus-prudent-frandroid

Our wattmeter confirms it unambiguously: the USB voltage stayed at 14.5 V from start to finish, the classic 15 V PD profile, whereas the

fast phase with the official charger runs around 18 V. The iPhone 18 Pro never negotiated AVS with our 120 W brick, it settled for the previous 40 W.

In other words, if you plug the 18 Pro back into your 17 Pro's charger, or into almost any GaN charger bought in the last three years, you keep last year's charging. Apple's 15 minutes exist, but they're sold separately for 45 euros.

## Price: 150 euros more

1,479 euros for 256 GB, 1,729 for 512, 2,229 for 1 TB, 2,979 for 2 TB. That's 150 euros more than last year at every tier, an increase that the price of memory partly explains, and which is concentrated on the large capacities, the jump from 512 GB to 1 TB now costs 500 euros.

Apple lifted the price of all its older iPhones the same evening, so the gap between an 18 Pro and a 17 remains the same as last year. It's the 2026 market, and Samsung does the same.
