# a-pas-besoin-de-lui-demander-de-raflachir-a-les-astuces-pour-bien-utiliser-claude-opus-5-5

<!-- source: https://www.frandroid.com/culture-tech/intelligence-artificielle/anthropic-claude-ai/3257879_pas-besoin-de-lui-demander-de-reflechir-voici-les-astuces-pour-bien-utiliser-claude-opus-5-5 -->

If you use Claude every day, you have surely accumulated little reflex phrases in your prompts or even in the general instructions from your account settings to calibrate responses exactly as you want them. With Claude Opus 5.5, launched this Tuesday, September 22, Anthropic explains in a guide that some of these habits are no longer useful. The model now thinks before each response, and it decides on its own how much time to devote to it.

Anthropic presents Opus 5.5 as the first of a new family, and says it is 40% cheaper and 30% faster than Opus 5. Beyond the numbers, it is above all its way of responding that changes: it works longer on its own, it explains more clearly what it has done, and it puts important information at the top of the response. Released on the same day as OpenAI's GPT-6 Sol, it does not force you to relearn Claude. But if you are coming from Opus 5, three or four reflexes are worth revisiting.

On the access side, no need to wait: Opus 5.5 is already available for Claude Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise subscribers, and not only via the API, since regular users of the app have access to it too. Anthropic is also taking the opportunity to raise usage limits on these paid plans.

## Give the whole task, then let it run

The first piece of advice in the guide, written by Addy Osmani, comes down to one idea: entrust the complete mission in a single message, clearly defining what the final result should correspond to. He gives two examples: "*the tests must pass*" or "*all endpoints must be migrated*".

This is where Opus 5.5 stands out from Opus 5: it holds up much better over long, multi-step tasks. According to the guide, testers let it chain coding tasks for hours, with very little supervision. With a clear finish line, it knows on its own when the work is done.

## Stop asking it to "think"

Let's now move on to the most useful change to remember in this Anthropic guide when coming from Opus 5 to Opus 5.5. All the instructions like "*think carefully*" or "*proceed step by step*" that you may have slipped into your saved instructions no longer have any reason to exist if the document is to be believed. Opus 5.5 thinks before each response and adjusts its effort on its own, so asking it to do so again only slows down the start. Anthropic says it clearly: "*No need to ask it to think*".

In Anthropic's tests on a chatbot, removing a "*think carefully*" instruction made responses start earlier, without any visible drop in quality. If you want a quick answer to a simple question, say so plainly: "*answer directly*". In Claude Code, you instead play with the effort level, that slider that appeared with the Opus 4.5 generation.

## Long sessions, images and verifications

For big projects in Claude Code, Anthropic's command-line tool, the guide recommends writing in the CLAUDE.md file when the model should continue on its own and when it should stop to ask you. Still keep confirmation requests before any risky action: deleting data, forcing a code push, or touching anything other than your project.

On the Claude app side, two habits change the game. Attach the graph, diagram or screenshot directly rather than retyping the numbers, because Opus 5.5 reads these images more precisely than Opus 5. For a long plan or report, ask it to track down inconsistencies: dates, numbers and names that contradict each other. Anthropic also shares the essentials of this advice on its X account.

One last point to know: Opus 5.5 is the first Opus launched with reinforced safeguards for biology and cybersecurity. If a message is flagged, Claude most often switches to an older model and your work continues there. To switch back to Opus 5.5, simply reselect it in the menu, or type the /model command in Claude Code.

If you only take one thing away from moving from Opus 5 to Opus 5.5, it is to clean up your old instructions: the "think carefully" prompts are no longer useful, and a clear finish line is worth more than a long list of instructions. For back-and-forth exchanges where you read every response, a fast mode is available from launch in preview, at the cost of a surcharge per token.
