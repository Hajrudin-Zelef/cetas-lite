## Learn more about the Asus Zenbook 14 (UX3480, Snapdragon)

The Asus Zenbook 14 (UX3480, Snapdragon) is an ultraportable laptop that is part of the brand's Copilot+ PC range. Available from €899.99, this device focused on mobility stands out for its energy-efficient Qualcomm Snapdragon processor and its chassis made of Ceraluminum.
### Design

The Asus Zenbook 14 (UX3480) features a slim metal structure combining a Ceraluminum lid, a high-tech ceramic material offering increased resistance to scratches, impacts, wear and fingerprints. It measures 31.11 x 21.58 cm with a thickness ranging from 1.39 to 1.69 cm, and weighs 1.1 kg. The "Easy Lift" hinge allows the lid to be easily opened with one finger while keeping the screen stable. The computer comes in three nature-inspired colors: Zabriskie Beige, Komodo Coral and Arctic Blue. The backlit chiclet keyboard is paired with a dedicated Copilot key and an enlarged ErgoSense touchpad supporting smart gestures. For biometric security, an FHD infrared camera compatible with Windows Hello facial recognition is housed in the top bezel of the screen, complementing the Microsoft Pluton security processor. The device also meets the requirements of the US military standard MIL-STD 810H.
### Display

The ultraportable has a 14-inch non-touch OLED panel in 16:10 format, displaying a screen-to-body ratio of 89%. This screen offers a response time of 0.2 ms and a refresh rate of 60 Hz. Brightness reaches 300 cd/m² and the panel covers 100% of the DCI-P3 color gamut. It includes TÜV Rheinland certifications aimed at reducing blue light emissions and eliminating flickering.
### Performance and storage

The machine is equipped with a Snapdragon X X1-26-100 chip with 8 cores (8 threads) clocking up to 2.97 GHz, supported by the Qualcomm Adreno graphics unit. The processor integrates a Qualcomm Hexagon NPU providing up to 45 TOPS for local processing of artificial intelligence tasks. Its cooling system with an enlarged fan supports a TDP reaching 28 W. On the memory side, the laptop includes 16 GB of LPDDR5X RAM (depending on configurations, 8 GB variants exist). Internal storage relies on a 512 GB M.2 NVMe PCIe 4.0 SSD (or 256 GB in PCIe 3.0), complemented by an M.2 2280 PCIe 4.0 x4 expansion slot. The device runs on the Windows 11 Home operating system. The audio section consists of two dual-magnet speakers compatible with Dolby Atmos and microphone arrays with AI noise reduction.
### Battery and battery life

The Asus Zenbook 14 includes a 3-cell Li-ion battery with a capacity of 50 Wh. The announced battery life reaches up to more than 30 hours of video playback and up to 18 hours of web browsing. Charging is done via the USB-C ports with support for fast charging from 45 W to 68 W, allowing 50% charge in 30 minutes. The compact 68 W USB-C power adapter is not always included depending on the region.
### Connectivity

The chassis brings together the following physical connectors:
- 2 USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C ports (charging and display);
- 1 USB 3.2 Gen 1 Type-A port;
- 1 HDMI 2.1 TMDS port;
- 1 3.5 mm combo audio jack.
