> **Status:** consolidated delta volume · **Cutoff:** 2026-09-22 · **Sections:** 23 · **Source files:** 5 (2,660 lines) · **Language:** English
>
> This volume is a **delta** against `ai-industry-knowledge-base-2026.md` (the main KB). It contains only facts that are new or corrected relative to the main KB's coverage, consolidated and deduplicated from five wave-6 source files and verified on 2026-09-22. It does **not** merge into or modify the main KB. One-line cross-references ("covered in main KB §X") replace re-explained background.
>
> **Provenance labels** (carried on every factual claim): `[VENDOR]` — vendor's own materials; `[SECONDARY]` — press/analyst coverage; `[COMMUNITY]` — community/hacker sources; `[UNVERIFIED]` — single-source or uncorroborated; `[DIRECTIONAL]` — inference or trend reading; `[CONTRADICTED]` — claim checked and found false.
>
> **Hard rules applied throughout:** (1) every URL is copied verbatim from the source files, never invented or repaired; (2) Artificial Analysis scores are never compared across methodology versions (v4.1.1 → v4.2 → v4.3 rebased within a single week in early September 2026); (3) vendor-reported metrics are never restated as independent results; (4) model, checkpoint, harness, eval effort, benchmark snapshot, and benchmark variant are never conflated; (5) SWE-bench Pro's three number classes (Scale public, Scale commercial, vendor-aggregate) are never mixed; (6) rumor → preview → GA chronologies are preserved, not flattened.
