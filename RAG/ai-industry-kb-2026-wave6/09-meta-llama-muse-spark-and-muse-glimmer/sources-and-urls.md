### Source-quality notes (methodology for this expansion)
- Llama 3.x figures rest on tertiary/secondary summaries (S1–S4); layer counts and token counts for older releases are vendor-remembered rather than re-verified against the original model cards.
- S1 is a Wikipedia mirror reached through a proxy URL; its content is the Llama (language model) Wikipedia article and is treated as tertiary.
- Llama 4 launch-day specs are corroborated across S5, S6, S7, and S8; the Scout INT4-on-H100 claim and the LMArena checkpoint identity are single-sourced.
- Behemoth delay reporting is single-sourced to S9 for the capability-concern cause; timeline slippage is corroborated by S10 and S11.
- All Muse benchmark figures are vendor-reported via secondary coverage; none were independently reproduced in the sources found.
- The Spark 1.3 benchmark card (S22) is the most detailed vendor disclosure in this section and the only one pinning reasoning modes (`max` vs `xhigh`) to scores.
- S16 (Concord News Now) is the sole source documenting the contributor-vs-standard data-use distinction; the pricing tiers themselves are corroborated by S13.
- S30 and S31 corroborate the Vibes standalone test start date and initial markets; the freemium-pricing status is single-sourced to S30.
