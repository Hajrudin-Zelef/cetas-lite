# §21. Licenses and Open-Weight Politics (part 5)

- Kimi K3 Modified MIT: Moonshot committed to publishing weights by July 27, 2026 under a Modified MIT license; the modification adds one attribution clause triggering above 100M monthly active users [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://orphentisai.com/kimi-k3-vs-deepseek-v4-pro-vs-glm-5-2-open-trillion-scale-moe-models-compared-on-benchmarks-license-and-serving-cost/ and https://cirra.ai/articles/en/pdfs/kimi-k3-release-open-weight-models.pdf
- K3 license-term discrepancy to ledger: one comparison states the trigger as "above 100M monthly users or $20M monthly revenue" while two others state only the 100M MAU figure — whether the revenue prong exists is unresolved; read the LICENSE file per repo [SECONDARY — contradiction]. Sources: https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/deepseek-v4-1-flash-vs-kimi-k3 versus https://orphentisai.com/kimi-k3-vs-deepseek-v4-pro-vs-glm-5-2-open-trillion-scale-moe-models-compared-on-benchmarks-license-and-serving-cost/
- Until the K3 weights published, K3 was API-and-app-only — the "open weights" label described a commitment with a date (July 27), not a downloadable file [SECONDARY]. Source: https://orphentisai.com/kimi-k3-vs-deepseek-v4-pro-vs-glm-5-2-open-trillion-scale-moe-models-compared-on-benchmarks-license-and-serving-cost/
- DeepSeek V4 Pro and V4 Flash are MIT-licensed with weights on Hugging Face from day one — unrestricted commercial use, fine-tuning, and self-hosting [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://orphentisai.com/kimi-k3-vs-deepseek-v4-pro-vs-glm-5-2-open-trillion-scale-moe-models-compared-on-benchmarks-license-and-serving-cost/ and https://kimi-k2.org/blog/56-kimi-k3-vs-deepseek-v4-flash-0731 (284B/13B, MIT, text)
- "Apache 2.0 for Qwen, MIT for DeepSeek" is the standing summary for the two Chinese open-weight flagships' 2026 generations [SECONDARY]. Source: https://toknow.ai/posts/qwen36-deepseek-v4-china-open-weight-frontier-models/index.pdf
- GLM-5.3-Flash: MIT license, weights at huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-5.3-Flash, announced August 26, 2026 [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://www.explainx.ai/blog/glm-5-3-flash-ox-alpha-official-launch-august-2026?ref=hackernoon.com, https://chinafactor.news/2026/08/28/zhipu-glm-5-3-flash-domestic-chips-ox-mystery/, https://the-decoder.com/the-chinese-ai-model-glm-5-3-flash-runs-without-nvidia-and-costs-a-fraction-of-what-the-competition-does/
- GLM-5.3 (non-Flash) was NOT open weights at launch: launched August 14, 2026 with open weights and API access staged behind safety review; "a promised checkpoint is not a downloadable one, and the license terms were not published" at release [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://emergent.sh/learn/what-is-glm-5-3 and https://www.explainx.ai/blog/glm-5-3-flash-ox-alpha-official-launch-august-2026?ref=hackernoon.com
- Z.ai's rationale for staging GLM-5.3: "a downloadable model this capable at finding and reasoning about vulnerabilities is a capability you cannot un-ship" — safety review before release rather than after [SECONDARY]. Source: https://emergent.sh/learn/what-is-glm-5-3
- Gemma 4's license transition: google/gemma-4-31B-it and google/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it are Apache 2.0 — "Google's transition to Apache 2.0 for Gemma 4 eliminates custom usage policies" [COMMUNITY]. Source: https://github.com/ufal/atrium-project/issues/9
- Qwen license confirmations from the same audit: Qwen3.6-27B Apache 2.0 ("permits unrestricted fine-tuning, integration, and commercial monetization"), Qwen3.5-9B Apache 2.0, Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507 Apache 2.0 [COMMUNITY]. Source: https://github.com/ufal/atrium-project/issues/9
- The audit's caveat applies corpus-wide: "downstream platform runtimes may differ" — the weights license and the serving platform's terms are separate documents (noted on the Mistral-Nemo row) [COMMUNITY]. Source: https://github.com/ufal/atrium-project/issues/9
- FunASR Model Open Source License v1.1 (FunAudioLLM/SenseVoiceSmall): commercial scope ambiguous — "provided for reference and learning purposes only" may limit the grant or may be warranty disclaimer only; flagged pending legal review, do not treat as MIT-equivalent [COMMUNITY]. Source: https://github.com/mikeschirtzinger/silent-notetaker/blob/HEAD/docs/research/model-licenses.md
- Qwen3.5-Omni Light's exact license remains UNVERIFIED — no LICENSE-file confirmation; one source reads the whole Omni line as API-only [UNVERIFIED]. (Carried from wave3; unchanged.)
- ECCN 4E091 (BIS interim final rule): new ECCN covering "parameters" of AI models trained with 10^26 or more computational operations, unpublished, controlled for Regional Stability (RS) and Anti-Terrorism (AT) reasons for exports, reexports, and in-country transfers worldwide [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://sanctionsnews.bakermckenzie.com/bis-issues-interim-final-rule-and-call-for-comments-on-artificial-intelligence-and-advanced-computing-integrated-circuits/ and https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2025-00636.pdf?1736775933
- ECCN 4E091 explicitly EXCLUDES open-weight models, and also excludes closed-model weights less powerful than the most advanced open-weight models (as determined by the AI Safety Institute and DOE) — the control threshold floats with the open frontier [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://sanctionsnews.bakermckenzie.com/bis-issues-interim-final-rule-and-call-for-comments-on-artificial-intelligence-and-advanced-computing-integrated-circuits/ and https://www.nextgov.com/emerging-tech/2025/01/commerce-announces-new-export-control-us-ai-products/402131/?oref=ng-home-top-story
- License Exception AIA (Artificial Intelligence Authorization) permits export/reexport/transfer of otherwise-controlled closed weights by companies headquartered in the US and listed allies/partners, except to arms-embargoed destinations [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://admin.govexec.com/media/general/2025/1/ai_embargoed_press_release.pdf and https://sanctionsnews.bakermckenzie.com/bis-issues-interim-final-rule-and-call-for-comments-on-artificial-intelligence-and-advanced-computing-integrated-circuits/
- New foreign direct product rule at EAR §734.9(l): applies 4E091 controls to model weights produced abroad using US technology/equipment — a Chinese company needs a BIS license even for in-China transfers of covered weights trained on controlled chips [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://theaicounsel.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/01_25_bis.pdf and https://www.caixinglobal.com/2025-01-15/detailed-explanation-of-new-us-export-controls-on-ai-chips-overseas-ai-model-training-restricted-102279207.html
- License applications for 4E091 exports to Country Group D:5 and Macau face a presumption of denial [SECONDARY]. Source: https://sanctionsnews.bakermckenzie.com/bis-issues-interim-final-rule-and-call-for-comments-on-artificial-intelligence-and-advanced-computing-integrated-circuits/
- BIS reserves the right to impose controls later on currently-excluded less-powerful closed weights — the exclusion is policy, not statute [SECONDARY]. Source: https://sanctionsnews.bakermckenzie.com/bis-issues-interim-final-rule-and-call-for-comments-on-artificial-intelligence-and-advanced-computing-integrated-circuits/
- July 24, 2026 double event: Claude Opus 5 went live AND the DeepSeek V4 alias retirement took effect — model IDs changed on both sides of the Pacific the same day [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://medium.com/@automation.labs/opus-5-sonnet-5-haiku-4-5-which-claude-model-for-which-job-bce5e8346233 and wave6/03-model-weights-wave3.md (alias retirement chronology)
- July 27, 2026: Moonshot's committed date for publishing Kimi K3 weights — the "open" in K3's launch story was a dated commitment [SECONDARY]. Source: https://orphentisai.com/kimi-k3-vs-deepseek-v4-pro-vs-glm-5-2-open-trillion-scale-moe-models-compared-on-benchmarks-license-and-serving-cost/
