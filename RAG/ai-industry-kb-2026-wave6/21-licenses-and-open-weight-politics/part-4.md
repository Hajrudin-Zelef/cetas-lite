# §21. Licenses and Open-Weight Politics (part 4)

- Apache License 2.0 §2 (copyright grant), canonical text: "each Contributor hereby grants to You a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to reproduce, prepare Derivative Works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute the Work and such Derivative Works in Source or Object form" — the grant underlying every Apache-2.0 model release in this corpus [SECONDARY — canonical license text, unchanged since January 2004]. Source: https://registry.ollama.com/library/qwen3:0.6b-q8_0/blobs/d18a5cc71b84 (full Apache 2.0 text as shipped with Qwen3 weights, "Copyright 2024 Alibaba Cloud")
- Apache 2.0 §3 (patent grant) includes the retaliation clause: "If You institute patent litigation against any entity ... alleging that the Work or a Contribution incorporated within the Work constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement, then any patent licenses granted to You under this License for that Work shall terminate" — the clause that makes Apache 2.0 a patent peace treaty, not just a copyright grant [SECONDARY]. Source: https://registry.ollama.com/library/qwen3:0.6b-q8_0/blobs/d18a5cc71b84
- Apache 2.0 §§7–8 (warranty disclaimer and liability cap), as shipped with Qwen3: "Unless required by applicable law or agreed to in writing, Licensor provides the Work ... on an 'AS IS' BASIS, WITHOUT WARRANTIES OR CONDITIONS OF ANY KIND" and "In no event and under no legal theory ... shall any Contributor be liable to You for damages" — model redistributors inherit these disclaimers verbatim [SECONDARY]. Source: https://registry.ollama.com/library/qwen3:0.6b-q8_0/blobs/d18a5cc71b84
- The full Apache 2.0 text travels with weights even in registry-quantized form: the Ollama blob for qwen3:0.6b-q8_0 embeds the complete license with the Alibaba Cloud copyright notice — quantization or reformatting does not strip the license [SECONDARY]. Source: https://registry.ollama.com/library/qwen3:0.6b-q8_0/blobs/d18a5cc71b84
- MIT license, canonical grant (the text behind DeepSeek V4, GLM-5.3-Flash, LongCat-2.0, Phi-4): "Permission is hereby granted, free of charge, to any person obtaining a copy ... to deal in the Software without restriction, including without limitation the rights to use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and/or sell copies of the Software" — no user caps, no field-of-use limits, no attribution beyond the copyright notice [SECONDARY — canonical SPDX text]. License attributions: https://www.explainx.ai/blog/glm-5-3-flash-ox-alpha-official-launch-august-2026?ref=hackernoon.com (GLM-5.3-Flash MIT), https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/educatordeveloperblog/phi-4-small-language-models-that-pack-a-punch/4464167 (Phi-4 MIT)
- MiniMax H3 Community License Agreement (effective August 2, 2026) — the most restrictive "open weights" license documented in this wave: "Applicable Territory" is defined as worldwide EXCLUDING the European Union, the United Kingdom, the Republic of Korea, and the United States of America; "You may not use, reproduce, modify, distribute, or display the MiniMax H3 Works or any of their Outputs or results outside the Applicable Territory" [SECONDARY — quoting community transcriptions of the license text]. Sources: https://github.com/onigirikiller/minimax-h3-webui, https://huggingface.co/OpenVDN/vdn-minimax-h3/blob/main/README.md, https://github.com/utensils/mold/blob/HEAD/docs/qualification/minimax-h3.md
- The H3 license's commercial terms: commercial use permitted at no separate fee for organizations under $20 million in yearly revenue WITH prominent "MiniMax H3" attribution in the UI; above $20M, separate written authorization from MiniMax is required before commercial use [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://www.spheron.network/blog/deploy-minimax-h3-gpu-cloud/ and https://runaihome.com/blog/minimax-h3-open-weights-local-ai-hardware-guide-2026/
- The H3 license's redistribution terms: distributions must include the agreement, modified files must carry modification notices, distributions to third parties other than through hosted services must include the NOTICE file, and the distribution notice must read "MiniMax H3 is licensed under the MiniMax H3 Community License Agreement, Copyright © 2026 MiniMax. All Rights Reserved" [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://github.com/makhmudovmurod/autodirector/blob/HEAD/Docs/license-notes.md and https://huggingface.co/OpenVDN/vdn-minimax-h3/blob/main/README.md
- The H3 license's use restrictions: outputs may not be used to train or improve other AI models; AI-generated content must be clearly disclosed as AI-generated; Exhibit A lists 20 restricted uses (military, discrimination, disinformation, malware, unauthorized professional services, etc.) [SECONDARY]. Source: https://github.com/makhmudovmurod/autodirector/blob/HEAD/Docs/license-notes.md
- The H3 territory exclusion traces to active litigation: Disney, Universal Pictures, and Warner Bros. Discovery sued MiniMax alleging H3 was trained on copyrighted material; a US court rejected MiniMax's motion to dismiss in May 2026; MiniMax's Head of Developer Relations Ryan Lee confirmed "U.S. restrictions stem from a generative video copyright lawsuit involving major Hollywood studios" [SECONDARY]. Source: https://www.spheron.network/blog/deploy-minimax-h3-gpu-cloud/
- MiniMax's compliance path for excluded regions: individual authorization for applicants who commit to a content-compliance mechanism meeting US legal requirements, via MiniMax's own licensing channel — an application process, not a default right; the hosted Hailuo API remains available globally and is a different legal relationship [SECONDARY]. Sources: https://www.spheron.network/blog/deploy-minimax-h3-gpu-cloud/ and https://runaihome.com/blog/minimax-h3-open-weights-local-ai-hardware-guide-2026/
- MiniMax Music 3 license (effective August 6, 2026) contains NO territorial definition and names no excluded countries — Western users can deploy it; coming eleven days after the H3 restriction, this reads as a deliberate correction, not an oversight [SECONDARY]. Source: https://digitalmatters.me/artificial-intelligence-ai/minimax-music-3/
- MiniMax Music 3 commercial terms: attribution plus authorization above $20M revenue — the revenue trigger persists even where the territory trigger was dropped [SECONDARY]. Source: https://digitalmatters.me/artificial-intelligence-ai/minimax-music-3/
- The same analysis contrasts MiniMax Music 3 with Meta's Muse Glimmer, released three days earlier under Apache 2.0 with no revenue trigger and no attribution requirement: "Both are described as open weights in coverage. Only one is open source" [SECONDARY]. Source: https://digitalmatters.me/artificial-intelligence-ai/minimax-music-3/
- A hardware guide's summary of the H3 license: "a license restriction, not a technical block. The weights download the same everywhere, and nobody's GPU checks a passport. It's a civil-contract line" — enforceability against hobbyists is untested, but the guide declines to advise ignoring it [SECONDARY]. Source: https://runaihome.com/blog/minimax-h3-open-weights-local-ai-hardware-guide-2026/
- NVIDIA Open Model License §3.1 NOTICE requirement, exact text: "Licensed by NVIDIA Corporation under the NVIDIA Open Model License" — first-party re-hosts of NVIDIA models (e.g. nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b) need this NOTICE file [COMMUNITY]. Source: https://github.com/mikeschirtzinger/silent-notetaker/blob/HEAD/docs/research/model-licenses.md
