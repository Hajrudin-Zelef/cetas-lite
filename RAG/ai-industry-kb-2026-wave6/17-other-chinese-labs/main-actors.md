## Implications
1. **"Preview" is a deployment status, not a license** — Hy4 Preview, Step 5 Preview, and openPangu 2.0 are all gated or staged; none should be cited as fully open weights without the qualifier.
2. **Unresolved conflicts stay unresolved** — ERNIE's pricing, Ling's license, openPangu's license are presented with their tags, not averaged or assumed; downstream users must re-verify.
3. **The fine-tune economy is a strategy** — 01.AI (exit to fine-tuning) and Perplexity (fine-tune-only 2026) show labs choosing the application layer over base-model training.
4. **Falcon's 2026 line rehabilitates the "dead lab" narrative** — H1 Arabic through Perception-300M is a real multimodal release sequence from TII.
5. **Cheapest-open-API is now a tracked slot** — Laguna S 2.1 at $0.09/$0.18 holds the published-rate floor; DeepSeek V4.1 Flash off-peak holds the frontier-class floor.
6. **License-before-deploy is the section's throughline** — Hy3/Hy4 split, Ling unverified, openPangu unverified: read the per-release terms, not the lab's reputation.
