# Lambda — up to $3B pre-IPO talks at $12B+, 2026-08-25 [SECONDARY]

### Lambda — up to $3B pre-IPO talks at $12B+, 2026-08-25 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-08-25** — Bloomberg reported Lambda (Nvidia-backed neocloud) is in talks to raise **up to $3B at $12B+ valuation**, with multiple term sheets already received; terms not finalized. The round is positioned as a **pre-IPO financing**, IPO as soon as 2027. [SECONDARY]
- Expected 2026 revenue: **>$1.5B**. Backers: Nvidia, TWG Global, US Innovative Technology Fund, Andrej Karpathy, ARK Invest. Lambda has a **multibillion-dollar agreement with Microsoft** to deploy AI infrastructure on tens of thousands of Nvidia GPUs. [SECONDARY]
- Prior round: **>$1.5B Series E announced November 18, 2025** from TWG Global and USIT at **$5.9B post-money** — closed above the $4–5B whisper range Bloomberg had reported. [SECONDARY]
- January 2026: The Information reported a **$350M round** in talks with Mubadala Capital as lead — a smaller bridge-style raise two months after the $1.5B Series E. [SECONDARY]
- Hardware line: GB300 NVL72 and HGX B300 appliances (Blackwell Ultra); **Vera Rubin NVL144 systems** in build-out (Rubin chips debuted at CES January 2026: 50 petaflops NVFP4, 5× Blackwell); Superclusters rentable up to 165,000 Nvidia chips; 12 US data centers with liquid cooling. [SECONDARY]
- **2026-08-28** — Lambda secured **$1B in debt** to buy Nvidia AI chips, planning to lease them to major clients including Microsoft — the debt finances GPU acquisition directly, with repayment from lease revenue; announced alongside the $3B equity talks, giving Lambda a two-track (debt + equity) pre-IPO capital structure. [SECONDARY]

### Nscale — $2B Series C at $14.6B, 2026-03-09 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-03-09** — UK AI hyperscaler Nscale announced a **$2B Series C** at **$14.6B post-money**, claimed as the **largest venture capital raise in European history**, led by industrial heavyweight **Aker ASA and 8090 Industries**. Participants: Astra Capital Management, Citadel, Dell, Jane Street, Lenovo, Linden Advisors, Nokia, **Nvidia**, Point72. Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and J.P. Morgan acted as joint placement agents; the round includes the earlier $433M Pre-Series C SAFE. [SECONDARY]
- Board additions: **Sheryl Sandberg** (ex-Meta COO), **Susan Decker** (ex-Yahoo president), **Nick Clegg** (former UK Deputy PM, ex-Meta) — a governance coup for a company founded in 2024. [SECONDARY]
- Flagship project: **Stargate Norway** — a $1B hydropower-fed data center targeting **100,000 NVIDIA GPUs by late 2026**, with **OpenAI as anchor tenant**; clients also include Microsoft. [SECONDARY]
- IPO trajectory: hired Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan as underwriters (Reuters); Bloomberg (August 2026) reported Nscale reporting **$51B in contracted revenue** ahead of a potential September IPO — note one outlet caveats the annualized revenue run-rate is only ~$400–500M, the rest being multi-year contracted revenue. [SECONDARY]
- **2026-09-18/19** — Nscale **filed its S-1** in New York: aims to raise **up to $3B at ~$30B valuation**, ticker **NSCL** on NYSE; bookrunners Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Morgan Stanley. Footprint at filing: **17 contracted data center sites (5 active)**, **1.37 GW active + contracted**, planned expansion to **10 GW** across US, UK, Portugal, Iceland (Norway hydro origins). Spun out of Arkon Energy (bitcoin-mining) by CEO Josh Payne in 2024 — the second crypto-to-neocloud conversion after Crusoe/CoreWeave. [SECONDARY]
- Analyst split at filing: bull case = AI market opportunity; bear case = **customer concentration and high debt** — the standard neocloud risk pair. [SECONDARY]
- Capital stack context: $155M (Dec 2024) → $1.1B Series B (Sept 2025, largest European Series B) → $433M SAFE → $2B Series C — over $4.5B raised in equity in ~six months. [SECONDARY]

### xAI Colossus — the physical buildout behind the capital [SECONDARY]
- **2026-05-03** — xAI brought **Colossus 2** online: **550,000 Nvidia GB200/GB300** accelerators (two GPUs per node, >1M total accelerators), the world's largest single-site training deployment at **>1 GW**. Colossus 1: 230,000 GPUs (150k H100, 50k H200, 30k GB200). Combined: >1M H100-equivalents. [SECONDARY]
- Scale targets: **1.5 GW within months, 2 GW long-term** (≈1.5M households' peak load); third site in Southaven, Mississippi ("MACROHARDRR"); roadmap to **1M GPUs by late 2026** for Grok 5 (rumored 6T parameters). Musk claims **300,000 GPUs installed per month**, outpacing every hyperscaler. [SECONDARY]
- **Environmental-legal overhang**: April 2026 — NAACP Mississippi + SELC/Earthjustice filed a **Clean Air Act lawsuit** over **27–59 unpermitted methane turbines** at Colossus 2 (Southaven) — the first major federal test of AI compute vs 1970-vintage air permitting. DOJ moved June 15, 2026 to intervene and dismiss. [SECONDARY]
- **Merchant-compute gate**: Google's **September 30, 2026 delivery gate** — if xAI fails to deliver ~110,000 committed GPUs by 09-30, Google may walk away or accept pro-rata-reduced fees after a one-month grace period. First hard test of whether xAI's build speed meets its merchant commitments. [SECONDARY]
- Grok 5 training at Colossus 2/3 slipped past Musk's Q1/Q2 2026 targets; now expected H2 2026. [SECONDARY]

### Google→Anthropic — up to $40B + 5GW TPU, 2026-04-24 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-04-24** — Google committed **up to $40B to Anthropic** in a package combining cash and **5 GW of TPU compute through 2027** (one secondary breakdown: $10B cash + $30B milestone payments + compute). The deal locked Anthropic workload onto Google Cloud against TPUs — the chips Google builds to compete with Nvidia. [SECONDARY]
- Simultaneously, Google–Broadcom–Anthropic signed a **multi-gigawatt agreement for next-generation TPU capacity expected online from 2027** ("Ironwood" generation). [SECONDARY]
- Five gigawatts is roughly Google's entire Ironwood capacity coming online through 2030, per one secondary read — allocating it to a single tenant tightens TPU supply for all other Google Cloud customers. [SECONDARY]

### Amazon→Anthropic — up to $25B + $100B AWS spend commitment, April 2026 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-04-20** — CNBC reported Amazon agreed to invest **up to $25B in Anthropic**; in return Anthropic committed to spend **>$100B on AWS technologies over the next 10 years** and secured **up to 5 GW of Trainium capacity** for training and deploying Claude. Amazon disclosed Anthropic's Trainium commitment on **2026-04-29**. [SECONDARY]
- Anthropic's disclosed compute posture (April 2026): **10 GW total — 5 GW Google TPU + 5 GW AWS Trainium** — the only frontier lab with multi-gigawatt commitments on two hyperscaler stacks. [SECONDARY]

### OpenAI–AWS expansion — +$100B over 8 years [SECONDARY]
- March 2026: OpenAI and AWS **expanded their existing $38B multi-year agreement by $100B over 8 years**; OpenAI committed to consume ~**2 GW of Trainium capacity** through AWS infrastructure. AWS also became the **exclusive third-party cloud distribution provider for OpenAI Frontier**, enabling agent teams on AWS. [SECONDARY]
- One secondary CI compilation states AWS has locked OpenAI and Anthropic into **>$200B in combined AWS infrastructure commitments** — the single largest model-choice anchor in the cloud market. [SECONDARY]
