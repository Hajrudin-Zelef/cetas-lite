# Nscale — $2B Series C at $14.6B, 2026-03-09 [SECONDARY]

### Nscale — $2B Series C at $14.6B, 2026-03-09 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-03-09** — UK AI hyperscaler Nscale announced a **$2B Series C** at **$14.6B post-money**, claimed as the **largest venture capital raise in European history**, led by industrial heavyweight **Aker ASA and 8090 Industries**. Participants: Astra Capital Management, Citadel, Dell, Jane Street, Lenovo, Linden Advisors, Nokia, **Nvidia**, Point72. Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and J.P. Morgan acted as joint placement agents; the round includes the earlier $433M Pre-Series C SAFE. [SECONDARY]
- Board additions: **Sheryl Sandberg** (ex-Meta COO), **Susan Decker** (ex-Yahoo president), **Nick Clegg** (former UK Deputy PM, ex-Meta) — a governance coup for a company founded in 2024. [SECONDARY]
- Flagship project: **Stargate Norway** — a $1B hydropower-fed data center targeting **100,000 NVIDIA GPUs by late 2026**, with **OpenAI as anchor tenant**; clients also include Microsoft. [SECONDARY]
- IPO trajectory: hired Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan as underwriters (Reuters); Bloomberg (August 2026) reported Nscale reporting **$51B in contracted revenue** ahead of a potential September IPO — note one outlet caveats the annualized revenue run-rate is only ~$400–500M, the rest being multi-year contracted revenue. [SECONDARY]
- **2026-09-18/19** — Nscale **filed its S-1** in New York: aims to raise **up to $3B at ~$30B valuation**, ticker **NSCL** on NYSE; bookrunners Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Morgan Stanley. Footprint at filing: **17 contracted data center sites (5 active)**, **1.37 GW active + contracted**, planned expansion to **10 GW** across US, UK, Portugal, Iceland (Norway hydro origins). Spun out of Arkon Energy (bitcoin-mining) by CEO Josh Payne in 2024 — the second crypto-to-neocloud conversion after Crusoe/CoreWeave. [SECONDARY]
- Analyst split at filing: bull case = AI market opportunity; bear case = **customer concentration and high debt** — the standard neocloud risk pair. [SECONDARY]
- Capital stack context: $155M (Dec 2024) → $1.1B Series B (Sept 2025, largest European Series B) → $433M SAFE → $2B Series C — over $4.5B raised in equity in ~six months. [SECONDARY]

### Together AI — $800M Series C at $8.3B, 2026-07-01 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-07-01** — Together AI closed an **$800M Series C** at **$8.3B post-money**, **led by Aramco Ventures**, with Vista Equity Partners, General Catalyst, Emergence Capital, **Nvidia**, Salesforce Ventures, March Capital, Pegatron, S Ventures (SentinelOne), Schneider Electric, Lux Capital, Geodesic, and PSP Partners. Separately, commitments for **>500 MW of compute capacity** financed by new investors. [SECONDARY]
- August 2026: **Humain partnership in Saudi Arabia** — 250 MW of power, 120,000 chips; the company expects it to generate **$5B in annual business** once fully deployed. [SECONDARY]
- Strategy signal: the company told investors it plans to expand compute capacity roughly **50× over five years** (Reuters). [SECONDARY]
- Valuation arc: $1.25B (March 2024) → $3.3B (Feb 2025, $305M Series B led by General Catalyst/co-led Prosperity7) → $8.3B — a 2.5× step-up in 17 months. Total funding ≈$1.33B. [SECONDARY]

### Fireworks AI — $1.505B Series D at $17.5B, 2026-07-15 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-07-15** — Fireworks AI closed a **$1.505B Series D** at **$17.5B post-money**, led by **Atreides Management, Index Ventures, and TCV**, with Evantic, Lightspeed Venture Partners, **Nvidia**, Bessemer Venture Partners, Insight Partners, Lone Pine Capital, Menlo Ventures, Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan, and others. [SECONDARY]
- Unit economics signal: **>$1B annualized revenue** in July 2026 → ~17.5× revenue multiple; the company told investors it targets **60% gross margins** via GPU optimization and utilization efficiency. [SECONDARY]
- The Series D replaced May 27, 2026 Bloomberg talks of a round at **$15B**; it is a 4.4× step-up from the **$250M Series C (October 2025) at $4B** (led by Lightspeed, Index, Evantic; $230M primary + $20M secondary; Sequoia participating). Preceded by $52M Series B (July 2024, $552M valuation, led by Sequoia) and a $25M Series A. Total funding >$1.8B. [SECONDARY]
- The July 1–15 sequencing (Together's $800M close, then Fireworks' $1.505B) made the inference layer the hottest 2026 VC tranche; TensorWave's $350M Series B at $1.55B and Upscale AI's $500M at $2B confirm the category repricing. [SECONDARY]

### Baseten — $1.5B Series F, 2026-06-22, split-priced at $13B/$11B [SECONDARY]
- **2026-06-22** — Baseten announced a **$1.5B Series F** completed **across two tranches at $13B and $11B valuations** (split pricing). Led by **Altimeter Capital, Conviction, and Spark Capital**; co-leads Sands Capital and Wellington Management; plus IVP, 01A, Greylock, Durable Capital Partners, Blackbird, Verified Capital, Battery Ventures, D.E. Shaw Ventures, and existing investors. Fourth fundraise in 18 months; total funding >$2B. [SECONDARY]
- Operating scale: **>1B inference calls/day** across **87 clusters in 18 clouds**; ~**20× YoY revenue growth**; headcount tripling in 2026; customers include Cursor, Notion, Lovable, Harvey, HubSpot, Abridge, Clay, Mercor, OpenEvidence. Baseten states leading application-layer companies now direct **30–50% of model spend to custom/post-trained models**. [SECONDARY]
- Prior: **$300M Series E in January 2026** at **$5B** (anchor investors IVP, CapitalG, **Nvidia**) — a 160–160%+ step-up in five months. [SECONDARY]

### Groq — Nvidia's $20B talent licensing deal + $350M raise at $3.5B [SECONDARY]
- **December 2025** — **Nvidia paid Groq $20 billion in a non-exclusive technology licensing agreement** and hired founder/CEO Jonathan Ross, President Sunny Madra, and core engineering staff — a talent-acquisition-via-licensing structure (same playbook Alphabet and Meta used with smaller AI startups). [SECONDARY]
- **June 2026** — Groq raised **$650M** to begin its pivot from AI chipmaker (LPUs) to neocloud operator running Nvidia systems. [SECONDARY]
- **2026-08-17** — Groq raised **$350M**, led by Disruptive with planned Nvidia participation, at **$3.5B** — down from **$6.9B (Sept 2025, $750M round)**. A company spokesperson told TechCrunch this is "not a down round" but a new valuation for the "post-Nvidia-licensing-deal version of Groq" — a materially different business (inference cloud vs. proprietary accelerator). [SECONDARY]
- Forward plan: scale from **54 MW to >200 MW in 2027**; 13 data centers across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and APAC; 6M+ developers. [SECONDARY]
