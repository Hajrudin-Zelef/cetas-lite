# Anthropic's second 2026 raise — $30B+ at $900B+ pre-money, May 2026 [SECONDARY]

### Anthropic's second 2026 raise — $30B+ at $900B+ pre-money, May 2026 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-05-12** — Bloomberg reported Anthropic in talks to raise **at least $30B** at a **pre-money valuation exceeding $900B** (some reports as high as $950B). [SECONDARY]
- **2026-05-15** — the FT reported **agreed terms** on the $30B round at ~$900B pre-money, co-led by **Sequoia Capital, Dragoneer Investment Group, Altimeter Capital, and Greenoaks Capital Partners** (~$2B each); existing backers Founders Fund and General Catalyst in secondary allocation. Round came together in weeks after inbound proposals in late April; CFO Krishna Rao ran a 48-hour allocation process. [SECONDARY]
- If closed, Anthropic would pass OpenAI's $852B (March 2026) as the most valuable AI startup — first time since the GPT-4 era the lead changed hands. At $30B on $900B pre-money, dilution is ~3.2% — unusually founder-friendly at this scale. [SECONDARY]
- Valuation velocity: $61.5B (Mar 2025) → $183B (Sep 2025) → $380B (Feb 2026) → $900B+ (May 2026) — ~15× in 14 months. [SECONDARY]
- Revenue behind it: annualized run-rate $87M (Jan 2024) → $9B (Dec 2025) → $14B (Feb 2026) → $19B (Mar) → **$30B (Apr 2026)** — Anthropic crossed OpenAI (then ~$24B run-rate) in April; Q2 2026 projected at $10.9B revenue with first profitable quarter expected. Amodei: "80× annualized growth" in Q1 2026 alone. [SECONDARY]
- Compute behind the raise (per secondary/Yahoo Finance): Anthropic arranged a **~$45B compute deal with SpaceX**, a **$1.8B agreement with Akamai**, and chips/cloud from Google. 2026 compute spend on training+inference estimated ≈$19B — roughly matching full-year revenue. [SECONDARY]
- Distinction the corpus must preserve: the May 2026 round is a **second $30B round**, 14 weeks after the February Series G ($30B at $380B post-money) — two distinct raises, same headline number. [DIRECTIONAL]
- **Compute commitments behind the raise**: ~$45B SpaceX compute deal, $1.8B Akamai agreement, Google chips/cloud — 2026 training+inference spend ≈$19B, roughly matching full-year revenue. The raise funds compute, not headcount. [SECONDARY]
- **Valuation velocity**: $61.5B (Mar 2025) → $183B (Sep 2025) → $380B (Feb 2026) → $900B+ (May 2026) — ~15× in 14 months, the fastest repricing of a private company in the dataset. [SECONDARY]

### xAI Colossus — the physical buildout behind the capital [SECONDARY]
- **2026-05-03** — xAI brought **Colossus 2** online: **550,000 Nvidia GB200/GB300** accelerators (two GPUs per node, >1M total accelerators), the world's largest single-site training deployment at **>1 GW**. Colossus 1: 230,000 GPUs (150k H100, 50k H200, 30k GB200). Combined: >1M H100-equivalents. [SECONDARY]
- Scale targets: **1.5 GW within months, 2 GW long-term** (≈1.5M households' peak load); third site in Southaven, Mississippi ("MACROHARDRR"); roadmap to **1M GPUs by late 2026** for Grok 5 (rumored 6T parameters). Musk claims **300,000 GPUs installed per month**, outpacing every hyperscaler. [SECONDARY]
- **Environmental-legal overhang**: April 2026 — NAACP Mississippi + SELC/Earthjustice filed a **Clean Air Act lawsuit** over **27–59 unpermitted methane turbines** at Colossus 2 (Southaven) — the first major federal test of AI compute vs 1970-vintage air permitting. DOJ moved June 15, 2026 to intervene and dismiss. [SECONDARY]
- **Merchant-compute gate**: Google's **September 30, 2026 delivery gate** — if xAI fails to deliver ~110,000 committed GPUs by 09-30, Google may walk away or accept pro-rata-reduced fees after a one-month grace period. First hard test of whether xAI's build speed meets its merchant commitments. [SECONDARY]
- Grok 5 training at Colossus 2/3 slipped past Musk's Q1/Q2 2026 targets; now expected H2 2026. [SECONDARY]

### Google→Anthropic — up to $40B + 5GW TPU, 2026-04-24 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-04-24** — Google committed **up to $40B to Anthropic** in a package combining cash and **5 GW of TPU compute through 2027** (one secondary breakdown: $10B cash + $30B milestone payments + compute). The deal locked Anthropic workload onto Google Cloud against TPUs — the chips Google builds to compete with Nvidia. [SECONDARY]
- Simultaneously, Google–Broadcom–Anthropic signed a **multi-gigawatt agreement for next-generation TPU capacity expected online from 2027** ("Ironwood" generation). [SECONDARY]
- Five gigawatts is roughly Google's entire Ironwood capacity coming online through 2030, per one secondary read — allocating it to a single tenant tightens TPU supply for all other Google Cloud customers. [SECONDARY]

### Amazon→Anthropic — up to $25B + $100B AWS spend commitment, April 2026 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-04-20** — CNBC reported Amazon agreed to invest **up to $25B in Anthropic**; in return Anthropic committed to spend **>$100B on AWS technologies over the next 10 years** and secured **up to 5 GW of Trainium capacity** for training and deploying Claude. Amazon disclosed Anthropic's Trainium commitment on **2026-04-29**. [SECONDARY]
- Anthropic's disclosed compute posture (April 2026): **10 GW total — 5 GW Google TPU + 5 GW AWS Trainium** — the only frontier lab with multi-gigawatt commitments on two hyperscaler stacks. [SECONDARY]

### OpenAI–AWS expansion — +$100B over 8 years [SECONDARY]
- March 2026: OpenAI and AWS **expanded their existing $38B multi-year agreement by $100B over 8 years**; OpenAI committed to consume ~**2 GW of Trainium capacity** through AWS infrastructure. AWS also became the **exclusive third-party cloud distribution provider for OpenAI Frontier**, enabling agent teams on AWS. [SECONDARY]
- One secondary CI compilation states AWS has locked OpenAI and Anthropic into **>$200B in combined AWS infrastructure commitments** — the single largest model-choice anchor in the cloud market. [SECONDARY]

### The 2026 capital-concentration thesis [DIRECTIONAL]
- June 2026 was the corpus's **capital-concentration peak**: Groq $650M, Baseten $1.5B, IREN $3.65B, Applied Digital $550M, DeepSeek's first external capital — five infrastructure financings in one month, each >$500M, each GPU-adjacent. [SECONDARY]
- September 2026 was the **neocloud liquidity month**: Crusoe $3.9B, CoreWeave $3B+$2.92B, Nscale S-1, Lambda $3B+$1B — four neocloud capital events in 30 days, ~$12B+ of announced financing. [SECONDARY]
- The sovereign-wealth pattern: Mubadala, GIC, QIA, OIA, Aramco Ventures, MGX, HUMAIN appear across xAI, Crusoe, Nscale, Lambda, Fireworks, Baseten, Together, Databricks — **Gulf + Singapore capital anchors nearly every 2026 AI infrastructure syndicate**. The corpus should track SWF participation as a leading indicator of round completion. [DIRECTIONAL]
- The circularity critique (Wedbush) now has a hardware-side ledger: Nvidia invests in the neoclouds (Crusoe, Nscale, Lambda, Fireworks, Baseten, Together) that buy Nvidia chips with Nvidia-blessed debt to serve Nvidia's largest customers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta). The circle is complete and disclosed — the question is whether it's a flywheel or a daisy chain. [DIRECTIONAL]
- **What breaks the circle**: customer concentration (CoreWeave 93% from existing), debt service ($640M/quarter and rising), and the possibility that model efficiency (DeepSeek's 890 B/token, V4.1-Flash's $0.003 cached input) reduces the GPU-hours per token faster than demand grows. The corpus should watch **revenue-per-GW** as the canary metric. [DIRECTIONAL]
