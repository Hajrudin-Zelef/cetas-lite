# The 2026 capital-concentration thesis [DIRECTIONAL]

### The 2026 capital-concentration thesis [DIRECTIONAL]
- June 2026 was the corpus's **capital-concentration peak**: Groq $650M, Baseten $1.5B, IREN $3.65B, Applied Digital $550M, DeepSeek's first external capital — five infrastructure financings in one month, each >$500M, each GPU-adjacent. [SECONDARY]
- September 2026 was the **neocloud liquidity month**: Crusoe $3.9B, CoreWeave $3B+$2.92B, Nscale S-1, Lambda $3B+$1B — four neocloud capital events in 30 days, ~$12B+ of announced financing. [SECONDARY]
- The sovereign-wealth pattern: Mubadala, GIC, QIA, OIA, Aramco Ventures, MGX, HUMAIN appear across xAI, Crusoe, Nscale, Lambda, Fireworks, Baseten, Together, Databricks — **Gulf + Singapore capital anchors nearly every 2026 AI infrastructure syndicate**. The corpus should track SWF participation as a leading indicator of round completion. [DIRECTIONAL]
- The circularity critique (Wedbush) now has a hardware-side ledger: Nvidia invests in the neoclouds (Crusoe, Nscale, Lambda, Fireworks, Baseten, Together) that buy Nvidia chips with Nvidia-blessed debt to serve Nvidia's largest customers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta). The circle is complete and disclosed — the question is whether it's a flywheel or a daisy chain. [DIRECTIONAL]
- **What breaks the circle**: customer concentration (CoreWeave 93% from existing), debt service ($640M/quarter and rising), and the possibility that model efficiency (DeepSeek's 890 B/token, V4.1-Flash's $0.003 cached input) reduces the GPU-hours per token faster than demand grows. The corpus should watch **revenue-per-GW** as the canary metric. [DIRECTIONAL]

### Meta–CoreWeave — +$21B, ~$35B total through 2032, 2026-04 [SECONDARY]
- **April 2026** — Meta committed an **additional $21B to CoreWeave**, bringing the total Meta–CoreWeave relationship to approximately **$35B through December 2032** — a long-term agreement deploying NVIDIA's next-generation **Vera Rubin** architecture. CoreWeave CEO Michael Intrator stated no single customer would represent more than 35% of total sales. [SECONDARY]

### CoreWeave Q2 2026 — $2.58B revenue, $104B backlog, $35.6B debt [SECONDARY]
- **2026-08-11** — CoreWeave reported Q2 2026: **$2.58B revenue (+112% YoY)**, beating the $2.56B consensus and landing at the high end of $2.45–2.6B guidance; **$626M net loss** after **$640M quarterly interest expense (+140% YoY)**; $49M operating loss on $2.624B opex. Gross margin compressed 74% → 65.9% as cost of revenue rose 181%. [SECONDARY]
- **Backlog $104B (+246% YoY)**, with >$25B added in early Q3; **93% of revenue growth from existing customers expanding contracts** (per 10-Q), not new logos. Total indebtedness **$35.6B** (from ~$25B end-Q1). Shares rose ~19% on results; Bernstein holds a Sell, warning decelerating AI advancement could cut infrastructure demand. [SECONDARY]
- Technology milestone: CoreWeave became the **first neocloud to deploy Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72** in production (72 Rubin GPUs + 36 Vera CPUs per rack-scale unit); Nebius and IREN had not yet introduced it. Footprint: **1.5 GW active / 4.2 GW+ contracted, 51 data centers**; anchor deals Meta, Anthropic, OpenAI. [SECONDARY]
- **2026-09-17** — CoreWeave announced a **$3B convertible** plus a **$2.92B ATM** program alongside Crusoe's Series F — the neocloud capital-raising day. [SECONDARY]

### Nebius Q2 2026 — $582M revenue (+454%), $3.0B ARR, minimal debt [SECONDARY]

Shares +34% on results, +166% YTD. Strategy contrast vs CoreWeave: **minimal debt**, $3.7B cash, partnerships with Meta and Microsoft. [SECONDARY]

### IREN — $3.65B GPU-backed financing (Microsoft) then $2.8B more, 2026 [SECONDARY]
- **~June 2026** — IREN closed a **$3.65B GPU-backed financing** structured as a **securitization of GPU hardware + future cash flows**: $2.1B US private placement + $1.55B term loan. Capital funds AI cloud expansion under an **AI cloud supply agreement with Microsoft**; capacity target **480 MW by end-2026**. [SECONDARY]
- **2026-08-28/29** — IREN signed a **multi-year contract with an unnamed frontier AI lab** and secured **$2.8B in new financing** ($2.4B facility led by Blue Owl + PIMCO) for AI chip purchases. Same quarter: $684M net loss including a $450.4M non-cash impairment from decommissioning bitcoin mining hardware; revenue down 27% to $137.2M. The company said 2026 compute capacity is **largely sold out**, with late-stage 2027 discussions. [SECONDARY]
- The Microsoft-tied securitization is a novel financing form — raising against hardware value rather than corporate debt or equity dilution — and signals institutional confidence in AI-cloud revenue streams. [DIRECTIONAL]

### Applied Digital — up to $550M revolving credit facility, 2026-06-08 [SECONDARY]
- **2026-06-08** — Applied Digital closed a revolving credit facility with **$350M committed capacity + $200M accordion** (up to $550M total), arranged by **Goldman Sachs**, secured by certain non-data-center project assets, maturing 2029-05-29. Proceeds fund pre/post-lease data center development, working capital, and general corporate purposes for its AI/HPC pipeline. [SECONDARY]

### Fluidstack — the €10B French supercomputer deal collapsed [SECONDARY]
- Fluidstack's planned **€10B French gigawatt-scale supercomputer** (anchored at the Paris AI summit) collapsed — coverage framed it as a capital-reality mismatch: only ~$4.5M in previously disclosed equity against a €10B project with a 2026 deadline, 10 full-time employees, and no major financial anchor (Google had committed a $1.8B backstop to a different US project, not the French one). Useful as the corpus's **negative case** for headline-vs-funded deals. [SECONDARY]

### Hyperscaler capex 2026 — two tallies, two methodologies [SECONDARY]
- **Q1-2026 earnings tally (FT, ~May 2026)**: Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta collectively guiding **~$725B capex in 2026**, up **77%** from 2025's ~$410B record. Breakdown: Amazon ~$200B, Microsoft ~$190B, Alphabet $175–185B, Meta $115–135B (Meta later raised to **$125–145B**). Evercore and BofA project combined big-tech capex **>$1T in 2027**. Alphabet raised a $25B bond sale against an AI order book above $115B. [SECONDARY]
- **January 2026 tally (Introl)**: Big Five (Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta, Oracle) projected at **$602B** in 2026 (+36% YoY), ~75% ($450B) targeting AI infrastructure; hyperscalers raised $108B in debt in 2025, with $1.5T projected. Goldman Sachs projected $1.15T total 2025–2027 vs $477B in 2022–2024. [SECONDARY]
- **CONTRADICTION to document, not reconcile**: the January $602B projection and the post-Q1 $725B guidance are successive estimates, not competing facts — guidance drifted upward as earnings confirmed. Never average them. [DIRECTIONAL]
- Meta specifics: ad revenue $55B in Q1 (impressions +19%, ad prices +12%) as AI spending drove core returns; Zuckerberg at the **2026-05-27 shareholder meeting** said external compute sales are "definitely on the table" if capacity runs ahead of internal demand — Meta signaling a potential cloud-compute product posture. Meta cut ~10% of workforce (~8,000 employees) concurrently. [SECONDARY]
- Cloud economics: Microsoft fiscal 2026 capex $190B (Azure capacity-constrained); Amazon Q1 capex $44.2B, shifting to in-house Trainium/Graviton; Alphabet Q1 capex $35.67B (TPUs + Vertex AI); capex as % of revenue at the Big Four rose from pre-AI 10–15% to ~25–30%, forecast 35% in 2027. [SECONDARY]

### Databricks — $5B Feb 2026 at $134B, then $5B Aug 2026 at $190B [SECONDARY]
- **February 2026** — Databricks raised **$5B in equity + $2B in debt capacity** at a **$134B private valuation** (Series K lineage; prior: $10B late 2024 at $62B, $100B step, Jan 2025 $62B). [SECONDARY]
- **2026-07-16** — Databricks signed a **term sheet for a $3B strategic round at $188B**, led by Coatue, expected to close late summer. [SECONDARY]
- **2026-08-13** — Databricks closed **$5B at $190B**: led by Coatue with Blackstone, **MGX**, T. Rowe Price-affiliated funds, and new investor Sixth Street Growth; ~two dozen VCs participated. Ghodsi: the company wanted $1B, investors wanted in for $15B — settled on $5B. [SECONDARY]
- Operating facts at close: **>$7B annualized revenue run rate**, **80% YoY growth**, cash-flow positive; flagship lakehouse data warehouse >$1.5B run rate (100% YoY growth, vs Snowflake ~$83B market cap); AI products $1.7B run rate; Lakebase crossed $100M run rate (16M+ Postgres instances spun up daily); **>1,000 enterprise customers at $1M+ ARR**, 100+ at $10M+. [SECONDARY]
- M&A: Panther (AI cybersecurity, June), Electric/PGlite (lightweight Postgres for agents, August). KARL RL tool disclosed to bend the agent-workload cost curve. [SECONDARY]
