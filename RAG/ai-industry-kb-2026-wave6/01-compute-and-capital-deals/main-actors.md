# Main actors

## Main actors

- **NVIDIA** — the central capital-deployer: $30B OpenAI investment; >$40B early-2026 AI-equity binge; up to $10B into Anthropic; signatory of the July 24 open-weights letter and founder of the Open Secure AI Alliance (see corpus wave6/02). [SECONDARY]
- **OpenAI** — capital recipient: restructured NVIDIA investment; $122B record round close; AMD 6GW compute-warrant partner. [SECONDARY]
- **Microsoft** — up to $5B Anthropic investor; $30B Azure compute counterparty; retains Anthropic relationship alongside AWS and GCP availability. [VENDOR]
- **Anthropic** — pact partner with two distinct $30B figures (compute commitment vs Series G); signed a no-warrant AMD deal in July 2026. [VENDOR]
- **AMD** — 6GW OpenAI partnership originator of the penny-warrant template; Meta reused the template in February 2026. [VENDOR]
- **Meta** — reused the AMD warrant structure in its February 2026 AMD deal. [SECONDARY]
- **Matthew Bryson (Wedbush)** — analyst who coined the "circular investment theme" critique of NVIDIA's deals. [SECONDARY]
- **CoreWeave, Nebius, Lumentum, Coherent, Marvell, Corning, IREN** — public-company counterparties in NVIDIA's 2026 equity deals. [SECONDARY]

### SPCX IPO day-one anatomy — the largest IPO in history [SECONDARY]
- **2026-06-12** — SPCX opened at **$150** (+11%), hit **$176.52** intraday, closed at **~$161** (+19%), valuing SpaceX at **>$2.1T** — the largest IPO in history ($75B raised at $135, 555.6M shares, $1.77T at offer). Previous record: Saudi Aramco $29.4B (2019). [SECONDARY]
- Deal mechanics: **4× oversubscribed**, retail orders >$100B, **~30% retail allocation** ($22.5B — vs typical 5–10%); brokerages Robinhood, Fidelity, Schwab, SoFi, E*TRADE offered IPO-price access. Greenshoe: 83.3M shares ($11.2B). [SECONDARY]
- Governance: Musk holds **~82.4% voting power**; SpaceX operates as a Nasdaq "controlled company." Only **~5% of shares in public float** — thin-float dynamics cut both ways. [SECONDARY]
- Financials behind the listing: **~$4.9B lost in 2025, ~$4.3B lost in Q1 2026**; rockets = ~20% of revenue — the listed company is "a satellite telecom funding an AI buildout, with a launch business attached" (Starlink 10,000+ satellites, Grok/X AI division, Terafab chip project with Tesla). Trading at **~94× 2025 revenue**. [SECONDARY]
- Musk became the world's **first trillionaire on paper** at the close. Former Tesla board member Steve Westly: SpaceX must succeed in 2 of 3 "moonshots" (Starship, Starlink, AI) to justify the cap. [SECONDARY]
