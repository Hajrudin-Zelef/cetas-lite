# Hyperscaler capex 2026 — two tallies, two methodologies [SECONDARY]

### Hyperscaler capex 2026 — two tallies, two methodologies [SECONDARY]
- **Q1-2026 earnings tally (FT, ~May 2026)**: Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta collectively guiding **~$725B capex in 2026**, up **77%** from 2025's ~$410B record. Breakdown: Amazon ~$200B, Microsoft ~$190B, Alphabet $175–185B, Meta $115–135B (Meta later raised to **$125–145B**). Evercore and BofA project combined big-tech capex **>$1T in 2027**. Alphabet raised a $25B bond sale against an AI order book above $115B. [SECONDARY]
- **January 2026 tally (Introl)**: Big Five (Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta, Oracle) projected at **$602B** in 2026 (+36% YoY), ~75% ($450B) targeting AI infrastructure; hyperscalers raised $108B in debt in 2025, with $1.5T projected. Goldman Sachs projected $1.15T total 2025–2027 vs $477B in 2022–2024. [SECONDARY]
- **CONTRADICTION to document, not reconcile**: the January $602B projection and the post-Q1 $725B guidance are successive estimates, not competing facts — guidance drifted upward as earnings confirmed. Never average them. [DIRECTIONAL]
- Meta specifics: ad revenue $55B in Q1 (impressions +19%, ad prices +12%) as AI spending drove core returns; Zuckerberg at the **2026-05-27 shareholder meeting** said external compute sales are "definitely on the table" if capacity runs ahead of internal demand — Meta signaling a potential cloud-compute product posture. Meta cut ~10% of workforce (~8,000 employees) concurrently. [SECONDARY]
- Cloud economics: Microsoft fiscal 2026 capex $190B (Azure capacity-constrained); Amazon Q1 capex $44.2B, shifting to in-house Trainium/Graviton; Alphabet Q1 capex $35.67B (TPUs + Vertex AI); capex as % of revenue at the Big Four rose from pre-AI 10–15% to ~25–30%, forecast 35% in 2027. [SECONDARY]

### Databricks — $5B Feb 2026 at $134B, then $5B Aug 2026 at $190B [SECONDARY]
- **February 2026** — Databricks raised **$5B in equity + $2B in debt capacity** at a **$134B private valuation** (Series K lineage; prior: $10B late 2024 at $62B, $100B step, Jan 2025 $62B). [SECONDARY]
- **2026-07-16** — Databricks signed a **term sheet for a $3B strategic round at $188B**, led by Coatue, expected to close late summer. [SECONDARY]
- **2026-08-13** — Databricks closed **$5B at $190B**: led by Coatue with Blackstone, **MGX**, T. Rowe Price-affiliated funds, and new investor Sixth Street Growth; ~two dozen VCs participated. Ghodsi: the company wanted $1B, investors wanted in for $15B — settled on $5B. [SECONDARY]
- Operating facts at close: **>$7B annualized revenue run rate**, **80% YoY growth**, cash-flow positive; flagship lakehouse data warehouse >$1.5B run rate (100% YoY growth, vs Snowflake ~$83B market cap); AI products $1.7B run rate; Lakebase crossed $100M run rate (16M+ Postgres instances spun up daily); **>1,000 enterprise customers at $1M+ ARR**, 100+ at $10M+. [SECONDARY]
- M&A: Panther (AI cybersecurity, June), Electric/PGlite (lightweight Postgres for agents, August). KARL RL tool disclosed to bend the agent-workload cost curve. [SECONDARY]
