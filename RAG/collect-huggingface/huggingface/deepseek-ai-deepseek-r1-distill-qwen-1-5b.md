## Full summary

DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B is one of six dense models distilled from the DeepSeek-R1 reasoning model, fine-tuned on samples generated by R1. It is based on Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B (Qwen2.5 series), and DeepSeek states it slightly changes the config and tokenizer, so their settings should be used to run it. The purpose of the distilled family is to show that reasoning patterns from a large model can be transferred into much smaller, widely-used dense models, producing better performance than reasoning learned via RL directly on small models. This 1.5B checkpoint is the smallest of the family, which also includes Qwen-7B, Llama-8B, Qwen-14B, Qwen-32B, and Llama-70B variants.

Despite its tiny size, the model performs strongly on math and reasoning benchmarks relative to its scale: AIME 2024 pass@1 = 28.9 (cons@64 = 52.7), MATH-500 pass@1 = 83.9, GPQA Diamond = 33.8, LiveCodeBench = 16.9, and CodeForces rating = 954. The card lists the full distilled-model evaluation table for comparison, where the largest distills reach AIME 2024 pass@1 of 72.6 (Qwen-32B) and 70.0 (Llama-70B).

The model is a standard Qwen2 architecture (qwen2 tag) with ~2B parameters stored in BF16 and a chat template. It can be used like any Qwen or Llama model. For serving, the card shows vLLM (`vllm serve ... --tensor-parallel-size 2 --max-model-len 32768 --enforce-eager`) and SGLang (`python3 -m sglang.launch_server --model ... --trust-remote-code --tp 2`) examples, plus standard Transformers pipeline and AutoModelForCausalLM usage. Usage recommendations apply to the whole R1 series: temperature 0.5–0.7 (0.6 recommended), no system prompt, and for math prompt with "reason step by step, put final answer within \boxed{}". The license is MIT, with the caveat that Qwen-derived distills originate from the Apache 2.0 Qwen-2.5 series and were fine-tuned on 800k R1-curated samples. The model has 444,960 monthly downloads and 264 quantized derivatives.

## Key points

- Smallest member (1.5B) of the DeepSeek-R1 distilled dense family, based on Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B.
- Fine-tuned on 800k samples curated with DeepSeek-R1; MIT license (base Qwen2.5 is Apache 2.0).
- Standard Qwen2 architecture, ~2B params, BF16, with chat template.
- Benchmarks: AIME 2024 28.9 (cons@64 52.7), MATH-500 83.9, GPQA-D 33.8, LiveCodeBench 16.9, CodeForces 954.
- Runs like Qwen/Llama via Transformers, vLLM, or SGLang.
- Usage: temp 0.5–0.7 (0.6), no system prompt, force `<think>\n`, boxed final answers.
- Large ecosystem: 323 adapters, 658 finetunes, 264 quantizations on the Hub.
- 444,960 downloads/month; arXiv 2501.12948.
