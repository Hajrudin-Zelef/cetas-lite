## Full summary

DeepSeek-R1 is DeepSeek-AI's first-generation open reasoning model, released alongside DeepSeek-R1-Zero and six distilled dense models. DeepSeek-R1-Zero was trained via large-scale reinforcement learning directly on the base model without any supervised fine-tuning (SFT) as a preliminary step, which caused strong reasoning behaviors such as self-verification, reflection, and long chains of thought to emerge naturally — the first open research to show reasoning can be incentivized purely through RL. However, R1-Zero suffered from endless repetition, poor readability, and language mixing. DeepSeek-R1 addresses this by incorporating cold-start data before RL, using a pipeline with two RL stages (to discover improved reasoning patterns and align with human preferences) and two SFT stages (seeding reasoning and non-reasoning capabilities). It reaches performance comparable to OpenAI-o1 across math, code, and reasoning tasks.

The full model is a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) with 671B total parameters and 37B activated parameters, based on DeepSeek-V3-Base, with a 128K context length. The card also open-sources six distilled dense models fine-tuned on reasoning data generated by DeepSeek-R1: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B/7B/14B/32B (from Qwen2.5-Math / Qwen2.5 bases), DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B (from Llama-3.1-8B), and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B (from Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct). The 32B distill outperforms OpenAI-o1-mini on several benchmarks.

Benchmark highlights for DeepSeek-R1 include MMLU 90.8, MMLU-Pro 84.0, DROP 92.2, GPQA-Diamond 71.5, LiveCodeBench 65.9, Codeforces rating 2029, AIME 2024 79.8, and MATH-500 97.3. The license is MIT, and the series supports commercial use, modification, and derivative works including distillation into other LLMs; distill models inherit Apache 2.0 (Qwen) or Llama licenses.

Recommended usage: temperature 0.5–0.7 (0.6), avoid a system prompt (put all instructions in the user prompt), and for math include "Please reason step by step, and put your final answer within \boxed{}." The model can bypass thinking (`<think>\n\n</think>`), so forcing it to start with `<think>\n` is advised. It runs via vLLM/SGLang (distills behave like Qwen/Llama); native Transformers support was not direct at release.

## Key points

- Open reasoning model (MoE) with 671B total / 37B activated parameters and 128K context.
- R1-Zero showed pure-RL reasoning without SFT; R1 adds cold-start data plus 2 RL + 2 SFT stages.
- Performance comparable to OpenAI-o1 on math, code, and reasoning.
- Six distilled dense models (1.5B–70B) based on Qwen2.5 and Llama3 released.
- MIT license, commercial use and distillation permitted; distills inherit base licenses.
- Recommended temperature 0.6, no system prompt, force `<think>\n` to avoid bypassing reasoning.
- DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B surpasses OpenAI-o1-mini on multiple benchmarks.
- Hub reports 684B params (BF16/FP8) and 815k monthly downloads.
