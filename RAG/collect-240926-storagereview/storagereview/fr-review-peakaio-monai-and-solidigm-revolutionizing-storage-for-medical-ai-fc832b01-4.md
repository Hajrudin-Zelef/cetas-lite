PEAK:ARCHIVE was designed to meet the specific needs of the medical sector, where data must be retained for years, even decades. MONAI: The future of AI in healthcare
MONAI is an open-source initiative created by King's College London and other partners, including NVIDIA. MONAI was designed to accelerate the development and deployment of AI models in healthcare, particularly in medical imaging. Given the sensitivity of patient data and strict privacy regulations, MONAI ensures that data does not leave the clinic, thereby addressing one of the main concerns of medical AI.
MONAI's open-source framework allows for flexibility and customization, enabling healthcare institutions to adapt AI models to their needs. The platform is designed to work seamlessly with existing health systems and provides a robust infrastructure for deploying AI in clinical environments. PEAK:AIO's storage solutions are optimized for MONAI and ensure that the vast amounts of data required by AI models are readily available and processed efficiently.
NVIDIA and MONAI: A partnership of innovation
NVIDIA's partnership with MONAI integrates GPU-accelerated computing, essential for training complex AI models. The synergy between NVIDIA's hardware and MONAI's software constitutes a robust toolkit for building AI applications in healthcare. However, the effectiveness of these tools depends heavily on the underlying storage infrastructure, in which PEAK:AIO and Solidigm play an important role.
"Training the next generation of AI models, which can help researchers and clinicians better understand data, can require many I/Os. Even with advances in accelerated computing, if the pixels stored on disk cannot be moved to the GPU at high speed, the entire model training process slows down considerably. Solutions like PEAK:AIO with Solidigm QLC SSDs, specialized in secure, high-volume, professional-grade storage, connected via GPUDirect, can enable more models to run in less time." - Brad Genereaux, Global Lead Healthcare Alliances, NVIDIA.
By leveraging the advanced storage capabilities of PEAK:AIO systems, healthcare institutions can avoid AI workload bottlenecks due to data access speeds. The collaboration between PEAK:AIO, MONAI, and NVIDIA represents a holistic approach to deploying AI in healthcare, with each player bringing its strengths to solve complex problems.
