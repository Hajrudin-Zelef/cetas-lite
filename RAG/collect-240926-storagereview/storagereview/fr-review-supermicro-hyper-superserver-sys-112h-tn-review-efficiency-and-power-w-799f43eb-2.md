# fr-review-supermicro-hyper-superserver-sys-112h-tn-review-efficiency-and-power-w-799f43eb

The Supermicro Hyper SuperServer SYS-112H-TN is a versatile server platform designed to meet the demanding needs of modern enterprise environments. Featuring a single Intel Xeon 6 processor, this 1U system offers a blend of performance and efficiency in a compact form factor.
With support for a range of storage and I/O options, the server can be optimized for various workloads, including virtualization, cloud computing, Big Data analytics, and AI inference applications. The SYS-112H-TN positions itself as a mainstream server solution that stands out for its adaptability rather than its performance in a specific category.
Supermicro SYS-112H-TN powered by Intel Xeon 6 processors
The main highlight of the SYS-112H-TN is the Intel Xeon 6 processor, making it one of the first servers to ship with these latest chips. Xeon 6 processors offer a balance of cores and threads, making them perfectly suited for common enterprise tasks. With support for up to 16 DIMM slots and up to 1 TB of DDR5 ECC memory, the server ensures high-speed data processing and reliability, which is crucial for tasks requiring high data throughput and minimal latency. This memory capacity allows the SYS-112H-TN to efficiently handle intensive workloads.
The arrival of Intel Xeon 6 processors, and particularly the Sierra Forest 6780E and 6766E that we tested on an Intel launch server, constitutes a major asset for the market. Designed to deliver efficiency and scalability, these processors are perfectly suited to the target applications of the SYS-112H-TN. Thanks to an optimal balance between core count and power consumption, the Xeon 6 range allows the SYS-112H-TN to deliver robust performance without excessive energy consumption. This server thus represents an attractive option for businesses looking to optimize the cost-effectiveness of their IT infrastructure.
Xeon 6 processors significantly improve the performance/power consumption ratio, particularly for workloads that leverage a large number of cores and high energy efficiency. Businesses whose tasks span a wide range of needs, from AI inference to media transcoding to general computing, will undoubtedly benefit from this improvement. For a detailed analysis of these processors, we invite you to consult our comprehensive overview of the Intel Xeon 6 range, which covers performance indicators and real-world applications. In this test, we examine their performance within a Supermicro server.
Components and features of the Supermicro Hyper 1U SYS-112H-TN
The Supermicro Hyper 1U SYS-112H-TN supports PCIe Gen5, including two PCIe 5.0 x16 slots and an additional PCIe 5.0 AIOM slot. This enables high-bandwidth connectivity for various expansion cards, such as GPUs or network cards.
The server also includes up to eight hot-swappable 2.5″ drive bays supporting NVMe, SAS, and SATA drives. For users needing additional storage, there is a twelve-bay expansion option with four additional hot-swappable 2.5″ SAS/SATA drive bays. This configuration is complemented by two M.2 NVMe slots on the board.
The networking capabilities of the SYS-112H-TN, like the AIOM slot, are compatible with OCP NIC 3.0, offering flexible, high-bandwidth network configurations suited to data-intensive applications and virtualized environments. The server also features a dedicated 1GbE LAN port for out-of-band management, ensuring that administrative tasks do not interfere with primary data operations. This separation of management and data traffic helps maintain optimal performance and security.
In terms of efficiency and ease of management, the SYS-112H-TN is compatible with Supermicro's full suite of management tools, including SuperCloud Composer and Supermicro Server Manager. These tools simplify monitoring and management of hardware resources, improving operational efficiency.
The server includes 1200W Titanium-level redundant power supplies, offering high energy efficiency and reliability.

Supermicro SYS-112H-TN Design and Build
The Supermicro SYS-112H-TN measures 30.66″ deep, making it a relatively compact server.
The front panel houses the eight hot-swappable 2.5″ drive bays and, in our review model, the space where four optional SATA/SAS drive bays can be added for future expansion. The front panel also includes essential controls and indicators, such as power and UID buttons, status LEDs for monitoring system health, and a USB 3.0 port for quick access to external devices.
The rear of the server includes two PCIe 5.0 x16 slots for full-height, full-length cards (one is double-width) and the OCP slot, enabling high-bandwidth network or storage expansion. The server's Titanium-level redundant power supplies ensure reliability and uninterrupted operation with options up to 1600W. Additional connectivity for USB and OoB management is also located at the rear.
Inside the SYS-112H-TN, the layout is organized to optimize airflow and maintain efficient thermal management. The server has eight internal fans to ensure consistent cooling of all critical components. The single Intel Xeon 6 Scalable processor is located in the center, flanked by 16 DIMM slots supporting DDR5 memory. The motherboard also includes two M.2 NVMe slots.
