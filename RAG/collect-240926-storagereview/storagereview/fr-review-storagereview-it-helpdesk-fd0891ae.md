# fr-review-storagereview-it-helpdesk-fd0891ae

<!-- source: https://www.storagereview.com/fr/review/storagereview-it-helpdesk -->

Being an administrator of any sort is hard. I don't care if it's virtualization, storage, networking, or babysitting the dweebs in application development, there are always problems and it's always the administrator's fault. Add to that the classic "do more with less" mantra that your idiot CTO bellows between bourbons and the return-to-office wave that is upon us now, and being an IT administrator can be rough. When their constituents have problems, they turn to the helpdesk. But where do haggard IT pros go when they need help?
We feel the administrators' pain. In the StorageReview lab, we are always sent hardware that is designed to be "quick and easy." The number of "configured in 15 minutes" bays and clusters we have received that took hours or days to configure is measurable. Then there is all the maintenance of systems during our testing and managing the perpetual performance issues. And we don't run much production infrastructure.
What if there was a hardware solution to help IT administrators get a little assistance in these trying times? Enter the StorageReview IT Helpdesk or for IT administrators on the go, the BODEGAcooler Mini 12V portable freezer.
StorageReview IT Helpdesk
For IT administrators stuck in the datacenter, we hand-crafted the StorageReview IT Helpdesk. We started by looking for the perfect solution, alas one did not exist. So we gathered the brightest minds at StorageReview to create our own solution. Here's how it's made.
At the heart of the StorageReview IT Helpdesk is a lockable 6U rack drawer, offering an optimal ratio of storage capacity and discretion. Too small, it would be hard to access; too large, it would be immediately spotted. This model also includes a large, easily sealable tub, thus preventing water leaks onto your precious all-flash system, server, or UPS.
For insulation, we used 1″ closed-cell rigid foam to line the compartment to keep drinks cold and add another layer of waterproofing.
Since we aren't completely insane, everything was completely sealed with polyurethane caulk. First the metal drawer, all the seams and rivet holes. Once that was dry, we added the foam and sealed all the edges of the foam, including the top edge so that no water could seep through that layer. I can't emphasize how much thought and consideration went into this project, nor how much the hardware is directly under the Helpdesk.
Without much work, the StorageReview IT Helpdesk was designed in a few hours, using standard parts and supplies from Home Depot. In total, we had less than $150 in supplies.
BODEGAcooler Mini 12V Portable Freezer P15
Not all IT administrators are located in a datacenter, some may be located in the field. For those individuals, we found a ready-made option that fits in the back of a work truck or transport vehicle. The BODEGAcooler Mini 12V portable freezer is a compact 15-liter portable refrigerator/freezer that has an operating temperature range of -4-68F and is capable of running off 12/24V, 120V batteries, or optionally. This is not a Peltier chip-based cooler, but a cooler equipped with a compressor to reach and maintain sub-zero temperatures.
The size is very compact, capable of holding a day or two of drinks depending on how much "Helpdesk" assistance is needed in the field. While the sizing may initially seem too small compared to a traditional cooler that requires ice to maintain its temperature, the entire internal volume can be dedicated to food or drinks inside this unit.
The controls are easy to use, with physical buttons and a display to set or view the unit's temperature. It also has Bluetooth connectivity for some advanced settings via an app. Besides the obvious temperature, the settings are pretty basic.
You can toggle between eco and max power modes for energy conservation and for vehicle use, set voltage cutoff limits so the truck always starts after those long-distance deployments. The noise during operation is minimal, staying completely in the background without drawing attention to itself.
The BODEGAcooler Mini 12V portable freezer offers three ways to power itself. It includes both an AC adapter and a car power outlet adapter. Being rated for 12/24V conditions gives it some flexibility. We enjoyed using the DC power outlet with a portable power station for extended runtime. The unit also supports optional built-in batteries, though we did not receive those for testing. Instead, we used the two battery bays to store extra snacks.
At the time of this article, the BODEGAcooler Mini 12V portable freezer has an MSRP of $359.

Final Thoughts
For IT administrators in a datacenter, the StorageReview IT Helpdesk is a fun way to blow off steam and have fun in a datacenter. Yes, it can also sow fear in the hearts of IT administrators when they calculate the cost of all that equipment under a pile of ice, but can you really put a price on happiness?
For those who want a similar experience without the professional liability, there are other interesting options. The BODEGAcooler Mini 12V P15 portable freezer offers similar capacity, without the need for ice. This allows it to work perfectly in any datacenter you can sneak it into or at a remote work site. It also fits perfectly under a desk.
