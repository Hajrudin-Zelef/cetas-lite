# fr-review-dell-poweredge-xe7740-inside-the-architecture-of-enterprise-ai-inferen-a8c6e9c2

The Dell PowerEdge XE7740 is designed to meet the demands of enterprise inference. Its dual-zone thermal architecture, structured PCIe topology, high-bandwidth memory architecture, and scalable network capacity form a system capable of supporting sustained production workloads. These design choices are not accidental; they reflect an infrastructure model where inference runs continuously, scales predictably, and integrates seamlessly with existing data center operations. In this evaluation, Intel Gaudi 3 demonstrates that the XE7740 delivers optimal inference performance today on dense and multi-expert (MoE) architectures, with predictable scaling behavior and high memory-bound throughput. Software optimizations will continue to improve, but the platform architecture already rests on solid foundations.
More importantly, the XE7740 establishes a sustainable model for enterprise AI infrastructure. Organizations can standardize their infrastructure through a consistent chassis, management stack, and deployment model, while evolving their acceleration strategy over time. As models grow, workloads diversify, and inference becomes more deeply integrated into business operations, the need for stable and adaptable infrastructure will only grow. The PowerEdge XE7740 is designed to meet this evolution. It offers the architectural balance, operational maturity, and expansion headroom needed for enterprise AI, from rapid adoption to long-term integration.
