# fr-review-smarter-media-anywhere-axle-ai-brings-intelligence-to-the-edge-5fd9550f

Traditionally, logging is one of the most laborious and often overlooked aspects of the media capture process. Axle AI automatically logs media with AI-generated scene descriptions, instead of traditional simple metadata fields. This allows the editor to read the content of a clip before browsing through it. It also offers semantic search tools that allow editors and producers to quickly find relevant content through concepts and themes, rather than specific tags.
Scalability is also essential, because not every project requires fast, on-site media production. Axle AI can be installed on a more powerful stationary system, with access to a media company's backlog via a network share. This allows editors and producers to find relevant media more quickly, or even discover media they might not have previously considered and that was "lost" in the archives. Content creators and broadcasters can benefit considerably from this type of backlog index, deriving more value from media that would otherwise remain idle on a server. Although it is not infallible, it can give media companies a head start on some of the most time-consuming aspects of the media creation process.
The HPE ProLiant DL145 Gen11 serves as the hardware foundation for this edge AI deployment, offering an impressive balance between compute density, storage throughput, and GPU acceleration in a compact form factor. Equipped with a low-power 48-core AMD EPYC 8434P processor from the 8004 series, this system is optimized for multithreaded workloads while maintaining a low-power envelope. The configuration includes 256 GB of RAM and a single NVIDIA L4 GPU, a high-performance accelerator perfectly suited to edge AI inference. Storage is provided by six Solidigm D7-PS1010 E3.S SSDs, each with a capacity of 7.68 TB, delivering high-performance flash storage for media assets and metadata-intensive workloads. With support for up to three single-width GPUs and six E3.S drives in total, the DL145 Gen11 is perfectly positioned to scale to demanding video and AI use cases. This configuration underscores the importance of carefully assembling modern hardware components, including the processor, GPU, and SSDs, to enable exceptional performance for edge-focused AI workflows, such as those powered by Axle AI.
Conclusion
Axle AI is transforming the way video content is produced, especially for teams working in real time with limited resources. By combining intelligent media asset management with powerful inference tools, Axle AI delivers a level of automation and responsiveness unmatched by traditional MAM systems. Combined with the HPE ProLiant DL145 Gen11 edge server, the NVIDIA L4 GPU, and Solidigm Gen5 SSDs, the platform we designed provides a compact, high-performance environment optimized for modern media workflows. Whether used in the field or as part of a hybrid editing pipeline, this configuration provides a scalable and efficient way to ingest, tag, search, and distribute video content faster than ever, without compromising quality or control. For teams tasked with staying ahead in a constantly evolving content environment, the combination of edge infrastructure and AI-assisted workflows offers a considerable competitive advantage.
