# fr-review-ubiquiti-sfp-wizard-review-a914f8b3

<!-- source: https://www.storagereview.com/fr/review/ubiquiti-sfp-wizard-review -->

Ubiquiti once again enriches its ecosystem with a compact optical tool designed for network administrators, installers, and laboratory environments. The new SFP Wizard (UACC-SFP-Wizard) is a pocket-sized optical programmer and diagnostic module, designed to simplify the testing, cloning, and management of SFP and QSFP transceivers in the field. The SFP Wizard packs impressive features into a format comparable to a matchbox. It operates completely autonomously thanks to its rechargeable Li-ion battery. It is priced at $49 on the UniFi store, but was out of stock at the time of this review.
This tool stands out for its ability to instantly copy, restore, or reprogram optical module profiles, ensuring universal compatibility with a wide range of SFP, SFP+, SFP28, QSFP+, and QSFP28 transceivers. Additionally, it offers real-time monitoring of DDM parameters, including temperature and optical power. It can even power a module's laser for a live link test, without requiring a switch or router.
The SFP Wizard connects via Bluetooth to the UniFi mobile app (iOS and Android), which continuously receives OTA updates enriching its features. With its comprehensive diagnostics, regularly updated firmware improvements, and compact design, the SFP Wizard is one of Ubiquiti's most practical additions for fiber optic infrastructure management and field validation of optical components.

SFP Wizard: Creation and Design
The Ubiquiti SFP Wizard is a compact and ultra-portable diagnostic and programming tool, designed for network professionals working in the field with fiber optics. Measuring 90.8 x 52.1 x 20.2 mm and weighing only 84 grams, it easily slips into a pocket or tool bag. Its polycarbonate enclosure gives it great durability and optimal lightness, allowing it to withstand the harsh conditions of regular use in data centers and laboratories.
At the center, a 1.9-inch LCD touchscreen provides quick access to module information and real-time diagnostics. The interface remains bright and responsive, even in direct sunlight. Combined with a single power button, its design is clear and intuitive. The layout of the SFP Wizard's components is well thought out. On top, a USB-C port allows for charging and connectivity. Below, Ubiquiti has integrated SFP and QSFP ports, enabling connection of a wide range of transceivers, including SFP, SFP+, SFP28, QSFP+, and QSFP28. This design simplifies testing, reprogramming, and direct validation of modules, without intermediate network hardware.
The device is powered by a rechargeable Li-ion battery rated at 3.7 V and 300 mAh, offering up to 75 minutes of battery life for continuous diagnostics of SFP transceivers with the laser active, or approximately 35 minutes for testing QSFP modules. It recharges via USB-C (5 V DC / 0.5 A) and consumes a maximum of 0.6 W, ensuring prolonged and efficient use.
Connectivity is provided by Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), allowing the SFP Wizard to connect to the UniFi mobile app on iOS and Android devices. This connection enables over-the-air (OTA) firmware updates, module profile management, and real-time diagnostic data viewing.
Designed for professional environments, the SFP Wizard operates within a temperature range of 0 to 40 °C (32 to 104 °F) and a non-condensing relative humidity of 10 to 90%. With its simple two-port configuration, touchscreen interface, and robust construction, it is a practical, field-ready tool for anyone working with optical network equipment.
UniFi SFP Wizard Mobile App
Getting started with the SFP Wizard is simple, and its integration with the UniFi mobile ecosystem is complete. The UniFi Network app is compatible with Android and iOS devices. Once the SFP Wizard is powered on, it becomes discoverable via Bluetooth and ready to be paired.
In the UniFi app, the SFP Wizard appears in the "Available Devices" section. Once the connection is established, you can view information such as the device's MAC address, firmware version, and battery level, giving you a complete overview of its status.
The app also allows you to perform various maintenance tasks, including firmware restoration, collecting diagnostic and support files, and removing the device from the app to restore it to factory settings. Everything is done wirelessly, making setup and management quick and convenient, without requiring a wired connection.
Using the Built-in Interface
The SFP Wizard's LCM touchscreen interface offers a clear and intuitive presentation, prioritizing ease of use. Once the transceiver is inserted into the SFP or QSFP port, three main actions are offered: Write, Copy, and DDM Info. These functions allow you to reprogram the module, duplicate an existing optical profile, or view Digital Diagnostic Monitoring (DDM) data, including temperature, transmit power, and receive power.
Quick controls allow you to enable or disable the laser, simplifying link checks and basic troubleshooting without requiring connection to additional hardware. This feature allows you to confirm optical output or test a transceiver before deployment.
Accessing system settings is just as simple. By swiping your finger from the top to the bottom of the screen, you open the menu to adjust brightness, enable or disable rotation lock, and access other essential options. The interface, minimalist and responsive, is designed for quick access and smooth navigation, even with one hand.
Conclusion
Ubiquiti's SFP Wizard is a compact and capable tool that addresses a real need for network professionals who frequently work with fiber optic equipment. Its portability, ease of use, and well-thought-out software integration make it one of the most practical optical testing and programming tools currently available at this price.
From its compact design to its touchscreen interface, to its two-port configuration, the SFP Wizard seems to have been designed to facilitate diagnostics and configuration in the field. The ability to copy, write, and view DDM data directly on the device, as well as laser control for link validation, considerably optimize its use for troubleshooting and maintenance.
The combination of hardware with the UniFi mobile app further enhances its use, offering users direct access from their phone to device information, firmware updates, and diagnostic tools. The overall experience is simple, efficient, and polished, true to Ubiquiti's goal: to offer professional features in a user-friendly format.
At $49, the SFP Wizard offers excellent value for money for installers and IT professionals alike. This small, compact tool will quickly become indispensable for any network equipment.
