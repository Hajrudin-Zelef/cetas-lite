# fr-review-peakaio-monai-and-solidigm-revolutionizing-storage-for-medical-ai-fc832b01

Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming industry sectors of all types, all around the world. Patient care is at the forefront of this AI revolution in medical practice and research. The integration of AI in healthcare has opened the way to new hopes and possibilities, enabling faster and more accurate diagnoses and treatments by relying on vast amounts of patient data. However, the implementation of AI in this sector is fraught with pitfalls, particularly when it comes to storage. The demand for storage and access to massive datasets, including MRIs, X-rays, test results, and historical patient data, has never been greater.
The modern healthcare ecosystem relies on fast and efficient storage and retrieval of data, to ensure their accessibility to doctors, hospitals, and multiple teams. This data must be timely, accurate, and securely protected to improve patient care and support ongoing medical research. Given the sensitivity and importance of this information, hospitals are increasingly turning to robust, high-capacity storage solutions capable of meeting these requirements. This is where PEAK:AIO, MONAI (Medical Open Network for AI), and Solidigm come into play, offering a trio of technologies designed to revolutionize the way medical data is stored and used.
Who are the players?
Our key players are at the forefront of this intersection between AI and medical research:
- Solidigm: A leader in storage innovation, Solidigm offers some of the largest enterprise-class QLC SSDs on the market. These SSDs are essential for managing the vast amounts of data generated by AI applications in healthcare and offer high density and efficiency at a competitive cost.
- PEAK:AIO: Specializing in high-capacity storage systems suited to medical AI applications, PEAK has made significant progress in this area by integrating Solidigm's QLC SSDs with MONAI's advanced AI tools. Their storage solutions ensure that healthcare institutions can handle large data requirements while maintaining performance and efficiency.
- MONAI: MONAI is an open-source initiative created by King's College London and its partners, including NVIDIA. MONAI is designed to accelerate the development and deployment of AI models in healthcare (particularly in medical imaging), while ensuring data privacy and security.
MONAI and PEAK:AIO: Revolutionizing on-premises AI for hospitals
The MONAI framework has brought about a significant change in hospitals, notably by enabling the seamless integration of AI into clinical workflows. One of the most important advantages of MONAI is its ability to enable hospitals to use pre-trained AI models and further refine them on-site. This capability ensures that patient data can be entered into real-time AI-driven analyses while remaining securely within the hospital's infrastructure.
This approach considerably reduces wait times for radiology technicians and doctors, enabling faster diagnoses and reducing patient anxiety. The technology improves the patient experience through fast and accurate results, offering more effective and efficient care. PEAK:AIO's storage solution underpins the entire process, which relies on Solidigm's dense and efficient QLC SSDs. By ensuring that all operations comply with privacy regulations and guaranteeing that data never leaves the hospital, PEAK:AIO's solution maintains the highest standards of patient confidentiality.
Efficient and small: the ideal solution for all hospitals
Another key advantage of the collaboration between PEAK:AIO and MONAI is the Edge component, which ensures that the solution can be deployed efficiently in any hospital environment. The small footprint of PEAK:AIO storage systems, combined with their energy-efficient design, makes these systems ideal for a wide range of healthcare facilities. Whether in a large metropolitan hospital or a regional clinic, the efficiency of the solution meets the needs of healthcare providers without requiring much space or resources.
This capability allows hospitals to benefit from cutting-edge AI technologies without requiring large-scale infrastructure changes. As a result, PEAK:AIO's solution is scalable and accessible, bringing advanced AI-based healthcare to more patients and improving outcomes at all levels.
Solidigm: Pioneering innovations in storage technology
Solidigm has made considerable progress in the field of storage technology, notably with the launch of its enterprise-class QLC SSDs. Now in their fourth generation, Solidigm's QLC SSDs are among the best-performing on the market, offering unprecedented storage density. The value of QLC technology lies in its ability to store more bits per cell, which translates into higher capacities and better storage capability throughout the device's lifespan. This represents a particularly attractive asset for sectors like healthcare, where data production is continuous and voluminous.
Solidigm's QLC SSDs are not just about capacity: they also offer a favorable performance-per-watt ratio, which is crucial for data centers managing AI workloads and even more so in edge scenarios. The balance between density, performance, and efficiency makes Solidigm's solutions particularly well-suited to medical AI applications, where the need to process and store large amounts of data is constant. These drives have enabled PEAK:AIO to develop storage systems that primarily meet the needs of the healthcare sector.

Our QLC SSDs offer unmatched density and energy efficiency, which are essential for managing the immense data needs of medical AI. Partners like PEAK:AIO play a crucial role in bringing these capabilities to market, enabling institutions to harness the full potential of AI while ensuring data security, scalability, and operational efficiency. Together, we are driving innovations that not only transform data storage but also revolutionize patient care. — Greg Matson, Senior Vice President of Strategic Planning and Marketing at Solidigm
We conducted extensive testing and research on Solidigm QLC devices. These rigorous tests and in-depth analyses provide additional insights into their performance, endurance, and compute capabilities.
How PEAK:AIO uses Solidigm to address these challenges
PEAK:AIO maximizes the use of Solidigm's high-capacity drives, delivering the performance of a high-performance computing (HPC) cluster within a single 2U node. This optimization reduces the need for multiple storage nodes, thereby reducing space, power consumption, and costs while meeting the high-performance requirements of AI workloads. For example, the 160 GB/s performance demonstrated in a single node would previously have required eight nodes and multiple switches to achieve similar results.
As AI projects grow, their storage needs also increase. PEAK:AIO ensures that these requirements are met without significantly increasing physical space or power consumption. This capability is essential for AI projects in resource-constrained environments, such as small hospitals or research laboratories, where scalability and flexibility are crucial for operational continuity.
With the growing adoption of AI in healthcare, the need for data retention and compliance becomes more pressing, especially when considering the ethical implications and potential risks of AI diagnostic errors. The PEAK:ARCHIVE solution, developed with contributions from MONAI developers, directly addresses this need by providing a scalable and efficient method of archiving data, ensuring that it is securely retained while remaining accessible when needed.
