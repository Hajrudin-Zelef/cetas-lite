# fr-review-coolit-5d9994f0

For businesses looking to integrate high-power AI systems into their data centers, liquid cooling is a no-brainer. 8-way GPU servers will move away from air cooling, opting for DLC loops like this one or, at minimum, a closed loop and a radiator. Either way, some amount of liquid will seep into the data center. With substantial electricity savings and modest performance improvement, there are many reasons why businesses should adopt DLC servers.
CoolIT is an undisputed leader in this field and its relationship with Dell brings a wide variety of liquid cooling solutions to market in an easily consumable way, with very few worries. We look forward to exploring our small loop further and are eager to see more liquid-cooled servers in the lab.
