128K 100% Read/Write
Our final test is the 128K 100% read/write test. This test involves large blocks and demonstrates the highest sequential transfer speed the drives can achieve. For SMB performance, the WD Gold 24TB achieved 1.38 GB/s read and 2.3 GB/s write, reaching 2.08 GB/s read and 2.27 GB/s write for iSCSI.
WD Gold 24TB Pricing and Warranty
While specific pricing details may vary depending on the retailer and potential bulk purchase discounts, the WD Gold 24TB enterprise hard drive currently costs $630 in the Western Digital store and comes with a limited five-year warranty.
Conclusion
The WD Gold lineup marks a significant milestone in expanding storage capacity with its 24TB model. Offered exclusively in the SATA interface, this high-capacity drive is designed to meet the rapidly growing need for larger storage solutions in enterprise environments. The 24TB model retains the characteristics that have made the Gold series a reliable choice for businesses, including a helium-filled CMR 7200 RPM SATA design. At the same time, its power efficiency represents reduced power consumption compared to previous models.
Regarding performance, the 24TB Gold hard drive presents a mixed picture in our test suite. During our rigorous testing with eight drives in our standard QNAP NAS configuration, the 24TB model indicated a drop in performance in 4K read tests compared to its 22TB counterpart, where we noted a marked decrease in read speeds and higher latency. The Seagate Exos X24 also outperformed it.
That said, the integration of advanced technologies, competitive pricing, and a solid set of warranties make it a strong choice for businesses considering expanding their storage capabilities. The WD Gold series remains a relevant and reliable option for modern data centers seeking a balance between performance, reliability, and future growth potential.
