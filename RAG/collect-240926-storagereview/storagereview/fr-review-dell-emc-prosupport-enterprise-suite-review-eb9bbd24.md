# fr-review-dell-emc-prosupport-enterprise-suite-review-eb9bbd24

<!-- source: https://www.storagereview.com/fr/review/dell-emc-prosupport-enterprise-suite-review -->

Dell Services launched its ProSupport service in 2008, and the market reaction turned it into an award-winning service with high customer satisfaction. Five years later, Dell EMC launched its ProSupport Enterprise Suite to address the growing complexity of the converged IT environment in enterprises. As the IT landscape evolves with virtualized systems, Big Data, the cloud, and converged infrastructure, the need for enterprise-class support is increased. To meet these needs, Dell EMC offers 3 tiers of support, as well as support for the data center. Enterprise customers can choose from their Basic, ProSupport, or ProSupport Plus plans or select the ProSupport Enterprise suite for the data center, which offers 4 tiers of support.
The primary function of IT is to support the mission-critical workloads of the organization in which they are employed. Instead, IT finds that most of its time and effort is spent on maintenance, troubleshooting, and corrective measures. Dell EMC ProSupport acts as a permanent extension of IT, reducing maintenance costs, maintaining better availability, and using automated proactive and predictive technologies to resolve issues before they arise. The support also works across vendors for software and hardware. A good way to think about it is that rather than outsourcing their IT service, they are only outsourcing the maintenance/corrective action part while the rest remains as it always has been. IT won't have to put out fires on its own, which will free up time to focus on the core business and try new technologies without fear that they will disrupt their infrastructure.
In addition to being an IT support wing and the central point of responsibility, the ProSupport Suite can bring expertise in almost every area. If a company wants to introduce something new into its data center, it will immediately have experts to help it and prevent any form of disaster. This also means that organizations will be able to obtain information and increase their productivity more quickly.
At the heart of the ProSupport Suite is the Technology Service Manager. The technology service manager is a designated Dell EMC support expert who understands the specificities of the company for which they work and its IT needs. This manager will act as a central point of contact for issues and will help find the expertise for anything that comes up. Over time, they will be able to create personalized recommendations and best practices for the individualized environments in which they work. The other benefits of ProSupport are access to engineers to help correct issues, recommendations and predictive analytics, and proactive monitoring. Users can extend this support for up to seven years.
Dell EMC also offers a separate package for the data center, ProSupport One for Data Center. This level of support takes the above and applies it to the complex and changing nature of data centers with more than 1,000 assets. A major difference with this service is that on-site field engineers will be trained on the environment they support and can be called upon if needed.
As part of the StorageReview lab, Dell EMC is a major player. The lab has many of the company's devices in operation, including:
- 12 PowerEdge R730 Servers
- 14 PowerEdge R740xd Servers
- PowerEdge FX2 Platform
- 2 Dell Networking S4048 10GbE switches
- 2 Dell Networking Z9100 100GbE switches
- Dell Networking S3100 switch
- Dell EMC Unity 450f
As part of Dell EMC's support services, our most recent project included a complete overhaul of the lab's networking, replacing older equipment with the S4048, Z9100, and S3100 switches. Although we have thousands of hours of experience with servers and storage platforms, it is true that networking is our weak point of the three. Fortunately, however, the switches all come with Dell ProSupport, which we took full advantage of throughout the network refresh project.
During the deployment of the new Dell EMC network switches, Dell EMC was able to coordinate an on-site representative to assist with the setup and operation of all the switches. This included switch OS updates, proper uplink paths between our labs, and merging the new switches with some of our existing legacy elements. Once this process was complete and the on-site representative had left, we realized that a change needed to be implemented across our entire network regarding per-port settings. This was a bit outside of what we could handle, so we opened a case with Dell ProSupport. What began as a call describing our unconventional configuration and the hope that it would be within the scope of what support could handle turned into a full-service support case. We had a designated representative who walked through all our issues and was ready to take remote control to handle all the changes remotely, port by port.
With the size of our environment, as well as the limited access to the network port of each switch in the configuration we exploited, the process took quite a while, to say the least. We had three days of continuous access to WebEx support with our representative, who stayed with us the entire time. During the process, an additional representative was brought in with extensive VMware experience to help and provide guidance. This level of depth offered by ProSupport is one of Dell EMC's main strengths. One support movement handles software, compute, storage, and networking, and they are all able to leverage the same resources to get a job done. Organizations without as much under the same umbrella may or may not have been able to assign the same resources or resolve issues as quickly as we did. Throughout the process, including completely reversing some changes after the settings clashed with differences between the test lab and the production level, the representatives remained friendly and willing to help without any hesitation. In terms of support experiences at all levels of hardware, consumer or enterprise, Dell ProSupport easily ranks as a fantastic encounter.
The Dell EMC ProSupport Enterprise Suite certainly saved us from an untenable situation, and looking around us, this seems fairly common. ProSupport also helps Dell EMC connect the different pillars of the company; arguing in favor of Dell EMC vertical integration. This may not be intended for everyone, as many organizations turn to vendors for specific solutions, but even in that case, ProSupport was able to help us with a variety of issues that are not entirely related to Dell EMC equipment. Overall, our experience exceeded all expectations and the ProSupport team had a direct impact on the speed at which we deployed the new network.
