# products-comparisons-cisco-meraki-wireless-lan-vs-fortinet-fortigate-e635edb4

Fortinet FortiGate and Cisco Meraki Wireless LAN compete in the network security domain. FortiGate excels in pricing and customization, while Meraki offers extensive features suitable for dynamic environments.
Features: Fortinet FortiGate is valued for its high-performance security capabilities such as firewall protection, intrusion prevention, and VPN support, providing a multi-layered security approach with features like SD-WAN and a user-friendly interface. Cisco Meraki Wireless LAN is known for its advanced cloud management, seamless integration, and strong network visibility, making it ideal for dynamic environments with its feature-rich and easily manageable interface.
Room for Improvement: Fortinet FortiGate could enhance its cloud-based management, streamline the setup process, and expand comprehensive visibility features to match Meraki’s seamless integration approach. Cisco Meraki Wireless LAN, on the other hand, would benefit from more competitive pricing, enhancing customization options, and providing more extensive feature sets without increasing initial investments.
Ease of Deployment and Customer Service: Cisco Meraki Wireless LAN offers straightforward cloud-based deployment, simplifying setup and providing quick operation with responsive tech support and detailed documentation. Fortinet FortiGate involves a more complex on-premise setup, offering detailed configuration options that allow high customization and flexibility, benefiting those who need tailored solutions.
Pricing and ROI: Fortinet FortiGate appeals to organizations with budget constraints due to its lower setup costs, offering significant ROI through robust security features. Cisco Meraki Wireless LAN, despite having a higher initial investment, delivers increased ROI by simplifying network management and reducing operational overhead, appealing to those looking for long-term operational savings.
