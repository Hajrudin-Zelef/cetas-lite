Why You Might Still Pay for Meraki
While UniFi offers significant cost savings, Meraki's premium OpEx model delivers tangible value for specific business requirements. Understanding when the investment makes sense helps businesses choose the right platform for their operational model.
Meraki's Core Value Propositions
24/7 Enterprise Support with SLA Guarantees
Meraki includes phone support with sub-1-hour response times for critical issues. For non-technical business owners or lean IT teams, this "peace of mind" factor often justifies the licensing cost. UniFi relies primarily on community forums and chat support, which may not meet urgent business needs.
Advance Hardware Replacement (Standard)
Meraki ships replacement hardware before you return the failed unit, minimizing downtime. UniFi requires purchasing UI Care (~$49 per device) for equivalent advance replacement service. For businesses where network downtime directly impacts revenue, Meraki's included advance replacement provides significant operational value.
Automated RF Optimization
Meraki's automated channel planning and interference mitigation requires minimal manual configuration. This reduces the technical expertise needed for deployment and ongoing management, particularly valuable for businesses without dedicated network administrators.
Compliance & Audit Requirements
Industries requiring HIPAA, PCI-DSS, or other regulatory compliance benefit from Meraki's detailed audit trails, security certifications, and vendor-backed documentation. These compliance features are built into the platform rather than requiring third-party integration.
When Meraki Makes Financial Sense
Choose Meraki if:
- Your business operates 24/7 and network downtime costs exceed $1,000/hour
- You manage 10+ remote locations requiring centralized zero-touch provisioning
- Compliance requirements mandate vendor-supported infrastructure with SLA guarantees
- Your IT team has high turnover and training simplicity is critical
- Network licensing represents less than 3% of your total IT budget
- You require advanced location analytics or customer presence tracking
Choose UniFi if:
- You have capable internal IT staff or work with a reliable MSP
- Cost optimization is a primary business objective
- You prefer capital expenditure over recurring operational expenses
- Your business can tolerate community-based support for non-critical issues
- You value hardware ownership and long-term equipment lifecycle flexibility
