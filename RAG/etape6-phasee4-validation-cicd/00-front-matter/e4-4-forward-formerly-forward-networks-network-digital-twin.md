# E4.4 — Forward (formerly Forward Networks): network digital twin

## E4.4 — Forward (formerly Forward Networks): network digital twin

### E4.4.1 Company, rebrand, customers

- Forward Networks rebranded to "Forward" (May 2026, Computer Weekly) `[independent]` — https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366643339/Forward-rebrand-brings-further-autonomous-networking-insight.
- Customers: Goldman Sachs, PayPal, S&P Global, IBM, Dell, plus other Fortune 500 enterprises and government agencies `[vendor-reported]` (Computer Weekly, May 2026).
- CEO/co-founder David Erickson; Chief AI Officer/co-founder Nikhil Handigol `[independent]` (Computer Weekly).

### E4.4.2 Forward AI (announced January 2026, GA April 2026)

- "Forward AI" unveiled 29 January 2026 as the "world's first network digital twin" for enterprises `[vendor-reported]` — https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366637819/Forward-Networks-claims-first-network-digital-twin-for-enterprises.
- Concept: agentic AI grounded in a mathematically accurate digital twin; turns human intent into validated network answers; natural-language questions with evidence-backed answers; agentic workflows with inspectable evidence `[vendor-reported]` (same source).
- Included in the Forward Enterprise platform; GA scheduled April 2026 `[vendor-reported]` (same source; GA status as of Sept 2026 not independently re-verified `[unverified]`).
- Forward MCP Server extends the intelligence to custom agents and closed-loop workflows across ServiceNow, Slack, Infoblox `[vendor-reported]` — https://www.forwardnetworks.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/IDC-Spotlight-May-2026.pdf (IDC Spotlight, May 2026).

### E4.4.3 Forward Predict (May 2026)

- Forward Predict: pre-change impact verification — every proposed change runs against the digital twin of the production network before being made, giving "deterministic proof" of effects; positioned as the prerequisite for autonomous networking `[vendor-reported]` — https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366643339/Forward-rebrand-brings-further-autonomous-networking-insight.
- Multi-vendor and multi-cloud coverage: AWS, Azure, GCP, IBM `[vendor-reported]` (IDC Spotlight).

### E4.4.4 Reported customer outcomes

- One mobile operator (unnamed; TMN understands engagements with Vodafone and Verizon): 70% faster deployment velocity, 33% reduction in unplanned downtime; another customer calculated $11M savings from change-management/automation outage elimination `[vendor-reported]` — https://the-mobile-network.com/2026/01/forward-networks-launches-digital-twin-hails-mobile-operator-success/.

### E4.4.5 NetBox ecosystem

- Forward Networks is a NetBox Labs discovery/reconciliation partner (announced January 2026) alongside IP Fabric, SuzieQ, Slurp'it `[official]` — https://netboxlabs.com/blog/netbox-labs-stardust-systems-network-observability-partnership/.

### E4.4.6 Gaps

- Pricing not public `[unverified]`.
- "Mathematically accurate digital twin" is a vendor marketing claim; no independent formal verification found `[unverified]`.
- Forward AI GA status (April 2026 planned) not re-verified as of September 2026 `[unverified]`.

## E4.5 — Cisco Crosswork and other commercial assurance

### E4.5.1 Cisco Crosswork portfolio

- Cisco Crosswork Network Automation portfolio (launched 2018, service-provider oriented): Change Automation (large-scale change, closed-loop control), Health Insights (smart sensors/alerts/remediation), Data Platform (open-source + commercial data analytics), Network Insights (cloud analytics for large-scale routing issues), Situation Manager (ML-based event correlation with social/ChatOps tooling) `[official]` — https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2018/m02/cisco-leadership-in-automation-continues-with-new-network-automation-portfolio-for-service-providers.html.
- Built on Cisco NSO (Network Services Orchestrator) and WAE (WAN Automation Engine); multi-vendor, multi-domain closed-loop automation with open APIs `[official]` — https://blogs.cisco.com/news/cisco-crosswork-network-automation-the-industrys-most-comprehensive-closed-loop-automation-solution.
- 2026 status: no 2026-specific Crosswork announcements surfaced in research; portfolio appears mature/stable in service-provider segment `[unverified]` — treat current packaging as subject to Cisco's 2026 portfolio updates.

- Cisco NSO underpins Crosswork's orchestration layer; intent-driven deployments with automatic validation `[official]` (Cisco blogs, same as above).
- NSO deep-dive is out of scope for E4 (belongs to orchestration/automation, partially covered in Phase E2).
