# 3.3 10Gtek

### 3.3 10Gtek

- 10Gtek product roadmap extends through 400G; company claims an in-house compatibility lab and a proprietary coding method `[vendor-reported]` — https://cn.10gtek.com/storage/document/20210402/20210402111918_60668d36d1ffe.pdf
- Compatibility list includes Cisco, HPE, Huawei, Mellanox, Arista, Juniper, Intel, Extreme, Brocade, Dell, Lenovo, Nokia, Edgecore, and others `[vendor-reported]` — same roadmap PDF.
- Historical coding-surcharges and "encrypted coding" characterizations (see §1.4) are dated vendor claims, not universal current rules `[vendor-reported]` — https://cn.10gtek.com/new-905
