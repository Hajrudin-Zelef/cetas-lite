# 5. June 2026

## 5. June 2026

**2026-06-11 — FIFA World Cup 2026 opens.** Mexico played the opening match at the
Estadio Azteca in Mexico City. On June 12, Canada opened at BMO Field in Toronto and
the United States at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood. The 23rd World Cup was the first
hosted by three countries (Canada, Mexico, United States), the first with 48 teams
(12 groups of four; top two plus the eight best third-place teams advancing to a new
round of 32), and the first to total 104 matches. `[official][secondary]`

**2026-06-13 — NBA Finals, Game 5: Knicks 94, Spurs 90.** The New York Knicks closed
out the San Antonio Spurs 4–1 to win the 2026 NBA championship — the franchise's
first title since 1973, ending a 53-year wait. Jalen Brunson scored 45 points in the
clincher and was named Finals MVP. ESPN's post-title analysis credited the
Brunson–Karl-Anthony Towns–Josh Hart core and a path in which New York outlasted the
East. `[independent]`

**2026-06-23 — NBA Draft.** The Washington Wizards selected BYU forward AJ Dybantsa
first overall, the prize of a draft class described as one of the best in recent
memory. Utah took Kansas guard Darryn Peterson second; Memphis took Duke forward
Cameron Boozer third; Chicago picked fourth. The first 14 picks, with trades applied:
1 Washington, 2 Utah, 3 Memphis, 4 Chicago, 5 LA Clippers (via Indiana), 6 Brooklyn,
7 Sacramento, 8 Atlanta (via New Orleans), 9 Dallas, 10 Milwaukee, 11 Golden State,
12 Oklahoma City (via LA Clippers), 13 Miami, 14 Charlotte. `[independent]`

**June 24–27 — World Cup group stage ends; June 28 — round of 32 begins.** Group
winners included Mexico (A), Switzerland (B), Brazil (C), the United States (D),
Germany (E), the Netherlands (F), Belgium (G), Spain (H), France (I), Argentina (J),
Colombia (K) and England (L). Notable round-of-32 pairings: Mexico vs Ecuador,
Switzerland vs Algeria, United States vs Bosnia and Herzegovina, Germany vs Paraguay,
Belgium vs Senegal, France vs Sweden, Colombia vs Ghana, England vs DR Congo. `[secondary]`

**2026-06-29 — Wimbledon begins (runs to July 12).** The 139th Championships at the All
England Club carried £64.2m in prize money. Defending champion Jannik Sinner was the
men's top seed; Carlos Alcaraz was absent with a wrist injury and Britain's Jack
Draper withdrew with a recurrence of an arm injury. `[independent][secondary]`

**NHL.** The Stanley Cup playoffs concluded in June; the 2026 champion was not
re-verified in this pass. `[unverified]`
