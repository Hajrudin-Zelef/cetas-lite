# 15. Sports games and product releases

## 15. Sports games and product releases

**F1 26 video game — cancelled.** In a surprise announcement (November 18–19, 2025),
EA Sports and Codemasters confirmed there would be **no standalone F1 26 game** — the
first break in the annual release cycle since Codemasters began making F1 games in
2009. Instead, F1 25 (released May 30, 2025 for PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC) receives
a paid expansion carrying the 2026 season's cars, teams, drivers and sporting
regulations; the Madrid street circuit's inclusion was unconfirmed at announcement.
EA framed it as a "strategic reset": a fully new game, described as looking, feeling
and playing differently, is targeted for 2027. The decision followed Codemasters
layoffs and redeployments within EA Sports and Nacon's takeover of the WRC license
from EA/Codemasters. `[independent]`

**EA Sports FC 26.** Released worldwide September 26, 2025 (early access September 19
via the Ultimate Edition) — just before this file's coverage window, noted here as
context. Standard Edition $69.99/£69.99; Ultimate Edition $99.99/£99.99; platforms
PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, PC, Amazon Luna, Nintendo Switch and Switch 2
(first franchise appearance on Switch 2). Cover star Zlatan Ibrahimović featured as a
new Icon. The game faced new competition from the French-made *Rematch* (five million
players in its first month) and the mobile *FIFA Rivals*. Announcements for the next
edition (FC 27, expected September 2026) were not re-verified at cutoff. `[secondary]`

**NBA 2K26.** Released September 2025 (outside the window); noted as context only.
Pre-launch marketing for NBA 2K27 (expected September 2026) was not re-verified at
cutoff. `[unverified]`

**Kits, balls and gear.** The official 2026 World Cup match ball, tournament kits and
the new-season club kits (2026-27) launched across the summer; specific product SKUs
and prices were not re-verified in this pass. `[unverified]`

## 17. Glossary

- **AET** — after extra time.
- **DPOY** — Defensive Player of the Year (NBA).
- **R32 / R16** — round of 32 / round of 16 (World Cup knockout rounds).
- **Golden Boot / Ball / Glove** — World Cup awards: top scorer / best player / best goalkeeper.
- **EL / UECL** — UEFA Europa League / UEFA Conference League.
- **Lottery odds** — NBA draft lottery probabilities; the three worst-record teams each held 14.0% odds in 2026.
- **Protected pick** — a traded draft pick that stays with its original team if it falls within a protected range (Washington's and Utah's 2026 picks carried top-eight protection).
- **Deadline day** — the final day of a transfer window (September 1, 2026, for England and Spain).
- **Hydration breaks** — mandatory three-minute pauses per half introduced at the 2026 World Cup.
- **Mouth-covering red card** — 2026 World Cup rule: automatic sending-off for covering the mouth while confronting an opponent.
