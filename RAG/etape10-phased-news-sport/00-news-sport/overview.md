## 0. Scope, method and provenance

This file is the sports track of Step 10. It covers the final sports-news phase of the project:
the FIFA World Cup 2026 (won by Spain), the 2025-26 European club season and the summer
2026 transfer window, the 2025-26 NBA season and 2026 NBA Draft, the Milano Cortina 2026
Winter Olympics, the four tennis Grand Slams, the 2026 Formula 1 season up to the
2026-09-16 standings (no champion named — the season was not finished at cutoff),
cycling, athletics, the Super Bowl LX and selected other events, plus sports video-game
releases.

**How it was researched.** Public web search on 2026-09-22, cross-checked across
independent outlets wherever possible. Every factual claim carries a provenance tag:

- `[official]` — the governing body or rights holder (FIFA, UEFA, NBA, ATP Tour, usopen.org, formula1.com, EA).
- `[vendor-reported]` — a rights-holding broadcaster or club reporting on its own event.
- `[independent]` — reputable independent press (ESPN, Reuters, AP, BBC, The Guardian, USA Today, Fox Sports, SI, Sky Sports).
- `[secondary]` — aggregators, Wikipedia, blogs and data pages; fact-checked but not primary.
- `[unverified]` — a claim that could not be corroborated in this research pass and must not be treated as settled.

**Rules applied.** No invented scores, winners, transfers, fees, records, dates or URLs.
Where sources conflict, both figures are given and the conflict is logged in
Section 13 ("Conflicts and gaps"). Lines are dense and chronological; month sections
give the narrative, per-sport deep dives give the tables.
