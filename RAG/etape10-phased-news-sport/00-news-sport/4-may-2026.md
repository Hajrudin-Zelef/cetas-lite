# 4. May 2026

## 4. May 2026

**2026-05-10 — NBA draft lottery (Chicago).** In what was reported as likely the final
year of the current lottery system, the Washington Wizards — 17–65, the league's worst
record, with 14.0% odds — won the No. 1 overall pick, the first time since 2019 the
worst-record team actually drew the top pick. Utah (tied for the fourth-best odds)
jumped to No. 2, Memphis (sixth-best odds) to No. 3, Chicago (ninth-best odds) to No. 4.
Indiana and Brooklyn, the other two 14.0%-odds teams, fell to fifth and sixth;
Indiana's pick at No. 5 went to the LA Clippers as part of the February Ivica Zubac
trade, with Indiana instead recovering its 2031 pick. Commissioner Adam Silver had
fined the Utah Jazz $500,000 and the Indiana Pacers $100,000 during the season for
conduct described as detrimental to the league amid aggressive tanking. `[independent][secondary]`

**2026-05-10 — Barcelona clinch La Liga.** Barcelona secured the Spanish title,
finishing ahead of Real Madrid; the clinching date of May 10 is reported by season
summaries. `[secondary]`

**2026-05-13 — PSG clinch Ligue 1.** Paris Saint-Germain sealed a fifth consecutive
French title — the club's 14th — with a 2–0 win over second-placed Lens: Khvicha
Kvaratskhelia scored in the first half and Ibrahim Mbaye added the second after the
break. Final table: PSG 76, Lens 70, Lille 61, Lyon 60, Marseille 59. Angers forward
Esteban Lepaul finished as the league's top scorer with 21 goals. Paris FC were
promoted, returning to the top flight after 46 years. Lens won the Coupe de France,
so the Europa League place attached to the cup passed down the league table. `[independent]`

**2026-05-20 — UEFA Europa League final (Beşiktaş Stadium, Istanbul).** Aston Villa
defeated SC Freiburg 3–0 to win their first Europa League title, their second European
trophy after the 1982 European Cup, and their first trophy of any kind in 30 years
(since the 1996 League Cup). Youri Tielemans volleyed Villa ahead late in the first
half from a corner routine, Emiliano Buendía curled in a sublime second seconds before
half-time, and Morgan Rogers sealed it with 30 minutes left. Buendía was named Man of
the Match; referee François Letexier officiated before 37,324 spectators. Villa had
finished second in the league phase and beaten Lille, Bologna and Nottingham Forest
in the knockouts — overturning a first-leg defeat with a 4–0 home win over Forest in
the semifinal. Unai Emery won his fifth Europa League (Sevilla 2014, 2015, 2016;
Villarreal 2021), the most by any manager. Joint top scorers: Igor Jesus (Nottingham
Forest) and Petar Stanić (Ludogorets), 7 goals each; best player Morgan Rogers; best
young player Johan Manzambi (Freiburg). As winners, Villa earned a 2026 UEFA Super Cup
meeting with PSG — and because Villa had already qualified for the 2026-27 Champions
League through the league, the title-holder's league-phase spot was vacated and the
access list rebalanced. `[independent][secondary]`

**2026-05-24 → 2026-06-07 — Roland-Garros (Paris).** Alexander Zverev won his first
Grand Slam, defeating Flavio Cobolli 6–1, 4–6, 6–4, 6–7(5–7), 6–1 in the men's final.
Mirra Andreeva won the women's title, beating Maja Chwalińska 6–3, 6–2 — at 19, the
youngest Roland-Garros women's champion since Monica Seles in 1992. Doubles champions:
Marcel Granollers / Horacio Zeballos (men), Kateřina Siniaková / Taylor Townsend
(women), Sara Errani / Andrea Vavassori (mixed). `[independent][secondary]`

**2026-05-27 — UEFA Conference League final (Red Bull Arena, Leipzig).** Crystal
Palace defeated Rayo Vallecano 1–0 to win their first European trophy in their debut
continental campaign. Jean-Philippe Mateta tapped in a 51st-minute rebound after Adam
Wharton's shot was parried by Augusto Batalla; Yéremy Pino hit both posts with a free
kick. It was the perfect farewell for manager Oliver Glasner, who had announced in
January he would leave at season's end — his third trophy in two years after the 2025
FA Cup and the Community Shield, the most successful period in the club's 120-year
history. Palace had beaten Shakhtar Donetsk 5–2 on aggregate in the semifinal; Rayo,
the surprise package, had beaten Strasbourg 2–0 on aggregate. Palace finished only
15th in the Premier League but banked about £21.5m in total prize money and qualified
for the 2026-27 Europa League — the competition they had originally qualified for
before being demoted to the Conference League over multi-club ownership rules, with
Nottingham Forest taking their place. Chairman Steve Parish publicly framed it as
vindication. Palace became the third London club to win the competition after West Ham
United and Chelsea. `[independent]`

**2026-05-30 — UEFA Champions League final (Budapest).** Paris Saint-Germain defeated
Arsenal 4–3 on penalties after a 1–1 draw to retain the European Cup. PSG became only
the second club to defend the title in the Champions League era. Kylian Mbappé
finished as the tournament's top scorer with 15 goals. `[secondary]`

**May domestic champions.** Arsenal were confirmed as Premier League champions,
Inter Milan as Serie A champions and Bayern Munich as Bundesliga champions; with
Barcelona (La Liga) and PSG (Ligue 1) that completed the five major European league
winners for 2025-26. `[secondary]`

**NBA conference finals (May).** In the East, the New York Knicks advanced to the NBA
Finals; in the West, the San Antonio Spurs advanced. The 2025-26 MVP was announced on
May 17 on Amazon Prime: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, his second straight, beating
finalists Nikola Jokić and Victor Wembanyama. Bam Adebayo was named Social Justice
Champion (May 22); Joe Mazzulla was named Coach of the Year (May 26). `[independent]`
