## 13. Deep dive — tennis 2026 (Grand Slams)

**Season summary.** Four different men contested the four finals; Alexander Zverev won
two majors (Roland-Garros, US Open), Jannik Sinner defended Wimbledon, and Carlos
Alcaraz completed the career Grand Slam at the Australian Open. Elena Rybakina won two
of the three women's majors held by cutoff (Australian Open, US Open); Mirra Andreeva
won Roland-Garros and Linda Nosková won Wimbledon. Kateřina Siniaková won three of
the four women's doubles majors, partnering Taylor Townsend at Roland-Garros and the
US Open — the pair completing a career Grand Slam together at Flushing Meadows. `[independent][secondary]`
