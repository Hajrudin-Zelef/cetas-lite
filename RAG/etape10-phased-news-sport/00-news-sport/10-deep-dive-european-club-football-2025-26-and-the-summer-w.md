**UEFA Champions League.** Final: PSG 4–3 on penalties vs Arsenal (1–1 AET), May 30,
2026, Budapest. PSG retained the trophy — the second successful defense of the
Champions League era — with Kylian Mbappé the tournament's top scorer on 15 goals. `[secondary]`

**Europa League.** Final: Aston Villa 3–0 SC Freiburg, May 20, 2026, Istanbul. Villa's
first European trophy since the 1982 European Cup and first trophy in 30 years; Unai
Emery's record fifth title in the competition. `[independent]`

Palace's first European trophy; Oliver Glasner's farewell; Europa League qualification
for 2026-27. `[independent]`
