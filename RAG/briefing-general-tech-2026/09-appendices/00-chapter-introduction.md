## 10. Appendices

> The appendices gather the reference material for the whole dossier: a glossary of the technical terms used across chapters, an index of the companies, labs and institutions that appear in the February–September 2026 narrative, and the methodological notes that document how every sensitive claim was verified. Definitions are generic and deliberately conservative — no invented specifications, no vendor figures smuggled into a definition.
