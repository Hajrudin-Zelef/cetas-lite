# Pricing, availability and market forecasts

<a id="g07-5"></a>
### 7.5 Pricing, availability and market forecasts

The commercial facts are crisp. The iPhone Duo starts at $1,999 for 256 GB of storage and
rises through the storage tiers to $3,199 for the 2 TB configuration. Preorders open on
October 16, 2026; the device goes on sale on October 23, 2026, in more than 70 countries.
All of this is announced and planned — as of this dossier's September 22 cutoff, no unit has
been sold to a consumer, no queue has formed, and no sellout has been reported. Every sales
figure in this section is therefore a forecast, and is labeled as one.

The most-cited forecast comes from TrendForce, published September 10, 2026, under the
headline "iPhone Duo Expected to Capture Nearly 25% of Foldable Smartphone Market in 2026."
The numbers: approximately 5 million iPhone Duo units shipped in 2026, representing a 24.8%
share of the foldable smartphone market — good for second place among brands. But the
headline's "nearly 25%" needs its full context, because the second-place position is not
Apple's alone. TrendForce has Apple tied with Huawei: roughly 5 million units and 24.8%
each. Samsung leads outright at approximately 7.1 million units and 35.1%. The total 2026
foldable market, in this projection, is approximately 20.2 million units — up just 0.5%
year over year.

That last figure reframes the whole story. A 0.5% market growth rate means Apple is not
entering a booming category; it is entering a stagnant one and being forecast to take a
quarter of it almost immediately, largely by converting its own installed base rather than
expanding the pie. The forecast's internal logic is worth spelling out: 5 million units at
an average price near $2,400 is roughly $12 billion in implied first-year revenue — illustrative arithmetic combining two forecasters’ figures, not a published estimate. Apple's quarter-share by units understates its
share by value, because the Duo's average selling price sits well above the category mean.

Several caveats attach to any forecast built ten days after an announcement. TrendForce's
model necessarily assumes the October 23 launch holds, that supply meets demand through the
holiday quarter, and that the $1,999 price does not suppress conversion among the iPhone
installed base. None of these is guaranteed — foldables have a history of supply-constrained
launches, and the Duo's 254-gram weight and missing Face ID are precisely the kind of
details that can dampen enthusiasm between announcement and purchase. Conversely, the
forecast may understate Apple's advantage: no other foldable maker can market to the iPhone installed base with trade-in offers, carrier financing, and same-day in-store
pickup. The honest summary is that 24.8% is a credible central case, not a promise.

What the forecast does not include is equally instructive. There is no verified data on
preorder volumes — Apple does not disclose them — and no analyst has published a sell-through
model for the 70+ country launch beyond the TrendForce shipment estimate. The first hard
data will arrive with Apple's December-quarter results and with the supply-chain reporting
that typically follows a major launch. Until then, the Duo's commercial story is a launch
plan and a projection, and this dossier treats it as exactly that.

The pricing ladder, with an honesty note about what is and is not verified:

| Configuration | Price | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| 256 GB (entry) | $1,999 | Confirmed |
| Intermediate tiers | — | Apple announced a range; per-tier prices between 256 GB and 2 TB were not among the verified facts at cutoff |
| 2 TB (top) | $3,199 | Confirmed |

The $1,200 spread between entry and top is itself information: Apple is
treating storage as the primary upsell lever on the Duo, consistent with a
device positioned as phone-plus-tablet, where the tablet half of the promise
(all-day media, large-canvas work) rewards capacity. What the ladder does
not show — because no verified source showed it — is how steeply each
intermediate step climbs. That granularity becomes public on October 16,
when preorders open.

The launch calendar, all dates announced/planned as of September 22:

| Milestone | Date | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Preorders open | 16/10/2026 | Apple's direct and carrier channels, per the announcement |
| On sale | 23/10/2026 | 70+ countries |
| First hard sales data | December-quarter reporting and subsequent supply-chain coverage | Not available at cutoff |

Three caveats define what could move the TrendForce number: the October 23
launch holding (a slip compresses the selling window); supply meeting demand
through the holiday quarter (on a titanium foldable with a novel display
stack, yields — not demand — may set the ceiling); and the Duo's compromises
(254 g, no Face ID, $1,999) not denting installed-base conversion. Each
assumption is plausible; none is guaranteed.

What to watch: October 16 preorder velocity, October 23 launch-day
availability across the 70+ countries, and the December-quarter unit
disclosures — the first hard data on whether "nearly 25%" was conservative
or ambitious.
