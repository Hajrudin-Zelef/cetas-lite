# Networking and optics — introduction

## 6. Networking and optics

This chapter covers the physical layer of the 2026 build-out: the networking and
optics story that made the AI data-center boom actually move bits. 2026 was the
year 1.6-terabit Ethernet crossed from sampling into shipment — with Nvidia and
Google already integrating, Meta and Oracle following in 2027 — and the year the
supply chain around it (transceivers, DSPs, coherent pluggables, co-packaged and
linear optics) accelerated in lockstep. The September signal cluster is unusually
dense: FS.com's AI-networking press release (September 7), the OIF 1600ZR
Implementation Agreement (September 9), the Credo ZeroFlap 1.6T launch
(September 15), the Telxius–Nokia 800G deployment (September 15), MACOM's ECOC
demos announcement (September 15), ECOC 2026 itself opening in Malaga on
September 21, and FS.com's Wi-Fi 7 campus announcement the same day. The chapter
also reads the caveats honestly: vendor self-claims are labeled as such, ECOC
was still ongoing on September 22 (no show reports available yet), and guidance
is never presented as booked revenue.
