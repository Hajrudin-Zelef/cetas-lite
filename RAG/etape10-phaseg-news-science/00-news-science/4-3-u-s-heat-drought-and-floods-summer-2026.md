## 5. Deep dive — Health & medicine 2026

### 5.1 Alzheimer's: the cancer-drug connection

- **April 2026, *Cell*** (Walsh group, Boston Children's/Harvard/Broad): deep (>1,000×) sequencing of **311 brain samples** finds somatic single-nucleotide variants in **cancer-driver genes enriched in Alzheimer's microglia-like cells**, associated with clonal expansion and a hematopoietic origin — "to some extent, Alzheimer's disease is a little like cancer" [secondary] — https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-04-unexpected-cancer-mutations-brain-immune.html ; https://neurosciencenews.com/alzheimers-cancer-mutation-microglia-30565/
- **July 2026, *Nature*** (Jaiswal group): bone-marrow-derived immune cells **routinely cross the blood-brain barrier during aging** and become indistinguishable from resident microglia — reframing whether CHIP (clonal hematopoiesis) mutations protect or promote Alzheimer's depending on clone size [secondary] — https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/puzzling-cancer-mutation-may-lower-alzheimers-disease-risk-2026a1000szy
- **Repurposed cancer drugs** (letrozole + irinotecan): UCSF/Gladstone computational screen of 1,300 drugs → mouse-model combination reversed amyloid/tau pathology and restored memory (2025 papers); human clinical trials anticipated, not yet reported in 2026 window [secondary] — https://www.psypost.org/repurposed-cancer-drugs-show-promise-as-combination-therapy-for-alzheimers-disease/
- **Phase III monoclonal-antibody** anti-amyloid trials reported significant slowing of cognitive decline vs placebo (podcast summary of 2026 pharma news; trial names not captured) [secondary] — https://podscan.fm/podcasts/pharma-and-biotech-daily/episodes/gene-therapy-breakthroughs-and-fda-accelerations-transforming-pharma

### 5.2 Avian influenza: watchful, not alarming

- **May 18, 2026** joint FAO/WHO/WOAH assessment: global public-health risk from HPAI A(H5) **low, unchanged**; occupational risk low-to-moderate; no sustained human-to-human transmission; viruses remain avian-adapted [official] — https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/influenza/human-animal-interface-risk-assessments/updated-joint-fao-who-woah-public-health-assessment-of-recent-high-pathogenicity-avian-influenza-a(h5)-virus-events-in-animals-and-people--18-may-2026.pdf?sfvr
- **Aug 26–27, 2026** PAHO update: Americas animal outbreaks **down 25.7%** (455 outbreaks Jan–Jul 15; mammals −65%); **no human cases in the Americas in 2026** (last: Nov 2025); globally **9 human cases / 2 deaths** in 2026 through Aug 7 (1,002 cases / 479 deaths since 2003) [official] — https://www.paho.org/en/news/27-8-2026-avian-influenza-americas-animal-outbreaks-decline-paho-calls-continued-vigilance
