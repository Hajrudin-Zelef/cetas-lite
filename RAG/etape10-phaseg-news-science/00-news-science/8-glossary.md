# 8. Glossary

## 8. Glossary

- **ACE (Accumulated Cyclone Energy)** — integrated measure of tropical-storm strength × duration; 2026 stood at 6.2, 93% below normal.
- **ApJ Letters** — *The Astrophysical Journal Letters*, rapid-publication astronomy journal.
- **Artemis II/III/IV** — NASA's crewed lunar-program missions; II = 2026 lunar flyby; III = redefined 2027 LEO docking demo; IV = 2028 landing target.
- **Berkeley Earth** — independent climate-data group; corroborated hottest-June-2026.
- **Block 3 / V3** — third Starship vehicle generation (Raptor 3, taller, ~100 t LEO).
- **CHIP** — clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential; age-related blood-stem-cell mutations.
- **CONUS** — contiguous United States.
- **Copernicus C3S** — EU climate monitoring service.
- **COSMOS field** — well-studied extragalactic survey region in Sextans.
- **ECLSS** — Environmental Control and Life Support System (Orion).
- **HPAI A(H5)** — highly pathogenic avian influenza A(H5) viruses.
- **K2-18b** — sub-Neptune exoplanet, "Hycean world" biosignature candidate.
- **LC-39B** — Launch Complex 39B, Kennedy Space Center.
- **LSST** — Legacy Survey of Space and Time (Rubin Observatory, 10 years from June 2026).
- **Mengzhou** — China's next-generation crewed spacecraft.
- **Net facility gain** — fusion plant producing more energy than stored/consumed by the whole facility (never yet demonstrated).
- **NHC** — U.S. National Hurricane Center.
- **PAHO** — Pan American Health Organization.
- **PHEIC** — public health emergency of international concern (WHO).
- **RPOD** — Rendezvous Proximity Operations Demonstration (Artemis II).
- **SLS** — Space Launch System (NASA heavy rocket).
- **SPARC** — Commonwealth Fusion Systems' compact high-field tokamak.
- **SST** — sea-surface temperature.
- **Tiangong** — China Space Station.
- **Tianwen-2** — Chinese asteroid-sample-return mission (target: 2016 HO3).
- **Virtual shot** — high-fidelity simulation of a fusion target (Inertia, Sept 2026).
- **WOAH** — World Organisation for Animal Health.
- **Artemis I** — uncrewed SLS/Orion lunar test flight (Nov–Dec 2022); heat-shield ablation findings fed Artemis II prep.
- **Berkeley Earth** — independent climate-data group; corroborated hottest-June-2026.
- **CaSSIS** — Colour and Stereo Surface Imaging System (ExoMars TGO camera).
- **COSMOS field** — well-studied extragalactic survey region in Sextans.
- **Cryo system at 8 K** — SPARC's full superconducting-magnet cooling loop milestone (Sept 2026).
- **Dodger Dog satellites** — Starship Flight 12's two inspection satellites that imaged Ship 39's heat shield from orbit.
- **ECLSS** — Environmental Control and Life Support System (Orion).
- **Far-side flyby** — Artemis II's lunar trajectory (no landing).
- **Free-return trajectory** — lunar trajectory that loops back to Earth without major burns.
- **Hohlraum** — gold/depleted-uranium (or lead, in Inertia's design) cavity in laser-fusion targets.
- **HRSC** — High Resolution Stereo Camera (Mars Express).
- **Hycean world** — hydrogen-rich exoplanet with possible water ocean (e.g., K2-18b).
- **ILRS** — International Lunar Research Station (China-led lunar-base program).
- **Inertial confinement** — fusion approach compressing fuel pellets with lasers (NIF, Inertia).
- **ISO** — interstellar object (1I/'Oumuamua, 2I/Borisov, 3I/ATLAS).
- **K2-18b** — sub-Neptune exoplanet, "Hycean world" biosignature candidate.
- **LC-39B** — Launch Complex 39B, Kennedy Space Center.
