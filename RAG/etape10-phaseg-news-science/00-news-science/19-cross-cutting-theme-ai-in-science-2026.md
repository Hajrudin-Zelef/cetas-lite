## 21. Starship Flight 12 engineering dossier (May 22, 2026)

The most instrumented Starship test to date — details from secondary trackers and SpaceX-reported data:

- **Attempt history**: May 21 scrubbed (tower-hydraulic pin / propellant-densification issue); May 22 liftoff **22:30 UTC** (6:30 p.m. ET) [secondary] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starship_flight_test_12
- **Trajectory choice**: southerly track so that any debris from the intentionally high-stress profile falls over open Caribbean water [secondary] — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starship_flight_test_12
- **Booster 18**: first stage with the flight computer placed **inside the booster aft section**; three-center-Raptor landing burn demonstrated **mid-air engine relight** — the maneuver the catch system depends on [secondary] — https://www.tesery.com/blogs/news/a-goal-for-humanity-the-full-story-of-spacexs-epic-starship-flight-12
- **Ship 39**: payload bay carried **20 Starlink V3 mass simulators** (dummy satellite masses) + **2 inspection satellites** ("Dodger Dogs") that imaged the heat shield in orbit — first orbital inspection of a reentering Starship [secondary] — https://www.tesery.com/blogs/news/a-goal-for-humanity-the-full-story-of-spacexs-epic-starship-flight-12
- **Reentry**: 13 of 39 Raptor 3 engines relit for the braking burn; **controlled ocean splashdown** in the Indian Ocean [secondary] — https://www.tesery.com/blogs/news/a-goal-for-humanity-the-full-story-of-spacexs-epic-starship-flight-12
- **The recurring failure mode** (independent analysis): after the flip maneuver, **partial booster engine-ignition failures** on Flights 7, 8, 9, and 12 — attitude rotation → incomplete thrust vector → loss of control authority; the program's critical path to catching [secondary] — https://github.com/ericrenone/orbital-cordic-dual-mode-geometry-native-hardware-for-the-orbital-ai-era
- **Why Flight 12 matters**: it validated the V3/Raptor-3 architecture (the design intended to close the business case: 100 t to LEO, rapid reuse) even though the booster catch was sacrificed — Musk called it "epic" [secondary] — https://www.tesery.com/blogs/news/a-goal-for-humanity-the-full-story-of-spacexs-epic-starship-flight-12
