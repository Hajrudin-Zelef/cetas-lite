## 4. Deep dive — Climate & extreme weather 2026

### 4.1 The temperature record, month by month

- **Pre-year forecasts** (Dec 2025 / Jan 2026): UK Met Office projected **1.34–1.58 °C** above pre-industrial for 2026 — among the four hottest years since 1850, the **fourth consecutive year above 1.4 °C**; central estimate slightly cooler than 2024's 1.55 °C record. Adam Scaife: "the last three years are all likely to have exceeded 1.4 °C" — before this surge, no year had passed 1.3 °C [independent] — https://news.slashdot.org/story/25/12/18/1716218/2026-will-bring-heat-more-than-14c-above-preindustrial-levels-uk-met-office-says?sdsrc=nextbtmprev ; https://sigmaearth.com/met-office-warns-2026-likely-to-be-more-than-1-4c-warmer-than-preindustrial-levels/
- **January**: 5th warmest Jan (13.23 °C; Europe's coldest January since 2010 — weather ≠ climate) [secondary] — https://natureweatherhub.com/hottest-year-record-2026-climate-data/
- **February**: 5th warmest Feb (13.26 °C) [secondary] — https://natureweatherhub.com/hottest-year-record-2026-climate-data/
- **May**: **2nd warmest May** (15.81 °C); extra-polar SST 20.90 °C, 2nd highest for any May [secondary] — https://natureweatherhub.com/hottest-year-record-2026-climate-data/
- **June**: **hottest June in NOAA's 174-year record**, corroborated by NASA, Berkeley Earth, Copernicus [secondary] — https://natureweatherhub.com/hottest-year-record-2026-climate-data/
- **August**: **hottest month ever recorded globally**, tying July 2023; June–August the warmest northern summer on record, tying 2024 [secondary] — https://balancedweather.substack.com/p/2026-was-hottest-summer-on-record
- **Through August**: 2026 running at **+1.22 °C** (baseline dataset, secondary blog) vs 2024's +1.29 °C — i.e., **on track for one of the hottest years on record**, potentially challenging 2024 depending on the rest of the year. A looming **El Niño** (possibly "super" El Niño) threatens **record heat in 2027** [secondary] — https://southfloridareporter.com/earth-on-track-for-hottest-year-on-record-in-2026-as-looming-super-el-nino-threatens-2027/ ; https://climatedata.ca/news/2026-likely-to-be-among-the-four-hottest-years-on-record/
- **ENSO context**: weak La Niña (Dec 2024) cooled 2025; return to neutral in 2026 with El Niño possible later in the year [secondary] — https://climatedata.ca/news/2026-likely-to-be-among-the-four-hottest-years-on-record/
- **Five-year outlook**: ECCC projects **2026–2030 as the hottest five-year period on record** [official] — https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/news/2026/01/canada-forecasts-2026-to-be-among-the-hottest-years-on-record.html

- By September 22, 2026: **6 named storms, 0 hurricanes, 0 major hurricanes** — the first season since **1914** to reach this far into September without a hurricane; only 1907 and 1914 went full seasons without one [secondary] — https://parade.com/news/atlantic-hurricane-season-breaks-112-year-record-noaa-data-shows-not-a-single-hurricane-has-formed-in-2026
- **ACE 6.2** — 93% below normal; NOAA's 1991–2020 average by Sept 22 would be ~10 named storms, 4 hurricanes, 2 majors [secondary] — https://parade.com/news/atlantic-hurricane-season-breaks-112-year-record-noaa-data-shows-not-a-single-hurricane-has-formed-in-2026

## 5. Deep dive — Health & medicine 2026

### 5.1 Alzheimer's: the cancer-drug connection

- **April 2026, *Cell*** (Walsh group, Boston Children's/Harvard/Broad): deep (>1,000×) sequencing of **311 brain samples** finds somatic single-nucleotide variants in **cancer-driver genes enriched in Alzheimer's microglia-like cells**, associated with clonal expansion and a hematopoietic origin — "to some extent, Alzheimer's disease is a little like cancer" [secondary] — https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-04-unexpected-cancer-mutations-brain-immune.html ; https://neurosciencenews.com/alzheimers-cancer-mutation-microglia-30565/
- **July 2026, *Nature*** (Jaiswal group): bone-marrow-derived immune cells **routinely cross the blood-brain barrier during aging** and become indistinguishable from resident microglia — reframing whether CHIP (clonal hematopoiesis) mutations protect or promote Alzheimer's depending on clone size [secondary] — https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/puzzling-cancer-mutation-may-lower-alzheimers-disease-risk-2026a1000szy
- **Repurposed cancer drugs** (letrozole + irinotecan): UCSF/Gladstone computational screen of 1,300 drugs → mouse-model combination reversed amyloid/tau pathology and restored memory (2025 papers); human clinical trials anticipated, not yet reported in 2026 window [secondary] — https://www.psypost.org/repurposed-cancer-drugs-show-promise-as-combination-therapy-for-alzheimers-disease/
- **Phase III monoclonal-antibody** anti-amyloid trials reported significant slowing of cognitive decline vs placebo (podcast summary of 2026 pharma news; trial names not captured) [secondary] — https://podscan.fm/podcasts/pharma-and-biotech-daily/episodes/gene-therapy-breakthroughs-and-fda-accelerations-transforming-pharma

### 5.2 Avian influenza: watchful, not alarming

- **May 18, 2026** joint FAO/WHO/WOAH assessment: global public-health risk from HPAI A(H5) **low, unchanged**; occupational risk low-to-moderate; no sustained human-to-human transmission; viruses remain avian-adapted [official] — https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/influenza/human-animal-interface-risk-assessments/updated-joint-fao-who-woah-public-health-assessment-of-recent-high-pathogenicity-avian-influenza-a(h5)-virus-events-in-animals-and-people--18-may-2026.pdf?sfvr
- **Aug 26–27, 2026** PAHO update: Americas animal outbreaks **down 25.7%** (455 outbreaks Jan–Jul 15; mammals −65%); **no human cases in the Americas in 2026** (last: Nov 2025); globally **9 human cases / 2 deaths** in 2026 through Aug 7 (1,002 cases / 479 deaths since 2003) [official] — https://www.paho.org/en/news/27-8-2026-avian-influenza-americas-animal-outbreaks-decline-paho-calls-continued-vigilance
- **Testing gap**: only one FDA-approved H5N1-specific rapid test exists (contemporary-strain validation unclear); WHO's first near-point-of-care mpox test approved late October 2024 — under-testing remains the blind spot [independent] — https://www.statnews.com/2024/11/12/mpox-h5n1-bird-flu-triple-rapid-tests/
