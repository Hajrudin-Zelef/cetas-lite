# Step 10 Phase G — Science & Planet News (February → September 2026)

**Scope:** Science, space, climate, health, and environment news from **February 1, 2026 to September 22, 2026** — the general-news science track of Anicet's RAG data-collection project. One file per news phase; no consolidation with other phases.

**Research date / cutoff:** 2026-09-22. All events below are dated and fall inside the February→September 2026 window unless explicitly flagged as pre-window context (e.g., forecasts issued December 2025 / January 2026) or forward-looking projections.

**Method:** Web research via read-only search and page fetch. Section 1 is a month-by-month chronology of dated events. Sections 2–6 are thematic deep dives (space, astronomy, climate, health, energy science, environment). Sections 7–9 are the gaps/conflicts register, glossary, and verbatim source index.

**Provenance legend (every factual claim carries one):**
- `[official]` — space agencies, governments, journals' press offices, institutional releases
- `[vendor-reported]` — company claims (SpaceX, fusion startups, pharma), not independently verified
- `[independent]` — established media / analytical outlets with editorial process
- `[secondary]` — aggregators, blogs, podcasts, wikis, single-source reposts
- `[unverified]` — single weak source, contested claim, or outcome not yet confirmed by cutoff

**QC note:** No discoveries, dates, or figures invented. Where sources conflict, both claims are preserved and marked. Forward-looking items (e.g., "planned for") are labeled as such. Synthetic-looking sources (e.g., benchmark fixtures) were excluded and logged as gaps.
