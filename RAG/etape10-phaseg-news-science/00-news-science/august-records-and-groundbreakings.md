# August — records and groundbreakings

- **Beta Pictoris d** (July 15, ApJ Letters) is a methodological milestone: the planet was found **through its atmospheric chemistry, not its brightness** — a new discovery channel for directly imaged worlds [secondary] — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8hLgKApvbSU
- Rubin's **July 31 COSMOS release** (Early Data Preview 2) is the first science-grade LSST-camera catalog — the moment Rubin transitioned from commissioning to facility science [official] — https://www6.slac.stanford.edu/news/2026-07-31-doe-nsf-rubin-observatory-opens-deep-window-famous-cosmic-field

### August — records and groundbreakings

- August 2026 delivered a **double heat record**: hottest month ever globally (Copernicus) and hottest U.S. summer (NOAA) — with drought and flood extremes side by side [secondary] — https://balancedweather.substack.com/p/2026-was-hottest-summer-on-record
- **Pacific Fusion's $1B groundbreaking** (Albuquerque) is the largest single fusion-construction commitment to date, explicitly targeting the never-demonstrated **net facility gain** [secondary] — https://www.tradeandindustrydev.com/region/new-mexico/news/nm-pacific-fusion-breaks-ground-first-kind-fusion-36237
- The **August 6 NOAA outlook** update is notable for what it didn't change: below-normal season maintained even as the climatological peak approached [official] — https://www.noaa.gov/news-release/noaa-maintains-prediction-for-below-normal-atlantic-hurricane-season

### September — the quiet season's record

- **Edouard's** Louisiana landfall (Sept 1–2) showed the 2026 season's character: weak winds (60 mph) but **extreme rainfall** (17.71 in) — NOAA's warning that "inland rainfall is a major cause of damage" validated [official][secondary] — https://www.noaa.gov/news-release/noaa-maintains-prediction-for-below-normal-atlantic-hurricane-season ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Atlantic_hurricane_season
- The **September 18 week** was fusion's week: Inertia's virtual-shot milestone and SPARC's 8 K cryo run landed the same day — private fusion's two tracks (inertial, magnetic) advancing in parallel [independent] — https://thefusionreport.com/this-weeks-fusion-news-september-18-2026/
- By **September 22**, the Atlantic's zero-hurricane streak entered **112-year-record** territory — a season that will be studied for years [secondary] — https://parade.com/news/atlantic-hurricane-season-breaks-112-year-record-noaa-data-shows-not-a-single-hurricane-has-formed-in-2026
