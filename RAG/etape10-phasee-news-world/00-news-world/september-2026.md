# September 2026

### September 2026

- On 9 September 2026, SOUTHCOM announced the 69th vessel strike of the anti-cartel campaign (three killed in the Caribbean); on 19 September it announced the 70th (four killed) [secondary via Just Security] (https://www.justsecurity.org/124002/timeline-vessel-strikes-related-actions/).
- Just Security's timeline, current as of 19 September 2026, counted 70 strikes, 234 people killed (including 23 missing and presumed dead) and 3 known survivors since the campaign began [secondary] (https://www.justsecurity.org/124002/timeline-vessel-strikes-related-actions/); AP's count, cited in secondary press, put the 19 September operation as the 69th strike with at least 231 killed — a methodological conflict flagged in Section 4 [independent via secondary press] (https://wltreport.com/2026/09/20/watch-u-s-military-delivers-another-devastating-blow-to-caribbean-drug-route/).
- On 10 September 2026 the ECB raised its deposit rate by 25 basis points to 2.50% — its second hike of 2026 — at a Governing Council meeting in Berlin, hoping to tame inflation "driven entirely by higher energy costs from the Iran war"; inflation was reported "well past 3%" across the 21-country eurozone [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/).
- The ECB's own statement attributed the decision to the Middle East conflict "driving up prices," with inflation likely above the 2% target "for quite a while"; it lifted the 2026 growth projection to 0.9% from 0.8%, and saw inflation averaging 3.0% in 2026 and 2.5% in 2027 [official] (https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/press_conference/visual-mps/2026/html/mopo_statement_explained_september.en.html).
- ECB deposit facility rate: 2.50% from 16 September 2026 (up from 2.25%) [independent] (https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/economy/ecb-hikes-rates-raises-both-inflation-growth-forecasts).
- ECB main refinancing operations rate: 2.65% from 16 September 2026 (up from 2.40%) [independent] (https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/economy/ecb-hikes-rates-raises-both-inflation-growth-forecasts).
- ECB marginal lending facility rate: 2.90% from 16 September 2026 (up from 2.65%) [independent] (https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/economy/ecb-hikes-rates-raises-both-inflation-growth-forecasts).
- The 2.50% deposit rate was described as the upper end of the 1.75%–2.50% "neutral" range, so any further increase would shift ECB policy into restrictive territory, per economists cited by Morningstar [independent] (https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/economy/ecb-hikes-rates-raises-both-inflation-growth-forecasts).
- The BRICS summit was held in New Delhi on 12–13 September 2026 under India's presidency, with the theme "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability" [secondary] (https://www.livemint.com/news/india/brics-summit-2026-dates-venue-timings-and-complete-itinerary-11788941919316.html).
- Secondary reporting described a 140-point New Delhi declaration covering global governance, trade, payments, AI, finance, health and sustainability; the G20 summit in Miami was announced for December 2026, i.e., after the cutoff [secondary] (https://globalleaderstoday.online/brics-summit-sets-new-agenda-for-global-cooperation/).
- On 16 September 2026 the Federal Reserve raised its benchmark rate by 25 basis points to 3.75%–4% — the first US rate hike since 2023 — on a 12–0 vote, against a backdrop of persistent inflation and high energy prices [secondary; official statement text via Morningstar/Dow Jones] (https://www.usatoday.com/story/money/economy/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-meeting-updates--live/91746863007/; https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202609165837/text-of-feds-september-monetary-policy-statement).
- On 18 September 2026, ISW/AEI's China–Taiwan update described Taiwanese exercises combining traditional platforms with loitering munitions; analytical commentary, not an official communiqué [secondary] (https://www.aei.org/commentary/china-taiwan-update-september-18-2026/).
- On 21 September 2026, Bitcoin traded above $85,000 — its highest since January 2026 — without reaching its October 2025 record high; Mint reported about $85,222 with a market capitalization near $1.7 trillion; intraday prices vary by venue, so figures are venue- and time-specific [secondary] (https://www.livemint.com/market/cryptocurrency/bitcoin-price-today-85000-cryptocurrency-market-optimistic-rally-clarity-fed-september-eight-month-record-high-since-jan-11789989588018.html).
- 21 September 2026: the Nasdaq Composite gained 2.3% (+599.55 points) to a record 27,122.09 [secondary citing AP/Yahoo Finance] (https://ts2.tech/en/nasdaq-closes-at-a-record-sp-500-stops-0-4-short-as-oil-retreats/).
- 21 September 2026: the S&P 500 rose 1.5% (+114.20 points) to 7,764.70, up 13.4% year-to-date, finishing 0.4% below its record [secondary] (https://ts2.tech/en/nasdaq-closes-at-a-record-sp-500-stops-0-4-short-as-oil-retreats/).
- 21 September 2026: the Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 366.19 points to 52,048.83, up 8.2% year-to-date; the Russell 2000 added 0.52% to 2,875.36, up 15.9% YTD [secondary] (https://ts2.tech/en/nasdaq-closes-at-a-record-sp-500-stops-0-4-short-as-oil-retreats/).
- 21 September 2026: Brent crude fell 3.4% to $100.34 per barrel [secondary] (https://ts2.tech/en/nasdaq-closes-at-a-record-sp-500-stops-0-4-short-as-oil-retreats/).
- 21 September 2026: the 10-year US Treasury yield eased to 4.95% from 5.01% the prior session [secondary] (https://ts2.tech/en/nasdaq-closes-at-a-record-sp-500-stops-0-4-short-as-oil-retreats/).
- 22 September 2026: the S&P 500 ended at 7,764.64 (down 0.06 points), 0.4% below its August all-time high [independent] (https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/).
- 22 September 2026: the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 185.14 points (−0.4%) to 51,863.69 [independent] (https://tvnewscheck.com/business/article/dow-drops-185-nasdaq-rises-122-sp-500-ends-flat/).
- 22 September 2026: the Nasdaq Composite rose 122.18 points (+0.5%) to its own record at 27,244.28; the Russell 2000 rose 14.56 points (+0.5%) to 2,889.92 [independent] (https://tvnewscheck.com/business/article/dow-drops-185-nasdaq-rises-122-sp-500-ends-flat/).
- 22 September 2026: Brent crude briefly fell below $98 intraday before settling at $99.25 per barrel (−1.1%) — down from nearly $110 the prior week, but far above the ~$72 pre-Iran-war level [independent] (https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/).
- For the week ending 22 September 2026: the S&P 500 was up 1.5%, the Dow up 0.4%, the Nasdaq up 2.7% and the Russell 2000 up 1.0% [independent] (https://tvnewscheck.com/business/article/dow-drops-185-nasdaq-rises-122-sp-500-ends-flat/).
- Year-to-date as of 22 September 2026: S&P 500 +13.4%, Dow +7.9%, Nasdaq +17.2%, Russell 2000 +16.4% [independent] (https://tvnewscheck.com/business/article/dow-drops-185-nasdaq-rises-122-sp-500-ends-flat/).
- The UN General Assembly opened on 22 September 2026; President Trump addressed it, vowing "unmatched military might," touting the anti-cartel campaign, pressure on Cuba, and describing the fight as an armed conflict with cartels — "Like ISIS, they should be killed, exiled or detained as enemy combatants without the possibility of release" [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/).
- Trump also used the UN platform to promote his "Donroe Doctrine" framing of Western Hemisphere policy and said Secretary of State Marco Rubio was "deep into negotiations with Cuba" [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/).
- On 22 September 2026, President Zelensky said he had discussed peace efforts and an energy ceasefire with President Trump and expressed willingness to meet in a trilateral format with Trump and Vladimir Putin; Kyiv was preparing for a possible resumption of talks in October, with the Donbas and security guarantees the outstanding disputes [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraines-zelenskiy-says-he-discussed-peace-efforts-energy-ceasefire-with-trump-2026-09-22/; https://www.reuters.com/world/kyiv-preparing-talks-resume-october-senior-ukrainian-official-says-2026-09-12/).
- Earlier in September, US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner had made separate trips to Russia and Ukraine without an announced breakthrough; Zelensky described US ideas presented on 8 September as "decent" [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/zelenskiy-says-us-envoys-presented-decent-ideas-peace-talks-2026-09-08/).
- Reuters' 21 September trade retrospective noted a Trump–Xi meeting planned for 24 September — after the cutoff — to be reported only as a scheduled event [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- Aon estimated global natural-catastrophe economic losses of about $111 billion in H1 2026 with at least 16,200 deaths, while Munich Re estimated about $112 billion for the same period; the two vendors' mortality figures differ, a methodological conflict noted in Section 4 [vendor-reported] (https://www.aon.com/getmedia/87b942cd-2658-456c-9269-f441b5ed4ddd/Global-Catastrophe-Report-2026_H1.pdf; https://www.tiempodeseguros.com/storage/app/media/uploaded-files/MunichRe-NatCat-HY-2026-Factsheet.pdf).
- Hurricane Lowell battered Hawaii's western islands in early September 2026 and was linked to at least two deaths, per AP/Scripps reporting [independent] (https://www.tmj4.com/science-and-tech/natural-disasters/hurricane-lowell-battered-hawaiis-western-islands-and-is-linked-to-at-least-2-deaths).
- Secondary press on 21 September 2026 cited roughly 13 million displaced people in Sudan, a figure not verified against UN/OCHA data in the sources consulted [unverified] (https://globalwatch.info/en_GB/articles/gc7/features/2026/09/21/feature-01).
- A NYT/Siena poll published 15 September 2026 put Democrats ahead 51% to 43% among likely voters for the November midterms (about 1,500 respondents, ±2.9-point margin of error) — a poll, not a result [independent] (https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/09/15/democrats-republicans-trump-new-poll-midterm-elections/91772998007/).
- The ECB's September meeting continued its tradition of holding one Governing Council meeting per year in a different eurozone city — 2026's was in Berlin [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/).
- Morningstar's economists, after the September ECB press conference, revised their call to expect an additional 25bp hike to 2.75% at the 17 December meeting — a forecast, not a decision [secondary] (https://www.morningstar.com/economy/ecb-hikes-rates-raises-both-inflation-growth-forecasts).
- Germany's 10-year yield at 3.482% — the highest since 2011 — showed how the renewed hiking cycle was repricing sovereign borrowing costs across the eurozone's most indebted economies [secondary] (https://business-news-today.com/ecb-raises-rates-to-2-5-as-100-oil-pushes-eurozone-inflation-outlook-higher/).
- The Fed's 12–0 vote on 16 September signaled unanimity behind the first US hike since 2023; the statement text's emphasis was persistent inflation compounded by energy prices [secondary] (https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202609165837/text-of-feds-september-monetary-policy-statement).
- Wall Street's 22 September session encapsulated the year's tension: AI-chip strength (SMH/SOXX +1.7%–2.1% on Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron) and record Nasdaq highs coexisted with Brent near $100 and AP-noted "jitters about whether stocks in the artificial-intelligence industry shot too high" [independent/secondary] (https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/nasdaq-jumps-to-close-at-record-as-chipmakers-climb-oil-prices-cool-aapl-meta-spcx-msft-vktx-in-focus/cZM5GHbRB7L; https://www.reflector.com/news/national/wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-falls-below-100-per-barrel/article_991814cc-8304-5c54-9e8b-186be3259451.html).
- Trump's 22 September UN address also folded the hemisphere campaign into a broader claim of "reasserting American power": decades of cartel dominance, uncontrolled migration and "hostile foreign adversaries" in the region were "not anymore," in his telling [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/).
- Zelensky's 22 September trilateral offer (Trump–Putin–Zelensky) was the most concrete diplomatic proposal of the September Ukraine sequence; Kyiv simultaneously readied for an October resumption of talks [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraines-zelenskiy-says-he-discussed-peace-efforts-energy-ceasefire-with-trump-2026-09-22/; https://www.reuters.com/world/kyiv-preparing-talks-resume-october-senior-ukrainian-official-says-2026-09-12/).
