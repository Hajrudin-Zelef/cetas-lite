# 10. Research log and methodological notes

## 10. Research log and methodological notes

- Research for this file was conducted on 22 September 2026 over the public web: wire services first (Reuters, AP), then official institutional pages (ECB, PMO Canada, UK Commons Library, ReliefWeb/OCHA), then established press (Le Monde, USA Today, Morningstar), then secondary and analytical outlets.
- No paywalled content was used; paywalled wire items (e.g., Bloomberg Law's G7 takeaways) were excluded rather than summarized from snippets.
- The Iran-war file is the weakest: only the war's existence, late-February start, oil shock and inflation consequences are wire-anchored; operation names, the 28 February date, Khamenei's reported death and regime-collapse claims are single-weak-source material and are fenced as [unverified] throughout.
- The Sahel April battle account rests on a single blog (panxmedia); ACLED figures reached the file only via press re-reporting (Guardian via blogs), so they are [secondary] even though ACLED is a reputable monitor.
- Cutoff discipline: events dated after 22 September 2026 — the planned 24 September Trump–Xi meeting, Israel's reported 27 October election, the December G20 in Miami, October Nobel announcements, Brazil's 2026 election — are reported strictly as planned or scheduled, never as completed.
- Deliberate exclusions by phase design: the Winter Olympics and FIFA World Cup (Phase 10D), AI/tech industry news (Phases 10A/10F), and science/space (Phase 10G) are not covered here even where they intersected with geopolitics.
- Casualty discipline: every human toll is attributed to the reporting party and dated; belligerent-issued figures are never presented as verified; conflicting counts are preserved side by side in Section 4.
- Moving figures are timestamped: billionaire estimates (March lists vs. later real-time trackers), intraday Bitcoin prices, and Brent settlements are all venue- and date-specific by construction.
- Known weak points a v2 should re-verify with wire reporting: the 25 April 2026 Mali assault details, the Trump tariff-dividend proposal (exact figures and status), the Israel 27 October 2026 election claim, Sudan's 13-million displacement figure, and the AI2379 hydraulic-failure account.
- Priority gaps for a follow-up pass: Myanmar, DRC/M23, Yemen/Houthis/Red Sea dedicated reporting, Syria, Haiti, the 2026 NATO summit, Bank of England and Bank of Japan decisions, Hungary/Portugal/Brazil 2026 elections, a full 2026 commercial-aviation accident list, and the 2026 typhoon/flood season.
