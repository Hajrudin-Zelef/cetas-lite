# May 2026

- On 21 April 2026, Pope Francis died at the age of 88, according to Reuters; his funeral was reported to have drawn about 200,000 attendees on 26 April, and a conclave held 6–8 May elected Cardinal Robert Francis Prevost as Pope Leo XIV [independent for the death; secondary for funeral and conclave details] (https://www.reuters.com/world/vatican-pope-francis-dies-aged-88-vatican-statement-says-2026-04-21/; https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/pope-leo-xiv-cardinal-robert-144530796.html).
- The new pope, aged 69 at election, was reported as the second-youngest pope of the modern era, the third non-European pope, and the second from the Americas [secondary] (https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/pope-leo-xiv-cardinal-robert-144530796.html).
- On 25 April 2026, jihadist fighters and Tuareg separatist allies launched what one secondary outlet called the largest assault on Mali in nearly 15 years, targeting Gao, Menaka and Kidal; the same report claims the northern town of Kidal was captured and that Mali's Defence Minister Sadio Camara was killed — none of which was corroborated by a wire source consulted, so treat as [unverified] (http://panxmedia.blogspot.com/2026/05/alliance-of-sahel-states-confirms-joint.html).
- The Alliance of Sahel States (AES) — Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger — responded with what Niger's government described as "intense air campaigns" in Malian territory; the AES joint anti-jihadist force, initially 5,000 troops, was reported increased to 15,000 in mid-April [secondary] (http://panxmedia.blogspot.com/2026/05/alliance-of-sahel-states-confirms-joint.html).
- In Gaza, Reuters reported on 30 April 2026 that Israeli strikes killed four Palestinians, according to medics, amid a new push in Cairo to revive the October 2025 truce; the report noted that phase two of the plan — further Israeli withdrawal, a transitional authority, a multinational force and Hamas disarmament — was making little progress [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/israeli-strikes-kill-four-gaza-medics-say-amid-new-ceasefire-push-2026-04-30/).
- The ECB's June rate decision was foreshadowed by rising energy-driven inflation: euro-area inflation forecasts for 2026 were revised up to 3.0% with a projected peak of 3.4% in Q3–Q4 2026, and GDP growth was cut to 0.8%, raising stagflation concerns in secondary analysis [secondary] (https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/06/23/european-central-bank-inflation-2026/).
- The papal funeral on 26 April 2026 reportedly drew about 200,000 attendees; the conclave that followed ran 6–8 May [secondary] (https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/pope-leo-xiv-cardinal-robert-144530796.html).
- In the Sahel aftermath of 25 April, Burkina Faso's Defence Minister Célestin Simporé, speaking for the AES at Camara's funeral, vowed to "hunt down" the "assassins," while about a thousand people rallied in Niamey in solidarity with Mali — both details from the same single secondary source and [unverified] (http://panxmedia.blogspot.com/2026/05/alliance-of-sahel-states-confirms-joint.html).
- The Azawad Liberation Front's spokesman Mohamed Elmaouloud Ramadane publicly called on Burkina Faso and Niger to "stay out" of the Mali fighting hours after the 25 April attacks began [secondary] (http://panxmedia.blogspot.com/2026/05/alliance-of-sahel-states-confirms-joint.html).
- April's Gaza diplomacy centered on Cairo talks aimed at reviving the October 2025 truce; the 30 April strikes that killed four (per medics) underscored how fragile the truce remained [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/israeli-strikes-kill-four-gaza-medics-say-amid-new-ceasefire-push-2026-04-30/).
