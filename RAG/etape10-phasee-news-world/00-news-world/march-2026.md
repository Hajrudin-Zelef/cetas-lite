# March 2026

- The US–Israeli war on Iran began in late February 2026, a start date corroborated by Reuters reporting on 10 September 2026, which described the conflict as "the U.S.-Israeli war on Iran, which began in late February," and noted that "all hopes are gone for a quick end" to it [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/).
- Secondary outlets place the opening of the joint offensive on 28 February 2026, describing a US operation dubbed "Epic Fury" alongside an Israeli operation dubbed "Roaring Lion"; these operation names and the precise start date could not be corroborated by any wire or official source consulted and are therefore [unverified] (https://upscmentorpiyush.com/iran-us-israel-war-2026/; https://sesamedisk.com/iran-us-israel-conflict-2026-timeline/).
- The war triggered an immediate energy shock: Brent crude, which AP reporting puts at about $72 per barrel before the war with Iran began, rose sharply in the weeks that followed [independent] (https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/).
- Secondary sources claim the offensive killed Iran's Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei and that the Iranian regime collapsed in the months that followed; no wire or official source consulted confirms either claim, so both remain [unverified] (https://datacommlab.com/booths/docs/US-Iran-Conflict-20260327.pdf).
- On the tariff front, Reuters' retrospective timeline reports that in February 2026 the US Supreme Court struck down President Trump's emergency tariffs, prompting Trump to impose a temporary worldwide 10% levy under a different legal authority [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- The US campaign of lethal strikes against alleged drug-trafficking boats in the Caribbean and Eastern Pacific, which began in early September 2025, continued through February 2026; CNN reported on 2 November 2025 that the operation that month was the 15th known strike since the campaign began in early September [independent] (https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/02/politics/us-strikes-drug-boat-caribbean-intl-hnk?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta).
- The pre-war oil baseline matters for everything that followed: AP's September 2026 reporting put Brent at about $72 per barrel before the Iran war began — the level against which the subsequent doubling to nearly $110 was measured [independent] (https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/).
- The February 2026 Supreme Court decision striking down the emergency tariffs forced the administration onto a different legal authority for its tariff regime; the temporary worldwide 10% levy was the bridge instrument while new tariff rounds were prepared [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- February 2026 also sat inside the Winter Olympics window (covered in Phase 10D, Sport) and preceded the March billionaire-list season that would document the AI-driven wealth surge [secondary].
- No February 2026 Ukraine ceasefire was reported; the war's diplomatic track would only visibly accelerate in September 2026 [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraines-zelenskiy-says-he-discussed-peace-efforts-energy-ceasefire-with-trump-2026-09-22/).
