# 3.8 US–China: from tariff war to reported truce

- The Trump administration's campaign of lethal strikes on alleged drug-trafficking vessels in the Caribbean and Eastern Pacific ran through the whole period; it began in early September 2025 and by 19 September 2026 had reached roughly 70 strikes [secondary via Just Security; independent via AP counts in press] (https://www.justsecurity.org/124002/timeline-vessel-strikes-related-actions/; https://wltreport.com/2026/09/20/watch-u-s-military-delivers-another-devastating-blow-to-caribbean-drug-route/).
- Just Security's running timeline, current as of 19 September 2026, counted 70 strikes, 234 people killed (including 23 missing and presumed dead) and 3 known survivors; AP's count, as relayed in secondary press, described the 19 September operation as the 69th strike with at least 231 killed — the discrepancy is methodological (missing/presumed-dead accounting) and is flagged in Section 4 [secondary/independent] (https://www.justsecurity.org/124002/timeline-vessel-strikes-related-actions/; https://wltreport.com/2026/09/20/watch-u-s-military-delivers-another-devastating-blow-to-caribbean-drug-route/).
- The administration designated drug cartels as foreign terrorist organizations and framed the campaign as an armed conflict with cartels; Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth announced strikes on X, identifying the dead as "narco-terrorists" and comparing the campaign to the fight against al-Qaeda; rights groups countered that the strikes, with more than 220 killed, amounted to extrajudicial killings [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/; https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/02/politics/us-strikes-drug-boat-caribbean-intl-hnk?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta).
- In March 2026 Trump launched the "Shield of the Americas" coalition (Argentina, Ecuador, El Salvador, later Colombia and Peru; Brazil and Mexico absent), convening regional leaders and foreign ministers to back the aggressive approach; at the UN General Assembly on 22 September 2026 he vowed "unmatched military might" and said Rubio was "deep into negotiations with Cuba" [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/).
- Trump branded the approach the "Donroe Doctrine," a play on his own name picked up in conservative media [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/).
- On domestic economics, Trump floated tariff-revenue-funded direct payments to Americans (reported figures of $2,000, later $5,000, per-person "dividends"); secondary press in September 2026 reported both market enthusiasm (S&P 500 futures surging on the $2,000 announcement) and pushback from a White House economic adviser who said there was not enough tariff revenue to fund them — the proposal's status and the adviser's identity were not pinned down in the sources consulted, so the episode is [unverified] with an internal conflict noted (secondary press, September 2026; exact URLs not retained — see Section 4).
- The November 2026 US midterm elections had not occurred by the cutoff; a NYT/Siena poll published 15 September 2026 gave Democrats a 51%–43% lead among likely voters (n≈1,500, margin of error ±2.9 points) — a survey snapshot, not a result [independent] (https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/09/15/democrats-republicans-trump-new-poll-midterm-elections/91772998007/).
- Pre-period context with 2026 consequences: on 3 January 2026 a US operation captured Venezuelan leader Nicolás Maduro and Cilia Flores in Caracas; it shaped regional diplomacy through 2026 but predates this file's February–September window and is included only as background [secondary] (https://www.beaconjournal.com/story/news/politics/2026/01/03/how-us-attacked-venezuela-captured-nicolas-maduro/88008790007/).

### 3.8 US–China: from tariff war to reported truce

- February 2026: the US Supreme Court struck down Trump's emergency tariffs; Trump responded with a temporary worldwide 10% levy under a different legal authority [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- May 2026: Trump and Xi met in Beijing; the reported outcomes were planned trade and investment councils and Chinese purchase commitments — the pivot Reuters described as "from tariff war to trade truce" [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- July 2026: the US imposed new tariffs of 12.5% on goods from 60 trade partners, showing the truce did not end tariff escalation [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- 24 September 2026: a further Trump–Xi meeting was planned (after the cutoff); reported only as scheduled [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- On 22 September 2026, market coverage noted "top U.S. tech leadership to be present at Trump-Xi Summit," tying equity moves to the expected meeting [secondary] (https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/nasdaq-jumps-to-close-at-record-as-chipmakers-climb-oil-prices-cool-aapl-meta-spcx-msft-vktx-in-focus/cZM5GHbRB7L).
- The exact tariff rates, product coverage and legal authorities for each 2026 round were not reconstructed in the sources consulted; treat headline rates above as the reported figures and see Section 4.

### 3.9 China–Taiwan: exercises and deterrence signaling

- Taiwan's August 2026 Han Kuang exercises emphasized resilience: a simulated presidential evacuation, internet-disruption drills and drone employment, per ISW's 14 August 2026 update (analytical product, not an official communiqué) [secondary] (https://understandingwar.org/research/china-taiwan/china-taiwan-update-august-14-2026/).
- ISW/AEI's 18 September 2026 update described Taiwanese exercises combining traditional platforms with loitering munitions [secondary] (https://www.aei.org/commentary/china-taiwan-update-september-18-2026/).
- Large-scale Chinese exercises around Taiwan reported by Reuters in December 2025 predate the window and are included only to note that cross-strait military pressure was already elevated entering 2026 [independent, contextual].
- No 2026 cross-strait crisis or blockade was reported in the sources consulted; the picture is one of sustained signaling and exercise activity on both sides [secondary].
