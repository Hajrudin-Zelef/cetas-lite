# 3.2 The US–Israel–Iran war (from late February 2026)

- The war in Ukraine continued through the entire February–September 2026 window with no ceasefire agreement in force; diplomacy intensified in September but produced no breakthrough [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraines-zelenskiy-says-he-discussed-peace-efforts-energy-ceasefire-with-trump-2026-09-22/).
- In early September 2026, US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner conducted separate diplomatic trips to Russia and Ukraine; no breakthrough was announced [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/zelenskiy-says-us-envoys-presented-decent-ideas-peace-talks-2026-09-08/).
- On 8 September 2026, President Zelensky said US envoys had presented "decent ideas" in peace talks, without detailing them [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/zelenskiy-says-us-envoys-presented-decent-ideas-peace-talks-2026-09-08/).
- On 12 September 2026, a senior Ukrainian official said Kyiv was preparing for talks to resume in October [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/kyiv-preparing-talks-resume-october-senior-ukrainian-official-says-2026-09-12/).
- On 22 September 2026, Zelensky said he had discussed peace efforts and an energy ceasefire with President Trump and declared himself willing to meet in a trilateral format with Trump and Vladimir Putin [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraines-zelenskiy-says-he-discussed-peace-efforts-energy-ceasefire-with-trump-2026-09-22/).
- The reported outstanding disputes remained the status of the Donbas and the shape of security guarantees for Ukraine — the same issues blocking a settlement all year [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraines-zelenskiy-says-he-discussed-peace-efforts-energy-ceasefire-with-trump-2026-09-22/).
- The UK House of Commons Library maintained an updated research briefing on the conflict through 2026, reflecting continued parliamentary scrutiny in London [official/secondary] (https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-12198/).
- Casualty figures for the Ukraine war during this period were not established from the sources consulted; any tolls circulating in secondary press should be attributed to the publishing belligerent or outlet and treated as contested — see Section 4.
- The September diplomacy sequence (Witkoff/Kushner trips → "decent ideas" → October-preparation → Zelensky's trilateral offer) was the densest US engagement of the year, yet Reuters reported no breakthrough at any step [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/zelenskiy-says-us-envoys-presented-decent-ideas-peace-talks-2026-09-08/; https://www.reuters.com/world/kyiv-preparing-talks-resume-october-senior-ukrainian-official-says-2026-09-12/).
- The "energy ceasefire" concept Zelensky discussed with Trump on 22 September echoed earlier war practice of tacit or negotiated pauses around energy infrastructure; no terms were published in the sources consulted [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraines-zelenskiy-says-he-discussed-peace-efforts-energy-ceasefire-with-trump-2026-09-22/).

### 3.2 The US–Israel–Iran war (from late February 2026)

- Existence and timing of the war are independently corroborated: Reuters (10 September 2026) called it "the U.S.-Israeli war on Iran, which began in late February," adding that "all hopes are gone for a quick end" [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/); the ECB's official September 2026 statement attributed persistent above-target inflation to the "Middle East conflict" driving up energy prices [official] (https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/press_conference/visual-mps/2026/html/mopo_statement_explained_september.en.html).
- The economic footprint is independently documented: Brent crude at about $72 before the war rose to nearly $110 in mid-September 2026 before easing to $99.25 on 22 September [independent] (https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/).
- One secondary outlet reported Brent at about $105 on 10 September after another escalation in shipping attacks, linking oil moves to attacks on shipping in the Middle East [secondary] (https://business-news-today.com/ecb-raises-rates-to-2-5-as-100-oil-pushes-eurozone-inflation-outlook-higher/).
- Beyond those anchors, the available narrative rests on weak sources and must be flagged [unverified]: secondary outlets date the joint offensive's opening to 28 February 2026, name a US operation "Epic Fury" and an Israeli operation "Roaring Lion," claim the death of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, describe regime collapse and a regional expansion in March 2026 (strikes on Hezbollah, Houthi and Iraqi-militia targets; Iranian proxy retaliation), and report an Iranian MP announcing a Strait of Hormuz closure (https://upscmentorpiyush.com/iran-us-israel-war-2026/; https://sesamedisk.com/iran-us-israel-conflict-2026-timeline/; https://datacommlab.com/booths/docs/US-Iran-Conflict-20260327.pdf).
- Pre-period context (for orientation, not as 2026 events): in June 2025 Israel launched an aerial bombardment campaign against Iran's nuclear programme; the US joined with strikes on Fordow, Natanz and Esfahan on 21–22 June 2025; at the June 2025 G7 summit in Kananaskis, leaders issued a statement that Iran "can never have a nuclear weapon" and called for de-escalation including a Gaza ceasefire; Trump left that summit a day early as the Israel–Iran confrontation intensified [independent] (https://www.taxtmi.com/news?id=46783).
- No credible casualty figures for the 2026 Iran war were found in the sources consulted; claims of "thousands killed" in secondary outlets are not corroborated and belong in Section 4.
- Strategic consequence noted by Reuters: surging oil and natural gas prices pushed eurozone inflation "well past 3%," and gas storage levels sat below historic norms heading into the winter heating season [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/).
- The war's persistence into September — "all hopes are gone for a quick end," per Reuters — is what converted a relative energy-price shock into a monetary-policy shock on both sides of the Atlantic, forcing the ECB's second hike and the Fed's first hike since 2023 [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/).
- Analytical anniversary pieces circulating in August 2026 framed the war as the culmination of a 2025 Iranian uprising followed by the bombing campaign; these are secondary narratives, not wire-verified history, and are cited only to show how the war was being narrated — the exact URLs were not retained in this research pass, so the framing is [unverified].
- What remains independently solid about the 2026 Iran war: it began in late February as a US–Israeli operation; it was still underway in September with no quick end expected; it roughly doubled Brent from ~$72; and it drove eurozone inflation past 3% — everything else in this subsection's secondary narrative is [unverified].
