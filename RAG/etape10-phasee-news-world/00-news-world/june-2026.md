# June 2026

### June 2026

- The 2026 G7 Leaders' Summit was hosted by France (under the French G7 presidency; the Évian venue was reported by Canadian government communications): Prime Minister Mark Carney visited France on 12 June and Ireland on 13–14 June 2026 around the summit [official via PMO Canada release] (https://windows.einnews.com/pr_news/920308118/prime-minister-carney-secures-new-partnerships-in-defence-and-critical-minerals-at-the-2026-g7-leaders-summit).
- In France on 12 June, Carney and President Emmanuel Macron announced a new GSOIA agreement to strengthen defence and industrial cooperation, and advanced collaboration in aerospace and quantum science [official] (https://windows.einnews.com/pr_news/920308118/prime-minister-carney-secures-new-partnerships-in-defence-and-critical-minerals-at-the-2026-g7-leaders-summit).
- At the summit, France invited outreach leaders from Brazil, Egypt, India, Kenya, South Korea, Saudi Arabia, Ukraine and the United Arab Emirates; leaders agreed to broaden the Critical Minerals Production Alliance (announced at the June 2025 Kananaskis summit) into the Critical Minerals Resilience and Production Alliance, a framework for cooperation across the value chain [official/secondary] (https://windows.einnews.com/pr_news/920308118/prime-minister-carney-secures-new-partnerships-in-defence-and-critical-minerals-at-the-2026-g7-leaders-summit).
- Note: several secondary outlets conflate the June 2025 Kananaskis summit (six joint statements on critical minerals, AI, wildfires, quantum, migrant smuggling and transnational repression) with the 2026 France-hosted summit; the 2025 chair's summary is archived at the University of Toronto G7 site and should not be cited as 2026 outcomes [independent for the archive] (https://www.g7.utoronto.ca/summit/2025kananaskis/250617-summary.html).
- On 11 June 2026, the ECB raised its key policy rates by 25 basis points to 2.25% — its first hike since 2023 — after eight cuts had brought the deposit rate down from a 4.00% peak to 2.00%; the move was driven by energy costs from the Iran war pushing inflation higher [independent/secondary] (https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/06/23/european-central-bank-inflation-2026/; https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/economy/ecb-hikes-rates-raises-both-inflation-growth-forecasts).
- Colombia held its presidential election on 21 June 2026: Abelardo De La Espriella won, cementing Latin America's rightward shift, with Reuters noting the final count remained subject to verification procedures [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/de-la-espriella-win-colombia-cements-latin-americas-rightward-shift-2026-06-22/); Le Monde reported on 5 July that expatriate votes helped the far right clinch victory in both Colombia and Peru [independent] (https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/07/05/peruvian-and-colombian-presidential-elections-expatriate-vote-helps-far-right-clinch-victory_6755161_4.html).
- Peru's presidential election of 7 June 2026 was won by Keiko Fujimori with about 50.13% of the vote, a margin of just over 49,000 votes [independent] (https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/07/05/peruvian-and-colombian-presidential-elections-expatriate-vote-helps-far-right-clinch-victory_6755161_4.html).
- In July 2026 the United States imposed new tariffs of 12.5% on goods from 60 trade partners, per Reuters' retrospective trade timeline [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- Carney's Ireland leg (13–14 June) covered AI, health technologies and biomanufacturing, agri-food, climate and clean growth, and research and innovation — the economic complement to the France defence track [official] (https://windows.einnews.com/pr_news/920308118/prime-minister-carney-secures-new-partnerships-in-defence-and-critical-minerals-at-the-2026-g7-leaders-summit).
- The 2026 summit continued work begun at Kananaskis 2025: the Critical Minerals Action Plan and Production Alliance announced there in June 2025 were the baseline the Évian leaders broadened into the Resilience and Production Alliance [official/secondary] (https://windows.einnews.com/pr_news/920308118/prime-minister-carney-secures-new-partnerships-in-defence-and-critical-minerals-at-the-2026-g7-leaders-summit; https://www.g7.utoronto.ca/summit/2025kananaskis/250617-summary.html).
- The June 2025 Kananaskis six joint statements (critical minerals, AI, wildfires, quantum, migrant smuggling, transnational repression) remain the last fully documented G7 statement package in the sources consulted; 2026 Évian outcomes are documented here only via the Canadian PMO release [official for PMO; independent for the 2025 archive].
- The ECB's June hike ended an eight-cut easing run: the deposit rate had fallen from a 4.00% peak to 2.00% before the 11 June move to 2.25% [independent] (https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/economy/ecb-hikes-rates-raises-both-inflation-growth-forecasts).
- Peru's 7 June result (Fujimori ~50.13%, margin just over 49,000 votes) and Colombia's 21 June result (De La Espriella) were both described by Le Monde as far-right victories aided by expatriate voting — the outlet's characterization, reported here as such [independent] (https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/07/05/peruvian-and-colombian-presidential-elections-expatriate-vote-helps-far-right-clinch-victory_6755161_4.html).

### July 2026

- Reuters reported on 15 July 2026 that India's Aircraft Accident Investigation Bureau (AAIB) had prepared a cockpit voice recorder transcript, conducted a psychological autopsy, and moved into the final stages of its probe into the 12 June 2025 Air India Flight AI-171 crash of a Boeing 787 that killed 260 people shortly after takeoff from Ahmedabad [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/india/indian-investigators-have-conducted-psychological-autopsy-air-india-crash-probe-2026-07-15/).
- The AAIB court filing did not identify whose psychological autopsy was conducted or disclose findings; analysis of engine-monitoring-unit data retrieved in late May 2026 was still awaited, and the bureau expected remaining work to finish in about six weeks, subject to external dependencies [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/india/indian-investigators-have-conducted-psychological-autopsy-air-india-crash-probe-2026-07-15/).
- The filing also revealed investigators had interviewed Air India 787 pilots, crew who had flown with the crashed jet's pilots, technical personnel, air-traffic controllers, weather officials and human-factors specialists, and that the families of the flight crew had been approached at their residences early in the investigation [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/india/indian-investigators-have-conducted-psychological-autopsy-air-india-crash-probe-2026-07-15/).
- The father of the AI-171 captain, Pushkar Raj Sabharwal, had filed a lawsuit after an AAIB home visit during which he said officials implied his son cut the fuel to the engines after takeoff; the AAIB said media speculation and blame narratives had made some witnesses "restrictive and non-responsive" [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/india/indian-investigators-have-conducted-psychological-autopsy-air-india-crash-probe-2026-07-15/).
- On 30 July 2026, Nigerian President Bola Ahmed Tinubu said problems in Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger were "adding more fuel" to Nigeria's insecurity, noting some terrorists involved in recent attacks and abductions (including the Oriire schoolchildren incident) were non-Nigerians; on 6 August 2026 the AES foreign ministers jointly rejected the remarks as "rapid, very simplistic and surprising" [secondary] (https://zambianobserver.com/burkina-faso-mali-and-niger-reject-tinubus-claim-that-sahel-terrorism-is-fuelling-nigerias-security-crisis/).
- US trade policy: the July 2026 round of 12.5% tariffs on 60 partners (see June entry) kept trade tensions elevated through the summer [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- Euro-area inflation pressures persisted through the summer ahead of the ECB's September decision (see August–September entries) [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/).
- The AAIB's July court filing stressed the probe had entered the analysis phase "with findings and conclusions being drawn across operational, technical, human-factors and organisational areas" [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/india/indian-investigators-have-conducted-psychological-autopsy-air-india-crash-probe-2026-07-15/).
- The Tinubu–AES diplomatic spat illustrated the Sahel's regional spillover framing: Nigeria's president linked Sahel collapse to Nigerian insecurity (citing the Oriire schoolchildren abductions), while the AES bloc called the analysis "very simplistic" and pointed to its own forces' sacrifices [secondary] (https://zambianobserver.com/burkina-faso-mali-and-niger-reject-tinubus-claim-that-sahel-terrorism-is-fuelling-nigerias-security-crisis/).
- The July 12.5% tariff round on 60 partners was the second major US tariff escalation of the year after February's 10% worldwide bridge levy [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).

### August 2026

- Eurostat's preliminary data showed eurozone inflation rising to 3.3% in August 2026 from 2.9% in July, driven by energy prices; core inflation eased to 2.4% from 2.5% [independent via Morningstar] (https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/economy/ecb-hikes-rates-raises-both-inflation-growth-forecasts).
- On 10 August 2026 an earthquake struck Colombia; OCHA's 18 September situation update put the toll at 335 deaths and 486,917 people affected as of 17 September, figures flagged as provisional [official] (https://reliefweb.int/report/el-salvador/latin-america-caribbean-weekly-situation-update-18-september-2026).
- On 4 August 2026, Air India flight AI2379 (Phuket–Delhi, Airbus A320neo, registration VT-EXO) experienced a sudden in-flight upset: USA Today reported a sudden loss of altitude during cruise with 13 passengers and four crew hospitalized, the aircraft landing safely in Delhi [independent] (https://www.usatoday.com/story/travel/airline-news/2026/08/05/air-india-flight-sudden-drop-injuring-passengers/91184231007/); a specialist outlet, citing India's AAIB preliminary investigation, described a simultaneous loss of pressure across all three hydraulic systems at 36,000 feet and 24 people injured — a sharp conflict with the airline-attributed account, flagged in Section 4 [secondary] (https://aviationnews.eu/news/2026/09/air-india-a320neo-suffers-rare-triple-hydraulic-failure-at-36000-feet-injuring-24/).
- Taiwan held its Han Kuang military exercises in August 2026, focused on resilience: a simulated presidential evacuation, internet-disruption drills and drone employment, according to the Institute for the Study of War's China–Taiwan update of 14 August 2026 (analytical, not an official source) [secondary] (https://understandingwar.org/research/china-taiwan/china-taiwan-update-august-14-2026/).
- On 25 August 2026, SOUTHCOM announced its 68th vessel strike of the anti-cartel campaign, killing four people in the Caribbean; the 69th followed on 9 September (three killed) [secondary via Just Security timeline] (https://www.justsecurity.org/124002/timeline-vessel-strikes-related-actions/).
- On 27 August 2026, the US-based Foundation for Aviation Safety (FAS) wrote to AAIB Director General G. V. G. Yugandhar alleging that hundreds of non-public documents on the AI-171 aircraft (VT-ANB) — outlining what FAS called chronic electrical, electronic and flight-computer failures — had been omitted from public investigation reports, in alleged breach of ICAO Annex 13; the AAIB had not publicly responded in the sources consulted [secondary] (https://www.latestly.com/india/news/air-india-ai-171-probe-us-aviation-safety-group-alleges-aaib-ignored-hundreds-of-documents-on-system-failures-in-reports-7608295.html).
- The S&P 500 set its all-time high in August 2026, per AP reporting on 22 September that the index was sitting 0.4% below "its all-time high set last month" [independent] (https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/).
- Secondary press in September reported ACLED data putting Sahel jihadist-linked violence on course for record 2026 levels: 2,145 events already logged in 2026 versus 2,845 in all of 2025 and just 63 in 2016, with the annual total on pace to exceed 3,000; the same coverage cited UN figures of more than 9,000 deaths from security incidents across Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger in 2025 [secondary] (https://theenglishchronicle.com/News/31954/; http://knowafrika123.blogspot.com/2026/09/sahel-violence-al-qaida-is-record-levels.html).
- The August eurozone print (3.3% headline, 2.4% core) showed energy as the driver and underlying pressures as cooler — the split that framed the ECB's September decision to hike while noting second-round effects had not yet materialized [independent] (https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/economy/ecb-hikes-rates-raises-both-inflation-growth-forecasts; https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/).
- The Colombia earthquake's toll (335 dead, 486,917 affected as of 17 September) made it one of 2026's deadliest single natural disasters in the consulted sources; OCHA flagged the figures as provisional [official] (https://reliefweb.int/report/el-salvador/latin-america-caribbean-weekly-situation-update-18-september-2026).
- The AI2379 incident's casualty conflict (17 per the airline via USA Today vs. 24 per the specialist outlet citing AAIB) could not be resolved from the sources consulted; both versions are preserved in Section 4 [independent vs. secondary].
- ISW's August Han Kuang assessment was explicitly analytical rather than official Taiwanese reporting; it described resilience-themed drills (presidential-evacuation simulation, internet-disruption scenarios, drone use) [secondary] (https://understandingwar.org/research/china-taiwan/china-taiwan-update-august-14-2026/).
- The 68th and 69th vessel strikes (25 August: four killed; 9 September: three killed) showed the campaign's steady tempo through late summer; SOUTHCOM announced each with video and "confirmed intelligence" language without releasing the underlying intelligence [secondary] (https://www.justsecurity.org/124002/timeline-vessel-strikes-related-actions/).
