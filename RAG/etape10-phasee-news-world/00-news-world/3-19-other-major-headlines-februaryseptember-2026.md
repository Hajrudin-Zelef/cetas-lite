### 3.20 Cross-cutting patterns, February–September 2026

- The Iran war was the period's master variable: it roughly doubled oil prices, pushed eurozone inflation past 3%, forced the ECB into two hikes and the Fed into its first hike since 2023, and kept equity markets oscillating between AI-driven records and energy-driven anxiety [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/; https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/).
- Electoral politics tilted right in Latin America (Peru, Colombia) while the US approached its midterms with Democrats ahead in generic-ballot polling — directionally opposite signals whose outcomes were both still pending at the cutoff [independent] (https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/07/05/peruvian-and-colombian-presidential-elections-expatriate-vote-helps-far-right-clinch-victory_6755161_4.html; https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/09/15/democrats-republicans-trump-new-poll-midterm-elections/91772998007/).
- Wealth concentration accelerated on AI: Hurun's 114 AI-linked billionaires (46 new) and Forbes' record $20.1 trillion total both pointed to technology — and private valuations like SpaceX and xAI — as the dominant wealth engine of 2026 [vendor-reported] (https://pluboard.com/2026/03/06/wealth/hurun-global-rich-list-2026-billionaires-ai-boom/; https://newshub.medianet.com.au/2026/03/forbes-40th-annual-worlds-billionaires-list/143235/).
- Violence concentrated while global terrorism statistically receded: the Sahel headed for record jihadist-linked event counts even as the 2026 Global Peace Index reported terrorism deaths at their lowest since 2008 worldwide — a divergence the sources noted explicitly [secondary] (https://theenglishchronicle.com/News/31954/).
- Multilateralism fragmented and regrouped simultaneously: the G7 refocused on critical minerals and AI with an expanded outreach guest list, the BRICS met in New Delhi with a 140-point agenda, the AES bloc hardened outside ECOWAS, and the US built a parallel "Shield of the Americas" security architecture — four different geometries of order in one year [official/secondary/independent].
- Markets displayed the year's central paradox: record equity highs (Nasdaq, August S&P peak) alongside the first synchronized US–eurozone hiking cycle since the war shock, with corporate profits — not multiple expansion alone — cited by AP as the fundamental support [independent] (https://www.reflector.com/news/national/wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-falls-below-100-per-barrel/article_991814cc-8304-5c54-9e8b-186be3259451.html).
- Information quality itself was a 2026 story: the Iran war's core facts beyond wire-anchored economics, the Sahel's April battle details, Sudan's displacement totals and Gaza's casualty claims all circulated in weak-source form, requiring the attribution discipline applied throughout this file — see Section 4.

## 5. Glossary

- **AAIB** — India's Aircraft Accident Investigation Bureau; led the AI-171 probe [independent].
- **ACLED** — Armed Conflict Location and Event Data project; conflict-event monitor cited for Sahel figures [secondary via press].
- **AES** — Alliance/Confederation of Sahel States (Mali, Burkina Faso, Niger); junta-led bloc that left ECOWAS in January 2025 [secondary].
- **BRICS** — intergovernmental grouping (Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa plus 2024–2025 expansion members); 2026 summit in New Delhi [secondary].
- **Deposit facility rate** — ECB benchmark rate for bank overnight deposits; 2.50% from 16 September 2026 [official].
- **Donroe Doctrine** — Trump's label for his Western Hemisphere security policy, blending his name with the Monroe Doctrine [independent].
- **DTO** — Designated Terrorist Organization; US designation applied by the administration to cartels and cited by Hegseth for boat strikes [independent].
- **ECOWAS** — Economic Community of West African States; the AES trio completed withdrawal in January 2025 [secondary].
- **FAS** — Foundation for Aviation Safety; US group that challenged the AAIB's AI-171 transparency [secondary].
- **G7/G20** — groups of major advanced economies (G7) and major economies (G20); 2026 summits in France (G7) and scheduled Miami (G20) [official/secondary].
- **Han Kuang** — Taiwan's annual military exercises; 2026 edition focused on resilience and drones [secondary].
- **ICAO Annex 13** — international standards for aircraft-accident investigation, invoked by FAS against the AAIB [secondary].
- **JNIM** — Jama'at Nusrat ul-Islam wa al-Muslimin; al-Qaeda-linked jihadist coalition active in the Sahel [secondary].
- **Neutral rate** — policy rate estimated to neither stimulate nor restrict growth; ECB staff band cited as 1.75%–2.50% [independent].
- **OCHA** — UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs; source of the Colombia earthquake figures via ReliefWeb [official].
- **RSF/SAF** — Sudan's Rapid Support Forces (paramilitary) and Sudanese Armed Forces; the two warring parties [secondary].
- **SOUTHCOM** — US Southern Command; announced the Caribbean/Eastern Pacific vessel strikes [independent/secondary].
- **Shield of the Americas** — US-led anti-cartel coalition launched March 2026 [independent].
- **Brent crude** — international oil benchmark; the price series (~$72 pre-war → ~$110 mid-September → $99.25 on 22 September 2026) is the period's key energy indicator [independent].
- **bp / basis points** — 100bp = 1 percentage point; central-bank moves in 2026 were 25bp increments [official/independent].
- **FTO** — Foreign Terrorist Organization; US designation the administration applied to drug cartels [independent].
- **GSOIA** — Canada–France defence and industrial cooperation agreement announced 12 June 2026 [official].
- **UNGA** — UN General Assembly; opened 22 September 2026 in New York [independent].
- **YTD** — year-to-date; equity index gains cited as of 21–22 September 2026 [independent/secondary].
- **ETF** — exchange-traded fund; semiconductor ETFs (SMH, SOXX) tracked the September chip rally [secondary].
- **Psychological autopsy** — post-mortem behavioral reconstruction conducted in the AI-171 probe; the AAIB filing did not identify the subject [independent].
- **Trilateral format** — the Trump–Putin–Zelensky meeting structure Zelensky endorsed on 22 September 2026 [independent].
- **Go-fast vessel** — small high-speed boat type repeatedly named in SOUTHCOM strike announcements [secondary].
- **Joint Task Force Western Hemisphere** — the SOUTHCOM-directed task force publicly credited with executing the 2026 vessel strikes [secondary].
- **Generic ballot** — poll question on party preference for Congress; the NYT/Siena 15 Sep 2026 read was Democrats 51%, Republicans 43% [independent].
- **Expatriate vote** — votes cast by citizens abroad; Le Monde credited them with helping far-right victories in Peru and Colombia [independent].
- **Strait of Hormuz** — chokepoint for Gulf oil exports; an Iranian MP was reported (secondary, [unverified]) to have announced its closure during the 2026 war.
- **ACARS / AHM** — aircraft data-link and health-monitoring systems whose data streams FAS alleged showed pre-crash AI-171 system failures [secondary].
- **Board of Peace** — entity behind a May 2026 six-month Gaza report; institutional standing unverified [secondary].
