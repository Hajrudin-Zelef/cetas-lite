# 3.10 Europe: the EU, the ECB, and the G7

### 3.10 Europe: the EU, the ECB, and the G7

- The ECB raised rates twice in 2026 — 25bp to 2.25% on 11 June and 25bp to 2.50% on 10 September (deposit facility; effective 16 September) — both explicitly tied to energy-driven inflation from the Iran war; the September move put the deposit rate at the top of the 1.75%–2.50% "neutral" range [independent/official] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/; https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/press_conference/visual-mps/2026/html/mopo_statement_explained_september.en.html).
- Eurozone inflation reached 3.3% in August 2026 (from 2.9% in July), "well past 3%" per Reuters' September read, versus the ECB's 2% target; the ECB projected 3.0% average inflation for 2026, 2.5% for 2027 and 2.1% for 2028, with GDP growth of 0.9% in 2026 [independent/official] (https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/economy/ecb-hikes-rates-raises-both-inflation-growth-forecasts; https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/press_conference/visual-mps/2026/html/mopo_statement_explained_september.en.html).
- The ECB's own September statement noted the economy "holding up better than expected," with manufacturing helped by government defence and infrastructure spending, firms investing in AI, and exports picking up — while warning the outlook "remains highly uncertain" [official] (https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/press_conference/visual-mps/2026/html/mopo_statement_explained_september.en.html).
- Germany's 10-year government bond yield climbed to 3.482%, its highest since 2011, reflecting the renewed tightening cycle [secondary] (https://business-news-today.com/ecb-raises-rates-to-2-5-as-100-oil-pushes-eurozone-inflation-outlook-higher/).
- The 2026 G7 summit (France, June; see chronology) produced the broadened Critical Minerals Resilience and Production Alliance and a Canada–France GSOIA defence-industrial agreement; outreach guests included Brazil, Egypt, India, Kenya, South Korea, Saudi Arabia, Ukraine and the UAE [official/secondary] (https://windows.einnews.com/pr_news/920308118/prime-minister-carney-secures-new-partnerships-in-defence-and-critical-minerals-at-the-2026-g7-leaders-summit).
- A NATO summit in 2026 and EU-level electoral or institutional developments were not covered by the research for this phase — registered as gaps in Section 4.

### 3.11 Summits: G7, BRICS, and the road to the G20

- G7 2026 (France, June): critical-minerals alliance broadened; defence-industrial deals (Canada–France GSOIA); outreach to eight guest leaders [official/secondary] (https://windows.einnews.com/pr_news/920308118/prime-minister-carney-secures-new-partnerships-in-defence-and-critical-minerals-at-the-2026-g7-leaders-summit).
- BRICS 2026 (New Delhi, 12–13 September): theme "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability"; a 140-point New Delhi declaration reported covering governance, trade, payments, AI, finance, health and sustainability [secondary] (https://www.livemint.com/news/india/brics-summit-2026-dates-venue-timings-and-complete-itinerary-11788941919316.html; https://globalleaderstoday.online/brics-summit-sets-new-agenda-for-global-cooperation/).
- G20: the Miami summit was announced for December 2026 — after the cutoff — and is reported only as scheduled [secondary] (https://globalleaderstoday.online/brics-summit-sets-new-agenda-for-global-cooperation/).
- UN General Assembly (New York, opened 22 September 2026): Trump's address centered on the anti-cartel campaign, the "Donroe Doctrine," pressure on Cuba, and claims of reasserted American power in the hemisphere [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/).

### 3.12 World elections held or scheduled in 2026

- Peru (7 June 2026): Keiko Fujimori elected president with about 50.13% and a margin of just over 49,000 votes; Le Monde (5 July) reported expatriate votes helped the far right clinch victory [independent] (https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/07/05/peruvian-and-colombian-presidential-elections-expatriate-vote-helps-far-right-clinch-victory_6755161_4.html).
- Colombia (21 June 2026): Abelardo De La Espriella elected president, cementing Latin America's rightward shift; Reuters noted the final count was still subject to verification procedures at the time of reporting [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/de-la-espriella-win-colombia-cements-latin-americas-rightward-shift-2026-06-22/).

### 3.13 The macro picture: inflation, rates, and growth

- United States: the Federal Reserve raised its benchmark rate 25bp to 3.75%–4% on 16 September 2026 (12–0 vote), the first US hike since 2023, citing persistent inflation and high energy prices; the official statement text was reproduced by Morningstar/Dow Jones [secondary for the reporting; official statement text] (https://www.usatoday.com/story/money/economy/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-meeting-updates--live/91746863007/; https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202609165837/text-of-feds-september-monetary-policy-statement).
- Euro area: two ECB hikes (to 2.25% in June, 2.50% in September); August inflation at 3.3% headline and 2.4% core; 2026 inflation projected at 3.0%, growth at 0.9% [independent/official] (see Section 3.10).
- The common driver was the Iran-war energy shock: Brent roughly doubled from ~$72 pre-war to nearly $110 in mid-September before easing; natural-gas prices and below-normal European storage added winter-heating-season risk [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/; https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/).
- Growth proved more resilient than feared: the ECB lifted its 2026 euro-area growth projection to 0.9% from 0.8%, citing defence/infrastructure spending, AI investment and firmer exports [official] (https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/press_conference/visual-mps/2026/html/mopo_statement_explained_september.en.html); US corporate profit reports were described by AP as a main reason equities held near records despite expensive oil [independent] (https://www.reflector.com/news/national/wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-falls-below-100-per-barrel/article_991814cc-8304-5c54-9e8b-186be3259451.html).
- The Bank of England, Bank of Japan and other central banks' 2026 decisions were not covered by the research for this phase — a gap (see Section 4).

### 3.14 Energy and equity markets

- Oil: Brent settled at $99.25 on 22 September 2026 (after briefly dipping below $98 intraday), versus $100.34 on 21 September (−3.4%), about $105 on 10 September after shipping-attack escalation, nearly $110 the prior week, and ~$72 before the Iran war — a war-driven doubling followed by partial relief [independent/secondary] (https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/; https://ts2.tech/en/nasdaq-closes-at-a-record-sp-500-stops-0-4-short-as-oil-retreats/; https://business-news-today.com/ecb-raises-rates-to-2-5-as-100-oil-pushes-eurozone-inflation-outlook-higher/).
- US equities: the S&P 500 set its all-time high in August 2026 and closed at 7,764.64 on 22 September (+13.4% year-to-date), 0.4% below that record; the Nasdaq Composite closed at a record 27,244.28 on 22 September (+17.2% YTD); the Dow closed at 51,863.69 (+7.9% YTD); the Russell 2000 at 2,889.92 (+16.4% YTD) [independent] (https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/; https://tvnewscheck.com/business/article/dow-drops-185-nasdaq-rises-122-sp-500-ends-flat/).
- Market commentary pointed to strong corporate profits supporting valuations despite expensive oil, alongside "jitters about whether stocks in the artificial-intelligence industry shot too high"; Morgan Stanley strategist Michael Wilson identified another oil surge as his main near-term market risk [independent/secondary] (https://www.reflector.com/news/national/wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-falls-below-100-per-barrel/article_991814cc-8304-5c54-9e8b-186be3259451.html; https://ts2.tech/en/nasdaq-closes-at-a-record-sp-500-stops-0-4-short-as-oil-retreats/).
- The 10-year US Treasury yield eased to 4.95% on 21 September from 5.01% the prior session [secondary] (https://ts2.tech/en/nasdaq-closes-at-a-record-sp-500-stops-0-4-short-as-oil-retreats/).
- Chipmakers led the late-September rally: the VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) and iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) added 1.7%–2.1% on 22 September, tracking gains in Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom and Micron, ahead of the planned Trump–Xi meeting [secondary] (https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/nasdaq-jumps-to-close-at-record-as-chipmakers-climb-oil-prices-cool-aapl-meta-spcx-msft-vktx-in-focus/cZM5GHbRB7L).
