# February 2026

### February 2026

- The US–Israeli war on Iran began in late February 2026, a start date corroborated by Reuters reporting on 10 September 2026, which described the conflict as "the U.S.-Israeli war on Iran, which began in late February," and noted that "all hopes are gone for a quick end" to it [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/ecb-raises-interest-rates-fight-off-inflation-jump-2026-09-10/).
- Secondary outlets place the opening of the joint offensive on 28 February 2026, describing a US operation dubbed "Epic Fury" alongside an Israeli operation dubbed "Roaring Lion"; these operation names and the precise start date could not be corroborated by any wire or official source consulted and are therefore [unverified] (https://upscmentorpiyush.com/iran-us-israel-war-2026/; https://sesamedisk.com/iran-us-israel-conflict-2026-timeline/).
- The war triggered an immediate energy shock: Brent crude, which AP reporting puts at about $72 per barrel before the war with Iran began, rose sharply in the weeks that followed [independent] (https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/).
- Secondary sources claim the offensive killed Iran's Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei and that the Iranian regime collapsed in the months that followed; no wire or official source consulted confirms either claim, so both remain [unverified] (https://datacommlab.com/booths/docs/US-Iran-Conflict-20260327.pdf).
- On the tariff front, Reuters' retrospective timeline reports that in February 2026 the US Supreme Court struck down President Trump's emergency tariffs, prompting Trump to impose a temporary worldwide 10% levy under a different legal authority [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- The US campaign of lethal strikes against alleged drug-trafficking boats in the Caribbean and Eastern Pacific, which began in early September 2025, continued through February 2026; CNN reported on 2 November 2025 that the operation that month was the 15th known strike since the campaign began in early September [independent] (https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/02/politics/us-strikes-drug-boat-caribbean-intl-hnk?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta).
- The pre-war oil baseline matters for everything that followed: AP's September 2026 reporting put Brent at about $72 per barrel before the Iran war began — the level against which the subsequent doubling to nearly $110 was measured [independent] (https://www.sfntoday.com/2026/09/22/market-update-wall-street-holds-near-its-record-after-brent-oil-briefly-falls-below-98-per-barrel/).
- The February 2026 Supreme Court decision striking down the emergency tariffs forced the administration onto a different legal authority for its tariff regime; the temporary worldwide 10% levy was the bridge instrument while new tariff rounds were prepared [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- February 2026 also sat inside the Winter Olympics window (covered in Phase 10D, Sport) and preceded the March billionaire-list season that would document the AI-driven wealth surge [secondary].
- No February 2026 Ukraine ceasefire was reported; the war's diplomatic track would only visibly accelerate in September 2026 [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraines-zelenskiy-says-he-discussed-peace-efforts-energy-ceasefire-with-trump-2026-09-22/).

### March 2026

- President Trump launched the "Shield of the Americas" coalition in March 2026, gathering more than a dozen regional leaders in Florida to coordinate against drug cartels [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/).
- The coalition included governments closely aligned with Trump — Argentina, Ecuador and El Salvador — with Colombia and Peru joining after Trump-aligned presidents took power there in 2026; Brazil and Mexico did not join [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/).
- Ecuador had been conducting similar anti-trafficking missions with US forces since March 2026, while Guatemala later backed out of the campaign, according to secondary reporting on SOUTHCOM announcements [secondary] (https://www.marineinsight.com/u-s-forces-strike-suspected-narco-boat-in-caribbean-killing-4-individuals-in-latest-anti-drug-operation/).
- Forbes released its 40th annual World's Billionaires list in March 2026 (reported 11 March): 3,428 billionaires worth a combined $20.1 trillion; Elon Musk ranked first at $839 billion, followed by Larry Page ($257B), Sergey Brin ($237B), Jeff Bezos ($224B) and Mark Zuckerberg ($222B); 390 new billionaires joined; the United States counted 989 billionaires; Donald Trump's fortune was estimated at $6.5 billion, ranking him 645th [vendor-reported] (https://newshub.medianet.com.au/2026/03/forbes-40th-annual-worlds-billionaires-list/143235/).
- The Hurun Research Institute released the Hurun Global Rich List 2026 in March 2026: 4,020 billionaires worldwide, up 578 year-on-year — roughly two new billionaires per day, the fastest pace since the list began; Elon Musk topped it at $792 billion (+89%), ahead of Jeff Bezos ($300B) and Larry Page ($271B, entering the top three for the first time) [vendor-reported via secondary press] (https://pluboard.com/2026/03/06/wealth/hurun-global-rich-list-2026-billionaires-ai-boom/; https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-makers/hurun-global-rich-list-2026-elon-musk-leads-india-ranks-third-with-308-billionaires-ws-l-92876.htm).
- Hurun counted China as the leading billionaire hub with 1,110 (+287), ahead of the United States with 1,000 (+130) and India with 308; AI companies were the biggest driver of new wealth, with 114 AI-linked billionaires including 46 newcomers [vendor-reported via secondary press] (https://en.walaw.press/articles/elon_musk_tops_hurun_rich_list_with_$792_billion_as_billionaire_ranks_surge/GQPQMSFMGSGL).
- Fighting in Sudan's fourth year continued, with the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) dominant in Darfur after the fall of El Fasher in late October 2025 and the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) dominant in the north, east and center, per secondary reporting [secondary] (https://globalwatch.info/en_GB/articles/gc7/features/2026/09/21/feature-01).
- The Ukraine war continued with no ceasefire in place; Kyiv and Moscow remained far apart on territory (Donbas) and security guarantees, the issues that would still dominate diplomacy in September [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraines-zelenskiy-says-he-discussed-peace-efforts-energy-ceasefire-with-trump-2026-09-22/).
- The "Shield of the Americas" launch in Florida gathered "more than a dozen regional leaders," per Reuters' September retrospective — the coalition's founding moment [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/).
- On the cartels' designation: the Trump administration had designated drug cartels as foreign terrorist organizations upon returning to power "early last year" (2025), the legal framing underpinning the 2026 strike campaign [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/trump-vows-unmatched-military-might-he-rallies-regional-anti-cartel-allies-2026-09-22/).
- Forbes' March 2026 list was the 40th annual edition; the $20.1 trillion combined wealth and 3,428 billionaires both set records for the franchise [vendor-reported] (https://newshub.medianet.com.au/2026/03/forbes-40th-annual-worlds-billionaires-list/143235/).
- Hurun's March 2026 edition likewise set records: the first time the list exceeded 4,000 billionaires, with the 578 net additions arriving at roughly two per day — "the fastest pace since the list began" [vendor-reported via secondary press] (https://pluboard.com/2026/03/06/wealth/hurun-global-rich-list-2026-billionaires-ai-boom/).
- Among Hurun's AI newcomers: MiniMax founder Yan Junjie (about $3.6 billion) and Zhipu's Liu Debing (about $1.2 billion) [secondary] (https://en.walaw.press/articles/elon_musk_tops_hurun_rich_list_with_$792_billion_as_billionaire_ranks_surge/GQPQMSFMGSGL).
- Hurun's country reshuffle was historic: China's 1,110 billionaires (+287) retook the top spot from the United States' 1,000 (+130); India's 308 kept third place [vendor-reported via secondary press] (https://pluboard.com/2026/03/06/wealth/hurun-global-rich-list-2026-billionaires-ai-boom/).

### April 2026

- On 21 April 2026, Pope Francis died at the age of 88, according to Reuters; his funeral was reported to have drawn about 200,000 attendees on 26 April, and a conclave held 6–8 May elected Cardinal Robert Francis Prevost as Pope Leo XIV [independent for the death; secondary for funeral and conclave details] (https://www.reuters.com/world/vatican-pope-francis-dies-aged-88-vatican-statement-says-2026-04-21/; https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/pope-leo-xiv-cardinal-robert-144530796.html).
- The new pope, aged 69 at election, was reported as the second-youngest pope of the modern era, the third non-European pope, and the second from the Americas [secondary] (https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/pope-leo-xiv-cardinal-robert-144530796.html).
- On 25 April 2026, jihadist fighters and Tuareg separatist allies launched what one secondary outlet called the largest assault on Mali in nearly 15 years, targeting Gao, Menaka and Kidal; the same report claims the northern town of Kidal was captured and that Mali's Defence Minister Sadio Camara was killed — none of which was corroborated by a wire source consulted, so treat as [unverified] (http://panxmedia.blogspot.com/2026/05/alliance-of-sahel-states-confirms-joint.html).
- The Alliance of Sahel States (AES) — Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger — responded with what Niger's government described as "intense air campaigns" in Malian territory; the AES joint anti-jihadist force, initially 5,000 troops, was reported increased to 15,000 in mid-April [secondary] (http://panxmedia.blogspot.com/2026/05/alliance-of-sahel-states-confirms-joint.html).
- In Gaza, Reuters reported on 30 April 2026 that Israeli strikes killed four Palestinians, according to medics, amid a new push in Cairo to revive the October 2025 truce; the report noted that phase two of the plan — further Israeli withdrawal, a transitional authority, a multinational force and Hamas disarmament — was making little progress [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/israeli-strikes-kill-four-gaza-medics-say-amid-new-ceasefire-push-2026-04-30/).
- The ECB's June rate decision was foreshadowed by rising energy-driven inflation: euro-area inflation forecasts for 2026 were revised up to 3.0% with a projected peak of 3.4% in Q3–Q4 2026, and GDP growth was cut to 0.8%, raising stagflation concerns in secondary analysis [secondary] (https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/06/23/european-central-bank-inflation-2026/).
- The papal funeral on 26 April 2026 reportedly drew about 200,000 attendees; the conclave that followed ran 6–8 May [secondary] (https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/pope-leo-xiv-cardinal-robert-144530796.html).
- In the Sahel aftermath of 25 April, Burkina Faso's Defence Minister Célestin Simporé, speaking for the AES at Camara's funeral, vowed to "hunt down" the "assassins," while about a thousand people rallied in Niamey in solidarity with Mali — both details from the same single secondary source and [unverified] (http://panxmedia.blogspot.com/2026/05/alliance-of-sahel-states-confirms-joint.html).
- The Azawad Liberation Front's spokesman Mohamed Elmaouloud Ramadane publicly called on Burkina Faso and Niger to "stay out" of the Mali fighting hours after the 25 April attacks began [secondary] (http://panxmedia.blogspot.com/2026/05/alliance-of-sahel-states-confirms-joint.html).
- April's Gaza diplomacy centered on Cairo talks aimed at reviving the October 2025 truce; the 30 April strikes that killed four (per medics) underscored how fragile the truce remained [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/israeli-strikes-kill-four-gaza-medics-say-amid-new-ceasefire-push-2026-04-30/).

### May 2026

- President Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping held a summit in Beijing in May 2026; Reuters' retrospective reports the creation of planned trade and investment councils and Chinese purchase commitments as outcomes, describing the sequence as moving from tariff war to trade truce [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- CNN reported on 14 May 2026 that Gaza risked a lasting division as Israel and Hamas remained at odds over implementing the October 2025 ceasefire, with disagreement over the truce's terms threatening to entrench a split of the territory [independent] (https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/14/middleeast/gaza-israel-ceasefire-permanent-division-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta).
- A "Board of Peace" six-month report on Gaza dated May 2026 was published online, reviewing the first half-year of the post-truce arrangement [secondary] (https://israeled.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Board-of-Peace-first-6-month-report-on-Gaza-May-2026.pdf).
- The papal conclave of 6–8 May 2026 elected Pope Leo XIV (see April entry) [secondary] (https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/pope-leo-xiv-cardinal-robert-144530796.html).
- Ukraine diplomacy remained stalled through May, with the Donbas and security guarantees as the core disputes that would persist into the autumn [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/world/ukraines-zelenskiy-says-he-discussed-peace-efforts-energy-ceasefire-with-trump-2026-09-22/).
- The Beijing summit's reported deliverables — planned bilateral trade and investment councils plus Chinese purchase commitments — were the concrete face of the "tariff war to trade truce" pivot; the 24 September follow-up meeting was already on the calendar [independent] (https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/how-trump-xi-went-tariff-war-trade-truce-2026-09-21/).
- CNN's 14 May Gaza warning framed the risk as entrenchment: disagreements over truce implementation could harden into a lasting territorial split [independent] (https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/14/middleeast/gaza-israel-ceasefire-permanent-division-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta).
- The May 2026 "Board of Peace" Gaza report's provenance could not be established from the sources consulted; it is cited here as a document that exists, not as an authoritative assessment [secondary] (https://israeled.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Board-of-Peace-first-6-month-report-on-Gaza-May-2026.pdf).
