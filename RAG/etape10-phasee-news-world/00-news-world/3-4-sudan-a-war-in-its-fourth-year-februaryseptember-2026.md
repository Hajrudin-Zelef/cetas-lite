### 3.4 Sudan: a war in its fourth year (February–September 2026)

- Sudan's war between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF) continued through the period, entering its fourth year; secondary reporting in September 2026 described the SAF as dominant in the north, east and center and the RSF as dominant in Darfur after the fall of El Fasher in late October 2025 [secondary] (https://globalwatch.info/en_GB/articles/gc7/features/2026/09/21/feature-01).
- Drone strikes and displacement were still being reported in September 2026 in Darfur, Kordofan and Blue Nile State [secondary] (https://globalwatch.info/en_GB/articles/gc7/features/2026/09/21/feature-01).
- The UN warned of persistent targeting of civilians in Sudan, per a 21 September 2026 report citing UN sources [secondary] (http://www.fananews.com/language/en/un-warns-of-persistent-targeting-of-civilians-in-sudan/).
- One secondary outlet cited roughly 13 million displaced people as of 21 September 2026; the figure was not verified against UN/OCHA data in the sources consulted and is [unverified] (https://globalwatch.info/en_GB/articles/gc7/features/2026/09/21/feature-01).
- Allegations of genocide, massacres, or specific death tolls in Sudan (including claims around El Fasher) must be attributed precisely to the UN body or investigators making them; no verified overall death toll for 2026 was found in the sources consulted — see Section 4.
- The September 2026 reporting emphasized the war's geographic split (SAF north/east/center, RSF Darfur) and its continued use of drone warfare, with displacement still being generated in Darfur, Kordofan and Blue Nile — a protracted-war pattern rather than a decisive phase [secondary] (https://globalwatch.info/en_GB/articles/gc7/features/2026/09/21/feature-01).
