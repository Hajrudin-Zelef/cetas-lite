# April 2026

## April 2026

### Chips and supply chain

- **April 2026** — The Elec (via trade press) reported Samsung Display had struck a **3-year exclusive deal with Apple for foldable iPhone OLED panels**, with an initial phase of around 3 million panels using CoE (Color Filter on Encapsulation) technology and M14 material. BOE reportedly failed Apple's quality bar for foldable panels. [secondary] https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/04/samsung-just-won-the-contract-that-could-define-the-future-of-the-iphone.html
- **April 2026** — eWeek, citing Bloomberg and Reuters, reported Apple had held **early-stage talks with Intel** and evaluated **Samsung's Texas fabs** as possible US-based manufacturing options for future iPhone, iPad and Mac processors, aiming to reduce dependence on TSMC as AI-driven chip demand tightened supply. No orders resulted; discussions remained preliminary. [independent] https://www.eweek.com/news/apple-intel-samsung-us-chip-production-ai-crunch/

### Cybersecurity

- **6–12 April 2026** — Check Point Research's weekly threat roundup listed: the **Los Angeles Police Department / LA City Attorney** breach involving a digital storage system with **7.7 terabytes and 337,000+ files** exposed (personnel records, internal-affairs material, unredacted PII); **ChipSoft**, the Dutch healthcare software vendor behind the HiX platform, hit by **ransomware** that forced it to disable patient and provider services, with multiple hospitals disconnecting and possible patient-data access; the **Qilin ransomware group** claiming a late-March attack on German political party **Die Linke** (party said membership databases unaffected); and **Bitcoin Depot** (25,000+ crypto kiosks) disclosing attackers stole credentials tied to digital-asset settlement accounts and moved **50+ BTC (over $3.6M)** before access was blocked. [independent] https://research.checkpoint.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Threat_Intelligence_News_2026-04-13.pdf
- **3 April 2026** — The **Hong Kong Hospital Authority** suffered an insider-threat breach in its Kowloon East cluster affecting **56,000+ patients**: names, HKID numbers, dates of birth, hospital file numbers, appointment dates and surgical-procedure details were compromised. [secondary] https://zeron.one/healthcare-data-breach-2026/
- **9 April 2026** — **Signature Healthcare** was hit by **ANUBIS ransomware**. [secondary] https://zeron.one/healthcare-data-breach-2026/
- **10 April 2026** — **ACN Healthcare** was identified as a breach victim with the **Lynx ransomware group** claiming responsibility; patient count and data types were undisclosed as of mid-April. [secondary] https://zeron.one/healthcare-data-breach-2026/
- **April 2026 (discovered)** — A forum post claimed a full compromise of **National Oil Ethiopia PLC** including an 800GB+ ERP database, described as an intrusion chain from Exchange ProxyLogon exploitation to ransomware deployment. Claim only; no victim or authority confirmation found. [unverified] https://github.com/hatchepsoute/afrintel/blob/HEAD/CyberAttackAfrica/2026/04-april/victims.md

### Chips and hardware roadmaps

- **June 2026** — Intel confirmed (Goldman Sachs Technology conference, via Tom's Hardware) an **Arrow Lake refresh in 2026** with **Nova Lake later in the year**, acknowledging "holes to fill on the desktop front" while asserting confidence in the roadmap. The refresh was expected to keep core counts with clock/binning improvements. [independent] https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/intel-confirms-arrow-lake-refresh-set-for-2026-nova-lake-later-that-year-company-admits-there-are-holes-to-fill-on-the-desktop-front-says-it-is-confident-in-the-roadmap
- **June 2026** — Leaks reported **Nova Lake** (desktop, "Core Ultra 400" naming, new LGA 1954 socket, up to 52 hybrid cores, **bLLC — Big Last Level Cache — up to 144 MB** as Intel's answer to AMD 3D V-Cache, Xe3 graphics, DDR5-8000) targeting late 2026 / early 2027, with Computex-floor chatter pointing to a **Q1 2027** retail rollout announced at CES 2027. AMD's counterpart, **Zen 6 "Olympic Ridge" (Ryzen, AM5, up to 24 cores)**, was also expected in 2027. All roadmap specifics are leak-based. [secondary] https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/cpus/intels-next-gen-nova-lake-will-finally-tackle-amds-ryzen-x3d-but-only-with-pricey-k-models-144mb-big-last-level-cache-response-to-3d-v-cache-will-only-come-on-unlocked-desktop-parts https://wccftech.com/intel-nova-lake-desktop-cpu-whispers-early-2027-launch-52-core-power-thermal-details-multi-core-overclocking-z990-z970-boards/

[independent] https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/23/apples-2026-oled-lineup-will-reportedly-rely-entirely-on-samsung-and-lg/ https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/06/23/iphone-fold-touchscreen-macbook-pro-again-rumored-to-have-samsung-displays
