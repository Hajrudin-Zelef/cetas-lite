## Appendix I — Monthly analyst notes (synthesis, not new facts)

- **February** — The month belonged to infrastructure: financing rounds (Databricks $5B), platform tie-ups (Snowflake × OpenAI), and the first independent quantification of the gigawatt buildout (Epoch AI). Model news was integrative rather than generative — GPT-5.2 landing inside the data stack.
- **March** — Conference month: MWC's robot-phone/AI-PC theater and GTC's Vera Rubin platform set the hardware agenda for the year. Security's March was healthcare-heavy (CareCloud) with African public-sector victims (Stats SA) entering the ransomware ledgers.
- **April** — A supply-chain month: Apple's OLED diversification (Samsung vs BOE), US-foundry hedging (Intel/Samsung talks), and a dense cyber week (Check Point's 6–12 April roundup) with healthcare and public-sector victims dominating.
- **May** — Developer-conference month (I/O, Build) with a unified message: the agent layer is the platform now. Cowork's plugin system and the Prometheus 1-GW milestone extended the January–May arc from chatbots to background agents running on gigawatt infrastructure.
- **June** — Friction month: governments slowed launches (GPT-5.6 delay), costs hit consumers (memory-driven price hikes), and roadmaps slipped into rumor (Nova Lake timing). WWDC and Computex kept the product cadence moving underneath.
- **July** — Launch month: two frontier families on the same day (GPT-5.6 vs Muse Spark 1.1), Cowork's mobile/background leap, Google's robotics push, and the year's worst cloud week. China's regulatory/governance moves (companion law, Qwen-for-Apple, WAICO) marked the geopolitical high-water mark.
- **August** — Regulation month: the EU AI Act grew teeth on 2 August while xAI and Google shipped at startup speed (Grok 4.6, Grok Bot, Gemini 3.7 Flash at half price). Wall Street formally entered the buildout ($500B+ financing structures). The second cloud-outage cluster suggested July was pattern, not anomaly.
- **September (1–22)** — Duel month: Anthropic and OpenAI traded flagship launches 24–48 hours apart, then traded price cuts on the same day (22 September), while Apple's first foldable and the Amodei slowdown essay pulled the industry's attention in opposite directions — product acceleration vs. governance deceleration.
