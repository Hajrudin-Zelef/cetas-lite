# Step 10A — Tech News: AI, Chips, Cloud, Cybersecurity (February → 22 September 2026)

- **Track:** Step 10, Phase A (Tech). Companion tracks 10B–10G cover mobile/PC, sport, world, buzz and science separately.
- **Window:** 1 February 2026 → 22 September 2026. January events appear only as labelled context where a February item cannot be understood without them.
- **Cutoff / freshness:** all material compiled on 22 September 2026 (GMT). Items dated after 22 September 2026 are excluded by construction.
- **Method:** every factual claim carries one provenance tag and, where a citable URL was found, a verbatim URL. Tags used: `[official]` (company/government page or filing), `[vendor-reported]` (a vendor's own performance claim repeated by press), `[independent]` (established specialist or general press), `[secondary]` (trade blog, law-firm alert, trade recap, vendor press release reprinted), `[unverified]` (single aggregator/newsletter/GitHub repo, leak-site claim, or item that could not be corroborated in the research window).
- **Quality rule:** no invented events, dates, versions, prices, SKUs or figures. Gaps and contradictions are listed in the register at the end rather than smoothed over. Product names follow the sources verbatim, even when they look odd.
- **Scope note:** consumer mobile and PC launches are tracked in 10B/10C; this file keeps them only where they are a chip, AI or cloud story (e.g. Apple Silicon, foldable supply chain, Pixel/Tensor as Gemini vehicles).

## Provenance legend

- `[official]` — the originating company's own blog/newsroom, a government release, or a court/regulatory document.
- `[vendor-reported]` — benchmark/performance figures claimed by the vendor itself, quoted by press but not independently tested.
- `[independent]` — established technology or general press (Reuters, Bloomberg, TechCrunch, MacRumors, Ars Technica, USA Today, Tom's Hardware, Zacks, 9to5Mac, AppleInsider, Check Point Research, Verizon).
- `[secondary]` — trade outlets, law-firm analyses, curated newsletters, press-release aggregators, specialist blogs.
- `[unverified]` — single-source aggregator notes, leak-site claims, or items with no corroborating coverage found in this research window. Treat as leads, not facts.

## February 2026

### AI models and data platforms

- **9 February 2026** — Storage/AI-infrastructure vendors converged on an "inference close to the data" narrative: Cloudera extended AI Inference and its Trino-based warehouse to on-premises sites; Databricks closed a fresh **$5 billion financing** round to fuel its Lakebase serverless Postgres for AI agents and its Genie conversational assistant; Snowflake announced a **$200 million OpenAI tie-up** putting **GPT-5.2 inside Snowflake Cortex** for governed queries across AWS, Azure and GCP; Dell's PowerScale guaranteed at least 2:1 data reduction (customers seeing up to 16:1); Aerospike made Dynamic Data Masking a database-level default for PII protection; Egnyte opened a UAE data centre for regional data-residency rules. [secondary] https://www.pulse.bot/big-data/news/storage-news-ticker-9-february-2026-b51cfb15-5b99-41c9-b23d-1876894e45c8/
- **26 February 2026** — Samsung's Galaxy Unpacked (early-year edition) unveiled the **Galaxy S26 ($899), S26 Plus ($1,099) and S26 Ultra ($1,200)**, each $100 more than the previous year. Headline features: a per-app **privacy display** on the Ultra (masks notifications or password fields from shoulder-surfers), a previewed **agentic Gemini** that performs tasks such as hailing an Uber, a new multi-object "Circle to Search", and a **Perplexity partnership** (preloaded app, alarm/note APIs, browser search). Samsung phones now shipped with three AI assistants: Bixby, Google Gemini and Perplexity, plus Galaxy AI call screening with caller summaries. [independent] https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/26/everything-samsung-revealed-at-its-galaxy-unpacked-event/

### AI infrastructure and datacenters

- **Status as of February 2026** — OpenAI's **Stargate** had moved from plan to build: the **Abilene, Texas flagship** had two buildings operational since September 2025, with six more due by mid-2026, planned to house over **450,000 NVIDIA GB200 GPUs**. Six further US campuses were in development (Shackelford County TX, Doña Ana County NM, Lordstown OH, Milam County TX, a Midwest site, Saline Township MI). **SoftBank had completed its $41 billion investment in OpenAI**; the two jointly invested **$1 billion in SB Energy** for power infrastructure. OpenAI claimed over **$400 billion** of the $500B commitment was already in play. Four overseas Stargate projects had been announced (UAE, Norway, UK, Argentina), with South Korea as hardware/data-center partner. Broadcom was targeting **H2 2026** to start deploying racks with **OpenAI-designed AI accelerators** plus Broadcom networking, completing by end of 2029. [secondary] https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/openai-stargate-datacenter-details
- **February 2026** — Epoch AI's Frontier Data Centers Hub (independent tracking via satellite imagery, permits and filings) projected the first five AI data centers to cross **1 gigawatt** of facility power in 2026: **Anthropic–Amazon New Carlisle (January 2026)**, **xAI Colossus 2 (February 2026)**, **Microsoft Fayetteville (March 2026, borderline 1GW)**, **Meta Prometheus (May 2026)** and **OpenAI Stargate Abilene (July 2026)**. The 13 large US data centers tracked accounted for roughly 2.5 million of the ~15 million H100-equivalents delivered in recent years. [independent] https://www.einpresswire.com/article_pdf/864214321/five-ai-data-centers-will-hit-1-gw-in-2026-independent-analysis-shows
