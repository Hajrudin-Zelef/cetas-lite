## Glossary

- **Article 50 (EU AI Act)** — transparency duties: disclose AI interaction; label synthetic content machine-readably. Applicable from 2 August 2026.
- **AI Office** — European Commission body enforcing GPAI obligations; fining powers live since 2 August 2026.
- **bLLC (Big Last Level Cache)** — Intel's stacked-cache answer to AMD 3D V-Cache, reported for Nova Lake (up to 144 MB).
- **Cerebras CS-4/CS-6** — wafer-scale AI inference chips; CS-6 planned with 3D-stacked DRAM atop the wafer.
- **Daybreak (OpenAI)** — trusted-access program for high-capability models; Daybreak Blue = safeguarded general models, Daybreak Red = restricted cyber-research models.
- **GPAI** — General-Purpose AI; model providers face documentation, copyright-policy and (for systemic-risk models) evaluation duties under the EU AI Act.
- **Systemic-risk threshold** — models trained above 10²⁵ FLOP carry the heaviest EU duties (red-teaming, incident reporting, weight security).
- **Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744)** — in force 27 July 2026; delayed high-risk obligations to 2027–2028.
- **NVLink 6 / ConnectX-9 / BlueField-4 / Spectrum-6** — NVIDIA's GTC-2026 interconnect, networking, DPU and Ethernet lineup for the Vera Rubin platform.
- **Preparedness Framework (OpenAI)** — internal safety regime; "Critical" cybersecurity tier was reached by Astra in August 2026 testing.
- **WAICO** — reported global AI-governance body announced at WAIC Shanghai, July 2026 (unverified).
- **1 GW data center** — facility drawing ~1 gigawatt, roughly the electricity use of one million US homes; five AI sites were projected to cross it in 2026.
