### Cloud and infrastructure

- **Mid-July 2026** — A **~15-hour Google Cloud europe-west4 (Netherlands) outage** caused by a utility power loss combined with a cooling failure forced shutdowns of hosts, storage and network switches. [secondary] https://github.com/ahmedbinabdulaziznada/nt-executive-tech-office/blob/HEAD/research/2026/07/27/executive-technology-brief-2026-07-27.md
- **23 July 2026 (14:44–19:41 UTC)** — **Microsoft Azure West US** suffered a **~5-hour outage** from a bug in the automated maintenance-request system that wrongly marked extra devices as in-maintenance and stripped their IP routes, severing WAN connectivity; Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Copilot, Azure itself, Xbox Live and Microsoft Store were affected. Microsoft's disclosure named the specific automation bug. [secondary] https://github.com/ahmedbinabdulaziznada/nt-executive-tech-office/blob/HEAD/research/2026/07/27/executive-technology-brief-2026-07-27.md
- **24 July 2026** — **AWS us-west-2** suffered its **third distinct reliability incident in eleven weeks** (~80 minutes, a network-boundary connectivity failure on the path to the Seattle metro, briefly touching us-west-1), disrupting Apple Pay, Reddit, Hulu, DoorDash and PlayStation Network. AWS had not published a detailed root-cause account as of late July. ### Cybersecurity

- **July 2026 (Patch Tuesday)** — Microsoft's July Patch Tuesday fixed a reported **record 570 vulnerabilities** with AI assistance. Aggregator-sourced; Microsoft's own bulletin is the primary source of record. [secondary] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-20.md
- **27 July 2026** — The **ShinyHunters** ransomware gang claimed **Ernst & Young (EY)** on its leak site, weeks after EY disclosed that attackers accessed a third-party platform **28 March–12 April** and downloaded client documents potentially including personal and financial tax information; attackers demanded contact by 31 July. ShinyHunters has been tied to breaches at Sysco, Ralph Lauren, Cisco, Rockstar Games and Salesforce clients. [independent] https://cybernews.com/security/shinyhunters-ey-data-breach-ransomware/

[independent] https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/in-the-hugging-face-breach-openais-hacker-was-noisy-and-fast-but-not-unstoppable/
- **H1 2026** — The ITRC H1 2026 report counted **280.6 million notifications** tied to supply-chain incidents, underscoring third-party risk as the dominant breach multiplier. [secondary] https://www.hipaajournal.com/itrc-h1-2026-data-breach-report/
