## Appendix G — Event deep-dives (from the same sources, no new claims)

### GTC 2026 (16–19 March, San Jose)

- The Vera Rubin platform was NVIDIA's first full platform refresh of the Blackwell era: Vera CPU + Rubin GPU as the compute pair, NVLink 6 as the scale-up fabric, ConnectX-9 for scale-out networking, BlueField-4 DPUs for infrastructure offload, and Spectrum-6 for Ethernet switching — a stack aimed at keeping hyperscalers and sovereign buyers inside NVIDIA's software/hardware envelope. [independent] https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/16/nvidia-ups-stakes-ai-infra-turbocharged-vera-rubin-platform-launch/
- Hardware-press coverage treated the launch as a roadmap statement rather than a shipping announcement: partner systems and cloud availability timelines were the open questions, and all throughput figures quoted were NVIDIA's. [vendor-reported] https://hothardware.com/news/nvidia-unveils-vera-rubin-groq-3-gtc-2026

### Google I/O 2026 (19–20 May)

- Google's own recap is the authoritative list of what shipped vs what was previewed; trade recaps converge on Gemini 3.5 Flash as the flagship-efficiency model with an agent/tooling halo around it. The I/O cadence (May) now sits between the March AI drop and the July/August Flash iterations — roughly a six-week model-refresh rhythm. [official] https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-io-2026-all-our-announcements/

### Microsoft Build 2026 (May)

- Two independent trade reads agreed on the message: agents over devices. The "AI agent stack" (with "Neuron" named in coverage) displaced the Copilot+ PC as the developer story — a notable retreat from the 2024–2025 on-device narrative. [secondary] https://www.eweek.com/news/microsoft-build-2026-ai-agent-stack-neuron/ https://aphnetworks.com/news/31777-build-2026-microsoft-sent-clear-message-copilot-pcs-no-longer-matter

### WWDC 2026 (8 June)

- iOS 27 and macOS 27 shared the stage with the rebuilt Siri AI and new Apple Intelligence developer frameworks; September's event then confirmed the Siri overhaul would arrive in beta with iOS 27 — a three-month preview-to-beta arc. [independent] https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-11-biggest-announcements-apple-wwdc-2026/ https://www.macrumors.com/guide/september-2026-apple-event/

### Computex 2026 (4 June)

- The show's chip story was a three-way split: NVIDIA's RTX Spark positioned against Apple silicon, Intel's Arc G3 taking the handheld verdict, and AMD's RX 9070 GRE shipping — discrete/handheld GPU competition intensifying as AI PCs standardized NPUs. [secondary] https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317762/20260604/computex-2026-rtx-spark-targets-apple-intel-arc-g3-wins-handheld-verdict-rx-9070-gre-ships.htm

### Apple "Surprise and Shine" (9 September)

- The first Ternus-era event paired a form-factor bet (iPhone Duo, $1,999, October 23) with a silicon bet (2nm A20 Pro) and a software bet (Siri AI beta, Apple Reference Image provenance). The Duo's IP68 rating beat Samsung's Z Fold8 (IP48) and tied Google's Pixel 11 Pro Fold — a spec-sheet battle fought in the ingress-protection row. Financially it followed Apple's strongest June quarter ever ($109.4B revenue). [independent] https://www.macrumors.com/guide/september-2026-apple-event/ https://www.usatoday.com/story/tech/2026/09/09/apple-event-sept-9-live-updates--live/91599414007/ https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/2987429/apple-event-rundown-what-was-announced
