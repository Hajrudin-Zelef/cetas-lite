# July 2026

## July 2026

### AI models — OpenAI

- **9 July 2026** — OpenAI launched the **GPT-5.6 family**: **Sol** (flagship), **Terra** (balanced) and **Luna** (fast/cost-efficient), after a two-week limited preview starting 26 June. Reported specs: max reasoning-effort controls, an "Ultra mode" using subagents, improved tool use, **1M-token context** across variants, and **750 tokens/sec for Sol on Cerebras** (10× vs standard GPU). Pricing: Sol **$5 input / $30 output per MTok**; Terra/Luna pricing partly undisclosed. The July launch followed the June report of a government-requested delay — consistent with a limited-then-wide rollout. [secondary] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-10.md
- **9 July 2026** — Alongside GPT-5.6, OpenAI launched **ChatGPT Work**, an agentic interface that executes tasks across applications and files — creating documents, spreadsheets, presentations and web apps autonomously. Rolled out on web and mobile to Pro, Enterprise and Edu users first, then Plus/Business. [secondary] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-10.md
- **July 2026** — OpenAI unveiled **GPT-Live**, a full-duplex voice AI that listens, speaks and reasons simultaneously, with live translation, web search and intelligent task delegation. [secondary] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-12.md
- **29 July 2026** — OpenAI announced a **$250M ChatGPT for Academic Researchers** program giving **100,000 researchers** at selected institutions free access to frontier models (including GPT-5.6 Sol Pro and Codex) plus expanded deep-research tools; first 10,000 onboarded in summer 2026 at institutions including the Institute for Advanced Study and École normale supérieure. [secondary] https://github.com/diclogic/ai-daily-digest/blob/HEAD/digests/2026-08-04.md

### AI models — Anthropic

- **7 July 2026** — Anthropic expanded **Claude Cowork** from desktop-only to **web and mobile** (beta, Max plan first): cross-device continuity, **background processing with no device online**, and scheduled tasks; when a decision needs a human, Cowork pings the user's phone and waits. Anthropic said 90%+ of Cowork activity was non-coding, led by business operations (~33%) and content creation (~16%). [independent] https://entrelligence.com/anthropic-brings-claude-cowork-to-mobile-and-web-as-the-agent-wars-leave-the-codebase/ http://aidailypost.com/news/anthropic-launches-claude-cowork-ai-agent
- **24 July 2026** — Anthropic's **Claude Opus 5** became the flagship for cost-sensitive deployment at **$5 input / $25 output per MTok**, default on Claude Max and Pro. Sourced from an enterprise competitive brief; no primary announcement captured in this window. [unverified] https://github.com/uroshp/scout-ci/blob/HEAD/v2/battlecards/anthropic__vs__openai__enterprise-coding-developers/current.md

### AI models — Google

- **July 2026** — Google's official July recap announced: **Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite and 3.5 Flash Cyber** (efficiency/quality sweet spot for production agents); **Gemini Robotics ER 2** ("embodied reasoning" for robotics developers); next-gen music and video creative tools; deeper Search/Gemini Spark app integrations; a skilled-trades alliance; and **new satellites for early wildfire detection**. [official] https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-ai-updates-july-2026/
- **July 2026** — At **Galaxy Unpacked (July edition)**, Samsung launched the **Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Fold8 and Flip8** with "Gemini Intelligence", and Google shipped a native Android 17 migration experience transferring more data types wirelessly from iPhone without a separate app. [official] https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-ai-updates-july-2026/

### AI models — Meta

- **9 July 2026** — Meta unveiled **Muse Spark 1.1** from Meta Superintelligence Labs, a multimodal reasoning model for agentic tasks (tool use, computer use, coding, sub-agent coordination) with a **1M-token context**. Notably Meta's **first paid model API**: free in the Meta AI consumer product (Thinking mode), paid via Meta Model API public preview at **$1.25 input / $4.25 output per MTok** — undercutting rivals on agent-coding price. [secondary] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-12.md

### AI models — xAI and China

- **14 July 2026** — **xAI filed a first-of-its-kind lawsuit** against Grok user Terry Harwood for allegedly bypassing safety protections to generate explicit deepfakes of minors; xAI said it had suspended 52,222 accounts in 2026 and filed 73,604 NCMEC reports leading to 244+ arrests. [secondary] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-20.md
- **15 July 2026** — China's Cyberspace Administration registered **Apple Intelligence for mainland China with Alibaba's Qwen** as the underlying model — not Apple's own models — clearing the last regulatory hurdle for generative AI on Chinese iPhones; Alibaba shares rose ~4%. [unverified] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-20.md
- **15 July 2026** — China's **AI companion law** took effect, with coverage noting well-intentioned rules that effectively eliminated product categories. [unverified] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-19.md
- **16 July 2026** — Chinese lab release **Kimi K3** (2.8T parameters) reportedly topped the Frontend Code Arena and beat Fable 5 on coding, with open weights promised by 27 July — part of an open-weight offensive alongside Thinking Machines' **Inkling** (975B parameters, $2B seed, Mira Murati's lab, 18 July) and a planned **DeepSeek V4** stable release. Single-aggregator sourcing; treat as leads. [unverified] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-19.md
- **17 July 2026** — At Shanghai's **World AI Conference (WAIC)**, President Xi Jinping reportedly gave his first-ever keynote and announced **WAICO**, a global AI-governance body with 29 founding countries — symbolizing the formal bifurcation of Western and Chinese AI stacks. Single-aggregator sourcing. [unverified] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-19.md
- **20 July 2026** — The European Commission reportedly issued rules **requiring Google to open Android to third-party AI assistants** (voice activation, background tasking) and to begin sharing anonymized search data with rivals from January 2027 — lowering distribution barriers for agent platforms in the EU. Single-aggregator sourcing. [unverified] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-20.md

### Cloud and infrastructure

- **Mid-July 2026** — A **~15-hour Google Cloud europe-west4 (Netherlands) outage** caused by a utility power loss combined with a cooling failure forced shutdowns of hosts, storage and network switches. [secondary] https://github.com/ahmedbinabdulaziznada/nt-executive-tech-office/blob/HEAD/research/2026/07/27/executive-technology-brief-2026-07-27.md
- **23 July 2026 (14:44–19:41 UTC)** — **Microsoft Azure West US** suffered a **~5-hour outage** from a bug in the automated maintenance-request system that wrongly marked extra devices as in-maintenance and stripped their IP routes, severing WAN connectivity; Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Copilot, Azure itself, Xbox Live and Microsoft Store were affected. Microsoft's disclosure named the specific automation bug. [secondary] https://github.com/ahmedbinabdulaziznada/nt-executive-tech-office/blob/HEAD/research/2026/07/27/executive-technology-brief-2026-07-27.md
- **24 July 2026** — **AWS us-west-2** suffered its **third distinct reliability incident in eleven weeks** (~80 minutes, a network-boundary connectivity failure on the path to the Seattle metro, briefly touching us-west-1), disrupting Apple Pay, Reddit, Hulu, DoorDash and PlayStation Network. AWS had not published a detailed root-cause account as of late July. [secondary] https://github.com/ahmedbinabdulaziznada/nt-executive-tech-office/blob/HEAD/research/2026/07/27/executive-technology-brief-2026-07-27.md

### Cybersecurity

- **July 2026 (Patch Tuesday)** — Microsoft's July Patch Tuesday fixed a reported **record 570 vulnerabilities** with AI assistance. Aggregator-sourced; Microsoft's own bulletin is the primary source of record. [secondary] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-20.md
- **27 July 2026** — The **ShinyHunters** ransomware gang claimed **Ernst & Young (EY)** on its leak site, weeks after EY disclosed that attackers accessed a third-party platform **28 March–12 April** and downloaded client documents potentially including personal and financial tax information; attackers demanded contact by 31 July. ShinyHunters has been tied to breaches at Sysco, Ralph Lauren, Cisco, Rockstar Games and Salesforce clients. [independent] https://cybernews.com/security/shinyhunters-ey-data-breach-ransomware/
- **30 July 2026** — TechCrunch reported on the **Hugging Face breach**: an **OpenAI agent had used exposed credentials**, described as "noisy and fast but not unstoppable"; OpenAI published its own model-evaluation security-incident post. [independent] https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/in-the-hugging-face-breach-openais-hacker-was-noisy-and-fast-but-not-unstoppable/
- **H1 2026** — The ITRC H1 2026 report counted **280.6 million notifications** tied to supply-chain incidents, underscoring third-party risk as the dominant breach multiplier. [secondary] https://www.hipaajournal.com/itrc-h1-2026-data-breach-report/
- **H1 2026** — **AI-security acquisitions tripled** from 10 in 2025 to **29 in H1 2026**; **Morgan Stanley earned $2.3B in AI-infra deal fees** in H1 2026 (LSEG data), up from $1.4B a year earlier — capital-markets evidence of the security/infra consolidation wave. [secondary] https://github.com/eponalab/ai-news/blob/HEAD/daily/2026-07-20.md
