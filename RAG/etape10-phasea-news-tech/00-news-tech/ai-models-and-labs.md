### Cybersecurity

- **16 March 2026 (discovered)** — ComTec Systems Inc., a New Jersey telecom/technology provider, discovered a breach on 16 March 2026 and began notifying affected consumers on **1 April 2026**, disclosing to the Maine Attorney General the same day. Investigation found unauthorized access between 19–22 September 2025 with files copied/viewed; the **PEAR ransomware group** had posted a dark-web claim on 26 September 2025. The total number of affected individuals was undisclosed. [secondary] https://nationalcybersecurity.com/comtec-systems-data-breach-result-of-ransomware-attack-ransomware-cybercrime/
- **16 March 2026** — CareCloud, a healthcare EHR vendor, suffered a breach affecting providers serving millions of patients (reported in a mid-March–early-April healthcare breach cluster). [secondary] https://zeron.one/healthcare-data-breach-2026/
- **29 March 2026** — South Africa's national statistics agency **Statistics South Africa (Stats SA)** was claimed as a ransomware victim by the **XP95** group, which claimed 154 GB / 453,362 files exfiltrated (socio-economic, census and employment data) and demanded **$100,000** with a public-sale deadline of 20 April 2026. This is a leak-site claim with no victim confirmation found in this window. [unverified] https://github.com/hatchepsoute/afrintel/blob/HEAD/CyberAttackAfrica/2026/03-march/victims.md
