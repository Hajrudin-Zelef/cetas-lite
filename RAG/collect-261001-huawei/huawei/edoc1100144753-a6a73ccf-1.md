                   Foreword
    • The development of information technology has brought many convenience to people, and
         the educational mode has also changed. The boring textbooks have become colorful
         multimedia teaching aids, the heavy schoolbags have become light electronic schoolbags and
         so on. All these changes are inseparable from a mature and stable network environment.

    • The education scenario is mainly used in places where teachers and students are
         concentrated, such as classrooms, lecture halls, libraries, and laboratories. This scenario has
         high user density, high number of concurrent users, and large burst traffic, which is sensitive
         to network quality.

    • This document describes Huawei's smart education solution and how to plan and design
         WLAN networks for education scenarios.
