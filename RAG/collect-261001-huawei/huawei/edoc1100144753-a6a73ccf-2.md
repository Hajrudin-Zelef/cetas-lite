                       Factors to Consider for AP Selection
                                     Usually, there are 4 to 16 spatial streams. The more spatial streams there are, the higher throughput and the larger
                  MIMO
                                     access capacity. Therefore, you need to choose the right AP based on application scenario and access density.

                                     Indoor APs have three types of antennas: omnidirectional, directional, and smart antennas. (All APs from R19C10
                                     onwards support smart antennas.)
                 Antenna
                                     Smart antennas work the best. Select products with smart antennas if possible. If APs are installed at height, consider
                                     directional antennas. If the coverage area has a small capacity and low density, consider omnidirectional antennas.

                                     Different countries have different rules on Wi-Fi transmission power, controlled by the country code. The closer
          Max transmission power
                                     transmission power is to the upper limit specified by the country, the stronger the signal and the wider the coverage
            (combined power)
                                     radius. For details, see the channel power compliance table in product documentation.
                                     The larger the antenna gain, the stronger the signal, and the larger the coverage radius. Therefore, APs with the higher
               Antenna gain
                                     antenna gain is preferred.
                                     Choice of power supply depends on deployment scenario. PoE is used in most cases. DC, or PoE + AC/DC are also
               Power supply
                                     available options.
                                     The Wi-Fi standard has evolved to the sixth generation now. All new-generation standards are compatible with existing
              Wi-Fi standard         ones. The latest Wi-Fi 6 standard greatly improves the rate and capacity, lifting the throughput and capacity by four
                                     times. Therefore, APs that support the latest standard Wi-Fi 6 are recommended.
                                     IoT is a trend. Deploying a separate IoT network will lead to separate cabling, management and O&M systems, and high
              Other features         investment in hardware and O&M. It is recommended that IoT support be considered when choosing APs (IoT supported
                                     by all APs from R19C10 onwards).
