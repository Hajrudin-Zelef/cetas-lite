                                                                   Deployment suggestions and constraints
  •   Use APs that support CSI sensing (4 or more spatial streams). The inter-site distance between APs must be less than 12 m to ensure complete coverage. If the distance is greater than 12
      m, detection holes will occur.
  •   For office buildings with atriums, to reduce cross-floor interference, the distance between APs and glass curtains on the atrium side must be greater than 3 m, as shown in the preceding
      figure.
  •   If an AP is close to a wall made of glass, thin wood boards, or gypsum boards, signals will extend into the conference room, causing false detection. It is recommended that the AP be
      placed at least 3 m away from such walls and the sensing distance be adjusted to reduce false detection.
  •   CSI sensing is affected by the Wi-Fi signal strength. Different room structures and wall materials affect the identification effect. Therefore, you need to configure a proper sensing distance
      based on the site requirements.
  •   In indoor open energy-saving scenarios, the application system must consider the detection results of multiple APs in the area and make an energy-saving judgment. CSI sensing is not
      applicable to precise light control.
