                                                                  Suggestions for network planning and deployment
 1. Suites or large resort hotel rooms cannot be fully covered by a single AP. In this case, multiple APs are required for a room.
 2. The AP cannot be installed behind a TV, behind metal decorative panels, or inside thick wooden cabinets. Thin wooden panels or gypsum board can be used for decorative concealment.
 3. Ensure that no air-conditioning outlet is located within 1 meter around the AP to prevent false detections caused by airflow.
 4. When the AP is wall-mounted in a concealed manner, the area directly adjacent to the wall on the mounting side lies in the antenna's side-lobe null zone, where micro-motion detection may be ineffective.
 5. A centrally deployed AP cannot cover the doorway area, which may affect light-switch automation. As such, an additional independent sensor needs to be installed at the doorway for coordinated detection.
 6. Solid walls or partitions of bathrooms cause significant signal penetration loss. Additional independent sensors are required to cover these areas for coordinated detection.
 7. In hotel scenarios, the system supports motion and micro-motion detection, capable of recognizing small actions such as head raising, hand waving, or body swaying. Static (respiratory) detection is only supported
 directly in front of the AP (bed area). To avoid missed detections when transitioning from the human presence to absence state, it is recommended that the energy-saving application system require continuous detection
 of human absence for more than 10 minutes before triggering actions such as turning off lights, or configure the human absence aging time on the AP to at least 10 minutes.
 8. To prevent false detections outside the room, you need to configure the detection distance from an AP to the door.

                                                             Deployment suggestions and constraints

          1. Radar installation must follow the "one room, one plan" positioning requirement, aligned with the hotel's installation baseline. To ensure respiratory detection
          accuracy, the recommended deployment positions are as follows:
           a) Deploy the AP on the TV wall directly facing the bed, vertically 10 cm above the bed height, and horizontally offset less than 30 cm from the bed edge. The
                AP cannot be installed behind a TV, behind metal decorative panels, or inside thick wooden cabinets (attenuation > 5 dB).
           b) Ceiling-mount the AP in the center of the room. However, a centrally deployed AP cannot cover the doorway area, which may affect light-switch automation.
                As such, an additional independent sensor needs to be installed at the doorway for coordinated detection. Thin wooden panels or gypsum board (attenuation
                < 5 dB) can be used for decorative concealment.
          2. Add a sensor if there is a sink or solid wood cabinet in the doorway.
          3. Ensure that no air-conditioning outlet is located within 1 meter around the AP to prevent false detections caused by airflow.
          4. When the AP is wall-mounted in a concealed manner, the area directly adjacent to the wall on the mounting side lies in the antenna's side-lobe null zone,
          where micro-motion detection may be ineffective.
          5. Solid walls or partitions of bathrooms cause significant signal penetration loss. Additional independent sensors are required to cover these areas for coordinated
          detection.
          6. In hotel scenarios, the system supports motion and micro-motion detection, capable of recognizing small actions such as head raising, hand waving, or body
          swaying. Static (respiratory) detection is only supported directly in front of the AP (bed area). To avoid missed detections when transitioning from the human
          presence to absence state, it is recommended that the energy-saving application system require continuous detection of human absence for more than 10
          minutes before triggering actions such as turning off lights, or configure the human absence aging time on the AP to at least 10 minutes.
          7. To prevent false detections outside the room, you need to configure the detection distance from an AP to the door. If the light-on circle covers the entire room,
          the light-on and light-off circles are the same. If the light-on circle does not cover the entire room, the light-off circle needs to cover the entire room.
          8. In unregistered guest fraud detection scenarios, APs may be installed at unexpected positions. If the preceding positions are not allowed, contact the local
          product manager. Huawei R&D network planning experts will perform design and evaluation.
