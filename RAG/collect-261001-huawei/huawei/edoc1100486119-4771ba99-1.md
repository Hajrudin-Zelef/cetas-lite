                    Foreword
    ⚫     As a pioneering innovation in WLAN converged sensing and communication, channel state information
          (CSI) sensing has emerged against the backdrop of the evolution of wireless communication
          technologies and the diversification of application scenarios. With the rapid development of WLAN
          technologies, especially the popularization and performance improvement of Wi-Fi 7, people's
          requirements for wireless networks are no longer limited to improved data transmission rates. Also,
          environment sensing capabilities are required. It is in this context that CSI sensing technology emerges.

    ⚫     The core of CSI sensing lies in utilizing the propagation characteristics of wireless signals to obtain
          information, such as object movement and location changes in the environment, by analyzing CSI.



         For more information about CSI sensing technologies, see CSI Sensing Technology White Paper.
         https://e.huawei.com/en/material/enterprise/4f7083341340472f9527f107e2ea2872
