                       Key General Constraints on CSI Sensing
  Key general constraints on CSI sensing:
  1. The CSI sensing function can operate only on radios with Wi-Fi services enabled. When an AP is configured to enter the power-saving mode or its radios are disabled, the CSI
     sensing function of the AP becomes unavailable. CSI sensing requires that the channel utilization of Wi-Fi services be less than 50%, and the function increases the channel
     utilization by less than 3%. Otherwise, detection results may be inaccurate.
  2. If there are continuously moving objects or living beings within the sensing area, such as fans, robotic vacuum cleaners, curtains with metallic fittings (high reflection coefficient),
     mechanically vibrating equipment, trees outside the window, or pets, the system may misinterpret these as human motions, micro-motions, or breathing. Therefore, ensure that
     the environment is free of such interference sources.
  3. At least 40 MHz channel width must be configured for AP channels used for CSI sensing. (In hotel scenarios, at least 80 MHz channel width is required.) Currently, 20 MHz
     channel width is not supported.
  4. If obstacles that cause penetration loss greater than 10 dB in the 5 GHz frequency band (such as concrete walls, solid wood doors, and solid wood tables) completely block
     signals, the CSI sensing function will be unavailable.
  5. For obstacles with a 5 GHz penetration attenuation of less than 5 dB, such as glass, thin wood panels, or gypsum boards, the CSI sensing of APs is subject to the following
     restrictions:
  • If the door is located in a corner, you need to walk one or two steps into the room after opening the door to trigger the light to switch on. To prevent lights from being turned
     on by mistake when people walk outside the walls, it is recommended that the light-on distance be set to the radius of the inscribed circle.
  • If a person walks close to the glass outside the room, there is an extremely low probability that the lights are turned on by mistake (less than 0.1% for a single time, with a 1-
     meter error that cannot be completely eliminated).
  • If people keep walking in the corridor outside the room or in the adjacent room, the lights may not be turned off in a timely manner or may not be turned off at all. The lights can
     be turned off normally when no one is present at night.
           • Light-on distance: Set it to the radius of the inscribed circle in the room (to prevent false triggering).
           • Light-off distance: Set it to the radius of the external tangent circle (to avoid turning off the lights by mistake). If there is strong external interference, set it to the radius of
              the internal tangent circle (but there may be small-scale missing detection in the corners of the room).
  6. If existing AP locations are not adjusted for CSI network planning, or if the room layout is irregular or deployment is non-standard, CSI sensing may result in over-coverage or
      blind spots, and the CSI sensing effect cannot be guaranteed. It is recommended that only the "light-off" function be enabled and the detection distance be set to cover the
      entire room. False detection outside the room is allowed. Even if interference occurs, the light can still be turned off at night when no one is in the room.
  7. For X776 models with 4T4R radios, CSI sensing is not supported on 2T2R radios. Ensure that the 4T4R radios are enabled. (Note: If a model supports 4T4R only on 6 GHz radios,
      CSI sensing is available only in countries or regions where 6 GHz is supported). For models with only 2T2R radios, CSI sensing is supported only on radio 2 (third radio); therefore,
      ensure that radio 2 is enabled. For other capabilities, refer to the specifications.
  8. For XX73 series, CSI sensing is supported only on 5 GHz radios. In scenarios such as hotels that depend on breathing detection, the radio bandwidth must be set to at least 80
      MHz. In scenarios such as conference rooms that do not depend on breathing detection, the radio bandwidth must be set to at least 40 MHz.
