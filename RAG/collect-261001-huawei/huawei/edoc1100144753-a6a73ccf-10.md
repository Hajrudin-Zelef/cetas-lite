                                                                                         Network Planning and Deployment Suggestions


      •   If the floor height of the report hall is less than 6 m, refer to solution A. Use indoor APs with omnidirectional antennas installed on the ceiling. The spacing between APs is 12 m to 15 m.


      •   If the height of the report hall floor is greater than 6 m, refer to solution A or solution B. Use the AP with indoor external antennas and 35° directional antennas.27012565), ceiling or wall mounting, AP spacing 12 to 15 meters.


      •   The 2.4 GHz radios may need to be disabled to reduce co-channel interference when there are a large number of APs because there are fewer available 2.4 GHz
