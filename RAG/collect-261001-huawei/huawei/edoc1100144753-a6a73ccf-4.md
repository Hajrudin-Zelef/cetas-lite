                    Application Scenarios and Coverage Key
                    Messages of Common APs
      • AP of omnidirectional antenna
           ▫   APs support the ceiling installation （ recommended ， The top height is not more than 6 meters ） Or wall-mounted （ The installation height is about 3 meters ）.

           ▫   The campus indoor belongs to semi-open scenario ， Standard setting based on edge field strength -65 dBm ， 2.4 GHz maximum coverage: 35 m ， 5G maximum
               coverage: 15 m .

           ▫   Factors such as obstacles and terminal access quantity must be considered in the deployment of sites in each sub-scenario ，For details about the deployment
               distance, see the network construction standards in the next section 。

           ▫   When the AP is installed on a wall, the signals on the back of the AP are blocked by the bearing column or wall .


      • AP of external directional antenna （AirEngine6760-X1E/ AirEngine6761-21E ）
           ▫   This AP is mainly used in high-height scenarios such as auditoriums and lecture halls ， It is recommended that the ceiling be used. The installation height ranges
               from 6 m to 12 m.

           ▫   70°C directional antenna (27013720) is recommended in common scenarios. Deploy in an isosceles triangle shape with a distance of 15-20m according to the ceiling
               installation mode 。

           ▫   35° directional antenna (27012565) is recommended in high-density scenarios. Follow the installation method of the suspended ceiling or wall hanging. The
               isosceles triangle is arranged with a spacing of 12m.
