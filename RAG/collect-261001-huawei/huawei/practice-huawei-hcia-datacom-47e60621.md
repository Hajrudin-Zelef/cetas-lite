Frequently Asked Questions
How many questions are on the HCIA-Datacom (H12-811 V1.0) exam and how long is it?
Huawei's current H12-811 V1.0 outline allows 90 minutes but does not publish the question count or item-type mix.
What is the passing score for HCIA-Datacom?
The published passing score is 600 out of 1000. Huawei does not publish enough scoring detail to convert that scaled standard into a reliable number or percentage of questions you may miss.
How much does the exam cost and where do I take it?
The exam fee is US$200. It is delivered through Pearson VUE test centers, with OnVUE online proctoring available only outside mainland China. Register through Huawei's certification site or Pearson VUE.
How long is the HCIA-Datacom certification valid?
The certification is valid for three years. To maintain the credential you must recertify before it expires, either by retaking the current HCIA exam or by passing a higher-level Datacom certification.
Which domains carry the most weight on HCIA-Datacom?
Huawei's H12-811 V1.0 outline assigns 28% to Ethernet switching network construction and 27% to building an interconnected IP network. The remaining eight domains make up 45%.
Do I need hands-on Huawei VRP experience?
Strongly recommended. Many questions test VRP-specific commands such as port link-type, ip route-static, traffic-filter, and display ip routing-table, so spending time on a real or simulated Huawei device improves recall and judgement.
What can I do after passing HCIA-Datacom?
HCIA-Datacom positions you for junior network engineer roles in Huawei-based environments and serves as the foundation for higher Datacom certifications such as HCIP-Datacom and HCIE-Datacom, which open more advanced design and operations roles.
