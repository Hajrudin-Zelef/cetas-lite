Preparing for HCIA-Datacom (H12-811) Exam
Passing the HCIA-Datacom Exam requires a great deal of focus and determination. Familiarize yourself with the exam objectives, structure, and the core areas it covers. This foundational knowledge will serve as your compass throughout the preparation process. Furthermore, these steps will give you all of the necessary data and test information that you need to get through the exam –
Step 1 – Visit the Official Website
The most important and first step before beginning your preparation is to visit the Exam HCIA-Datacom (H12-811) official website. This should provide applicants with the necessary information as well as key test instructions. This website will provide applicants with accurate, original, and up-to-date information about the test.
Step 2 – Know the important details about the exam
The HCIA-Datacom (H12-811) exam is intended for engineers who want to master essential datacom knowledge and abilities while also developing skills in small and medium-sized network planning and design, deployment implementation, and O&M advancement. These are the basic details about the exam –
- Exam Name – HCIA-Datacom V1.0
- Exam Code – H12-811
- Language – ENU/CHS
- Exam Fees – 200 USD
- Exam Duration – 90 mins
- Passing Score – 600/1000
Step 3 – Go through the course Outline
You must acquire complete knowledge of these domains before you sit for the exam –
Domain 1 – Overview of Data Communication and Network
- Understanding of Routing and Switching
- Understanding network concepts, IP network architecture, and standardization organizations and protocols
- OSI and TCP/IP protocol models, functions of each layer, and packet encapsulation
- Learning about ARP Principles
- Learning about TCP/UDP Principles
- also, Learning about Data Forwarding Process
- Learning about concepts and the Life Cycle of the Campus Network
- Learning about the Principles and Operations of the VRP
Domain 2 – Overview of Building an IP network with Interconnection and Interworking
- Learning IPv4 basics (basic concepts, address classification, and subnet division)
- Learning basics of IP Routing and Forwarding Principles of Layer 3 Devices
- also, Learning principles of the static routing, basic principles of the OSPF protocol, and implementation in the VRP
- Learning to use the static route and OSPF technologies to build a small-sized routing network in Huawei Router.
Domain 3 – Overview of Ethernet Switching Network Construction
- Learning Ethernet technology and basic principles of switches
- Learning STP, RSTP, and VLAN Principles and Implementation in the VRP
- also, Learning principles and configuration of technologies such as link aggregation and stacking
- Learning to use technologies such as STP, RSTP, and VLAN to build a small-scale switching network.
Domain 4 – Overview of Cyber Security Infrastructure and Network Access
- Learning principles and Configuration Methods of ACLs
- also, Learning AAA Principles and Application Scenarios
- Learning principles of NAT
- Learning NAT Application Scenarios and Configuration Methods
Domain 5 – Overview of Network services and applications
- Learning principles of Telnet, FTP, TFTP,DHCP,DNS,NTP, and HTTP
- Learning configuration of FTP, DHCP, and Telnet
Domain 6 – Overview of WLAN basics
- Learning concepts of WLAN (802.11 Protocol Family, Basic Devices, and Basic Networking)
- also, Learning WLAN Basic Working Process
- Learning WLAN Configuration
Domain 7 – Overview of WAN Basics
- Learning WAN Concepts
- also, Learning PPP and PPPoE Configuration
- Learning Concepts of MPLS/SR
Domain 8 – Overview of Network management and O&M
- Learning concepts of Network Management
- also, Learning basics of SNMP Protocol
- Learning Concepts of Huawei SDN Solution
Domain 9 – Overview of IPv6
- Learning the basics of IPv6
- also, Learning IPv6 Address Configuration Method and Process
- furthermore, Learning IPv6 Static Route Configuration
Domain 10 – Overview of SDN and automation basics
- Learning basic SDN concepts and Huawei Products and Solutions
- also, Learning basic NFV concepts and Huawei Products and Solutions
- Learning basic concepts of Automatic O&M
- furthermore, Learning basics of Python

Some additional Tips to clear the exam
These are some of the tips that will help you organize your time and manage other activities efficiently as well –
1. Allow enough time for studying
Make a study schedule that works for you and doesn’t leave anything until the last minute. While some students appear to thrive on last-minute studying, it is often not the best approach for exam preparation. Moreover, Make a list of how many exams you have, how many pages you need to learn, and how many days you have left. Following that, organize your study habits accordingly.
2. Make use of flow charts and diagrams
When revising study material, visual aids can be especially beneficial. Moreover, Write down everything you already know about a topic at the start. Furthermore, As the exam date approaches, turn your revision notes into a diagram. As a result, visual memory can significantly aid your readiness when taking the exam.
3. Explain your responses to others
You could ace your exam with the help of family and friends. Also, Explain to them why you answered a particular question in a particular way. Moreover, Study groups can help you get the answers you need and complete tasks more quickly. Furthermore, Simply ensure that the group focuses on the subject and is not distracting.
