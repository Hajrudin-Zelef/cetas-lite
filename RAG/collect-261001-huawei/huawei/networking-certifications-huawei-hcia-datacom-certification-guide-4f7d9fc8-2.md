Tips for the HCIA Datacom certification
Practice regularly
Experience has shown that it is helpful to work on HCIA preparation on a regular basis. This can be fixed learning days or time slots to ensure continuity. It is also important to review the topics. This way, you do not lose what you have learned.
Make notes
In order to repeat topics later again, it makes sense to make your own notes. There are several ways to do this, whether physically on paper or online (e.g., a dedicated wiki or even just OneNote), so that it can be accessed at any time.
Join HCIA Datacom study groups and ask questions
If you have questions about the HCIA Datacom topics or the exam itself, it is important to have a contact person so that open points can be clarified quickly and preparation can continue efficiently. In a closed forum, we provide such a basis for all students with an active membership (monthly or annual).
HCIA Datacom exam strategies
Based on feedback from students who have taken the HCIA Datacom exam, we have put together the following strategies during the exam:
- Read each question carefully as Huawei exams sometimes include tricky wording (e.g. which answers are not right)
- Use the eliminitation method. For example, if there is only one correct answer (radio button), you can usually eliminate 1-2 answers because they make no sense.
- Keep an eye on the time, as the ~60 questions have to be answered in 90 minutes, and some of the questions are more challenging, involving interpretation of images, configuration templates, text, etc. You can see the remaining time during the exam.
- You can mark questions that you would like to look at again later and can jump back to a previous question at any time. This can be helpful as the answer to a question may be answered in a picture of another question.
- A calculator is available in the exam, but no conversion of DEC, HEX and BIN is possible. Such questions must be solved manually.
What's next after the HCIA?
Earning your Huawei HCIA certification puts you in a strong position to develop your skills and advance your career. The next steps on Huawei's certification path are the HCIP (Professional) and HCIE (Expert) certifications, which offer greater industry recognition and deeper technical knowledge.
HCIP Datacom (Huawei Certified ICT Professional)
The HCIP certification builds on the foundational knowledge acquired at the HCIA level, delving into more intricate networking concepts. You will learn about:
- Advanced routing protocols (e.g. BGP and MPLS)
- Network security and VPN technologies
- Advanced Ethernet Technologies
- IPv6
- Network O&M and troubleshooting
This course is ideal for: Network engineers, IT professionals and anyone responsible for managing enterprise-level Huawei network infrastructure.
HCIE Datacom (Huawei Certified ICT Expert)
HCIE is Huawei’s highest-level certification. It requires theoretical knowledge and hands-on lab experience. Topics include:
- Large-scale network design and architecture
- Advanced routing protocols and technologies
- Advanced troubleshooting and optimisation
- WAN Interconnection Network Planning und Deployment
- Network Automation
This course is ideal for: Senior engineers, architects and consultants who want to lead complex Huawei deployments.
Please contact us if you need support with your HCIE preparation. We will support you on your journey to obtaining your HCIE and your unique HCIE number.
