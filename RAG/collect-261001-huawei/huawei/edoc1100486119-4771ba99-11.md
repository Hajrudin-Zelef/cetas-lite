                      Room Scenarios (4/4)
                                                              Deployment suggestions and constraints

   • APs supporting CSI sensing (4 or more spatial streams) are recommended for coverage. 2T2R APs are recommended for small conference rooms when additional APs are
     required. Wall penetration coverage is not recommended.
   • Configure the intrusion detection function on iMaster NCE-CampusInsight. (This function is also applicable to conference rooms.)
   • When an AP is deployed in a conference room with partition walls made of glass, thin wood boards, or gypsum boards, Wi-Fi signals can easily penetrate the walls. In this
     case, signals are extended to the outside of the conference room and are easily affected by passers-by, causing misjudgments. Therefore, the AP must be deployed at least
     3 m away from glass walls, so as to control the sensing range within the room. The sensing distance sensitivity level can be adjusted to adjust the sensing distance and
     reduce misjudgments. For details, see the network planning guide.
   • In a conference room, there may be breath-like interference such as wind-blown curtains and disturbances outside glass walls. Therefore, you are advised to disable breath
     detection to prevent continuous misjudgments as human breaths.
   • In energy-saving scenarios such as conference rooms, the human absence-to-presence sensing delay is 3s (device: 2.3s, including 0.2s as the reserved network transmission
     delay, and 0.5s for other upper-layer applications, ensuring that the total delay meets the requirements of network transmission or application processing).
   • In conference room scenarios, motion and micro-motion detection is supported, allowing recognition of small actions such as raising the head, lifting a hand, or shifting
     the body. However, detection is not supported when the human body remains completely still or when only hand movements occur. When a person is stationary or
     moving, it may be incorrectly detected as unattended. Therefore, it is recommended that the application system should determine no person in a conference room when
     there is no person for more than 10 minutes, and then perform energy-saving processing such as turning off lights. Alternatively, set the presence aging time for APs to
     more than 10 minutes.
   • If the distance sensing sensitivity level needs to be adjusted due to partition walls (such as glass partitions), the sensing radio channel must be fixed and the AP installation
     direction must remain unchanged before the sensitivity adjustment. If the channel or direction is changed, the sensitivity needs to be re-adjusted. Note: In R025C00 and
     later versions, this limitation is eliminated because it is recommended that the sending distance range be configured.
