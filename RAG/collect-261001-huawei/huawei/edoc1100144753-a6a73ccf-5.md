           For example, 200 terminals access the network. Generally, the concurrency rate is 30%. That is, only 60 terminals generate services concurrently. When both APs and terminals support Wi-Fi 6, the
                   bandwidth of a single terminal is 8 Mbit/s. The dual-band AP (4 x 4) supports 30 concurrent terminals (2 x 2). Therefore, two APs are required to meet the capacity requirements.

          The WLAN physical rate refers to the rate at the physical layer of the air interface and the rate at the physical layer of the air interface. The rate in the product documentation is the
