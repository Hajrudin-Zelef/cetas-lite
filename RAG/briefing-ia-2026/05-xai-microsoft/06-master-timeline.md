# xAI and Microsoft: master timeline and conclusion

<a id="s05-27"></a>
### Master timeline: xAI and Microsoft in 2026

To finish, let us place all verified facts on a single time axis: it is the best way to grasp the year's dynamics.
February 2, 2026: SpaceX–xAI merger announced, all-stock, $1.25 trillion.
The year opens with the financial big bang: Musk merges his rocket and his AI lab, creating the conglomerate that will fund everything else.
February 19, 2026: Grok 4.1 Fast in preview on Copilot Studio.
xAI's first bridgehead in the Microsoft ecosystem — discreet, technical, seven months before the public announcement.
June 2–3, 2026: Build 2026 at Fort Mason, San Francisco.
Suleyman announces the seven MAI models "trained from scratch, no distillation" — the declaration of independence from OpenAI.
The same event unveils Scout (always-on assistant on OpenClaw, M365, Entra identity, Frontier program, VP Omar Shahine) and Project Solara (chip-to-cloud platform, MDEP OS on AOSP, Badge and Desk concepts, designs for Qualcomm/MediaTek, AccuWeather/Best Buy/CVS Health/Target pilots, devices late 2026/2027).
June 12, 2026: SpaceX IPO — Nasdaq SPCX, $75B at $135, ~$1.77T, historic record, +19% day 1.
The market validates the Musk conglomerate and hands it $75B of ammunition.
June 16, 2026: Copilot Cowork worldwide GA — long-running cloud agents via Work IQ, most adopted in Frontier program history, per-credit billing at $0.01, with Anthropic (Opus 4.8, Sonnet 4.6) and GPT-5.5 for Frontier customers, Cowork 1 (in-house fine-tune) coming.
Microsoft moves from announcement to worldwide-scale delivery.
July 8, 2026: Grok 4.5 — post-training upgrade on V9 base, 500K ctx, $2/$6, co-developed with Cursor, positioned "Opus-class" in coding/agents.
xAI proves the iterative method.
August 12, 2026: Grok 4.6 — same V9 upgrade logic.
In August: Grok 4.5 and 4.6 on GitHub Copilot; August 19: Grok 4.6 on AWS Bedrock (cross-region, $2/$6).
xAI industrializes distribution: Microsoft developers, Amazon cloud, same price.
September 3, 2026: MAI-Transcribe-2 — #1 FLEURS across 60 languages (WER 5.2%), 10× faster than GPT-Transcribe, diarization, word-level timestamps, $0.10/hour on promo through December 31 (vs $0.36 for the 1.5), public preview via Azure Speech/Foundry.
Microsoft strikes on transcription with performance and price.
September 12, 2026: Nadella announces Grok in Copilot — Grok models selectable in Word/Excel/PowerPoint, Frontier customers first, admin opt-in, EU/UK/EFTA excluded during preview.
xAI's first integration into the Microsoft ecosystem despite the Musk/Microsoft rivalry.
September 13, 2026: Grok 4.8 announced — 2.5 trillion parameters, in-house C++ stack.
The architectural bet: the largest model on the largest supercomputer (Colossus v2, 550,000 GPUs).
September 14, 2026: draft Code of Conduct for Humanist AI — 37 pages, six-week public consultation, "people matter more than AI," subordinate-tool AI, no hidden neuralese, no rights or legal personhood, model-welfare rejection, direct critique of Anthropic (Suleyman–Amodei clash), revised version late 2026 to guide 2027.
Microsoft occupies the norms terrain while xAI occupies the model terrain.
September 21, 2026: Grok 4.7 release — after five delays, prices unchanged at $2/$6, Musk publicly downgrades it before release.
The forced-maturity episode: xAI learns to manage imperfection in public.
As backdrop, all year: xAI burns $1.5B/month for a valuation of about $230B, and Colossus v2 (550,000 GPUs) is built as the world's largest AI supercomputer.
This timeline reveals three sequences.
February–June: preparation — merger, Copilot Studio bridgehead, Build and its founding announcements, record IPO, Cowork GA.
July–August: execution — Grok 4.5 then 4.6, Bedrock and GitHub Copilot distribution, MAI-Transcribe-2 lying in ambush.
September: confrontation — Grok in Copilot, 4.8 at 2.5T, Code of Conduct, chaotic 4.7.
Each month has its front, each front its provisional winner, and none is closed.

### Conclusion: what 2026 changes for what comes next

At the end of this journey, what should be retained from 2026 for xAI and Microsoft, and what does it say about 2027?
First lesson: independence is paid for, and it is proven.
Microsoft paid its own by training seven from-scratch models "no distillation"; xAI pays its own by burning $1.5B per month and building 550,000 GPUs.
In both cases, the message is the same: the time is over when you depended on a single supplier — of models for one, of venture capital for the other.
Second lesson: distribution is the new battlefield.
Models increasingly match one another — MAI-Thinking-1 matches Opus 4.6 on SWE-Bench Pro, Grok 4.5 is "Opus-class" — and what differentiates is the channel: Copilot and its hundreds of millions of users, Bedrock and its CIOs, GitHub Copilot and its developers.
Grok's integration into Copilot (September 12) is the perfect illustration: the best model without distribution remains a lab curiosity.
Third lesson: the agent is the product, the model is the component.
Scout (always-on), Cowork (long-running, worldwide GA, most adopted in Frontier program history), skills, Work IQ, Solara gadgets — all of Microsoft's 2026 strategy converges toward integrated, governed (Entra), usage-billed ($0.01 per credit) agents.
The MAI models are only the fuel of that agentic machine.
Fourth lesson: philosophy has become a competitive weapon.
The Code of Conduct for Humanist AI is not a soul supplement: it is a positioning against Anthropic, a commercial argument ("our AIs are safe, subordinate tools"), and a development framework for 2027.
The Suleyman–Amodei clash is not an ego quarrel, it is the fight for the norm that will govern the industry.
Fifth lesson: financial consolidation redraws the map.
SpaceX–xAI merger at $1.25 trillion, record IPO at $1.77T, xAI valuation at ~$230B: the AI race is now a conglomerate affair, for players able to mobilize hundreds of billions.
Venture-funded independent labs no longer have the scale of the game — they must lean on (Anthropic on Amazon/Google) or be absorbed.
Finally, a warning: 2026 also showed the method's limits.
Five delays for Grok 4.7, a public downgrade by its own founder, an EU/UK/EFTA exclusion for the Grok-in-Copilot preview, Solara pilots that will not ship until late 2026/2027: ambition collides with reality, and reality takes its time.
The revised Code of Conduct, expected late 2026 to guide 2027, will be the first test of Microsoft's maturity: turning principles into engineering practices.
And Grok 4.8, with its 2.5 trillion parameters on an in-house C++ stack, will be the first test of xAI's maturity: turning an announcement into a shipped model.
2026 laid the foundations — financial, technical, normative.
2027 will tell whether they hold.
