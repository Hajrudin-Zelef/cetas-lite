# Distribution: scale as a strategic weapon

<a id="s11-7"></a>
### 11.6 Distribution: scale as a strategic weapon

**Gemini passes one billion monthly users (11/08/2026).**
On 11 August 2026, the Gemini app crossed the one-billion-
monthly-users mark, becoming Google's 14th product to
reach this threshold. It is, on the documented facts, the
widest AI distribution in the world: no other assistant
or agent claims a monthly base of that size. The
strategic stake is clear — at comparable model
capability, distribution becomes the differentiator: a
billion monthly users means a billion touchpoints where
Google can deploy Gemini 3.8 Live, agentic features and
future bricks, at zero marginal acquisition cost. It is
also a considerable usage-data advantage for model
improvement. In the personal-agent race, Google thus
starts with the heaviest artillery on the distribution
side, even if its 2026 consumer agentic announcements
are less striking than Meta's or Microsoft's.

**Muse, the most downloaded app on iPhone in the United
States.** For its part, Meta conquered the top of the
iPhone download charts in the United States with the Muse
app — a launch success that echoed as far as the stock
market, contributing to AMD's rally on 21/09/2026. (The
exact link between the app's success and the AMD rally is
not detailed in the verified facts; we simply note the
reported concomitance.) This contrast is instructive:
Google dominates by monthly installed base (1 billion),
Meta dominates by launch adoption velocity (#1 US
downloads). Two metrics, two narratives: ecosystem depth
versus product-launch strength. For 2026, the lesson is
that an agent's distribution no longer plays out only in
the stores: WhatsApp as a channel for Muse, native
cross-app for Siri AI, dedicated devices for Solara show
that each player is seeking its own vector beyond the
classic mobile app.

**Attention as the battleground.** Beyond the numbers,
the distribution section reveals that the agent battle no
longer plays out on the models' terrain alone: it plays
out on attention and touchpoints. Google converts its
existing base (14th product to a billion); Meta buys
attention through the launch (#1 downloads); Apple
captures it through hardware (the iPhone 15 Pro+ fleet);
Microsoft seeks it in the enterprise (Microsoft 365,
GitHub Copilot). Each player exploits its own
distributional asset rather than confronting the others
on their terrain. For 2027, the question is therefore
not only "which agent is best", but "which agent is
already there, at the moment and place where the user
needs it" — and the answer will vary depending on
whether the user lives in WhatsApp, in Teams, or in the
Apple ecosystem.
