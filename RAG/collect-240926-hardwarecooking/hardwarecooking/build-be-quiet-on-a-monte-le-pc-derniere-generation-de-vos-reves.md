# build-be-quiet-on-a-monte-le-pc-derniere-generation-de-vos-reves

Today in the Hardware Kitchen, we're building **a high-end configuration entirely from be quiet!**. Indeed, as a base we have the new large **Dark Base Pro 901** case to which we've added **three Silent Wings Pro 4 140 mm fans** on top and **one Silent Wings 4** at the front to complement the two pre-installed ones. We're treating ourselves with the beautiful **ASUS ROG MAXIMUS Z790 DARK HERO** motherboard and an **Intel Core i9-14900K** processor cooled by the equally new **be quiet! Dark Rock Elite** CPU cooler. Let's look at all of this in more detail throughout this article!

## A full be quiet! build in a Dark Base Pro 901 case!

In this configuration, we wanted to make a fairly imposing, high-end, but above all silent build. So, we're using the large **be quiet! Dark Base Pro 901** case and its **Silent Wings 4** fans that are both very quiet and performant. In this large and beautiful chassis, we're putting the beautiful **ASUS ROG MAXIMUS Z790 DARK HERO** motherboard accompanied by an **Intel Core i9-14900K** processor, the brand new flagship of Intel's mainstream CPUs. The latter is therefore cooled by our **be quiet! Dark Rock Elite CPU cooler**, the company's new flagship in air cooling. To go with that, we're using our **NVIDIA RTX 4090 Founders Edition** graphics card. To power everything, we're using our **be quiet! Straight Power 12 1500 Watt** power supply.

For the **RAM**, we went with a CORSAIR memory kit from the **DOMINATOR PLATINUM RGB** range. A high-end kit that still packs just as much punch despite the release of the even more luxurious DOMINATOR TITANIUM range. Regarding storage, we're using four **WD Red SN700 500 GB SSDs**. The first is dedicated to the operating system and the other three to games, programs, and cold storage via a Raid 5 configuration. To perfect everything, we had a nice black Cablemod cable left in stock for the 12V EPS of the socket.

Here's the complete list of components found in this machine:

## be quiet! Dark Base Pro 901 Case

The **Dark Base Pro 901** case from **be quiet!** is the brand's new flagship. We have here a very imposing chassis offering **very high-end features** and of course accepting E-ATX format motherboards. The top panel and the front panel are interchangeable, so you can choose between maximum airflow or silent operation thanks to a panel with soundproofing foam. Just like the Pro 900, it's possible to install your configuration in reverse via a reversible motherboard tray.

To talk about the **cooling** system, the case comes with **three Silent Wings 4 PWM fans** for virtually inaudible operation and excellent cooling performance. It's possible to install three 120/140 fans at the front and on top of the case in addition to three 120 mm on the side and a 120/140 on the bottom as well as on the rear. As for **radiators**, whether for AiOs or custom watercooling, the Pro 901 accepts up to 420 mm at the front and 360 mm on top. Note that the case is pre-equipped with a hub to more easily manage the many fans that can be placed in it.

The case's front panel has also been very carefully designed and we have touch buttons and we still have the area serving as a wireless charger (Qi technology) for your compatible phone. The connectivity includes four USB 3.0 ports as well as a 3.1 Type-C.

We must admit, this premium case from be quiet! is ultra high-quality down to the smallest details. We've rarely seen a front panel exuding as much quality as this one, and the possibilities of swapping parts to optimize either airflow or soundproofing is just perfect. Likewise, the cover for the graphics card power cable is certainly imposing, but it gives a massive effect that we like, even if this point is fairly subjective and will essentially depend on each person's tastes.

## Cooling: be quiet! Dark Rock Pro Elite

For the cooling system, no watercooling and not even an AiO. Indeed, we chose to use our beautiful **be quiet! Dark Rock Elite** CPU cooler. It's the brand's new high-end and very quiet CPU cooler that can easily cool a large processor.

The Dark Rock Elite targets gamers seeking performance through high airflow and a design benefiting from **aRGB lighting** on top. It also features a unique rail system for the front fan. Of course, this dual-tower CPU cooler comes with Silent Wings fans and a speed adjustment switch. The manufacturer states that its rail system for the front fan guarantees compatibility with the majority of memory modules and motherboards.

So, seen like that, we're going to say: but the memory sticks are too tall, they won't fit under the fan! Well, you're absolutely right, they don't fit. On the other hand, and this is where this Dark Rock Elite pulls off a feat, is that the fan is mounted on a rail and it's therefore possible to slide it upwards in order to be able to install it while using our CORSAIR DOMINATOR DDR5 memory kit! So yes, the offset between the top of the fan and the heatsink itself looks strange, but it works!

## be quiet! Straight Power 12 1500W Power Supply

So we're here with a new range in the brand's catalog. Here, we have an **80+ Gold** certification ensuring an energy efficiency of 90% at 20% load, 92% at 50% load and 89% at 100% load. Likewise, we have **Cybenetics Gold** certification for efficiency and **A- regarding noise (ETA and Lambda)**. Note that the PX-G also exist in 1000 and 1200W. This range therefore now moves to the new ATX 3.0 standard with a native PCIe 5.0 port. This unit is fully modular as you can see in the photo below and offers a semi-passive mode depending on the system load. But we also have Japanese 105°C capacitors and an LLC Half Bridge topology with a modular DC-DC design.

## ASUS ROG MAXIMUS Z790 DARK HERO, the enthusiasts' motherboard!

Our **ASUS ROG MAXIMUS Z790 DARK HERO** motherboard is an ATX format board. It features a power stage with **20+1+2 power phases**. So we're dealing with a motherboard based on the **LGA-1700** socket and supporting **12th, 13th and 14th generation processors** such as the 12000, 13000 and now 14000 series (i9-12900K, i9-13900K and i9-14900K for example). Officially, this motherboard supports **DDR5** memory up to a frequency of **8000 MHz**. We can fit **five M.2 SSDs** and all equipped with a heatsink to avoid thermal throttling. Note that the first slot is PCIe 5.0 and the other four are PCIe 4.0. For the networking part, we have a single 2.5 Gigabit Ethernet port. As for wireless, we're on the latest **WiFi 7** standard.

On the rear connectivity, we can count on the presence of **two Thunderbolt 4 Type-C ports**. We also find an HDMI port, but no DisplayPort. Obviously, there are also the two buttons for Clear CMOS and BIOS FlashBack. On the rear connectivity, we have six USB ports capable of going up to 10 Gbit/s. The **header** for **internal USB Type-C** intended for the case's front panel is capable of delivering a throughput of **20 Gbit/s** and supports **Quick Charge 4+** allowing power delivery of up to **60W** provided that the 6-pin power connector right next to it is plugged in.

## An Intel Core i9-14900K processor to rule them all

Let's get straight to the heart of the matter and look at the technical characteristics of this processor. We still find the hybrid architecture with two distinct types of cores. On one side, we have 16 high energy-efficiency "Efficient" cores which are called E-Cores with the codename Gracemont. On the other side, we have the P-Cores (codename Golden Cove), still eight in number. This is an important distinction to make, and for several reasons. It must be taken into consideration that the high energy-efficiency cores do not benefit from Hyperthreading, which is why it is a processor with **24 cores** (16 E-Cores and 8 P-Cores) and **32 threads** and not 48 threads as it would be logical to have when you have 24 cores.

We have **a 36 MB L3 cache** and the **L2 is** at **32 MB**. Its announced frequencies are **3.2/5.6** in **Base/Max Turbo, 5.8 GHz** in **Turbo Max 3.0** and up to **6 GHz** via **Thermal Velocity Boost** (TVB for those in the know). For the **TDP**, it is **125 W**. The **PL1 and PL2 are at 253 W and the Max Turbo Power is at 253W**. We're dealing with a 10 nm manufacturing process here and it should be noted that compared to the 13900K, we essentially gain 200 MHz on the frequencies.

## CORSAIR DOMINATOR Platinum: the high-end classic

For this configuration, we're using the **DDR5 CORSAIR DOMINATOR Platinum RGB 2 x 16 GB 6000 MHz CL30 AMD** memory kit. Indeed, this is a memory kit dedicated to the new AMD AM5 platform since we benefit from an AMD EXPO profile in DDR5-6000 CL30-36-36-76 at a voltage of 1.4V. However, it also works perfectly on an Intel platform.

Everyone now knows CORSAIR's DOMINATOR Platinum range since these kits are very visually elaborate and are truly high-end in the world of RAM. We therefore find the best of CORSAIR's expertise. These modules feature LED lighting composed of 12 Cappelix RGB LEDs that are individually configurable and offer very high brightness (+60%) while consuming less energy than standard LEDs (-40%). Indeed, we go from a volume of 2.8 mm³ to only 0.2 mm³. CORSAIR's DOMINATOR range is guaranteed for life and that's once again good news.

We have here a custom high-performance 10-layer printed circuit board that guarantees signal stability to optimize overclocking performance. In addition, the chips are hand-sorted to once again offer the best overclocking potential and therefore good performance. The cooling system of these sticks relies on a Dual-Path DHX patent that cools the memory and the PCB. Let's remember that these modules benefit from hand-selected and rigorously tested memory chips, all to ensure good performance.

This kit is available in many different frequencies as well as different capacities. Generally, these DOMINATOR memory kits easily win the Gold HC medal in our tests and sometimes even the favorite award. A reliable and elegant product that integrates perfectly into any configuration.

## WD Red SN700 500 GB: a simple but effective SSD

For the SSD, we took from our stock **two 500 GB SN700s** that came directly from Western Digital. Let us recall that the RED series was initially designed to run 24/7 in a NAS, but works just as well within a PC. In an M.2 2280 format, we are dealing here with a PCIe 3.0 4x SSD equipped with a DDR4 memory cache and 3D NAND TLC chips. The read IOPS are announced at 420,000 and 380,000 in write on our 500 GB model. As for the announced throughput, WD states 3430 MB/s read and 2600 MB/s write. The warranty on these SSDs is 5 years and the announced endurance is 1000 TBW.

## An NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Founders graphics card

For our super gaming PC, what could be better than the **NVIDIA RTX 4090 Founders Edition** graphics card? Indeed, it is nothing less than **the most powerful card on the consumer market**. Let us recall that in our tests it shows, in a UHD definition, an average of 123 FPS with a 1% Low at 82 FPS. Enough to play very comfortably in any games on excellent monitors. In terms of consumption, we measured 345 Watts in gaming use (in a UHD definition of 3840 x 2160 pixels). Be careful though, under OCCT the consumption peak rises up to 460 Watts. A 1000 Watt power supply is really the minimum; fortunately we are using a 1500 Watt unit to optimize efficiency.
