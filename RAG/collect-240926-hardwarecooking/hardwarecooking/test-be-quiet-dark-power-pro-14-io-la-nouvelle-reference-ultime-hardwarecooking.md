# test-be-quiet-dark-power-pro-14-io-la-nouvelle-reference-ultime-hardwarecooking

<!-- source: https://www.hardwarecooking.fr/test-be-quiet-dark-power-pro-14-io/ -->

Today in the Hardware Kitchen, we're testing a big truly high-end power supply from be quiet! with the Dark Power Pro 14 IO, following on from the Dark Power Pro 13. Certified **80 Plus Titanium** and **Cybenetics Titanium**, the German company delivers the best of its expertise in this model. So let's take a detailed look at everything this power supply, built by CWT, has to offer.

## Introduction: be quiet! Dark Power Pro 14 IO power supply

With the **Dark Power Pro 14 IO**, be quiet! once again pushes the boundaries of high-end by offering a power supply with a colossal output of **1600 W**. Designed to power workstations and the most extreme gaming configurations, this unit benefits from dual certifications **80 PLUS Titanium** and **Cybenetics Titanium**, guaranteeing an impressive electrical efficiency of up to **94.9%**. But be careful, the Cybenetics Titanium certification applies at 115V; in France we're on 230V, so the certification is actually Platinum here.

This first-rate energy efficiency not only reduces energy waste and electricity costs, but also considerably limits heat generation inside the case. Inside, the German brand has made no compromises: the unit features a fully digital **LLC Full Bridge** topology paired with Japanese capacitors certified to **105 °C**, ensuring impeccable voltage stability and maximum longevity.

The real revolution of this Dark Power Pro 14 IO lies in the integration of the intelligent **be quiet! IO Center** ecosystem (available via desktop app and web version). By connecting the power supply to the motherboard via an internal USB cable, you unlock complete real-time management of the unit. From a very intuitive dashboard, it's possible to monitor instantaneous electrical efficiency and power consumption in Watts, but also easily switch between a **6-rail 12V (Multirail)** configuration and a monstrous **Monorail** built for extreme overclocking. In addition, the software includes an energy cost calculator and allows you to synchronize the display of key data on other brand components equipped with an LCD screen.

Inheriting the brand's legendary expertise in silence, the Dark Power Pro 14 IO is equipped with an exceptional **135 mm Silent Wings** fan mounted on a 6-pole motor with hydrodynamic bearings. Directly integrated into an imposing aluminum heatsink, it almost completely eliminates vibrations. Thanks to the IO Center, the user can now choose between two fan modes: a **Performance (active)** mode ensuring a continuous and steady airflow, and a **Semi-passive** mode where the fan remains completely stopped at low loads and only kicks in under heavy demand.

Resolutely future-oriented, the Dark Power Pro 14 IO 1600W adopts the very latest **ATX 3.1** standard. It provides exceptional native connectivity thanks to its **two 12V-2×6 cables** ready to power the most demanding PCIe 5.1 graphics cards on the market, while still retaining **four PCIe 6+2 pin connectors** to ensure full compatibility with previous generations. In terms of design, the unit features a brushed aluminum chassis with a full mesh face. The fully modular cables benefit from very high-quality **individual sleeving** and come with multiple cable combs included in the box, allowing for impeccable cable management and rare elegance. Finally, be quiet! attests to the reliability of its flagship by covering it with a solid **10-year manufacturer's warranty**.

Regarding pricing, be quiet! offers its new **Dark Power Pro 14 IO** unit in **1300W** and **1600W** versions. The first version is announced at a price of **439.90 euros and we find it at 417.90 euros on the Amazon marketplace**, while the large version is listed at **479.90 euros, with a price displayed at 455 euros on Amazon**.

## Packaging and bundle

In terms of packaging, we find what we have on previous iterations of the Dark Power Pro. Indeed, we have a matte black cardboard box with a sleeve providing information about the unit with an image, the certifications and some technical specifications.

Once the box is opened, on the left is the unit protected in a cloth, comfortably installed in its cradle. On the right is the compartmentalized box containing the cables of course, but also the combs as well as the velcros and the user manual.

## Visual appearance

Visually, this aluminum unit features a refined design worthy of its range image. In a magnificent black aluminum dress, the edges are prominent and the grille protecting the fan is also of very good quality. Let's remember that we're dealing with a rather imposing unit measuring 200 mm in length and weighing 2.6 kg on the scale.

On the back of the unit, only aluminum, with just a "Do not remove" label in one corner. As for the grille covering the fan, it extends on both sides of the chassis limits, always in a very sober and very successful look. The fan is a 135 mm Silent Wings designed to optimize the unit's airflow.

On the side of the unit, one of the sides will display the reference of the “be quiet! Dark Power Pro” unit in shiny silver lettering offering a rather nice contrast with the black aluminum of the chassis. On the other side will be the details on the amperages and various regulatory logos as well as the serial number. A side that is therefore much less “pretty,” fortunately once the unit is in place this is a side that ends up hidden.

At the back of the unit, at the A/C input, there is a grille. This also helps with airflow, allowing better cooling of the components. Regarding this A/C input, it is type C19 and not C13 (we are dealing with a high-wattage unit). Remember, we also had this type of connector on the MSI MEG Ai1600T that we tested, but also on the CORSAIR HX1500i. The unit does not offer a physical button to switch from an active mode to a semi-passive mode, but it is via the latter that the power supply operates, or otherwise the active mode can be activated via the IO Center software.

On the connectivity side, we are dealing with a fully modular design. We therefore find the classic connectors for the motherboard and CPU socket, the SATA and Molex power connectors, and the PCIe ones with in addition the two PCIe 5.1 ports each capable of delivering 600W. Note the USB-C (IO USB) at the top allowing monitoring via the company’s IO Center software; we will come back to that very soon.
