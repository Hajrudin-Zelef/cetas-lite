# build-deepcool-du-pc-gaming-tout-blanc-et-un-i5-14600k-avec-une-rtx-4070

<!-- source: https://www.hardwarecooking.fr/build-deepcool/ -->

Today in the Hardware Kitchen, we made an entirely **DeepCool** configuration as much as possible and, as a bonus, it's a build on a white color theme. Indeed, like more and more brands, the company offers a catalog large enough to allow such a little pleasure. We have here the **CK560 WH** case, a **PX850G power supply** and an **AK620 WH cooler**. The motherboard accompanying this configuration is an **NZXT N7 Z790** with an **Intel Core i5-14600K** processor and an **NVIDIA RTX 4070 Founders Edition** graphics card.

## A full DeepCool build in a CK560 case for an all-white PC!

In this configuration, we wanted to make an **all-white configuration** except for the graphics card which will be more gray since we are using our beautiful NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Founders Edition. Thus, for this special DeepCool build, we are accompanied by our CORSAIR DOMINATOR TITANIUM First Edition memory kit in DDR5-7200 CL36 of 2 x 24 GB. For storage, we use two WD Red SN700 SSDs of 500 GB each.

The whole thing is supported by the Intel Core i5-14600K processor. A latest-generation CPU for gamers even if, objectively, a 13600K does just as well.

Here is the complete list of components found in this machine:

- **DeepCool CK560 WH case**
- **NZXT N7 Z790 motherboard**
- **Intel Core i5-14600K processor (tested here)**
- **DeepCool AK620 WH cooler**
- **2 x 16 GB CORSAIR Dominator Titanium RGB 2 x 24 GB DDR5-7200 C36 memory (tested here)**
- **NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Founders graphics card (tested here)**
- **x2 WD Red SN700 500 GB SSDs**
- **DeepCool PX850G power supply (tested here)**
- **DeepCool FC120 WHITE RGB fans**

Below is a small table grouping the components and their possible variants:

## DeepCool CK560 WH Case

The **CK560 WH** case from **DeepCool** is a mid-range case under 100 euros, whose finishes are very good and it offers an eye-catching design. All the more so since we are here on a version offering a white paint on the outside of the chassis, the inside remaining black. The front panel of the case offers a grill with a dust filter at the back in order to optimize airflow. Moreover, on the front three 120 mm RGB fans are pre-installed, controllable via a button on the front panel of the case.

This case can accommodate **up to six 120 mm fans** or **five 140 mm fans** thus optimizing airflow to improve the cooling of its configuration. For radiators, it is possible to accommodate up to two 280 mm.

For components, we have 380 mm of space for the graphics card, 175 mm of height for the CPU cooling system. On the front panel we find two USB 3.0 ports and a Type-C port in USB 3.1. We also have a combined audio output in jack. We have here dimensions of 471 mm in height counting the feet, 230 mm in width and 456 mm in depth. All for a weight of 8 kg.

## Cooling: DeepCool AK620 WH, a particularly elegant cooler

For the cooling system, we have here the **DeepCool AK620 WH** cooler. We are here on a high-performance dual tower design with no fewer than six copper heat pipes on a convex base. This cooler is capable of dissipating a TDP of 260W and is therefore perfectly suited to our Intel Core i5-14600K processor. At full load, the announced noise is only 28 dB with two fans having a rotation range from 500 to 1850 RPM. This cooler is compatible with sockets LGA2066, 2011-v3, 2011, 1700, 1200, 1151, 1150 and 1155 on the Intel side and AMD AM4 and AM5. And let's be honest, visually this cooler is particularly successful.

On the other hand, this cooler is limiting concerning the height of the RAM kit installed which only has 43 mm of height under the fan. Thus, our CORSAIR RAM kit is too tall and we had to change the position of the fans to make it fit. It's certainly a little less pretty, but at least it works!

## DeepCool PX850G Power Supply

We are therefore here on a new range in the brand's catalog. Here, we have an **80+ Gold** certification ensuring an energy efficiency of 90% at 20% load, 92% at 50% load and 89% at 100% load. Likewise, we have the **Cybenetics Gold** certification for efficiency and **A- concerning noise (ETA and Lambda)**. Note that the PX-G also exist in 1000 and 1200W. This range therefore now moves to the new ATX 3.0 standard with a native PCIe 5.0 port. This unit is fully modular as you can see in the photo below and offers a semi-passive mode depending on the system load. But we also have Japanese 105°C capacitors and an LLC Half Bridge topology with modular DC-DC design.

So yes, this power supply is not all white, but it is hidden by the case's power supply shroud, so why pay more for the white version which will be hidden anyway?

## NZXT N7 Z790 White, a motherboard that is both sober and above all all white

Our **NZXT N7 Z790 White** motherboard is an ATX format board. It features a 16+2+1 power stage with RENESAS ISL99360 60A power phases controlled via a Renesas RAA 229131 chip. So we're dealing with a motherboard based on the LGA-1700 socket and supporting 12th, 13th, and 14th generation processors such as the 12000, 13000, and now 14000 series (i9-12900K, i9-13900K, and i9-14900K for example). Officially, this motherboard supports DDR5 memory up to a frequency of 6400 MHz. We can fit three M.2 SSDs and these slots are of course PCIe 4.0 and all equipped with a heatsink to prevent thermal throttling.

## An Intel Core i5-14600K processor to rule them all

For the processor, we went with the brand new and very good Intel Core i5-14600K. Here, we have a CPU with **14 cores**, including **6 P-Cores** **(Performance cores)** and **8 E-Cores** **(high energy efficiency cores)**. It's important to take into consideration that the high energy efficiency cores do not benefit from Hyperthreading, which is why it's a 14-core processor with **20 threads** and not 24 threads as it would logically have.

We have **a 24 MB L3 cache** and the **L2 is** at **20 MB**. Its announced frequencies are **3.5/5.3 in Base/Max Turbo** for the **P-Cores** and **2.6/4 GHz in Base/Max Turbo for the E-Cores**. For the TDP, it's 125 W. The PL1 and PL2 are at 197 W. We're dealing with a 10 nm manufacturing process here.

As a reminder, this processor and more broadly its generation natively handle DDR4-3200 and DDR5-5600 memory just like the 13th generation. Additionally, there's an integrated graphics section based on an **Intel Iris Xe Graphics 32** (UHD Graphics 770). Let's note that recently, the maximum RAM capacity has increased to 192 GB instead of 128 GB. Let's also note that we're dealing with an LGA1700 socket CPU compatible with 600 and 700 Series chipsets.

## CORSAIR DOMINATOR Titanium RGB DDR5-7200 CL36, the new definition of high-end

Because we're very happy to have our "**First Edition**" kit of the new **CORSAIR DOMINATOR TITANIUM RGB** RAM, why not use it in this build? Indeed, the latter will be enhanced by our **2 x 24 GB DDR5-7200 CL36** kit. Thus, the DOMINATOR Titanium features a new design relegating the DOMINATOR Platinum to the past. For this new generation of DOMINATOR, the brand has designed a new design in superior quality forged aluminum with RGB lighting. We still have the patented DHX cooling system, the latter being of course improved. Regarding the memory chips used, high-end obliges, they are hand-sorted to use only the best. Obviously, we have a lifetime warranty here.

One of the new features of this memory kit lies in the top which is swappable. That is to say, we have two different tops and the ability to easily switch from one to the other. But here, we're going to keep the RGB top.

This kit is available in many different frequencies, but also in different capacities and there's something for everyone. Generally, these DOMINATOR memory kits easily win the HC Gold medal in our tests. A reliable and elegant product that integrates perfectly into all configurations.

## A WD Red SN700 500 GB SSD for storage

For the SSD, we took from our stock **two 500 GB SN700s** coming straight from Western Digital. Let's recall that the RED series is initially designed to run 24/7 in a NAS, but works just as well within a PC. In an M.2 2280 format, we're dealing with a PCIe 3.0 4x SSD equipped with a DDR4 memory cache and 3D NAND TLC chips. The read IOPS are announced at 420,000 and 380,000 in write on our 500 GB model. As for the announced speeds, WD communicates 3430 MB/s in read and 2600 MB/s in write. The warranty on these SSDs is 5 years and the announced endurance is 1000 TBW.

## An NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Founders graphics card

For our super gaming PC, we made the choice to go with an **NVIDIA RTX 4070 Founders Edition** graphics card. Indeed, having an 850W power supply and not 1000W, we're staying with the RTX 4070 which will go very well accompanied by our i5-14600K. To speak of the technical characteristics of this AD104 GPU from NVIDIA, it has 5,888 CUDA cores, 46 3rd generation RT Cores and 184 4th generation Tensors Cores. In comparison, the RTX 3070 is at 5,888 CUDA cores, 46 RT Cores and 184 Tensors Cores. We therefore notice that the quantities haven't moved, but we must take into account other factors such as the transition to a new more efficient generation of each of these cores as well as the significant increase in L2 cache memory (36 MB instead of only 4 MB). Similarly, the memory configuration improves with a transition to 12 GB of memory instead of 8 and we move to GDDR6X instead of GDDR6. With the memory chips operating at 21 Gbps, the bandwidth is therefore 504 GB/s against 448 GB/s. Similarly, the frequencies are clearly increasing on this RTX 4070 since we have 1920/2475 MHz in Base/Boost.
