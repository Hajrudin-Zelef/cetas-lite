# test-noctua-nh-d15-g2-chromax-black-un-excellent-ventirad-hardwarecooking

<!-- source: https://www.hardwarecooking.fr/test-noctua-nh-d15-g2-chromax-black-un-excellent-ventirad/ -->

Today in the Hardware Kitchen, after testing the NH-D15 G2 in its HBC version, we return to the **NH-D15 G2 chromax.black**. This is therefore the latest version of Noctua's large CPU cooler, but this time in an all-black outfit that integrates more easily into modern configurations than the Austrian brand's iconic beige color. Let's take a closer look at all of this throughout this review.

## Introduction, Noctua NH-D15 G2 chromax.black

Like its predecessors, the **Noctua NH-D15 G2 chromax.black** CPU cooler is a particularly imposing model that aims to be top of the line. You will need to carefully verify your case's compatibility before going with this model since it is 168 mm tall. With its 8 heatpipes, this cooler is capable of cooling even the biggest processor on the market. The big new feature of this model is therefore the color, since instead of Noctua's characteristic beige fans and aluminum fins, we have here an all-black outfit down to the SecuFirm2+ system, which is also all black. But at Noctua, this is not a simple paint job, since the company wants to offer the best, it took a long time to develop this black coating while maintaining the cooler's performance.

This new cooler also comes with new Noctua NF-A14x25r G2 PWM chromax.black fans. These fans are also available separately, allowing you to upgrade your NH-D15, NH-D15S, or NH-U14S cooler. These fans are available in a single pack or a dual pack for push-pull configurations, each being adjusted with a 50 RPM speed offset to avoid acoustic interaction phenomena such as humming.

Regarding socket compatibility, this model is of course compatible with AMD AM4 and AM5, and on the Intel side we go from LGA115X up to the upcoming LGA1954 which has not yet been released.

Regarding pricing, you should expect **159.90 euros as on Amazon**. A fairly substantial price that falls into the price range of some 360 mm AiOs. But concretely, that is the price to pay to have the best all-black CPU cooler from the Austrian brand! We will come back to this in the conclusion of this review.

## Visual appearance: Noctua NH-D15 G2 chromax.black

Visually, as was to be expected, the dual-tower cooler is very imposing with dimensions of 168 mm in height, 150 mm in width, and 152 mm in depth with fan, for a weight of 1.525 kg on the scale. But in terms of lines, we still find the dual-tower design with the two fans, this does not differ from the previous version since the new feature here is the black outfit. And speaking of this all-black outfit, this layer is designed to maintain high conductivity so as not to lose performance.

**Compared to the first generation of the NH-D15**, the cooler retains an asymmetric fin structure with a front block that has been slimmed down by 5 mm in order to balance airflow between the two fans. By reducing the spacing between the fins from 1.9 mm to 1.6 mm, Noctua manages to add 26 more of them, thus increasing the heat dissipation surface by 20%. This higher density is pulled upward by the new NF-A14x25r G2 fans, specially designed to maintain effective static pressure in a confined space.

Using the offset design of the NH-D15S, the heatsink avoids encroaching on the first PCIe x16 slot of most recent motherboards. A major asset for high-end configurations where this slot usually hosts an imposing graphics card or an NVMe expansion card.

At the top of the heatsink, the heatpipe ends benefit from the careful finish specific to the G2 range, complemented by a discreet Noctua logo on a black background. Integrated into a sober and dark configuration, the cooler knows how to visually blend in, which is precisely the hallmark of the chromax series.

The coldplate is made of nickel-plated copper and is therefore crossed by eight heatpipes instead of six on the first version of the NH-D15. Also note the larger coldplate. To protect it, a clipped plastic cover is present when unboxing.

We will also notice the brand's logo on the top of the heatsink.

It will be easily noticed that we are skipping over Noctua's new high-end fans. Indeed, we have here the **Noctua NF-A14x25r G2 PWM chromax.black**. This is the brand's best 140 mm fan and it features a round frame. Naturally, these fans also get Noctua's black coating without losing any performance. Let us recall that these fans deliver a maximum airflow of 155.6 m3 per hour for a static pressure of 2.56 mmH2O and a maximum noise level of 24.8 dB(A) at 1500 RPM, which represents the maximum rotation speed of this fan. However, let us note that on these two fans, a slight offset of around 25 RPM provides an advantage for sound quality. Finally, these fans can even stop for passive operation when the PC is idle. The **SSO bearing** (Self-Stabilising Oil-pressure) promises an impressive longevity of more than 150,000 hours, equivalent to 17 years of uninterrupted use.

To back up this durability, Noctua accompanies its fans with a 6-year warranty, irrefutable proof of the confidence the manufacturer places in the reliability of its components. This model is therefore perfectly suited for the brand-new Noctua 140 mm coolers, but also for optimizing previous models such as the NH-D15 or NH-U14S series. Note that included in the bundle are two low-noise adapters that reduce the maximum rotation speed from 1500 to 1250 RPM.

For RAM installation, a standard-height module (32 mm maximum) should be preferred if you use the cooler with both fans; otherwise, the outer fan will need to be slightly raised to clear the RAM, but be careful not to hit the glass or panel of your case since the total height of the cooler will be greater. On the other hand, switching to a single-fan configuration offers up to 59 mm of free space thanks to the raised lower fins, making the cooler compatible with almost all sticks equipped with imposing heat spreaders.

The whole thing is at first glance very high quality and at this point, we can't wait to install all of this to see concretely the performance gain on this new iteration of the NH-D15 G2 chromax.black! Let's move on to the assembly of this cooler on the next page.
