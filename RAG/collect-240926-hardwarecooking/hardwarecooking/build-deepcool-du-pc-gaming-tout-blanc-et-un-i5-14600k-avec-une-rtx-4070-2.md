# build-deepcool-du-pc-gaming-tout-blanc-et-un-i5-14600k-avec-une-rtx-4070

For the SSD, we took from our stock **two 500 GB SN700s** coming straight from Western Digital. Let's recall that the RED series is initially designed to run 24/7 in a NAS, but works just as well within a PC. In an M.2 2280 format, we're dealing with a PCIe 3.0 4x SSD equipped with a DDR4 memory cache and 3D NAND TLC chips. The read IOPS are announced at 420,000 and 380,000 in write on our 500 GB model. As for the announced speeds, WD communicates 3430 MB/s in read and 2600 MB/s in write. The warranty on these SSDs is 5 years and the announced endurance is 1000 TBW.

## An NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Founders graphics card

For our super gaming PC, we made the choice to go with an **NVIDIA RTX 4070 Founders Edition** graphics card. Indeed, having an 850W power supply and not 1000W, we're staying with the RTX 4070 which will go very well accompanied by our i5-14600K. To speak of the technical characteristics of this AD104 GPU from NVIDIA, it has 5,888 CUDA cores, 46 3rd generation RT Cores and 184 4th generation Tensors Cores. In comparison, the RTX 3070 is at 5,888 CUDA cores, 46 RT Cores and 184 Tensors Cores. We therefore notice that the quantities haven't moved, but we must take into account other factors such as the transition to a new more efficient generation of each of these cores as well as the significant increase in L2 cache memory (36 MB instead of only 4 MB). Similarly, the memory configuration improves with a transition to 12 GB of memory instead of 8 and we move to GDDR6X instead of GDDR6. With the memory chips operating at 21 Gbps, the bandwidth is therefore 504 GB/s against 448 GB/s. Similarly, the frequencies are clearly increasing on this RTX 4070 since we have 1920/2475 MHz in Base/Boost.
