# build-nzxt-une-config-a-4600-euros-avec-rtx-4090-et-i9-14900k-dans-le-h7-flow-rg

Our motherboard will accommodate an Intel Core i9-14900K processor. Indeed, we have a fairly large and well-ventilated case here, so we don't really have any limitations and we wanted to treat ourselves! We therefore still find the hybrid architecture with two distinct types of cores. On one side, we have 16 high-energy-efficiency "Efficient" cores called E-Cores with the Gracemont codename. On the other side, we have the P-Cores (Golden Cove codename), still numbering eight. This is an important distinction to make, for several reasons. It must be taken into consideration that the high-energy-efficiency cores do not benefit from Hyperthreading, which is why this is a processor with **24 cores** (16 E-Cores and 8 P-Cores) and **32 threads** and not 48 threads as would logically be expected with 24 cores.

We have **a 36 MB L3 cache** and the **L2 is** at **32 MB**. Its announced frequencies are **3.2/5.6** in **Base/Max Turbo, 5.8 GHz** in **Turbo Max 3.0** and up to **6 GHz** via **Thermal Velocity Boost** (TVB for those in the know). As for the **TDP**, it is **125 W**. The **PL1 and PL2 are at 253 W and the Max Turbo Power is at 253W**. We are dealing with a 10 nm manufacturing process here, and it should be noted that compared to the 13900K, we essentially gain 200 MHz in frequencies.

## CORSAIR DOMINATOR TITANIUM RGB DDR5-7200 CL36

CORSAIR DOMINATOR Titanium RGB kits feature a new design consigning the DOMINATOR Platinum to the past. For this new DOMINATOR generation, the brand has designed a new design in superior quality forged aluminum with RGB lighting. We still have the patented DHX cooling system, but this has of course been improved. As for the memory chips used, as befits a high-end product, they are hand-sorted to keep only the best.

One of the new features of this memory kit lies in the top, which is swappable. That is to say, we have two different tops and the ability to easily switch from one to the other. We therefore find the best of CORSAIR's expertise, notably with lighting composed of 11 RGB LEDs that can be individually configured. CORSAIR's DOMINATOR TITANIUM range is guaranteed for life, just like the Platinum range, and that is once again good news. The TITANIUM kits will be available in various capacities and different frequencies, so there will be something for everyone.

We have here a custom high-performance 10-layer printed circuit board that guarantees signal stability to optimize overclocking performance. In addition, the chips are hand-sorted to once again offer the best overclocking potential and therefore good performance. The cooling system of these modules relies on a patented Dual-Path DHX design that cools both the memory and the PCB.

## An NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Founders graphics card

For our super gaming PC, what better than the **NVIDIA RTX 4090 Founders Edition** graphics card? Indeed, while waiting for the next RTX 5000 cards under Blackwell, it is nothing less than the most powerful card on the consumer market. Let us recall that in our tests it shows, at UHD resolution, an average of 123 FPS with a 1% Low of 82 FPS. Enough to play any game very comfortably on excellent monitors. In terms of power consumption, we measured 345 Watts in gaming use (at a UHD resolution of 3840 x 2160 pixels). Be careful, however: under OCCT the power draw peaks at up to 460 Watts.

## NZXT F360 Core RGB

Among the new products in NZXT's catalog, we find the F360 Core RGB. Here, the principle is simple: three fans are placed in a single 360 mm frame. This allows effortless installation while ensuring exceptional cooling and stunning RGB lighting.

Here are the highlights of this product as presented by NZXT:

- The single-frame format with integrated fans greatly simplifies installation, configuration, and lighting control. No need to connect fans one by one.
- Simply connect a single cable to an NZXT controller (sold separately) to control lighting and speed via the NZXT CAM software. You can also connect to a 5V ARGB connector on any motherboard using the included splitter cable.
- The fan blade design increases static pressure to push air through tight spaces while ensuring optimal airflow. Suitable for intake or exhaust.
- Hydrodynamic bearings ensure stable, quiet operation with an exceptional lifespan of 60,000 hours.
- The light from eight individually configurable RGB LEDs on the hub of each fan diffuses through the semi-transparent blades for dazzling colorful visuals.
- PWM fans dynamically adjust rotation speed based on PC temperatures for optimal efficiency and reduced noise levels.
- Anti-vibration rubber corners effectively eliminate noise caused by vibrations for quiet performance at all speeds.
- Control lighting and fan speed via the CAM software when connected to an NZXT controller (sold separately).
