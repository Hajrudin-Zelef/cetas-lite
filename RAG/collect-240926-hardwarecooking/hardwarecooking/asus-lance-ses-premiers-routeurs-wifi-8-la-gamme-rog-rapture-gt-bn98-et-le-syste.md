# asus-lance-ses-premiers-routeurs-wifi-8-la-gamme-rog-rapture-gt-bn98-et-le-syste

<!-- source: https://www.hardwarecooking.fr/rog-rapture-gt-bn98-et-le-systeme-mesh-zenwifi-bn12-arrivent-en-france/ -->

ASUS is accelerating its transition to the new generation of wireless connectivity with the announcement of the commercial availability of **its first WiFi 8 devices**. After an **initial launch in Canada**, the manufacturer is now preparing a global rollout of its new range of routers and Mesh systems, with an **arrival planned in France as early as the end of September** for the **ROG Rapture GT-BN98** and in October for the **ZenWiFi BN12**.

With this announcement, ASUS intends to gain a head start in a market that has not yet fully adopted WiFi 8. The brand is highlighting a platform designed for current devices while preparing for the arrival of future equipment compatible with this new wireless standard.

## The ROG Rapture GT-BN98 is presented as the first WiFi 8 router to be commercially released

The new ROG Rapture GT-BN98 range comes in two versions: the **GT-BN98 Pro (BN30000) and the GT-BN98 (BN25000)**. ASUS announces a quad-band architecture capable of reaching up to 30,000 Mb/s of combined throughput, a particularly ambitious figure intended for gamers, content creators, and users with many simultaneous connections.

To support these performance levels, ASUS integrates a quad-core processor clocked at 2.6 GHz paired with 2 GB of RAM. Connectivity is also intended to be high-end, with two 10 Gb/s Ethernet ports as well as four 2.5 Gb/s LAN ports, making it possible to build a complete multi-gigabit network for workstations as well as home servers or network storage.

The manufacturer also states that it has designed an internal structure based on sixteen antennas distributed across eight external modules. This architecture is complemented by an optimized cooling system intended to maintain consistent performance even under heavy network load.

ASUS claims that users equipped with WiFi 7 devices can already benefit from some advantages related to this new generation of routers. The brand's engineers notably mention performance improvements observed during their internal tests, even before the massive arrival of the first WiFi 8 terminals.

## The ZenWiFi BN12 introduces the first WiFi 8 Mesh ecosystem

Alongside its ROG range, ASUS is also preparing the launch of the ZenWiFi BN12. Presented as the market's first tri-band, 12-stream WiFi 8 Mesh system, this model is aimed more at large homes and heavily connected environments.

The manufacturer announces coverage of up to approximately 650 m² thanks to improved obstacle-penetration technology. Two 10 Gb/s Ethernet ports are also included to enable very high-speed wired links between the different nodes of the mesh network.

The ZenWiFi BN12 also includes the ASUS AiProtection Pro security suite at no additional subscription as well as a complete parental control system. The integration of AiMesh technology also makes it possible to pair it with other compatible ASUS devices in order to gradually extend network coverage.

## Features designed for the future evolution of WiFi 8

ASUS is notably highlighting several technologies that will accompany the future WiFi 8 standard. Among them are UEQM (Unequal Error Quality Modulation), intended to improve connection stability in complex environments, as well as NPCA (Non-Primary Channel Access), designed to better manage interference and optimize the use of available channels.

While these features can only be fully exploited with WiFi 8-compatible client devices, ASUS believes that its ecosystem is already ready to support the gradual evolution of home and professional usage over the coming years.

## Price and availability in France

The global rollout of the ASUS WiFi 8 range will begin in October. For the French market, the ROG Rapture GT-BN98 router will go on sale as early as the end of September at the recommended price of **899.99 euros**.

The **ZenWiFi BN12 Mesh** system in its two-unit configuration will arrive in October **at a price of 1,099.99 euros**. With these products, ASUS is clearly seeking to position itself among the first players to offer a complete infrastructure around WiFi 8 even before the widespread adoption of this new standard by the general public.
