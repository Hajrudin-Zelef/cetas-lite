# build-nzxt-une-config-a-4600-euros-avec-rtx-4090-et-i9-14900k-dans-le-h7-flow-rg

<!-- source: https://www.hardwarecooking.fr/build-nzxt-h7-flow-rgb-2024/ -->

Today in the Hardware Kitchen, we put together an almost entirely **NZXT** configuration and as a bonus, it's an all-white **build** that's very pretty. Indeed, the brand offers a catalog large enough to allow for such little pleasures. Also, remember that this isn't the first time we've done this with NZXT; we also did it with a build based on an H9 Elite. For this new build, we have the **NZXT H7 Flow RGB** case, an **NZXT C1500 Platinum ATX 3.1** power supply, and an **NZXT Kraken Elite 360 RGB** AiO.

## A full NZXT build in the brand new H7 Flow RGB case

With this PC, we wanted to make an all-white configuration except for the graphics card, which will be more gray since we're using our mighty NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Founders Edition. We ultimately have very few limitations with this case since it's large enough to imagine whatever we want. So we're going to go for elegance and power with mainstream components among the most powerful available on the market at the time we're writing these lines. We've therefore created here the quintessential gaming PC: powerful, balanced, and above all, stylish. The motherboard accompanying this configuration is an **ASUS ROG Z790 FORMULA** with an **Intel Core i9-14900K** processor and an **NVIDIA RTX 4090 Founders Edition** graphics card. As for memory, we're using a **CORSAIR DOMINATOR Titanium RGB 2 x 24 GB DDR5-7200 CL36** kit.

If we do the math, we still end up with a configuration worth approximately 4600 euros, basing the prices on what we find on Amazon!

Here is the complete list of components found in this machine:

- **NZXT H7 Flow RGB case**
- **ASUS ROG MAXIMUS Z790 FORMULA motherboard**
- **Intel Core i9-14900K processor**
- **NZXT Kraken Elite 360 RGB AiO**
- **2 x 24 GB CORSAIR Dominator Titanium DDR5-7200 CL36 memory**
- **NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Founders graphics card**
- **Samsung 970 Evo Plus 2 TB SSD**
- **NZXT C1500 Platinum power supply**
- 
**NZXT F360 RGB Core fan**

## NZXT H7 Flow RGB case

So here we have a mid-tower case with a more or less classic design. This chassis is clearly designed to optimize airflow with a mesh panel at the front to let air in and exhaust it through the top and rear, but we also have intake from the bottom of the case with three 120 mm fan slots. Thus, it's possible to install up to 10 120 mm fans. Note that here the power supply location is vertical, specifically to place fans at the bottom to help cool the graphics card. Moreover, the H7 Flow RGB comes with an NZXT F360 RGB Core, which consists of a single block comprising three 120 mm fans installed at the front.

## Cooling: NZXT Kraken Elite 360 RGB

For the AiO (all-in-one watercooling), given the case, we went with a nice 360 mm with the all-white **Kraken Elite 360 RGB**. The particularity of this AiO is that it offers a **2.36″ LCD screen** with a very high-quality **640 x 640 pixel resolution at 60 Hz**, allowing various information to be displayed such as CPU temperature. As a small clarification, the tube length is 400 mm.

With the 360 mm radiator are three 120 mm fans of the F120 RGB Core reference. These fans operate in a range from 500 to 1500 RPM with an airflow of 13.94 to 50.18 m³/min. As for air pressure, we're in a range of 0.75 to 2.7 mmH2O. To manage the AiO, everything happens via the NZXT CAM software. The coldplate of the waterblock is obviously made of copper.

We have here compatibility with AMD AM5, AM4, sTRX4, and TR4 sockets. On the Intel side, compatibility is with LGA 1700, 1200, and 115x sockets.

## NZXT C1500 Platinum ATX 3.1 power supply

For the power supply unit responsible for providing current to our PC, we went with the **NZXT C1500 Platinum** reference. 1500W may seem like a lot, but not necessarily when you combine an Intel Core i9-14900K CPU with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Founders graphics card and remember that a PSU is at its full efficiency at 50% load. This model is based on a classic ATX format with dimensions of 180 x 86 x 150 mm and offers an 80 PLUS Platinum certification allowing energy efficiency of 92% at 20% load, 94% at 50% load, and finally 90% at 100% load. The unit is fully modular and the 120 mm fan is semi-passive. The brand offers a 10-year warranty on its product, a sign of quality and longevity.

The NZXT C1500 Platinum power supply offers highly efficient and digitally controlled power, compliance with ATX 3.1 standards, and PCIe 5.1 connectivity with a high-performance magnetic levitation fan.

## ASUS ROG MAXIMUS Z790 FORMULA: a magnificent all-white board!

Our **ASUS ROG MAXIMUS Z790 FORMULA** motherboard is a very special board that brand enthusiasts particularly appreciate. Indeed, not only are we dealing with a high-end board, but it is also all white and the cherry on top: it features a hybrid cooling system for the power delivery stage. ROG's Formula uses its **HybridChill VRM Block** technology, allowing the VRMs to be cooled by both air and liquid by integrating it into a custom watercooling system.

This is a standard **ATX** form factor board. As you may have guessed from its name, it is based on the **Intel Z790** chipset, which is the high-end option and allows for advanced overclocking. However, this is a **second generation of this chipset**, and it therefore incorporates more advanced technologies such as **WiFi 7**. As for the socket, it is **LGA1700**, making it compatible with **12th**, **13th**, and **14th** generation **Intel Core** processors such as the **i9-12900K**, **i9-13900K**, or **i9-14900K**. Since the latest generation in the mainstream segment, motherboards are capable of handling up to **192 GB** of **DDR5** memory via 48 GB modules. As for maximum frequency, this FORMULA will accept **DDR5-8000 MHz**, just like the DARK HERO.

In terms of connectivity, as befits a high-end motherboard, we are rather spoiled. Indeed, on the **rear I/O** we have **two Thunderbolt 4 ports** via USB Type-C, six USB ports at 10 Gbps (five Type-A and one Type-C) and four USB at 5 Gbps in Type-A. We also find an HDMI port as well as a DisplayPort. Obviously, there are also the two buttons for Clear CMOS and BIOS FlashBack. The **header** for the **internal USB Type-C** intended for the case front panel is capable of delivering a throughput of **20 Gbit/s** and supports **Quick Charge 4+**, allowing power delivery of up to **60W**, provided that the 6-pin power connector right next to it is plugged in.
