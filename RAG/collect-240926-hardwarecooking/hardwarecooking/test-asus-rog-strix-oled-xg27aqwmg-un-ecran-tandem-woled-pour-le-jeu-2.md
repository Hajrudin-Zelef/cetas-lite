# test-asus-rog-strix-oled-xg27aqwmg-un-ecran-tandem-woled-pour-le-jeu

To talk about the square base which ultimately takes up fairly little space on the desk, it has an almost rectangular shape and is particularly stable.

To attach the stand column, everything is done without screws. Indeed, simply snap the clips in from the top then lower it to secure it in its location. Then, slide the base onto the end of the column and tighten the screw by hand. It's as simple as pie.

The connectivity is located at the bottom of the screen and the power supply is on the other side with the USB ports. We will have the following ports:

- 2x HDMI 2.1
- 1x DisplayPort 1.4 DSC
- 2x USB 3.2 Gen 1 Type-A

Now that we know what it looks like and that we have assembled it, let's look at what this screen offers in terms of features.
