# asus-securise-l-avenir-de-ses-routeurs-wifi-et-prepare-l-arrivee-du-wifi-8-aux-e

<!-- source: https://www.hardwarecooking.fr/asus-securise-lavenir-de-ses-routeurs-wifi-et-prepare-larrivee-du-wifi-8-aux-etats-unis/ -->

ASUS announces that it has obtained conditional authorization from the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) for its entire range of consumer routers sold in the United States. A strategic step that allows the Taiwanese manufacturer to secure the availability of its products on the American market while actively preparing for the arrival of future WiFi 8 technologies.

In a context where American regulatory developments can create uncertainties for network equipment manufacturers, ASUS claims to have anticipated these changes in order to ensure the continuity of its operations. This FCC validation covers not only currently marketed products, but also future references intended for the American market, thus guaranteeing continuous availability with distributors, resellers, and online sales platforms.

## ASUS WiFi 8: a secure supply chain for the American market

For ASUS, this authorization is much more than a simple administrative formality. The company sees it as confirmation of the strength of its industrial and logistical organization. The manufacturer highlights the resilience of its global supply chain, designed to respond quickly to regulatory developments while maintaining a high level of compliance.

This approach aims to reassure both commercial partners and consumers. American resellers can thus continue to distribute ASUS network equipment without fearing potential disruptions related to import procedures or regulatory requirements. The company also emphasizes that its products will continue to benefit from regular software support as well as cloud security services over the long term.

While some industry players could face more complex logistical or administrative adjustments, ASUS believes it has gained a head start thanks to a compliance strategy implemented on a global scale.

## WiFi 8 in ASUS's sights

This validation also comes at an important time for the wireless networking industry. As the first work around WiFi 8 intensifies, ASUS wants to focus part of its resources on developing this future generation of connectivity.

The manufacturer explains that it is collaborating closely with various international technology partners to prepare for the next market developments. The goal is to offer solutions capable of delivering increased performance, better connection stability, and increasingly advanced security mechanisms, while complying with the regulatory requirements of different global markets.

With more than three decades of presence and engineering activities in the American market, ASUS states that it wants to support the evolution of consumer network infrastructure over the long term. The company believes that regulatory compliance and technological innovation must advance together to ensure a smooth transition to future connectivity standards.

## A global strategy focused on resilience

ASUS also highlights the organization of its global industrial network. Distributed across several strategic production sites, this infrastructure allows it to reduce risks related to logistical disruptions or local regulatory changes.

According to the company, this diversification of production is a key element in ensuring constant availability of network equipment across different international markets. This strategy also makes it possible to maintain a high level of compliance with import and certification regulations imposed by national authorities.

Through this announcement, ASUS is above all seeking to send a message of stability to its entire ecosystem. As the networking sector prepares to enter the WiFi 8 era, the manufacturer intends to position itself as a reliable partner capable of ensuring both the continuity of its operations and the development of the next generations of wireless technologies.
