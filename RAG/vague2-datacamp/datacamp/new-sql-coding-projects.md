## Full summary

This short announcement article presents four new interactive SQL coding projects on DataCamp, designed to help individuals or teams practice newly acquired skills on real, playful datasets. The queries used can be easily reused for business use cases such as spotting trends, working with sales data, or analyzing customer reviews. The projects are introduced by Izzy Weber.

The four projects are:

1. **Analyzing test results in New York public schools** — use descriptive statistics and filters to analyze test scores in New York public schools (project 1416).
2. **US baby name trends** — use categorization and ranking techniques to explore 101 years of American baby names (project 1441).
3. **What was the golden age of video games?** — use joins and set theory to discover the best years in video gaming (project 1413).
4. **Optimizing revenue for a sports e-commerce retailer** — analyze product data to help an online sports goods shop maximize revenue (project 1442).

The article also invites readers to share the projects with friends and colleagues via links.
