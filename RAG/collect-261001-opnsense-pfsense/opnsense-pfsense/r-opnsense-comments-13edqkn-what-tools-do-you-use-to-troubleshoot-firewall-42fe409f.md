# r-opnsense-comments-13edqkn-what-tools-do-you-use-to-troubleshoot-firewall-42fe409f

I was wondering if there is a way to "see" which path within the firewall rules a packet takes, as a way of understanding why the rules that one creates (Port Forward, Floating and regular rules, etc.) are not getting the job done the way they are intended.

I often find myself using traceroute, enabling logging on everything and capturing packets to get an idea of what's going on, but none of those methods are enough on their own and putting all the pieces together takes a lot of time and effort.
