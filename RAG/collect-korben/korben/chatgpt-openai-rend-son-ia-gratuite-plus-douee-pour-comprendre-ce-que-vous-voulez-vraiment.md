## Full summary

The article reports that OpenAI updated GPT-5.5 Instant, the free model that responds by default when you type a question in the ChatGPT app, with nothing for the user to activate. The whole point of the update is to better grasp what you really mean. The AI is supposed to understand the intent behind a request, even poorly worded, and adapt when you correct it or challenge its answer, instead of dumbly serving the same thing slightly reworded.

It also handles geographic context better. If you ask for a good restaurant or an address, it now takes into account where you are to give something useful, rather than a generic answer valid nowhere in particular. OpenAI additionally promises more natural and coherent formatting, with fewer indigestible blocks and endless lists: an AI that writes a bit more like a human and a bit less like a technical sheet filled out by the meter.

For reference, GPT-5.5 Instant has held the default model role since May. At launch, OpenAI already announced a big drop in errors, around half as many invented claims compared to the previous version, those famous hallucinations where the AI delivers false information with disconcerting aplomb. The good news is that all this costs nothing: as it is the free default model, the improvement arrives for everyone, paying subscribers and casual users alike, with no box to tick or hidden option.

OpenAI also takes the opportunity to give a bit more control over memory. You can now see which memories or old conversations the AI used to personalize an answer, and correct or delete what is no longer current, so as not to drag around forever an old piece of information that has become false. The article notes a remaining question that is hard to verify yourself: improving context understanding sounds great on paper, but these are OpenAI's own promises, and it is in use, on your own daily questions, that you will see whether the AI really understands better or whether it is mostly communication. Not a revolution, rather a good polish on the AI that hundreds of millions of people use every day without even choosing their model.

## Key points

- OpenAI updated GPT-5.5 Instant, ChatGPT's free default model, automatically for all users.
- Focus: better understanding of intent behind even poorly worded requests, and adapting when corrected.
- Improved geographic context for local questions (restaurants, addresses).
- More natural, coherent formatting with fewer long blocks and endless lists.
- GPT-5.5 Instant has been the default since May; at launch, around half as many hallucinations versus the prior version.
- Free for everyone, no option to enable.
- New memory controls: view which memories/old conversations were used and correct or delete them.
- The author notes these are vendor promises to be validated in real daily use; not a revolution but a polish.
