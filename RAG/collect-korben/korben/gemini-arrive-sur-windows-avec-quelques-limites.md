## Full summary

The article reports that Google launched the official Gemini application for Windows on 10 September. The shortcut Alt + Space now calls Gemini from Windows, opening a window over the software you are working in, so you can ask a question without hunting for the right browser tab.

Gemini can help draft a message, summarize a document or understand code. You can also send it an image for analysis, then return to your previous application. It therefore brings the web service's uses with more direct access from the desktop.

To install it, you go through the "Gemini pour ordinateur" page, download GeminiSetup.exe and sign in to your Google account. Windows 10 or newer, at least 8 GB of memory and 200 MB of free space are required. x64 and ARM64 PCs are supported.

Internet remains necessary. Installing this software does not run the AI model on your computer, and a professional or school account must have Gemini access authorized by the administrator. With the same account, Google synchronizes conversation history and memory across desktop, web and mobile.

For usage, the shortcut can be changed in the settings, avoiding conflict with another program already using Alt + Space. The app is also accessible from the taskbar and its icon in the Windows notification area. Integration with Google apps allows searching for information in Gmail or Drive to prepare a summary. Image creation with Nano Banana and video creation with Gemini Omni are also among the highlighted possibilities, but some features require a Google AI subscription and are reserved for adults, with variable availability.

Part of what makes a desktop assistant interesting is still missing. Screen-context sharing, dictation in other applications, and connecting local folders to Gemini Spark, the agent responsible for chaining tasks, are still presented as Mac-version features. Google announces their gradual enrichment on Windows, without a precise timeline. As a result, opening Gemini over a spreadsheet does not mean it can automatically read that spreadsheet: this first version should not be confused with all the on-screen assistance demonstrations offered on macOS. But it is a good start.

## Key points

- Google launched the official Gemini app for Windows on 10 September.
- Shortcut Alt + Space opens Gemini in a window over the current application.
- Install via GeminiSetup.exe; requires Windows 10+, 8 GB RAM, 200 MB free space; supports x64 and ARM64.
- Internet is always required; the model does not run locally.
- Pro/school accounts need administrator-authorized Gemini access; history and memory sync across desktop, web and mobile.
- The shortcut is customizable; app also reachable from the taskbar and notification area.
- Google apps integration (Gmail, Drive), Nano Banana image generation and Gemini Omni video; some features need a Google AI subscription and are adult-only.
- Missing vs Mac: screen-context sharing, dictation in other apps, and local folder connection to Gemini Spark, with no precise Windows timeline.
