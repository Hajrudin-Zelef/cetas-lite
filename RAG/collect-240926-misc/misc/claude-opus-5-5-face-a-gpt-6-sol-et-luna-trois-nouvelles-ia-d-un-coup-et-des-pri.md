# Claude Opus 5.5 vs GPT-6 Sol and Luna: three new AIs at once, and falling prices

Slowing down the AI race, step one: launch a new model
Ten days ago, Dario Amodei published a long essay on his website devoted to the pace of AI development. The CEO of Anthropic urged the handful of labs at the forefront of AI to jointly moderate the cadence of their progress, giving safety time to catch up with power.

	
We must slow the rate at which we improve the capabilities of AI models. Progress will always seem fast.


Amodei specified that this moderation did not imply any freeze on model training.

## Claude Opus 5.5: faster, cheaper, tested by external evaluators

This Tuesday, September 22, Anthropic unveiled Claude Opus 5.5, which it presents as its first release since that call. The model was tested before publication by external evaluators, including METR and Frontier Design. The publisher claims a level comparable to Claude Fable 5.1 on most tasks and asserts it is ahead of GPT-6 Astra on several benchmarks, among them Terminal-Bench 4.0 and Humanity's Last Exam.

The price drops: $4 per million tokens on input and $20 on output, versus $5 and $25 for Opus 5. Adding more frugal token consumption, Anthropic estimates the savings at 40% on common uses.

## GPT-6 Sol and Luna: OpenAI announces its new models the same day

Ninety minutes later, OpenAI presented GPT-6 Sol and Luna, streamlined versions of its flagship model GPT-6 Astra. Sol targets complex code and autonomous agents, Luna targets high-volume repetitive tasks. Expect $2 and $10 per million tokens for the former, $0.10 and $0.50 for the latter. The announced halving is calculated from the promotional prices of GPT-5.6 Sol; the official July price list set the latter at $5 and $30.

The two models are gradually rolling out to Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu subscribers, while Free and Go plan users access Luna via the desktop app.

In a single morning, the two main rivals in generative AI therefore renewed their lineup and revised their prices downward, ten days after the publication of Amodei's essay.
