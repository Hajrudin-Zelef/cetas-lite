Integer computing throughput comparison: the 2P AMD EPYC 9534 solution (estimated 1,070 in the SPECrate®2017_int_base benchmark, total TDP of 560 W, 128 cores total) offers approximately 1.34x the performance/W compared to the published results of the 2P AMD EPYC 7763 solution (estimated 800 in the SPECrate®2017_int_base benchmark, total TDP of 560 W, 128 cores total). Bert-Large NLP sparse INT8 comparison: the 2P AMD EPYC 9534 solution (345.6 items/s, total TDP of 560 W, 128 cores total) offers approximately 2.67x the performance/W compared to the published results of the 2P AMD EPYC 7763 solution (129.7 items/s, total TDP of 560 W, 128 cores total).
