#### Engineering Tools

**Data build tool (dbt)** – although analytics engineering is relatively recent, dbt has shaken up the world of analytics. dbt is based on ELT (Extract-Load-Transform), as opposed to ETL (Extract-Transform-Load), and facilitates transformations from data already loaded into the warehouse.

**Git/GitHub** – a version control system that tracks changes made to SQL scripts, data models, workbooks, etc. All information is centralized in repositories on GitHub, the platform built on Git, promoting better collaboration within the analytics team.

### Soft Skills

Beyond quantitative skills, the following qualities are essential in a good data analyst:

- Cross-functional collaboration
- Project management
- Ability to handle ambiguous/complex problems
- Attention to detail

## Data Analyst Job Description Template

Here is a job description template for a data analyst role. Use it as a base, but customize it as much as possible according to your company, your sector, and the projects to be carried out. Customization is key to attracting better-informed and better-targeted candidates.

**Job Title**: Data Analyst

**Location**: [City, Region] / [Full Remote] / [Hybrid]

**Job Description**:

*We are looking for a rigorous and methodical Data Analyst to join our team. You will collect, organize, and analyze large volumes of data to provide insights and inform decision-making within the organization. You will also be responsible for creating reports and visualizations to communicate your findings to stakeholders.*

**Responsibilities**:

- *Collect, clean, and organize data from various sources*
- *Perform statistical analyses and produce reports and visualizations to communicate results*
- *Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify trends and patterns in data*
- *Create dashboards and automated reports to track key performance indicators*
- *Continuously improve data collection, analysis, and reporting processes and systems*

**Qualifications**:

- *[optional] Bachelor's degree in a relevant field (computer science, mathematics, statistics, etc.)*
- *2+ years of experience as a data analyst or similar role. If you don't have 2+ years of experience, please share relevant projects you've completed.*
- *Excellent command of SQL*
- *Proficiency with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Excel, Looker, Google Sheets)*
- *Experience with statistical analysis software (e.g., R, Python) is a plus*
- *Excellent analytical and communication skills*
- *Autonomy and ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously*
