# les-30-questions-et-reponses-les-plus-frequentes-lors-d-entretiens-d-embauche-dans-le-domaine-de-l-i

Course

Agentic AI is rapidly being adopted across all sectors, and many new companies are now looking for experts in this field. This article includes real questions from job interviews for entry-level and mid-level positions, some that I myself have formulated, and others that provide a general understanding of the field.

Please note that in a real interview, you may be asked to first complete a practical exercise. You may also be asked to explain your approach to accomplishing such tasks, so please prepare accordingly.

Some questions covered here touch on more general topics, thus offering additional areas of study. I also recommend being authentic during the interview. Sometimes, even if you have all the required experience, it is just as important to have carefully thought through your answers in advance.

## Basic agentic AI interview questions

We will start with some fundamental questions that provide definitions and set the tone for the article. Some of them also provide guidance on what to prepare in advance.

### What AI applications have you worked on?

Recruiters will want to know about your experience in a personal and detailed way. They will not be satisfied with just reviewing the list of projects, since that is probably already on your resume, but they will also assess your ability to clearly explain each project and the specific role you played in it.

Please prepare your answer in advance and have a clear understanding of your past work. Practicing with a colleague or writing your ideas down can help you organize your thoughts.

### What libraries, frameworks, and tools are you used to using? What other libraries do you know?

As with the previous question, recruiters will want to know more than what is on your resume. Be ready to describe in detail each project you have worked on and to explain all the technologies used.

Please note that you may be asked many follow-up questions at this stage. It is essential for an employer to fully understand your specific skills. Please make sure to look into or familiarize yourself with libraries such as LlamaIndex or LangChain, as these are the most commonly used high-level development libraries. Also, familiarize yourself with model providers such as Huggingface or Ollama.

### What is agentic AI and how does it differ from traditional AI?

Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems capable of acting autonomously, defining their own goals, and adapting to changing environments. In contrast, traditional AI generally operates according to predefined rules, taking inputs and producing outputs.

For example, you can discuss your own projects or mention other agentic AI applications that you have used or heard about. For a more in-depth explanation of each of them, I recommend reading the following article on agentic AI.

### What excites you about working with agentic AI?

This is a common question aimed at understanding your motivations and interests. Generally very open-ended, this question allows you to discuss any topic and engage in a sincere conversation with the recruiter.

Prepare a relevant anecdote or explanation, make sure to be enthusiastic, specific, and try to talk about a topic related to the position. If you cannot identify a particular topic, try talking about a product you use and explain why it is exciting or interesting.

### Could you give us an example of an agentic AI application and present its components?

Let's take the example of a self-driving car. First, consider the goals the car must achieve: it must drive and navigate autonomously on roads, establish optimal routes, avoid obstacles, and, above all, ensure passenger safety.

Once the goals are defined, we can examine how the application could be structured. A main model could be responsible for driving the car, continuously or on demand receiving information from smaller models that handle tasks such as route optimization or gathering environmental information.

During the interview, you can go deeper into each of these elements. Feel free to offer your own examples as well.

### What LLMs have you used so far?

Be ready to discuss in detail the particular models you have worked with. Employers will want to assess your understanding of the model internally. For example, be prepared to discuss open-source models such as Llama or proprietary GPT models.

This is also an excellent opportunity to mention new models and demonstrate to the interviewer that you keep up with the latest trends. You can, for example, bring up the topic of Deepseek R1 and other reasoning models.

### What is your experience with using LLMs via the API?

This question concerns using LLMs via the API rather than through a chat window. Be prepared to discuss your projects if they use APIs. Please make sure to review API usage, secret key generation and storage, cost tracking, and the different model providers. This could also be a good time to bring up your engineering experience.

If you do not have enough experience using LLMs via the API, please refer to the following resources:

- GPT-4.5 API Tutorial: Getting Started with the OpenAI API
- DeepSeek API: A Guide with Examples and Cost Calculations
- Mistral OCR: A Guide with Practical Examples

### Have you ever used reasoning models?

With the emergence of reasoning models such as OpenAI o3 and DeepSeek-R1, employers will want to know your experience and familiarity with these tools. This goes beyond simply selecting a different model in an application or an API call, because these models produce thinking tokens and often require a different usage pattern.

You could make a good impression if you know how to fine-tune and run an open-source model locally, as this is something the company you are applying to might need. As an exercise, consider fine-tuning DeepSeek-R1 and running it locally:

- Fine-tuning DeepSeek R1 (reasoning model)
- How to set up and run DeepSeek-R1 locally with Ollama

### Do you use large language models (LLMs) in your daily workflow? If so, for what purpose?

If you use large language models (LLMs) in your workflow, this could be an opportunity to demonstrate your expertise. You can discuss the tools you have used, what you liked or disliked about them, and even the things you are looking forward to. Consider mentioning popular tools such as Cursor, NotebookLM, Lovable, Replit, Claude Artifacts, Manus AI, etc.

### What sources do you use to stay informed about the latest advances in agentic AI?

Some employers will want to know your level of knowledge or your ability to keep up to date with AI. Sources you could include in your answer are: AI conferences, forums, newsletters, etc.

### How comfortable are you with reading and understanding articles and documents?

Reading books, articles, and documentation is an integral part of almost every job in the AI field. You might also be asked about your general approach to learning or finding information. It is best not to give the impression of relying excessively on chatbots in your answer.

Be prepared to discuss a recent article you have read. For example, you can address the topic of Google's Titans architecture. Google's Titans architecture.
