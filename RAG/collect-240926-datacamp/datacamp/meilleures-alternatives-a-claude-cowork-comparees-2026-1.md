# meilleures-alternatives-a-claude-cowork-comparees-2026

<!-- source: https://www.datacamp.com/fr/blog/claude-cowork-alternatives -->

Cursus

Claude Cowork is a solid tool, powered by high-performing models, and it would be dishonest to claim otherwise.

That said, autonomous execution in the cloud remains a beta feature, and anything touching your local files still requires the desktop app to be open. For most users, this means: if your computer goes to sleep, so does your agent. You also need a Claude Pro subscription at $20/month (heavy users quickly moving up to Max at $100/month or $200/month), you remain locked into the Claude model family, and it's not ideal for many team workflows with shared access.

Plenty of reasons to look at other options. I compared 9 alternatives across four criteria that really drive the purchasing decision: cost, the ability to keep working when you close your computer, supported model families, and the setup effort required.

For context on Cowork and its place in Anthropic's lineup, see our Claude Cowork tutorial and our guide to learning Claude.

## In brief

- If you want the closest like-for-like swap, **ChatGPT Agent** is the best option.
- If your work revolves around email, calendar, and meetings, **Lindy** handles everything in the cloud while you're offline; if it's code, **Cursor**'s cloud agents are the way to go.
- If you're looking for a **free alternative**, **OpenClaw** is open source and free to run; you only pay for your own API keys and hosting.
- If **team collaboration** is the issue, **Notion Agents** acts on your shared workspace, or **Copilot Cowork** if you're already equipped with Microsoft 365.

## The best general-purpose Claude Cowork alternatives

General-purpose cloud agents are the closest replacements: hosted agents that take a task, work on the web, and deliver a finished result.

### ChatGPT Work

ChatGPT Work is OpenAI's autonomous task executor and the closest thing to a like-for-like Cowork replacement. It's one of the three modes in the new ChatGPT app, between Chat and Codex, and the alignment is almost a mirror of Anthropic's lineup: Chat, Work, and Codex versus Chat, Cowork, and Claude Code. If you've used Cowork, the mental model transfers immediately.

It browses the web on its own, executes multi-step tasks in a hosted environment, and, in the business and enterprise context covered by ChatGPT Work, maintains persistent workflows at the team level, rather than tied to a single person's session.

The difference that matters versus Cowork is where the work runs. Work has relied on OpenAI's infrastructure since launch, so a long research-and-compile task continues while your computer is in a bag. Cowork can now do this too, but only via a beta cloud mode, and never for tasks that touch your local files.

The trade-off is a model lock-in of a different kind. You get the GPT family and nothing else, which is fine until the day you want to compare outputs across providers. If you're mainly weighing model choice, our GPT-5.6 Terra vs Claude Sonnet comparison on benchmarks and pricing is a better starting point than any marketing page.

#### Key features

- **Autonomous web browsing:** The agent navigates sites, fills out forms, and extracts data without click-by-click supervision.
- **Hosted execution:** Tasks run server-side, without depending on your machine's sleep state.
- **Multi-device persistence:** Work conversations sync across web, mobile, and desktop, and persist via Projects and Scheduled Tasks. Team sharing goes through workspace projects, not shared live sessions.

#### Pricing

Work has no standalone price. It is included in the ChatGPT and Codex plans and deducted from your quota, like Codex. Free ($0) and Go ($8/month) do not include Work. Plus, at $20/month, is the entry point, similar to Anthropic's structure.

#### Limits

You are on GPT models without a BYOK option, so cost control across a mix of workloads is limited to the tier you purchased. Agent runs are also fairly opaque: you see the result, but reconstructing exactly which pages the agent read and in what order is harder than with a tool that logs every step.

### Perplexity Computer

Perplexity Computer executes desktop-type tasks with its built-in search engine, so the output includes live web sources rather than an unsourced summary. We tested it in parallel workflows in our hands-on Perplexity Computer tutorial, and source traceability is what sets it apart from every other tool here.

Where Cowork dives into your local files, Computer turns to the web. If your work is competitive analysis, market sizing, or anything that raises the question "where does this number come from?", that's a real asset. If your work is reorganizing a folder of spreadsheets on your Mac, it isn't.

#### Key features

- **Sourced task execution:** Every claim in a research deliverable links back to its source, which holds up during a colleague's review.
- **Auto-routed model selection:** Perplexity chooses the model per request from Claude, GPT, Gemini, and its Sonar family.
- **Cloud task execution:** Computer handles multi-step tasks in Perplexity's cloud, without depending on your machine.
- **Education pricing:** Verified students and teachers access Pro features at $10/month via SheerID.

#### Pricing

Perplexity publishes six tiers, and access to Computer is limited to the two highest: Max ($200/month) and Enterprise Max ($325/seat/month). Which brings us smoothly to the limits.

#### Limits

Computer is reserved for the Max plan at $200/month, the same price as Claude's Max 20x tier, and 10 times the Pro plan most people start on. Model routing is automatic with no BYOK option, so you can't force a specific model when you already know which one performs better.
