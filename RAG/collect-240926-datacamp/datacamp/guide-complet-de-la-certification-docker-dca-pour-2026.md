## What is Docker certification?

Docker certification attests to a professional's ability to effectively use Docker, a key technology in the modern IT field. The Docker Certified Associate certification, priced at $195, validates skills in tasks and responsibilities related to the Docker platform, with an emphasis on mastery of deploying and managing containerized applications.

## Deepen your knowledge of the Docker Certified Associate (DCA) certification

The Docker Certified Associate (DCA) certification is a recognized certification that attests to expertise in Docker technologies. Intended for professionals who use Docker daily, the DCA certification emphasizes the practical and concrete skills required for containerization and application deployment. The DCA is proctored by Examity and offered by Mirantis Training. The price is $195/€200.

### Key areas of the DCA curriculum

The DCA program is comprehensive and covers Docker's essential features:

- **Container orchestration**: Understanding the fundamental principles and tools of container orchestration, essential for deploying and managing containerized applications..
- **Docker Swarm and Kubernetes**: Knowledge of these two popular container orchestration tools is required, with Docker Swarm being user-friendly and Kubernetes being ideal for complex, high-demand applications..
- **Building, registering, and managing images**: Learning how to handle Docker images, which are snapshots of a container, is an important part of the program..
- **Installation and configuration**: Mastery of installing and configuring Docker, a skill regularly required in practical scenarios..
- **Security and networking**: A solid understanding of Docker's networking features and prioritizing security are essential..
- **Storage and volumes:** Knowledge of Docker volumes, which store information, is essential..
- **Docker Enterprise Edition**: Mastering Docker EE, designed for critical deployments, helps enrich your skills..

### Docker certification exam format

The DCA exam is a 90-minute test consisting of 55 questions, including multiple-choice and single-answer questions. It is recommended to have between 6 and 12 months of experience with Docker before taking the exam, which costs $195..

### Prerequisite knowledge required

To obtain the Docker Certified Associate certification, it is necessary to possess a distinct set of skills, essential for managing and deploying containerized applications. These skills include:

- **Using existing images**: Ability to run applications from images stored in a centralized registry.
- **Cluster-wide deployment**: Mastery of distributing images across different nodes in a cluster.
- **Troubleshooting**: Ability to identify and resolve issues reported by stakeholders.
- **Managing enterprise clusters**: Ability to configure enterprise clusters, including a UCP manager, a DTR replica, and a worker node.
- **Application migration**: Expertise in transitioning traditional applications to container-based environments.
- **Managing the Docker engine**: Proficiency in configuring and troubleshooting the Docker engine.
- **Routine maintenance and configuration**: Skills in performing general maintenance and configuration tasks.

Candidates seeking this certification must have at least six months to one year of hands-on experience with Docker, preferably with Docker Enterprise Edition. The skills required at this level include:

- **Container security**: In-depth understanding of security practices related to containerization.
- **Experience as a cloud service provider**: In-depth knowledge of at least one major cloud service provider.
- **Configuration management tools**: Mastery of configuration management and automation tools.

## Docker Certification FAQ

### Who should consider Docker certification?

Professionals in DevOps, software development, system administration, and IT operations who work with Docker technologies should consider obtaining Docker certification to validate their skills and enhance their career opportunities.

### How can I prepare for Docker certification?

Preparation involves hands-on experience with Docker, studying Docker documentation, and completing training or tutorials. DataCamp offers courses on containerization and Docker that can serve as a foundation for your Docker certification preparation.

### What topics are covered in the Docker Certified Associate (DCA) exam?

The DCA exam covers key Docker fundamentals, container orchestration, Docker security, networking, storage and volumes, as well as the Docker Enterprise platform.

### How long is the Docker certification valid?

Docker certification is generally valid for two years. Certified individuals must renew their certification through continuing education or by taking the exam again to stay up to date with Docker technologies.
