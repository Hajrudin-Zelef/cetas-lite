# ChatGPT's voice mode stops acting like a walkie-talkie and finally talks like a human

## Key takeaways AI-generated summary

1. OpenAI has launched GPT-Live, a full-duplex voice mode that allows ChatGPT to speak and listen simultaneously, eliminating the awkward silences of the classic walkie-talkie.
2. GPT-Live discreetly delegates complex requests to a more powerful model in the background while continuing the conversation, and handles live translation with on-screen info display.
3. The rollout covers iPhone, Android, web and CarPlay; free accounts get access to GPT-Live-1 mini, while Go, Plus and Pro subscribers get the full model.

ChatGPT has just learned to stop interrupting you like a boor. OpenAI has launched GPT-Live, a new voice mode that knows how to talk and listen to you at the same time, and it's pretty clean.

Until now, talking out loud with a chatbot was frankly painful, and looked more like a walkie-talkie, each taking turns, with awkward little pauses after every bit of a sentence, constantly reminding you that you're talking to a machine and not to a friend on the other end of the line.

GPT-Live runs in full-duplex, and that changes everything. Like a real phone call where both speakers can actually talk at the same time without it being too much of a mess. To be very concrete, you can cut it off in the middle of a sentence, ask it to slow down if it's jabbering too fast, and it even knows how to shut up when it senses you're searching for your words.

*Screenshot*

The cleverest part happens when it has to go fetch information from the web. Instead of leaving you in a heavy silence, it discreetly hands the complicated question over to a beefier model that churns away in the background, and it keeps chatting with you while the answer bubbles back up to the surface.

It also supports live translation, with a tiny bit of lag all the same, and can display cards on screen for the weather or the match score while it's talking to you.

On the rollout side, OpenAI didn't waste any time, since it's already launched on iPhone, Android, the web and even CarPlay in the car. Free accounts inherit a lightweight version dubbed GPT-Live-1 mini, while Go, Plus and Pro subscribers get the full model, smarter and more responsive.

You can clearly see the maneuver behind all this. The smoother the conversation, the more we tend to forget we're talking to a robot, and the more we tend to let slip slightly secret things, without even thinking about it. It's impressive and a bit creepy all the same.

An AI that talks without the slightest pause and listens to you continuously is ultra comfortable, but it's also the best way to forget that you're confiding in a machine.
