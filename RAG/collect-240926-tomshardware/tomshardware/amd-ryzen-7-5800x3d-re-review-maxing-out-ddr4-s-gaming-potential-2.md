 Using it to build from scratch with parts from retail seems a very bad investment.
 
 With 8GB of DDR4 on AM4 and perhaps without the ability to add another 8GB easily, you're in a tough spot for sure: you may just need to hunt around for 2nd hand parts.
 
 Don't expect magic replacing the 5600XT with a 5800X3D, especially if your DDR4 came from Xeon times and might be as slow as DDR4-2133: the basic assumption is that a 5800X3D with DDR4-3200 is nearly as good as a 7800X with DDR5-5200 (or rather better than all the Rocket Lake contemporaries).
 
 A lot of games aren't nearly as CPU dependent as the PC press seems to imply, while***some*** games require eliminating quite a few more bottlenecks besides just a CPU upgrade: you really need to know where you're hurting.
