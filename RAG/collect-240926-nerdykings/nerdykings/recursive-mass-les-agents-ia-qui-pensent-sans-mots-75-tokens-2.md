# Recursive Mass: The AI Agents That Think Without Words (-75% Tokens)

Let's be honest: Recursive Mass is not a new model. It is *proof that we can massively improve teams of agents without touching the models*. Just by changing the way they communicate. And that is exactly the same spirit as other papers coming out right now — for example DeepSeek teaching AIs to reason with visual primitives instead of describing everything in text, or MIT's RLMs making AIs navigate information instead of loading everything into memory.

The pattern is clear: **the next wave of gains in AI may not come from bigger models, but from smarter architectures around existing models.** And the fact that it costs 4 dollars to train opens the door to any team — not just labs with millions of GPUs.

What remains is the real underlying issue, and it is uncomfortable: we are building systems that think together in a language we can no longer read. **Can we really let that run on decisions that matter?** For now, no one really has an answer.
