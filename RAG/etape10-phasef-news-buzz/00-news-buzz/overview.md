# STEP 10 — Phase F: News, Buzz & Society (2026-02-01 → 2026-09-22)

> Coverage window: **2026-02-01 through 2026-09-22** (cutoff inclusive). Events dated after 2026-09-22 are excluded except where explicitly marked "scheduled after cutoff" or "upcoming at cutoff."
> All claims carry a provenance tag, a date, and a URL or clearly linked source. Conflicts and gaps are preserved, not smoothed over.

## 0. Provenance legend

- `[official]` — announcement by the rights-holder: studio, network, label, artist, organizer, government body.
- `[vendor-reported]` — self-reported platform metric (Netflix, Spotify, Billboard charts via trade press).
- `[independent]` — established newsroom reporting (Reuters, AP, Variety, The Hollywood Reporter, Deadline).
- `[secondary]` — entertainment press or aggregator recapping an event; used for most cultural facts below.
- `[unverified]` — single weak source, rumor, conflicting dates, or an uncorroborated claim. Treat with caution.
