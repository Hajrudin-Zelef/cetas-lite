## 12. Glossary

- **AOTY / ROTY / SOTY / BNA** — Grammy shorthand: Album, Record, Song of the Year; Best New Artist.
- **Palme d'Or** — top prize of the Cannes Film Festival.
- **Golden Bear** — top prize of the Berlin International Film Festival (Berlinale).
- **Golden Lion** — top prize of the Venice International Film Festival.
- **Silver Bear / Silver Lion** — second-tier festival prizes (Berlin / Venice).
- **Hot 100** — Billboard's flagship US singles chart.
- **Views (Netflix)** — Netflix's self-reported engagement metric; methodology (91-day vs half-year windows) affects rankings.
- **91-day window** — Netflix's standard measurement window for title performance.
- **Residency** — a concert series played at a single venue (here: 30 nights at Madison Square Garden).
- **In-the-round stage** — a stage with audience on all sides (Styles's MSG setup).
- **Halftime show** — the Super Bowl's mid-game entertainment set.
- **Expansion (gaming)** — a large add-on to an existing game (*WoW: Midnight*, *Diablo IV: Lord of Hatred*).
- **Remake vs remaster** — rebuilt game vs visually upgraded re-release (*Fatal Frame II* remake; *Dragon Quest VII Reimagined*).
- **AAA** — a big-budget, major-studio video game.
- **Switch 2** — Nintendo's console launched June 5, 2025.
- **Raccoon City** — the fictional setting of early *Resident Evil* games, revisited in *Requiem*.
- **Geffen Records** — Olivia Rodrigo's label.
- **Live Nation** — the tour promoter behind *The Unraveled Tour*.
- **ICE** — US Immigration and Customs Enforcement; the target of Grammy-night criticism.
- **Culture-war discourse** — public controversy framed as a clash of cultural/political values (used neutrally here).
- **Fandom** — an organized fan community (e.g., *Heated Rivalry*, *KPop Demon Hunters*).
- **"Golden"** — the breakout song from *KPop Demon Hunters* (Hot 100 No. 1, Oscar winner).
- **"drop dead"** — Olivia Rodrigo's June 2026 lead single (Hot 100 No. 1 debut).
- **"Aperture"** — lead single from Harry Styles's *Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally.*
- **Together, Together** — Harry Styles's 2026 tour.
- **The Unraveled Tour** — Olivia Rodrigo's 2026–2027 tour (starts Sep 25, 2026).
- **S2** — season 2 (of a series).
- **"Put a bell on your bicycle"** — recurring in-joke from Styles's 2026 tour that became a fan meme.
