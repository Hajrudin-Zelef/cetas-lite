## 12b. Glossary — extended

- **Auteur-driven** — filmmaker-led cinema, the Berlinale's curatorial emphasis in 2026.
- **Bankability** — an actor's perceived commercial reliability (used of Sandra Hüller post-Berlinale).
- **Box-office snapshot** — a gross figure valid only for its date; 2026 totals moved daily.
- **Commissioning trend** — what buyers and funders are ordering, inferred from festival lineups.
- **Craft prizes** — below-the-line festival awards (direction, screenplay, cinematography).
- **Culture-war** — see §12; used neutrally for values-clash controversies.
- **Day-and-date** — simultaneous theatrical and streaming release (not the 2026 norm for the titles here).
- **Debut (chart)** — entering a chart in the release week, distinct from climbing to No. 1 later.
- **Docuseries** — a multi-part documentary series (the *Sean Combs* project).
- **First full year** — 2026 as the Switch 2's first complete calendar year post-launch.
- **Flashpoint** — an event that ignites wider controversy (Grammy-night speeches).
- **Gender-bending (role)** — a part crossing period gender presentation (Hüller in *Rose*).
- **Grand Jury Prize** — second prize at Cannes (Grand Prix) and Venice (Silver Lion Grand Jury Prize).
- **Halftime headliner** — the featured Super Bowl mid-game performer.
- **In-joke (tour)** — a recurring bit meaningful mainly to attending fans ("put a bell on your bicycle").
- **Lead single** — the first single released from an album ("Aperture"; "drop dead").
- **Limited Series** — Emmy category for closed-ended series (*DTF St. Louis*).
- **MSG** — Madison Square Garden, New York.
- **Oscar-nominated (status)** — e.g., Çatak (*The Teachers' Lounge*) and Hüller (*Anatomy of a Fall*) entered 2026 with prior nominations.
- **Outing (tour)** — one concert-tour cycle (Rodrigo's third major outing).
- **Palme d'Or streak** — Neon's reported seven consecutive Palme-winning distributions.
- **Presale** — ticket sales before general on-sale (fan-club/verified-fan windows for the *Unraveled Tour*).
- **Second Palme** — a director's second Palme d'Or (Mungiu, tenth reported).
- **Setlist** — the ordered songs of a concert.
- **Simultaneous chart entries** — multiple tracks by one act in the chart at once (KPop Demon Hunters ×4).
- **Singalong** — audience-wide communal singing (MSG Parton tribute).
- **Streaming era precedent** — the note that a Netflix animated film's Oscar double had no close parallel.
- **Tentpole** — a major release anchoring a studio's year (*Superman*-scale titles; *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*).
- **Tour leg** — a geographic segment of a tour (Amsterdam/London/São Paulo/Mexico City).
- **Viral (usage here)** — spreading rapidly via social platforms, not a medical term.
- **Window (release)** — the exclusivity period before a film reaches streaming.
- **Year-end listicles** — ranked "best of the year" articles; 2026's contained invented entries and were excluded.
