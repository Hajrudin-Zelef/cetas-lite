## B26. Foldable market analysis (2026)

- **Three-way race by September 2026**: Samsung (Fold 8 $1,899 / Ultra $2,099 / Flip 8 $1,199), Google (Pixel 11 Pro Fold $1,899, IP68), Apple (iPhone Duo $1,999, IP68, ships Oct 23) `[secondary]`.
- **IP ratings became the differentiator**: Apple and Google at IP68 vs. Samsung at IP48 — a reversal of the usual durability narrative `[secondary]`.
- **Form-factor split**: wide/passport (Fold 8, iPhone Duo) vs. tall (Fold 8 Ultra, Pixel Fold) — no consensus yet on the "right" aspect ratio `[secondary]`.
- **Clamshells under pressure**: Flip-style devices faced the "last of its kind" narrative as book-style and tri-fold concepts advanced `[secondary]`.
- **Tri-fold**: Samsung's tri-fold ambitions discussed for 2027; Huawei's tri-fold remained China-constrained `[secondary]`.
- Analyst view: foldables still single-digit share of shipments but the highest ASP segment and the main hardware-innovation showcase `[independent]`.

## B27. Camera technology trends (2026)

- **1-inch sensors** went mainstream in Ultra flagships (Xiaomi 17 Ultra LOFIC, vivo/OPPO equivalents) `[secondary]`.
- **Variable telephoto** (Xiaomi 17 Ultra 75–100mm) replaced fixed periscopes at the top end `[secondary]`.
- **200MP** became the Ultra-tier standard (Samsung, Xiaomi, Honor, vivo) with pixel-binning for low light `[secondary]`.
- **All-48MP systems** (iPhone 18 Pro) simplified the pipeline: one sensor class across main/ultrawide/telephoto `[secondary]`.
- **Computational video**: 4K120 10-bit Log across focal lengths (vivo), 8K60 on Snapdragon Elite Extreme Gen 6 `[vendor-reported]`.
- **AI photography**: on-device generative edits (Samsung Photo Assist natural-language edits, Google Magic Capture) moved from cloud to NPU `[secondary]`.

## B28. Battery & charging trends (2026)

- **Silicon-carbon batteries** enabled 6,000–7,000 mAh in flagship-thin bodies (Xiaomi 17 Ultra 6,000/6,800 mAh, Honor Magic V6 6,600–7,000 mAh, Infinix 7,000 mAh) `[secondary]`.
- **Charging tiers**: 25W (Samsung base/Apple class), 45–60W (Samsung Ultra/Plus), 90W+ (Xiaomi), 50W wireless (Xiaomi HyperCharge) `[secondary]`.
- **Qi2.2 magnetic** standardized the Android wireless ecosystem (Pixelsnap, Samsung 20W) `[secondary]`.
- **Bypass charging** (Pixel 11) and battery-health longevity features spread to reduce degradation `[secondary]`.
