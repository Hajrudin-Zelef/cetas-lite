## B13. Wearables & smart glasses

### B13.1 Apple Watch Series 12 / Ultra 4 (Sept 9, 2026)

- **Series 12** from $399 (42mm aluminum GPS); **Ultra 4** from $799; pre-orders Sept 9, on sale Sept 18 `[official]`.
- Both: **S11 chip**, 4GB RAM, 64GB storage, new **Health Sensing System** (heart rate measured every 5 seconds all day) `[secondary]`.
- New software-driven health features: **Readiness score** (updates intraday), Recovery HRV, daytime vitals, improved step tracking, Sleep Score, sleep-apnea notifications, hypertension notifications (30-day trend-based; regulatory-gated in some markets, e.g. not at launch in Malaysia/UAE) `[secondary]`.
- **Audio Intelligence**: Sound Recognition, faster Shazam, Live Rewind, Siri Recap — some features arriving later in 2026, English-only at first `[secondary]`.
- Series 12: Ceramic Shield 2 (claimed 60% tougher than Series 11 Ion-X glass); new **ceramic case** option from $899 (pearl white, night blue); aluminum in dark bronze/black/light gold/space gray; titanium in radiant gold/natural `[secondary]`.
- Ultra 4: case **49 x 44 x 12mm — identical to Ultra 3** (DigiTimes thin-case rumor wrong); battery up to **50 hours** (from 42) / 45-hr extended workout; natural or black titanium; band compatibility retained `[secondary]`.
- **Not shipped**: Touch ID, blood-glucose sensing, real-time blood-pressure alerts `[secondary]`.
- Regional prices: Malaysia RM1,799 / RM3,699; UAE AED 1,599 / AED 3,199 `[secondary]`.
