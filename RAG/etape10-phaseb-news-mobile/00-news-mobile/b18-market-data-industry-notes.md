# B18. Market data & industry notes

## B18. Market data & industry notes

### B18.1 Shipments (2026)

- Q1 2026 global smartphone shipments roughly flat to +low-single-digits YoY; Samsung #1, Apple #2, Xiaomi #3 across IDC/Counterpoint/Canalys with firm-to-firm variance `[independent]`.
- Full-year 2026 projected at just **2–3% growth** (IDC, cited Jan 2026) — a replacement market `[independent]`.
- Foldables: continued growth off a small base; book-style gaining share `[independent]`.
- Premium ($600+) segment concentration increased; Apple + Samsung dominated value share `[independent]`.

### B18.2 Pricing trends

- Flagship base prices crept up: 128GB tiers eliminated (Pixel 11, iPhone 18 Pro line at 256GB base); effective $100 increases common `[secondary]`.
- Memory-chip shortages pressured BoM costs through 2026; vendors absorbed or passed through selectively (Samsung's reported 10–15% margin absorption on S26) `[secondary]`.
- Foldable entry stayed ~$1,799–$1,999 (book) / $999–$1,199 (clamshell); Apple's $1,999 Duo matched the top of the market `[secondary]`.

### B18.3 Regional notes

- **India**: key battleground — S26 ₹79,999+, Pixel 11 ₹89,999, iPhone 18 Pro ₹1,34,900+ `[secondary]`.
- **China**: Huawei/Honor/Xiaomi/vivo/OPPO dominated; Apple and Samsung fought for the premium remainder `[independent]`.
- **Europe**: regulatory (DMA, eco-design, repairability scoring) shaped launches and pricing `[secondary]`.
- **US**: carrier installment + trade-in remained the dominant purchase path; eSIM-only designs (iPhone Air lineage) normalized `[secondary]`.

## B19. Reviews consensus (Feb–Sept 2026)

- **Galaxy S26 Ultra**: "polished but incremental"; Privacy Display genuinely useful; 60W charging overdue; Exynos/Snapdragon split scrutinized `[secondary]`.
- **Xiaomi 17 Ultra**: camera-system praise (variable telephoto standout); HyperOS and update policy the caveats; €1,499 pricing debated vs. Samsung/Apple `[secondary]`.
- **Galaxy Z Fold 8 / Ultra**: wide format divisive (loved for reading, questioned for apps); Ultra's 200MP camera best-in-foldable; IP48 lagged Google/Apple `[secondary]`.
- **Pixel 11 series**: AI/camera software best-in-class; Tensor G6 efficiency praised; charging and modem switch watched; price-uplet tolerated for 256GB base `[secondary]`.
- **iPhone 18 Pro / Duo**: early hands-on (Sept 9–18) praised build quality and IP68 on the Duo; crease "barely there"; value debate at $1,999; full reviews pending at cutoff `[secondary]`.
- **Watches**: Series 12/Ultra 4 health-sensing depth praised, iterative hardware noted; Galaxy Watch 9's Wear Elite efficiency praised; Pixel Watch 5's 3000-nit display and 64GB storage welcomed `[secondary]`.
