# B4. Chronology — June 2026

## B4. Chronology — June 2026

### B4.1 Android 17 stable rollout

- **Android 17** reached stable and rolled out to supported Pixels in **June 2026**, ahead of most other Android brands `[secondary]`.
- The Pixel 11 series (August) shipped with Android 17 out of the box `[official]`.
- Samsung's One UI line stayed on Android 16 (One UI 8.5) for the S26 generation; Android 17-based One UI expected on 2027 flagships `[secondary]`.

### B4.2 WWDC 2026 (June): iOS 27 preview

- Apple previewed **iOS 27** at WWDC in June 2026, including the rebuilt **Siri AI** (personal context across Messages/Mail/Photos, on-screen awareness, Camera-app Siri mode) `[secondary]`.
- Apple disclosed that server-side AI features (Siri AI, photo editing, Image Playground) would carry **daily usage limits**, with expanded access via paid tiers/iCloud+ plans `[secondary]`.

### B4.3 Meta's $299 smart glasses (June 24, 2026)

- Meta launched a new lower-cost smart-glasses line at **$299**, made with EssilorLuxottica but **without** Ray-Ban/Oakley branding, as competition heated up `[secondary]`.
- The glasses offer photo/video, music, calls and Meta AI with live translation in 14 new languages `[secondary]`.
- Earlier in June, **Snap** announced **Specs**, standalone AR glasses launching later in 2026 at **$2,195** `[secondary]`.
- Apple smart glasses remained a **2027** rumor `[unverified]`.

### B4.4 Snapdragon Summit 2026 dates confirmed

- Qualcomm confirmed **Snapdragon Summit 2026: September 22–24, Maui, Hawaii** — the venue for the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 launch `[official]`.
- Pre-event leaks: **two** flagship chips expected (Elite Gen 6 and a higher-tier variant), both on **TSMC 2nm**, AnTuTu leak ~4,835,412 for the top chip `[secondary]`.
- Qualcomm's OnQ blog confirmed the Oryon CPU would hit **5GHz** — a smartphone first — with a new **FlexCache** pooled-L2 design `[vendor-reported]`.

## B5. Chronology — July 2026 (Galaxy Unpacked, London, July 22)

### B5.1 Galaxy Z Fold 8 / Fold 8 Ultra / Z Flip 8

- Samsung's July 2026 Unpacked (London) split the book foldable into **two** models `[official via secondary]`:
  - **Galaxy Z Fold 8** (new wide "passport" format): 5.5" 120Hz AMOLED cover, 7.6" 4:3 inner display, **201g** (Samsung's lightest foldable), Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy, 12/16GB RAM, up to 1TB, 4,800 mAh, dual 50MP wide+ultrawide. From **$1,899** (`$1,934.98` list) / **€1,999** `[secondary]`.
  - **Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra** (direct Fold 7 successor): 6.5" cover / 8" inner, 200MP main + 50MP ultrawide + 10MP 3x telephoto, 5,000 mAh, 45W charging. From **$2,099.99** / **€2,199** `[secondary]`.
  - **Galaxy Z Flip 8**: 6.9" inner / 4.1" FlexWindow, 50MP main, 4,300 mAh, 25W wired, 180g, 6.1mm unfolded (thinnest/lightest Flip), "Flex Titanium" hinge materials. From **$1,199.99** / **€1,299** / £1,149 `[secondary]`.
- All three: IP48 rating, pre-orders from July 22, general availability **August 7, 2026** `[secondary]`.
- Note: pre-launch rumors of an Exynos 2600 in the Flip 8 proved wrong; all shipped with Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy `[secondary]`.

### B5.2 Galaxy Watch 9 / Watch Ultra 2

- **Galaxy Watch 9** (40/44mm): Snapdragon **Wear Elite SDW6100 (3nm)**, 1.3"/1.5" Super AMOLED 3000 nits, sapphire crystal, 390/445 mAh batteries, Bluetooth 6.0, MIL-STD-810. Pre-order July 22, on sale August 7 `[secondary]`.
- **Galaxy Watch Ultra 2** (LTE): rugged line refresh, leaked €749 `[secondary]`.
- Samsung teased upcoming **intelligent eyewear** for later in 2026 `[vendor-reported]`.
