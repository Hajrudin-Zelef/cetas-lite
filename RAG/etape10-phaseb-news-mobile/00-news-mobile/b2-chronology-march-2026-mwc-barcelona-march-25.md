# B2. Chronology — March 2026 (MWC Barcelona, March 2–5)

## B2. Chronology — March 2026 (MWC Barcelona, March 2–5)

- MWC 2026's dominant theme was **AI across the board**, per Counterpoint analysts on the ground `[independent]`.

### B2.1 Xiaomi at MWC (February 28 / March 2026)

- Xiaomi launched the **Xiaomi 17 and Xiaomi 17 Ultra** globally on February 28, 2026, plus the surprise **Leica Leitzphone** commemorating Leica's 100th anniversary `[secondary]`.
- **Xiaomi 17 Ultra:** 1-inch LOFIC main sensor (50MP, f/1.67), **200MP variable telephoto (75–100mm, 3.2x–4.3x optical)**, 50MP ultrawide; Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5; 6.9" HyperRGB OLED; 6,000 mAh battery (6,800 mAh China variant); 90W wired, 50W wireless `[secondary]`.
- **Global price:** Xiaomi 17 Ultra from **€1,499** `[secondary]`.
- **Xiaomi 17:** compact flagship, Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, 6,330 mAh battery `[secondary]`.
- **Leica Leitzphone:** co-developed device with a physical **Camera Ring dial** and Leica Essential shooting mode `[secondary]`.
- Also shown: Xiaomi Pad 8 / Pad 8 Pro (144Hz, 5.75mm thin), Xiaomi Watch 5, an AirTag-like tracker, an ultra-slim power bank, and an electric hypercar concept for Gran Turismo `[secondary]`.

### B2.2 Honor: Magic V6 foldable + Robot Phone

- Honor launched the **Magic V6** book-style foldable: **4.0mm unfolded / 8.75mm folded** (thinnest at the time), 7.95" AMOLED inner display, 6.52" cover display, **6,600–7,000 mAh battery** (reports vary `[conflict]`), Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, Super Steel hinge rated 500,000 folds, LTPO 2.0 1–120Hz `[secondary]`.
- Honor showed the industry's first **"Robot Phone"** with agentic AI and ARRI cinema color-science partnership, plus a humanoid robot on stage; part of Honor's "Alpha Plan" `[independent]`.
- Honor highlighted cross-ecosystem work with Apple (notification sync, iCloud Connect, iWork editing demos) `[independent]`.
- Also: Honor MagicPad 4 (thinnest tablet claim, Snapdragon 8 Gen 5, 4.8mm body) and MagicBook Pro 14 global editions `[secondary]`.

### B2.3 Motorola: razr fold

- Motorola detailed the **razr fold**, its first book-style foldable (introduced at CES 2026): 8.1" 2K LTPO inner display, 6.6" outer display, first phone with **Corning Gorilla Glass Ceramic 3** (claimed 75% better drop performance), and a **FIFA World Cup 26 Collection** edition `[secondary]`.

### B2.4 vivo, OPPO, and camera flagships

- **vivo X300 Ultra** teased: 200MP sensor, Zeiss Telephoto Extender Gen 2 (world's first 400mm teleconverter kit), 4K 120fps 10-bit Log across all focal lengths `[secondary]`.
- **OPPO Find X9 Ultra** debuted globally at MWC 2026 after its China launch `[secondary]`.

### B2.5 Concepts and components

- **TECNO** showed a modular smartphone concept: 4.9mm base device with ~10 magnetic/wireless modules `[secondary]`.
- **TCL CSOT** showed a 165Hz Super Pixel display (pointing to a Redmi K100 upgrade) and NXTPAPER AMOLED tech `[secondary]`.
- **Infinix NOTE 60 Ultra**: 6.78" 1.5K 144Hz AMOLED, Dimensity 8400 Ultimate, rear Active Matrix Display, 200MP main + 50MP 3.5x periscope, **7,000 mAh** battery, two-way satellite calling, Pininfarina design `[secondary]`.
- **Nubia Neo 5 GT**: built-in fan, 4500-nit OLED, Dimensity 7400, 550Hz shoulder keys, **€399** `[secondary]`.
- **Qualcomm** announced the **Snapdragon Wear Elite** platform for smartwatches at MWC 2026 `[secondary]` (shipped in Galaxy Watch 9, July — see B6).

## B3. Chronology — April–May 2026

### B3.1 Q1 2026 market data

- Analyst firms (IDC, Counterpoint, Canalys) reported Q1 2026 smartphone shipments roughly flat to low-single-digit growth; Samsung and Apple led, Xiaomi held third `[independent]`. Exact vendor splits vary by firm `[conflict]`.
- Foldable shipments continued growing off a small base; book-style foldables gained share vs. clamshells `[independent]`.

### B3.2 Spring phone launches

- **Huawei** continued its China-first flagship cadence (Pura series) with HarmonyOS Next devices `[secondary]`; global availability remained limited `[secondary]`.
- Mid-cycle refreshes: OnePlus, realme, Redmi and iQOO spring editions with Snapdragon 8s-series and Dimensity chips `[secondary]`.
- **Nothing** Phone (4) rumors circulated for a summer launch `[unverified]`.

### B3.3 Mobile security & ecosystem news

- Supply-chain security stayed in the news (npm/Shai-Hulud-style incidents spilling into mobile build tooling discourse) `[secondary]`.
- Carriers expanded **satellite messaging** partnerships (see B9); T-Mobile/SpaceX-style D2D texting moved toward broader availability `[secondary]`.
