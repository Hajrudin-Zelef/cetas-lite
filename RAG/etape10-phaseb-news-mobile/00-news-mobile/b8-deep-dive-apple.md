# B8. Deep dive — Apple

## B8. Deep dive — Apple

### B8.1 iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max

- **Chip:** A20 Pro (Apple silicon; detailed specs via teardowns pending) `[secondary]`.
- **Design:** aerospace-grade aluminum unibody replacing titanium (Apple claims 20x thermal conductivity vs. titanium), laser-welded vapor chamber with deionized water, edge-to-edge camera plateau `[vendor-reported]`.
- **Cameras:** 48MP main + 48MP ultrawide + 48MP telephoto (8x optical-quality zoom) — all-48MP system `[secondary]`.
- **Battery:** Pro Max up to 39 hours video playback (Apple's longest) `[vendor-reported]`.
- **Storage:** 256GB base across the line; up to 2TB (now also on the smaller Pro) `[official]`.
- **Colors:** Burgundy, Glacier, Silver, Black `[secondary]`.
- **Prices:** $1,199 / $1,299 (US); €/£ equivalents higher; India ₹1,34,900+ for Pro `[secondary]`.

### B8.2 iPhone Duo (foldable)

- First foldable iPhone; 5.4" outer display; inner display size not officially detailed at keynote `[secondary]`.
- **IP68** dust-tight + water-resistant — first foldable with full IP68 alongside Pixel 11 Pro Fold `[secondary]`.
- $1,999 (256GB); pre-order Oct 16, on sale Oct 23, 2026 `[official]`.
- iOS 27.1 out of the box; $79 USB-C Apple Pencil support; custom case/folio with kickstand `[secondary]`.
- Samsung publicly mocked the Duo's crease ("still there, but barely") `[secondary]`.

### B8.3 iOS 27 & Apple Intelligence

- Released September 14, 2026 for iPhone 11 and later (iPhone 11 → iPhone 18 series) `[secondary]`.
- **Siri AI** beta: personal context (Messages/Mail/Photos), on-screen awareness, Camera-app mode; English first; EU excluded at launch; under-13 excluded; daily usage caps with paid expanded access via iCloud+ `[secondary]`.
- **Memory Integrity Enforcement**: new anti-mercenary-spyware hardening `[secondary]`.
- Visual Intelligence (Apple's Circle-to-Search equivalent), Live Translation `[secondary]`.

### B8.4 Watches & AirPods 5

- Series 12 ($399) / Ultra 4 ($799): S11 chip, 4GB RAM, 64GB storage, Health Sensing System, Readiness, Recovery HRV, daytime vitals, Sleep Score, hypertension notifications (30-day trend; regulatory-gated in some regions), sleep-apnea notifications `[secondary]`.
- watchOS 27: dynamic app grid, tap gesture for Smart Stack, Workout Buddy without nearby iPhone, Siri AI (English) `[secondary]`.
- **AirPods 5** launched alongside (Sept 9, 2026); details in wearables section (B11).

### B8.5 Leadership change

- **John Ternus** became Apple CEO; Tim Cook became executive chairman after 15 years as CEO `[secondary]`. Ternus hosted the September 9 event `[secondary]`.

## B9. Deep dive — Samsung

### B9.1 Galaxy S26 family (Feb 2026)

- Full specs (see B1.1): the Ultra carries Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy globally; S26/S26+ use Exynos 2600 (2nm) in most regions, Snapdragon in US/China `[secondary]`.
- **Privacy Display** (Ultra only): hardware-level viewing-angle restriction combined with software — pitched at enterprise/public-space use `[vendor-reported]`.
- **60W charging** on the Ultra ("Super Fast Charging 3.0"): ~75% in 30 minutes; 45W on S26+, 25W on S26 `[secondary]`.
- Review consensus: evolutionary hardware, strong AI software story (Photo Assist natural-language edits, Creative Studio, AI Document Scan), 7-year updates; price-hike criticism in some markets `[secondary]`.
- Samsung positioned 2026 as the "AI-first flagship" year, opening to third-party AI services (Perplexity integrations discussed) `[secondary]`.

### B9.2 Foldables (Jul 2026)

- Strategy split: **Z Fold 8** (wide/passport format, 201g, $1,899) for portability vs. **Z Fold 8 Ultra** (tall 8" format, 200MP camera, $2,099) for power users; **Z Flip 8** ($1,199) iterative `[secondary]`.
- The wide Fold 8 was designed to preempt the rumored iPhone foldable's aspect ratio `[secondary]`.
- All foldables: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy, IP48, One UI on Android 16, 7-year updates `[secondary]`.
- Flex Titanium hinge materials on the Flip 8 (titanium-alloy film + plate) `[vendor-reported]`.

### B9.3 Exynos 2600

- Samsung's first **2nm** mobile SoC (SF2 process), Xclipse 960 GPU; powers S26/S26+ in most regions `[secondary]`.
- Marked Samsung Foundry's return to flagship competitiveness after the Exynos 2500's limited deployment `[secondary]`.
- Performance-per-watt claims vs. S25 generation are vendor-reported; independent sustained-performance testing was mixed-to-positive `[independent/secondary]`.

### B9.4 Galaxy Watch 9 / Watch Ultra 2

- First watches on **Snapdragon Wear Elite SDW6100 (3nm)** — announced at MWC 2026, shipped July 2026 `[secondary]`.
- Watch 9: 40/44mm, Super AMOLED 3000 nits, sapphire, 390/445 mAh, Wear OS, Bluetooth 6.0 `[secondary]`.
- Watch Ultra 2: rugged refresh, LTE, ~€749 leaked pricing `[secondary]`.
- Samsung teased own-brand **intelligent eyewear** for late 2026 `[vendor-reported]`.
