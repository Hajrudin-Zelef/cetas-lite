# Step 10 — Phase B: Mobile & Devices News (February → September 2026)

## B0. Scope, method, provenance

- **Scope:** smartphone, wearable, tablet and smart-glasses news from February 2026 through September 22, 2026. Month-by-month chronology first, then per-brand/device deep dives, chipset analysis, OS/ecosystem, market data, reviews consensus, gaps register, glossary, source index.
- **Date range:** 2026-02-01 → 2026-09-22 (cutoff). Events are dated individually.
- **Method:** read-only web research (news outlets, vendor announcements, analyst firms). Claims carry one provenance tag:
  - `[official]` — vendor press release / keynote / spec sheet.
  - `[vendor-reported]` — vendor claim via media (benchmarks, "world's first").
  - `[independent]` — lab test, analyst firm, teardown.
  - `[secondary]` — tech press reporting.
  - `[unverified]` — rumor, leak, single-source or unconfirmed claim.
  - `[conflict]` — sources disagree; details preserved.
- **Important timeline correction:** the iPhone 17 series launched in **September 2025** (with iOS 26) `[secondary]`. The September **2026** Apple event launched the **iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max**, the foldable **iPhone Duo**, Apple Watch Series 12 / Ultra 4, AirPods 5, and **iOS 27** `[official]`. Claims in this file use the verified 2026 names.

## B1. Chronology — February 2026

### B1.1 Samsung Galaxy Unpacked: Galaxy S26 series (February 25, 2026)

- Samsung unveiled the Galaxy S26, S26+ and S26 Ultra at Galaxy Unpacked in **San Francisco on February 25, 2026** `[secondary]`. General sales began **March 11, 2026** in India and other first-wave markets `[secondary]`.
- **US pricing:** S26 from **$799**, S26+ from **$999**, S26 Ultra from **$1,299** `[secondary]`. Samsung reportedly absorbed a 10–15% margin hit to hold prices near S25 levels `[secondary]`.
- **India pricing:** S26 ₹79,999 / ₹87,999 (reports vary `[conflict]`), S26+ ₹1,19,999, S26 Ultra ₹1,39,999 `[secondary]`.
- **Philippines pricing:** S26 12/256GB PHP 58,990; S26+ PHP 74,990; S26 Ultra 12/256GB PHP 86,990, 16GB/1TB PHP 121,990 `[secondary]`.
- **Chips:** Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy in the Ultra (global), Exynos 2600 (Samsung's first **2nm** mobile SoC) in S26/S26+ in most regions including India `[secondary]`.
- **Headline feature:** world's first built-in **Privacy Display** on the S26 Ultra — hardware+software viewing-angle restriction `[vendor-reported]`.
- **Charging:** 60W wired on the Ultra ("Super Fast Charging 3.0", ~75% in 30 min); 25W on S26, 45W on S26+ `[secondary]`.
- **Cameras (Ultra):** 200MP main, 50MP ultrawide, 10MP 3x telephoto, 50MP 5x telephoto, 12MP front `[secondary]`.
- **Batteries:** 4,300 mAh (S26), 4,900 mAh (S26+), 5,000 mAh (Ultra); Qi2 wireless on the line `[secondary]`.
- **Software:** One UI 8.5 on Android 16, **7 years** of OS and security updates `[secondary]`.
- **Displays:** 6.3" FHD+ (S26), 6.7" QHD+ (S26+), 6.9" QHD+ (Ultra); 1–120Hz adaptive `[secondary]`.
- Review consensus (March–April): "highly polished, mostly incremental" — Privacy Display and 60W charging the real differentiators; 4.3/5-style scores `[secondary]`.

### B1.2 Xiaomi 17 China launch (February 2026)

- Xiaomi launched the Xiaomi 17 series in China ahead of its global MWC reveal `[secondary]`. The global debut followed on **February 28, 2026** in Barcelona (see B1.3).
- The China launch confirmed the Leica partnership's deeper technical integration and the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 platform `[secondary]`.
