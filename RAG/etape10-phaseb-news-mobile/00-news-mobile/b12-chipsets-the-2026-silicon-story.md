# B12. Chipsets — the 2026 silicon story

## B12. Chipsets — the 2026 silicon story

### B12.1 Qualcomm: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 / Extreme Gen 6 (Sept 22, 2026)

- Announced at Snapdragon Summit 2026, Maui (Sept 22–24) — on the cutoff day `[official]`.
- **Gen 6**: TSMC 2nm; Oryon CPU to **5GHz** (smartphone first); FlexCache pooled L2; Element Accelerator (transformer); NPU shared memory +50%; sensing hub (2 AI cores, ≤200M-param models, speaker recognition, Personal Scribe; claimed +85% perf, +20% efficiency) `[vendor-reported]`.
- **Extreme Gen 6**: on-device **30B MoE** inference; 8K60 / 4K240 video; APV codec; AI vocal boost + voice-bubble calls `[vendor-reported]`.
- First dual-flagship strategy; powers 2027 Android flagships (Galaxy S27 expected); Motorola Signature 27 first announced design win `[secondary]`.
- Pre-launch AnTuTu leak: ~4,835,412 (top chip) `[unverified]`.

### B12.2 Qualcomm: Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 (2025 → 2026 flagships)

- Announced Sept 24, 2025; powered Galaxy S26 Ultra, Xiaomi 17 series, Honor Magic V6, Galaxy Z Fold/Flip 8, OPPO Find X9 series `[secondary]`.

### B12.3 Google Tensor G6

- TSMC 2nm; +25% browsing / +15% app launches / +50% TPU (Google claims); MediaTek modem; Titan M3 post-quantum secure boot `[vendor-reported]`.
- Positioned for AI/camera efficiency over raw benchmarks `[secondary]`.

### B12.4 Apple A20 Pro

- Powers iPhone 18 Pro/Max and iPhone Duo; process node and core counts via teardowns pending at cutoff `[secondary]`.

### B12.5 Samsung Exynos 2600

- 2nm (SF2), Xclipse 960 GPU; S26/S26+ in most regions `[secondary]`.

### B12.6 MediaTek Dimensity

- Dimensity 9400-series powered 2026 flagships (vivo, OPPO); next-gen Dimensity details thin at cutoff — gap `[unverified]`.

### B12.7 Wearable silicon

- **Snapdragon Wear Elite SDW6100 (3nm)**: Galaxy Watch 9 / Ultra 2 `[secondary]`.
- **Snapdragon W5 Gen 2**: Pixel Watch 5 `[secondary]`.
