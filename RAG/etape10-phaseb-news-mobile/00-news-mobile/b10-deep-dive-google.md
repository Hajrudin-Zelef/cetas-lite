# B10. Deep dive — Google

## B10. Deep dive — Google

### B10.1 Pixel 11 family (Aug 2026)

- Four models: 11 ($899), 11 Pro ($1,099), 11 Pro XL ($1,299), 11 Pro Fold ($1,899); all 256GB base `[official]`.
- **Tensor G6** (TSMC 2nm): +25% browsing, +15% app launches, +50% TPU vs. G5 (Google claims); MediaTek modem; Titan M3 with post-quantum secure boot `[vendor-reported]`.
- **Pixel 11 Pro Fold**: IP68, thinner bezels, claimed 3x durability vs. Pixel 10 Pro Fold; 48MP main + 10.5MP ultrawide + 10.8MP 5x telephoto `[secondary]`.
- **Gemini-centric**: Magic Capture, proactive assistance, Night Sight Instant, on-device Gemini Nano `[official]`.
- **Pixelsnap** magnetic Qi2.2 ecosystem (25W wireless, cases, ring stand) `[official]`.
- Price analysis: effective $100 increase per model vs. Pixel 10, offset by doubled base storage; Pro XL up $100 even accounting for storage `[secondary]`.
- Review consensus (late Aug–Sept): best-in-class AI/camera software, Tensor G6 efficiency praised, charging speeds still behind Chinese rivals, modem switch to MediaTek closely watched `[secondary]`.

### B10.2 Android 17

- Stable release **June 2026**; Pixels first, then Samsung/OnePlus/Xiaomi rollouts through H2 `[secondary]`.
- Pixel 11 launched with Android 17; 7-year update policy (Pixel 8 and later) `[official]`.

### B10.3 Pixel Watch 5 & Pixel Tag

- Watch 5 (Aug 12, 2026): Snapdragon W5 Gen 2, 3000-nit AMOLED, 332/465 mAh, 64GB storage, Wi-Fi 6 `[secondary]`.
- Pixel Tag: Bluetooth item tracker in the Find Hub network `[secondary]`.
