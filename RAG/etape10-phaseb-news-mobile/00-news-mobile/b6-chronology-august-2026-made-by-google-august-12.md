# B6. Chronology — August 2026 (Made by Google, August 12)

## B6. Chronology — August 2026 (Made by Google, August 12)

### B6.1 Pixel 11 series

- Google unveiled four phones on **August 12, 2026** in New York City (hosted by Trevor Noah): **Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, Pixel 11 Pro Fold** `[secondary]`.
- Pre-orders opened the same day; on sale **August 20, 2026** `[official]`.
- **US pricing:** Pixel 11 **$899** (256GB), Pro **$1,099**, Pro XL **$1,299**, Pro Fold **$1,899** — 128GB tier dropped, 256GB now the base `[official]`.
- **India pricing:** ₹89,999 / ₹1,19,999 / ₹1,34,999 `[secondary]`.
- **Tensor G6** (Google's first **TSMC 2nm** chip): claimed +25% web browsing, +15% app launches, +50% TPU compute vs. Tensor G5; runs latest **Gemini Nano** on-device; new **MediaTek modem** (replacing Samsung modems) `[vendor-reported]`.
- **Titan M3** security chip with **post-quantum cryptography** for secure boot `[official]`.
- **Cameras:** Pixel 11 — 48MP main + 48MP ultrawide + 10.8MP 5x telephoto, up to 30x Super Zoom; Pro models — 50MP main (new sensor), 48MP periscope telephoto (5x, 32% more light), up to **120x** zoom on Pro XL; 13MP ultrawide front camera `[secondary]`.
- **Battery/charging:** 4,985 mAh (Pixel 11), 30W wired, **25W Qi2.2** wireless (Pixelsnap magnetic), bypass charging, reverse wired charging `[secondary]`.
- **Display:** 6.3" OLED 120Hz, up to 3,000 nits peak, Gorilla Glass Victus 2 `[secondary]`.
- **AI features:** Magic Capture, proactive Gemini assistance, Night Sight Instant; LED "HiLight" ring around the camera flash as a notification/Gemini indicator `[secondary]`.
- **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi 6E/7, Bluetooth 6.0, IP68, ultrasonic in-display fingerprint, Satellite SOS `[secondary]`.
- **Software:** Android 17, **7 years** of updates `[official]`.

### B6.2 Pixel Watch 5

- Announced August 12, 2026 alongside the phones `[secondary]`.
- Snapdragon **W5 Gen 2**, AMOLED 3000 nits (vs 2000 on Watch 3), 332 mAh (41mm) / 465 mAh (45mm), 2GB RAM / 64GB storage, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 6.0, Wear OS `[secondary]`.
- Colors: Canyon, Olive, Fog, Obsidian; prices carried over roughly from Watch 3 ($349/$399) `[secondary]`.

### B6.3 Pixel Tag

- Google's Bluetooth item tracker launched alongside the Pixel 11 line `[secondary]`.

### B6.4 Galaxy Tab S11 (late August 2026)

- Samsung's Galaxy Tab S11 series launched in the August window with high-refresh AMOLED displays and S Pen support `[secondary]`; full pricing/specs varied by region `[unverified]`.

## B7. Chronology — September 2026

### B7.1 Apple "Surprise and Shine" event (September 9, 2026)

- Apple's fall event took place **Wednesday, September 9, 2026**, at Apple Park — the first iPhone event hosted by new CEO **John Ternus** (Tim Cook moved to executive chairman) `[secondary]`.
- Announced: **iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max**, foldable **iPhone Duo**, **Apple Watch Series 12**, **Apple Watch Ultra 4**, **AirPods 5** `[official]`.
- **iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max:** A20 Pro chip; aluminum unibody with edge-to-edge camera bar; vapor chamber; all three rear cameras 48MP (main/ultrawide/telephoto with **8x optical-quality** zoom); Pro Max up to **39 hours** video playback; 2TB option now on the smaller Pro too. Colors: Burgundy, Glacier, Silver, Black `[secondary]`.
- **Pricing:** Pro from **$1,199** (256GB), Pro Max from **$1,299**; UK £1,199/£1,299; Canada $1,749/$1,899 `[secondary]`.
- **iPhone Duo** (first foldable iPhone): 5.4" outer display, **IP68 dust-tight** (beats Galaxy Z Fold 8's IP48), from **$1,999** (256GB); pre-orders **October 16**, on sale **October 23**; ships with iOS 27.1; works with a $79 USB-C Apple Pencil (not Pencil Pro); no Action Button `[secondary]`.
- The **standard iPhone 18 is delayed to spring 2027** (with iPhone 18e and iPhone Air 2) — first split-season iPhone lineup `[secondary]`.
- Pre-orders for Pro models: September 12; on sale **September 18** in 65+ countries `[official]`.
- **iOS 27 / watchOS 27** released **September 14, 2026**; Siri AI in beta (English first; FR/JA/KO/PT/ES in October; not in EU at launch; under-13 excluded) `[secondary]`.
- **Apple Watch Series 12** ($399): S11 chip, Health Sensing System (HR every 5 sec), Readiness score, Ceramic Shield 2 (60% tougher), ceramic case option from $899; **Ultra 4** ($799): same sensors, 50-hr battery, unchanged 49mm titanium case `[secondary]`.
- No blood-pressure alerts, no Touch ID, no glucose sensing on 2026 watches despite rumors `[secondary]`.

### B7.2 Snapdragon Summit (September 22–24, 2026, Maui)

- On **September 22, 2026** (cutoff day), Qualcomm announced **two** flagship chips `[official]`:
  - **Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6**: TSMC 2nm, Oryon CPU up to **5GHz** (smartphone first), FlexCache pooled L2, new Element Accelerator for transformers, +50% shared NPU memory, always-on **sensing hub** running ≤200M-parameter models (speaker recognition, Personal Scribe) `[vendor-reported]`.
  - **Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6**: higher tier; runs **30B-parameter MoE** models fully on-device; 8K60 and 4K240 video; Advanced Professional Video (APV) codec; AI vocal boost / voice bubble call isolation `[vendor-reported]`.
- Both target 2027 flagships (Galaxy S27 series expected); no launch phones named, only "major global brands" `[official]`.
- At the event, **Motorola** announced the **Motorola Signature 27** on the Extreme Gen 6, availability later in 2026 `[secondary]`.

### B7.3 September market notes

- Foldable competition intensified: iPhone Duo ($1,999, IP68) vs. Galaxy Z Fold 8 ($1,899, IP48) vs. Pixel 11 Pro Fold ($1,899, IP68) — Apple and Google both beat Samsung on dust protection `[secondary]`.
- Memory-chip shortages continued pressuring 2026 phone pricing (analysts flagged higher-storage variant premiums) `[independent]`.
